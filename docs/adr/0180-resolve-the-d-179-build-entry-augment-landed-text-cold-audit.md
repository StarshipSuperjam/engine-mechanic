# Resolve the D-179 Build-entry augment: landed-text cold audit clean (design), blocking Q38-deletion + serious core-wire findings resolved, four docs re-locked

*Decided 2026-06-06 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md) change-set is landed and **re-locked**. The mandatory landed-text cold audit ran with four independent no-shared-context lenses (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) on the as-written [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md), [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md), [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md), [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) edits, [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md), [R11](../reference/risks.md), the two scenarios, and wbs/core-build-roadmap.md slices 17/21/26. **The augment design cleared on all four lenses** — no over-claim (the "not a stronger gate" framing and the "never 'I cannot'" honest tier survive verbatim), no self-electable path, merge wall intact, Routine untouched, no [§12](../principles.md) jargon leak in operator-facing copy, and the [D-178](0178-resolve-the-d-177-plan-file-carve-out-landed-text-cold-audit.md) carrier supersession surgically correct (only the hook-firing half superseded; the Issue-logging half preserved, ground-truthed against the text). **Findings resolved before re-lock:**
  - **(blocking — adversarial/architect) [Q38](../reference/open-questions.md) still carried the falsified claim as live truth.** [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md) prescribed deleting Q38, but until done the corpus held the retracted "`ExitPlanMode` is not reliably observable to a hook" claim *outside* the append-only log, as an open question's premise (`validate.py` cannot catch this — the link resolves). **Resolved:** Q38 deleted from [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) (CLAUDE.md history-exception — resolution preserved here + in [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)).
  - **(serious — adversarial) [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md)'s `wires` `PostToolUse` cell undercounted owners.** As first written it named only the modes trigger, contradicting the locked [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md) owner inventory (validation · telemetry · modes) and the sibling `PreToolUse` cell's multi-owner enumeration. Ground-truthed: [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) (`PostToolUse` touched-file subset run) and [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md) (the `PostToolUse` writer) are both core-provided `PostToolUse` consumers. **Resolved:** the cell now enumerates all three. **Correcting [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)'s carrier note (append-only — D-179 stands; this supersedes the wording):** `PostToolUse` was **already** engine-wired by validation/telemetry; the modes consumer is the *new addition*, not "a new `PostToolUse` wire."
  - **(nit, folded) [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) acceptance bullet** said the plan-exit completion is "observable to **the gate**" — conflating it with the Explore `PreToolUse` write-gate (a distinct hook); corrected to "observable to **a hook**," and an explicit **fail-safe-to-Explore** clause added at the acceptance path (matching [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)'s prescribed durable principle).
  - **(nits dispositioned, no edit)** the leaf's "several target modes" is kept (more durable than pinning "three" against a future accept-mode); the [scenarios/product-design-intake.md](../architecture.md#product-design-intake) "(a direct 'build this')" gloss and an explicit slice-21 headless/Routine false-fire case are optional polish (Routine-cannot-fire is already established in [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md) + modes); [engine-architecture](../architecture.md) §7 staying the most abstract statement (it names no trigger) is correct under the law-not-leaf convention.
  **Re-lock set (4):** [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md), [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md), [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md), [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md). **Re-lock:** `python3 lock.py --relock` run on all four under this decision; `python3 validate.py` green. **[Q38](../reference/open-questions.md) resolved** (deleted).

## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious finding resolved before re-lock; it ran, the design cleared on all four lenses, and the two real findings both *strengthened* the change rather than reversing it — the core wire now matches the locked hooks inventory (and D-179's carrier note is corrected), and the modes text no longer conflates the acceptance `PostToolUse` hook with the Explore `PreToolUse` gate and states its fail-safe explicitly. The augment is landed: interactive Build is entered by the operator-typed verb **or** by accepting a plan, the falsified "not hook-observable" claim is corrected with the durable principle in the locked docs and the dated mechanism in the log + build-spec leaf, and [R11](../reference/risks.md) is de-risked.

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock with the as-first-written core `PostToolUse` cell** (rejected — it undercounted owners against the locked hooks inventory and read as if modes owned `PostToolUse` outright). **Leave [Q38](../reference/open-questions.md) in place** (rejected — a resolved question carrying the retracted claim outside the append-only log corrodes auditability; the history-exception rule requires deletion on resolution). **Keep "observable to the gate"** (rejected — it conflates the acceptance `PostToolUse` hook with the Explore `PreToolUse` write-gate, two distinct hooks). **Defer the re-lock** (rejected — the audit cleared; leaving four locked docs fingerprint-failing is the silent-drift state the lock gate forbids).
