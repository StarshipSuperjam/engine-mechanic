# Lock the `provisioning` system (terminal foundation lock): the bootstrap-paradox subsystem, reworked by a two-pass cold audit

*Decided 2026-05-26 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Lock [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md), the **last `designed` system** (the other 25 are locked) and the **terminal foundation lock** — after it the foundation layer is fully ratified and WBS authoring is blocked only on the independent cognitive foundations-as-required-packages reconciliation. All of provisioning's upstream anchors — [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md), [module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md), [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md), [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md), [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md), [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md), [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md), [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md), [state](../spec/systems/cognitive/state.md) — are already locked, so this is a clean upstream-anchored lock with **no neighbor re-lock**. The lock folds in nine provisioning-side reworks from the audit and disposes of the two residual open questions. **The reworks:** (1) the migration version-mismatch is a **migration-owned check** whose finding *surfaces* via [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)'s open-findings path on the protected-branch precedent (detection by the substrate, surfacing by boot) — boot needs no change; (2) the bootstrap **surfacing split** is stated as three clean owners (control-plane = contract, provisioning = mechanism, boot = the standing surface and the offer, per boot's locked orientation); (3) a **hard coherence finding at first-run pauses the apply phase** — the engine never proceeds on inconsistent wiring — surfacing *what* is inconsistent and one concrete next action (retry-after-repair or abort-and-report), resumable from the manifest checkpoint (user decision: pause-and-surface, not fail-open); (4) the ungated-instantiator bound is reframed from "no protected branch" to **resumability + reviewability** (interrupted runs re-enter idempotently; brownfield changes are surfaced and reviewable before they land; greenfield has no product to corrupt); (5) the brownfield **collision check also detects a product CODEOWNERS rule that shadows an engine path**, each collision stated as a concrete consequence + an operator choice (accept / leave-as-is / abort), with dot-namespacing demoted to defense-in-depth; (6) `remove` **discloses that an unnamespaceable shared entry** (e.g., a `permission` the operator also holds) is conservatively left, per [module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md)'s locked reversal firewall; (7) collision-surfaced product content is **expected, not re-flagged** by coherence; (8) the degraded-bootstrap banner **branches its next action by cause, never a dead-end** (forward-to-admin / team-identity escape / honest standing-degraded by informed acceptance); (9) the opening is reworded for the bootstrap-paradox. **Q3/Q4 disposed (user decision):** the residual operator-facing **copy** is reclassified as **build-spec leaves** (laws-not-leaves, [D-052](0052-foundational-law-layer-closed-the-implementation-lock-order.md)) — a new `## Build-spec leaves` section fixes the governing law for each leaf (pre-bootstrap explanation, deselection-confirm, degraded banner, module-selection glosses, memory-backup setup UX, the reactive add-module *offer*) and the `## Open questions` section is deleted; the reactive "install the missing module?" path is a **leaf over the settled `add` mechanism**, not unsettled design. `python3 lock.py systems/infrastructure/provisioning/README.md --decision D-077`; ratified_by D-077. **Q3 and Q4 are resolved and deleted from [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md).**

## Why

The cold-session design audit ([D-018](0018-cold-session-design-audit-required-before-any-lock.md)) ran in **two passes**. The first (adversarial, technical-feasibility, non-engineer-operator, architect) ran on the `designed` doc and drove the nine reworks: technical-feasibility **fully passed** (every load-bearing platform fact verified true against current docs — Use-this-template copies files-not-settings, the default token cannot create rulesets, the required-check rename-deadlock, wholesale ruleset PUT ⇒ read-modify-write, the device-flow scope-persistence bug already guarded, hooks do not fire unregistered, CODEOWNERS last-match-wins, evaluated-rules readable with the default token); the `admin:repo_ruleset`-vs-fine-grained-"Administration" nit is a non-issue (the design uses `gh auth refresh` OAuth scopes, where the name is correct, and it is enshrined in locked [constraints](../reference/constraints.md)). The adversarial/architect **boot-detector "BLOCKING"** (provisioning had asserted boot *compares* code-vs-data versions, which boot's locked doc does not document) resolved provisioning-side on boot's own deferral law and the [D-076](0076-lock-the-audits-system-re-founded-for-the-deployed-repo-hygi.md) no-re-lock precedent; the architect **memory-contract "BLOCKING"** dissolved on cross-check (memory's locked text already sanctions the automatic pre-migration snapshot as "one mechanism, two consumers"). **Because the reworks changed the doc materially, the audit re-ran on the reworked pre-lock doc** (the gate audits the artifact actually being locked, not the prior draft): feasibility confirmed the reworks introduced no new false platform claim; adversarial returned no blocker; the **architect** caught a [blocking] seam — the surfacing-split sentence re-described boot's re-run offer instead of deferring to it (fixed) — and a [serious] wording imprecision in the detector seam (fixed to the protected-branch precedent); the **non-engineer-operator** lens caught three [blocking] law gaps — collisions needed consequence-framing + an operator choice, the coherence pause needed a plain-language + concrete-action law (and the auto-heal phrasing removed), and the degraded banner dead-ended the operator-is-owner-but-org-blocked case — all fixed as **laws** (not deferred as copy). Every blocking/serious finding was resolved before the lock; one operator over-reach (degrade a hard coherence finding to advisory when hard to explain) was **rejected** as a reintroduction of the fail-open the user ruled out. The foundations-as-required-packages reconciliation and the unauthored WBS were verified **not to churn** provisioning (it references the [D-024](0024-the-engine-is-upgradeable-versioned-packages-upgraded-by-ove.md) package *grammar* — `status: required`, `depends`, `migrations` — never a specific roster). Propagation per the matrix: provisioning (locked end-state), this entry; [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) (Q3 + Q4 deleted); [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) R1/R5/R7 firmed (each closes when *built*, not at this design lock) and their dangling Q3/Q4 links repointed; [scenarios/first-run.md](../architecture.md#first-run-provisioning), [scenarios/upgrade-the-engine.md](../architecture.md#upgrading-the-engine), [scenarios/add-a-module.md](../architecture.md#adding-a-module-to-a-live-repo) reconciled to the coherence-pause, the migration-detector surfacing, and the residual-entry note; [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) verified still a truthful index; [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md) terms already present (no add/rename); [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) forward-note unchanged.

## What we ruled out

**Re-litigate [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)** to add the migration-mismatch detector (rejected — detection is the migration's; surfacing rides boot's existing open-findings path on the protected-branch precedent, a build-spec leaf with no re-lock per [D-076](0076-lock-the-audits-system-re-founded-for-the-deployed-repo-hygi.md)). **Author all operator-facing copy before locking** (rejected on the user's call — the build-spec-leaf precedent of [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)/[build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)/[audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md); design *laws* are fixed at the lock, only wording defers to build). **Proceed-and-flag (fail-open) on a hard coherence finding at first-run** (rejected — it would make broken wiring the silent operating baseline; pause-and-surface refuses that). **Degrade a hard coherence finding to advisory when hard to explain** (rejected — the operator-comprehension requirement is met by a plain-language + concrete-action *law*, not by weakening the gate back to fail-open). **Design the reactive add-module path now** (rejected — its `add` mechanism is settled; the reactive trigger is a leaf over it, [R6](../reference/risks.md)). **Lock the reworked doc without re-auditing it** (rejected — the gate audits the artifact actually being locked; the second pass caught four findings the first could not see). **Defer the provisioning lock** (rejected — it is the terminal upstream-anchored foundation lock; all anchors are locked and the only pending neighbor work is independent).
