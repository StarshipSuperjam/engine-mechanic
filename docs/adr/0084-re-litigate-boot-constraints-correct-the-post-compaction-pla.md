# Re-litigate `boot` (+ constraints): correct the post-compaction platform fact; soften "unconditional" to floor-vs-pack; verify-presence double-fault legibility; anti-habituation deferred owner-open (Q18); reliance-blocking stale-count

*Decided 2026-05-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Under the litigation-alarm protocol (operator-approved as part of the cognitive-substrate remediation plan) and after a **dedicated four-lens cold audit** (adversarial, architect/propagation, technical-feasibility, non-engineer-operator), re-litigate [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) with five corrections plus an additive platform fact in [constraints](../reference/constraints.md): **(5a, constraints, own sub-step)** record the compaction facts — `SessionStart` `source:"compact"` *can* inject `additionalContext` but is **documented-yet-field-unreliable** (tracked upstream); `PostCompact` is observe-only; `PreCompact` blocks-not-injects; root `CLAUDE.md` is re-read/re-injected after compaction (substantially reliable, not airtight) — after a who-cited sweep found boot the only consumer and no prior decision relying on an *absent* hook. **(E1)** the event-table claim "there is no post-compact re-injection hook" was **false** — a documented `SessionStart` `compact`-source hook exists; the reliable post-compaction floor is the re-injected `CLAUDE.md` + next scent, and a full boot-pack re-render on the `compact` source is a **deferred enhancement** (field-unreliable, never a grounding guarantee, gated on upstream reliability + its own budget tier + a guarantee it does not re-fire memory's `SessionStart`-borne consolidation sweep). **(E2)** "unconditional/guaranteed" softened to **floor-vs-pack** at both the header and the design commitment — the hook-independent `CLAUDE.md` floor is unconditional, the `SessionStart` pack is fail-open and registration-conditional and rides on top when the hook runs. **(E3, B9b)** the floor's "notice an absence" expectation-setter is replaced by an **unconditional verify-presence** check against a **named present marker** a healthy boot always renders (a titled project-status card; exact title/form a build-spec leaf) — the operator checks for one named token (decidable by a first-timer with no prior healthy boot), the matching card satisfies it, and the "by contrast" sentence is folded in. **(E4, B11b)** "loud must never become noise" softened — boot only **renders**; varying/escalating a *standing* alarm across sessions needs cross-session state boot does not hold and **no v1 substrate owns** (telemetry's locked de-dup is issue-keyed, its triage-pressure a backlog count — neither varies a repeated alarm), tracked **owner-open** as [Q18](../reference/open-questions.md); v1's mitigation is that governance alarms are **actionable, not merely loud** (the protected-branch alarm offers to fix it — B12 — so the operator clears it rather than habituating), with the non-actionable residual the subject of Q18. **(E5, S6b)** the degraded stale-count render replaces "possibly stale" with the **reliance-blocking** string byte-identical to [state](../spec/systems/cognitive/state.md)'s ([D-080](0080-re-litigate-state-honesty-name-the-floor-s-known-unbounded-f.md)). `python3 lock.py --relock systems/lifecycle/boot/README.md --decision D-084`; ratified_by D-084.

## Why

The efficacy scorecard found the post-compaction row stated a **false platform fact** (blocking), B9b (double-fault legibility) and B11b (alarm fatigue) Weak, "unconditional" an overclaim of a fail-open hook, and S6b's "possibly stale" too soft. The pre-relock cold audit drove four corrections to the drafted edits: B9b had to **name a concrete present marker** (verify-presence otherwise merely relocates the absence-notice — adversarial + operator lenses converged); B11b's Q18 had to be **owner-open, not telemetry-owned** (telemetry's locked design defines no anti-habituation channel; naming it owner asserts a seam [§16](../principles.md) forbids — architect) and to **lean on B12's offer-to-fix** as the real v1 mitigation (operator); E1 had to carry the **non-guarantee forcefully** plus the consolidation-sweep-re-fire risk (the sweep rides `SessionStart`, confirmed by technical-feasibility); E2 had to reconcile **both** "unconditional" instances and the master index. Technical-feasibility verified all four hook facts against current docs and that the compact-source injection is field-unreliable (upstream issues), while the `CLAUDE.md` re-injection floor is substantially reliable (disk reload) but not airtight — so v1 leans on the floor **plus** the next scent, not either alone. **[hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md) verified consistent — no edit, no relock:** its declension of `PostCompact` (the observe-only event) for capture-duplication is true and is untouched by correcting boot's *re-injection* claim; `SessionStart`-compact is a SessionStart variant boot already owns. Propagation per the matrix: [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) (re-locked end-state), [constraints.md](../reference/constraints.md) (5a additive), this entry; [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) L156 reconciled and L92 ("post-compaction re-orient" family member) verified still truthful after E1 (no edit); [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) Q18 added (owner-open); [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) "Boot pack"/"Orientation event family" verified truthful — the full re-render is deferred, so the cold-start assembly definition stands (no edit); [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md) and [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md) verified consistent (no edit); scenarios reference boot generically (unaffected).

## What we ruled out

**Name telemetry the Q18 owner** (rejected — its locked design defines no standing-alarm anti-habituation channel; asserting it would be the §16 anti-pattern of binding to a seam the owner never declared). **Adopt the full re-render on `compact` now** (rejected — field-unreliable injection, and it would re-fire the consolidation sweep). **B9b verify-presence without naming a concrete marker** (rejected — merely relocates the absence-notice). **Settle B11b as a bare logged no-op** (rejected — a cop-out; the honest answer is the actionable-alarm/B12 mitigation plus an owner-open Q18). **Keep "notice an absence" wording** (rejected — a non-engineer cannot notice what they have never seen). **Give boot cross-session state to vary the alarm** (rejected — boot is read-only; cross-session state belongs to an owner, undecided in v1).
