# Litigate engine-template #332: ambient turn-deltas dominate memory recall; recall surfaces curated episodic records, not raw verbatim; re-litigate locked [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) (+ packaging bundle [memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5](../spec/modules/memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5.md)) — coupled re-lock lands under D-274

*Decided 2026-06-29 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Build's memory store, in operation, exposed a tension the operator asked this workspace to litigate ([engine-template #332](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/332), engine-opened): memory recall keeps surfacing the **raw, turn-by-turn deltas** the engine appends every `Stop` hook instead of the **curated episodic records** written at consolidation. In the live store ~1,800 raw deltas vs ~43 episodic records; being verbatim, the deltas lexically out-match paraphrased records on FTS5/BM25 and **crowd the curated recall out of every result** — the expensive consolidation produces output almost nothing surfaces. (The separable capture-quality bug — storing Claude Code's command scaffolding as deltas — is fixed in engine-template PR #331 and is not this question.) **The litigation:** the locked design already says recall is **"episodic curation, not raw verbatim"** ([Capture](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md), [D-008](0008-memory-and-knowledge-are-distinct-substrates.md)), the turn delta is **"an append, not a summarization"** ([Durability](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)), and **"ambient (untagged) deltas simply do not participate"** ([the memory↔knowledge link](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)); the build's real non-conformance is that it made **ambient untyped deltas recall-eligible at all**, and the doc never states recall-membership explicitly, so a cold builder indexed the whole ledger. **The ruling (operator-chosen, option (b) of the issue's four): raw turn-deltas are capture fuel and the abandoned-session sweep's input — never recall content.** `search`/scent recall surfaces only **typed episodic records and gists**; ambient turn-deltas are **excluded from the recall (FTS) index**, staying canonical in the ledger and remaining the sweep's fuel. This **realizes the locked recall contract** rather than adding new retirement machinery. **This entry is the litigation alarm + operator authorization** (given in-session over a four-lens plan-stage cold audit) **and the design of record**; the **coupled re-lock lands under [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)** after the mandatory **landed-text cold audit** — the [D-264](0264-authorize-git-native-retention-for-the-pre-migration-memory.md)/[D-265](0265-resolve-coupled-re-lock-of-memory-memory-substrate-sqlite-ft.md) authorize-then-resolve pattern, the carriers **edited-but-not-relocked** until D-274 (the sanctioned transient — `validate.py` flags their lock fingerprints until the relock).

  **The laws the carriers settle (a four-lens cold plan-audit reshaped the fix from the issue's (a) to (b); none is a deferred leaf):** **(1) Recall-membership is type-derived.** The recall index admits **typed episodic records and gists**; **ambient turn-deltas are excluded**. Because membership follows from **record type** (an intrinsic field), it **survives compaction's rebuild automatically** — the FTS5 index is rebuilt from the ledger and a delta is simply a record the rebuild does not insert into the recall index — so it needs **no per-record retirement marker** and never touches an existing line (the **append-only-live-writes** law holds: no in-place delta edit). **(2) Sweep-orthogonality.** A delta is **excluded from recall yet visible to the abandoned-session consolidation sweep** — the two are independent: the sweep keys on the **absence of a consolidation marker** ([Durability](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)), **never on recall-eligibility**, so the safety net ("an abandoned session loses the reflection, not the content") is untouched and the recall-exclusion of deltas cannot suppress recovery. **(3) Logical/recall-only — never a path to erasure.** Excluding deltas from recall is **not** a new erasure tributary: deltas remain physically resident and fully recoverable in the canonical ledger; physical removal stays reachable **only** through Layer-2's audit-adjudicated, merge-gated compaction. The locked **"demotion can never reach erasure"** firewall and the **"low usage never deletes what evidence has not retired"** guarantee both hold unchanged. **(4) Recall-completeness disclosure ([§7](../principles.md) honesty).** Once a session is consolidated, its raw turn-by-turn notes **do not appear in search** — the episodic record stands in for them — but they remain **recoverable on request** ("ask the Engine for the exact original wording"); this is named honestly at the point of consumption (the [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) digest), the same "names what it preserves and does not" discipline the doc applies to the FTS-absent fallback ("preserves availability, not latency"). **(5) The role-weight lever is available, not the fix.** The existing `frecency × role-weight × recency` demotion may legitimately weight curated roles as **observed-usage** policy — distinct from the forbidden **birth-prior** — as defense-in-depth, but is not the structural answer.

  **Carriers (coupled, one semantic event — the [D-049](0049-re-lock-topology-and-module-system-file-precise-codeowners-o.md)/[D-265](0265-resolve-coupled-re-lock-of-memory-memory-substrate-sqlite-ft.md) coupled-relock precedent):** [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) (the recall-membership law in *Retrieval*, the sweep-orthogonality note in *Durability/Active forgetting*, the recall-completeness disclosure obligation) and its packaging bundle [memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5](../spec/modules/memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5.md) (whose `provides` carries the capture + `search`-interface recall description) **iff** the bundle's text shifts; both re-lock under [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md) after the landed-text cold audit. **Recorded verify-no-edit dispositions (no re-lock — to be confirmed against the landed text, [D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md) discipline):** [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) (a generic recall renderer + per-prompt scent — it surfaces whatever the index holds; excluding deltas yields the *desired* curated scent, finding-content not mechanism — the [D-264](0264-authorize-git-native-retention-for-the-pre-migration-memory.md)/[D-265](0265-resolve-coupled-re-lock-of-memory-memory-substrate-sqlite-ft.md) boot precedent; a NIT for the build: a whole-session of excluded deltas must not enumerate per-item readouts); [scenarios/](../architecture.md#build-session-lifecycle) (no scenario walks delta-granularity capture/recall/consolidation); [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) (the memory role line is a label, no recall mechanics); wbs/ (no module added/removed, no dependency edge changed ⇒ no topological re-sort). **Non-locked propagation (under [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)):** [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md) (extend *Deferred consolidation* / *Ambient capture* to state deltas are fuel + sweep input excluded from recall — **no new term**, the [R6](../reference/risks.md)/[D-261](0261-establish-the-artifact-warrant-discipline-a-7-17-application.md) anti-sprawl precedent); [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) ([R24](../reference/risks.md) mitigation touch — recall-membership survives the rebuild; **no new R-number**, the recall-quality gap is the one being *closed*). **Build-owe (engine-template #332, [§7](../principles.md) honest tier — the spec-side fix lands here, the built engine still carries the disease):** recall-membership excludes ambient deltas (type-derived); type-derived membership survives the compaction rebuild; the recall-completeness disclosure + the verbatim-recovery path; whole-session exclusion collapses to one audits-digest line.

## Why

the design already promised curated recall and already names deltas as cheap capture fuel; an engine that drowns its 43 curated records under 1,800 verbatim appends is failing the [D-008](0008-memory-and-knowledge-are-distinct-substrates.md) narrative mandate on its own store. The honest, minimal fix is **not** new retirement machinery but to **realize the recall contract the locked doc already states** — deltas are fuel, recall is episodic — and to **close the doc ambiguity** (recall-membership was never written down) so a cold builder cannot repeat the error. Option (b) is preferred over the issue's (a) because the plan-stage cold audit showed (a)'s "retire deltas on consolidation" would add two new laws — a strict retire-after-marker **ordering** discipline and a **carried retirement bit across compaction** — to buy only a narrow, short-lived window of cross-session recall of *un-consolidated* verbatim, which is mostly the noise being removed; (b) **dissolves** both hazards (membership is type-derived, so nothing to order and nothing to carry) and is structurally simpler. The capability is memory's by [D-061](0061-lock-the-memory-system-wave-2-the-episodic-ledger-store-q3-b.md) (sole owner of the recall mechanism), so the recall-membership law lands in memory and its bundle; boot, scenarios, and the rest consume and widen nothing. The authorize→resolve-after-landed-text-audit split keeps the irreversible coupled re-lock behind its own clean gate ([D-264](0264-authorize-git-native-retention-for-the-pre-migration-memory.md)/[D-265](0265-resolve-coupled-re-lock-of-memory-memory-substrate-sqlite-ft.md)).

## What we ruled out

**(c) Leave as designed** (rejected — accepts raw verbatim dominating recall until a slow frecency age-out; defeats the [D-008](0008-memory-and-knowledge-are-distinct-substrates.md) narrative mandate and the "episodic curation, not raw verbatim" contract — the curated recall the operator was promised never surfaces). **(d) Rank curated records above raw without excluding** (rejected as the primary fix — a fixed "summaries outrank raw" boost is a **static birth-prior the design explicitly forbids** ("never guess importance at birth; observe from usage") and is brittle against a ~40:1 volume ratio where verbatim lexically out-matches paraphrase; the legitimate observed-usage role-weight lever remains available as defense-in-depth, law (5), but does not close the gap). **(a) Retire deltas on consolidation** (rejected — the issue's framing; the cold audit showed it adds a sweep-ordering law (retire strictly after the durable consolidation marker, or a crash-mid-consolidation silently loses the abandoned-session recovery) and a survive-the-rebuild carried-bit (or the bug silently returns after the first compaction), to buy a narrow window of mostly-noise cross-session recall — the heavier mechanism the burden-of-proof posture rejects when (b) reaches the same steady state with neither law). **Build-owe only, no locked-doc edit (the D-268 shape)** (rejected by the operator — unlike [D-268](0268-adjudicate-engine-template-250-the-15-weakening-guard-s-guar.md)'s path-roster leaf that the locked docs already delegated, here the recall-membership contract is **unwritten** in the locked doc (the very ambiguity that caused #332) and the recall-completeness disclosure is **new operator-facing design**; both need a home in canon, so the honest move is a narrow re-litigation that closes the ambiguity, not a log entry a future cold builder never reads). **Mint a new glossary term or a new risk entry** (rejected — [R6](../reference/risks.md)/[D-255](0255-sharpen-the-post-v1-revisit-triggers-for-product-knowledge-g.md) anti-sprawl; the existing terms + [R24](../reference/risks.md) carry it, and the recall-quality gap is being closed, not opened). **Write the [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md) resolve entry now** (rejected — it records a cold audit that runs later; authoring it pre-audit would dress un-audited as audited, the [§7](../principles.md) violation this pattern exists to prevent).
