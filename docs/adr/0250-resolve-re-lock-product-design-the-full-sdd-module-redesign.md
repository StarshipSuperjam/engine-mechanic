## Why

product-design is the keystone and locks last, atop five now-settled carriers (lock discipline: never lock atop churning neighbors). The full five-lens + wall audit was the maximum-rigor gate the keystone warranted and earned its keep: the wall lens proved the most delicate move (the engine mechanically validating a product-owned corpus + a CI lock-integrity check on product files) does not breach the wall or deadlock the product on removal, and the technical + operator lenses caught the two BLOCKINGs — the under-specified teeth (the central trust mechanism) and the operator-vocabulary leak (the precise [§17](../principles.md)/[D-120](0120-lock-core-the-root-module-the-closure-wave-s-terminal-ratifi.md) failure this whole re-litigation exists to prevent) — both remediated and cold-re-checked clean. The result is the capability the operator drove issue #237 toward: a committed, validated, operator-governed, un-skippable spec — an improvement on this workspace's own SDD substrate rather than a watered-down bolt-on, built by reusing the engine's locked grammar.
