## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious finding resolved before re-lock; it ran (four lenses), the adversarial and architect lenses passed clean, technical-feasibility confirmed every load-bearing claim against the as-built `telemetry.py`/schema, and the two genuine folds (the card-rendering obligation, the conflict-trigger framing) were applied in the as-written text before re-lock. The two operator-lens rejections were ground-truthed — the planning glossary is maintainer-layer, and the conservative "reduces" wording is the honest tier — and logged, not silently dropped. The design is faithful to [D-196](0196-authorize-the-standing-situation-pointer-advance-re-litigati.md): build-orchestration owns the derivation and the per-tracked-build advance, state owns the cursor file + schema + the refined law + the named seam, the value is derived and rides the PR to land at merge, and the honest tiers (posture step; derived-not-hand-typed; the cursor is the last-merged floor, not the live now/next) are named, never over-promised.

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock without the landed-text audit** (rejected — HARD lock gate). **Treat the glossary "jargon leak" as a blocking/serious finding** (rejected — ground-truthed as an over-read: the planning glossary is maintainer-layer, not a shipped operator surface; the term reads soundly and two other lenses confirmed it). **Strengthen "reduces" to "categorical improvement"** (rejected — [§7](../principles.md) honest tier; the mis-derivation residual persists, so the conservative claim is the correct one). **Edit [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md) or [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) to capture the change** (rejected — telemetry is a disjoint co-writer that attaches additively per [§16](../principles.md); the card-rendering obligation is a boot build-spec leaf named by cross-reference, not a boot doc edit — the architect lens decisively confirmed both stay verify-no-edit and the scope is exactly two docs).
