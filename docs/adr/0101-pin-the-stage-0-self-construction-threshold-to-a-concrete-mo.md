# Pin the stage-0 self-construction threshold to a concrete module subset; correct the orchestrator-persona miscount

*Decided 2026-05-28 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Pin the stage-0 harness §8 **exit criterion** — the *self-construction threshold* — from prose to a concrete **minimal module subset**: [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) (the pre-graph build-order root) + [core](../spec/modules/core.md) (the boot/[state](../spec/systems/cognitive/state.md)/[knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) floors, the [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) Explore write-gate, session-lifecycle, build-readiness, and the [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) workflow [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md)) + [validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md) (the validation engine + engine-self-validation corpus that supersedes the §4 seed validator) + [memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5](../spec/modules/memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5.md) (the memory floor, `required`, [D-086](0086-cognitive-foundations-as-required-packages-reconciliation-me.md)) + the [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) bootstrap (the protected-branch ruleset, a one-time *setting*). `routine-mode` and `audit-library` — also `required` — are **out of the minimal threshold** (unattended execution and hygiene are not needed to *first* reach self-construction). This resolves [Q20](../reference/open-questions.md) sub-question (c). **Correction:** Q20's parked candidate named an "orchestrator persona" and inventoried "the modes write-gate + state/memory floor" as loose items; the [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) doc fixes the **orchestrator as the session, not a persona**, and [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md) folds session-lifecycle, build-readiness, and the orchestration workflow into `core` — so the threshold names `core` (which carries the write-gate, the floors, and the orchestration workflow) rather than a non-existent persona or a sub-part inventory.

## Why

The stage-0 doc is the build-order preamble; its exit criterion gates the handoff from hand-built construction to a self-hosting build instance, so an unpinned prose criterion left that handoff point undecidable and [Q20](../reference/open-questions.md)(c) open. The subset is exactly the minimal capability to **boot-orient** (`core`'s boot/state/knowledge floors + the memory floor), **write-gate itself** (`core`'s modes Explore write-gate), **run the validation suite** (`validators-core`), and **drive build-orchestration** (`core`'s orchestration workflow + the control-plane bootstrap so a reviewed-PR merge gate exists), atop the pre-graph [topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) root. It is a *subset* of the five-package `required` roster plus the topology root and the one-time control-plane setting, consistent with [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md) (session-lifecycle/build-readiness folded into `core`) and [D-086](0086-cognitive-foundations-as-required-packages-reconciliation-me.md) (`memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5` `required`; the knowledge floor rides `core`). A four-lens cold audit of the governing plan ran before approval; its architect lens caught the "orchestrator persona" error and confirmed the subset is otherwise consistent with [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md)/[D-086](0086-cognitive-foundations-as-required-packages-reconciliation-me.md)/stage-0 §8 and **does not alter the WBS module-order graph** (it names existing packages; it adds no module and no dependency edge). The stage-0 doc is outside the validator's frontmatter/coverage/lock gates, so this lands **unlocked** and freely revisable. Pinned **independently** of the in-progress cross-repo external-contribution mode — the threshold is sound regardless of that mode's fate and must not ride a contested design. Propagation: wbs/stage-0-harness.md §8 pinned; [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) Q20(c) marked resolved and its meta-decision narrowed to the engine-mechanic's design/home; this entry. Verified no-change: [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) (the subset names existing packages and adds no edge — the threshold is not catalog-tracked; the architect lens confirmed no build-order-graph change), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) (a WBS detail; the master names no new system).

## What we ruled out

Leave the exit criterion as prose (rejected — the handoff point stays undecidable and [Q20](../reference/open-questions.md)(c) open). Name an "orchestrator persona" in the subset (rejected — [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) fixes the orchestrator as the session, not a persona; the orchestration *workflow* rides `core`, [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md)). Inventory `core`'s sub-parts (write-gate, floors, session-lifecycle) as separate threshold items (rejected — they are all `core`; naming `core` is the honest unit and avoids implying they are separately installable). Include `routine-mode`/`audit-library` (rejected — unattended execution and hygiene are not needed to *first* reach self-construction; the threshold is the *minimal* self-constructing set, not the full required roster). Fold the threshold pin into the cross-repo/engine-mechanic design (rejected — the threshold must not ride a contested design; pinned standalone).
