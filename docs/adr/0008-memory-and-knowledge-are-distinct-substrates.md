# Memory and knowledge are distinct substrates

*Decided 2026-05-22 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Keep narrative memory ("how did I get here") and structural knowledge ("how does this world work") as separate substrates with separate scopes.

## Why

They answer different questions on different axes (episodic vs structural) and read differently (lexical recall vs graph query). The split is principled, confirmed by the prototype.

## What we ruled out

Collapse into a single substrate — rejected because it conflates two retrieval needs and muddies each.
