# Direct the structural-neighbors orientation render to traverse reverse adjacency (existing edges, bidirectional) so surfaces surface their bidirectional connective tissue — gate-clean, budget-neutral, build-owed; open Q38/Q39 for the residual lateral-collaboration gap (engine-template #37)

*Decided 2026-06-20 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The engine-template #37 structural-neighbors render does **forward-only, depth-1** traversal, so an ungoverned, non-check leaf's only neighbor is its module — `neighbors('tool:attention')` returns `['module:core']` only — and the flat "boot, attention, core" render conflates focus members with neighbors. (Forward edges vary by type: `provided_by`→module on every surface, `governed_by`→policy on *governed* surfaces, `targets`→surface on *checks*, `depends_on`→module on *modules*; the sparse case is the ungoverned non-check leaf.) The design never specified traversal *direction*; the build chose forward-only, which starves the orientation block the whole [D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md) enrichment serves. **Decision:** the structural-neighbors orientation render — and the cold-start adjacency walk that feeds it — traverses **both directions over the existing edge-set** (forward plus `neighbors(direction:in)`), so a **policy** surfaces the surfaces it governs (reverse `governed_by`), a **module** its surfaces and dependents, and **any surface** the checks that target it (reverse `targets`), not just its forward module edge; the render presents **focus and adjacent entities as two labeled parts** (authoritative for the build, not optional) with **honest sparsity disclosure** where a surface genuinely has no connective edge.
  - **This block is the model's briefing, not an operator surface.** The structural-neighbors block reaches the **AI**, not the operator's screen (the [operator-presentation relay](../reference/glossary.md); [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)); the operator's "Needs your attention" action list stays clean. Any operator relay of it is bound by the [§12](../principles.md) vocabulary leak-guard (no entity-ids/edge-names/"structural neighbors" echoed verbatim) and the operator-communication + alarms-pinned laws — the sparsity disclosure reads **neutral, not alarming**, and stays visually distinct from governance-critical alarms.
  - **Why this is gate-clean and needs no locked-doc edit.** It adds **no new edge types and no new attributes** — reverse is a query-time *direction* over the single stored forward edge, pinned in [D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md) gate 3 ("reverse = `neighbors(direction:in)` at query time from the single stored forward edge") and exposed by the locked [D-116](0116-q27-3-re-litigation-the-knowledge-retrieval-interface-operat.md) op-set. The structural-neighbors **candidate population and trim order are explicit build-spec leaves** owned by [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) (its "Build-spec leaves" section; it "supplies structural adjacency"), and **no locked law mandates forward-only** (verified against [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md), [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md), [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) by a four-lens cold audit). So this is direction to the build within a declared leaf — the [D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md) build-spec-leaf posture — **no locked-doc edit, no litigation alarm, no re-lock**.
  - **Budget-neutral by construction.** Reverse candidates compete for the **same fixed structural-neighbors budget slice** under attention's existing trim order; the slice size is unchanged — this improves *what fills* the slice, not its size. [D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md)'s pinned invariant ("the cold-start walk's traversal edge-set is pinned to today's edges") is **respected**: same edge *types*, both directions. Depth>1 stays on the on-demand PULL path (`get-entity / find / neighbors / relate`), unchanged.
  - **Build owes conformance** (downstream, in `engine-template` #37, not authored here): the render/walk traverses `direction:in` over `governed_by`/`targets` (and any future edge type) to surface bidirectional connective tissue; renders focus vs. neighbors as two labeled parts (authoritative); the **honest sparsity disclosure obeys the operator-communication + [§12](../principles.md) leak-guard + alarms-pinned laws** (neutral wording, distinct from governance alarms), verified by the build's own conformance/audit pass; the operator action-list stays clean.
  - **Residual → [Q38](../reference/open-questions.md)/[Q39](../reference/open-questions.md).** Reverse traversal surfaces only connective tissue that *exists*; a surface with no governing policy and no targeting check stays at `[module]` only, and the true "these components collaborate to deliver feature X" relationship is either **belief** (memory's, walled out by [D-008](0008-memory-and-knowledge-are-distinct-substrates.md)) or **undeclared**. That residual — and the empirical question of whether the derived self-map carries enough lateral signal to justify its cold-start budget slot — is parked as **[Q38](../reference/open-questions.md)**; the concrete gate-clean lever `delegates_to` is tracked as its own trigger-keyed task **[Q39](../reference/open-questions.md)** (litigate once agents are fully built — requires a litigation alarm + re-lock on the locked [skills](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md)/[agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) surfaces), so it is not dropped.
  - **Propagation.** No locked-doc edit (render/population/trim are build-spec leaves; no forward-only law). [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) **[R8](../reference/risks.md)** gains a one-line affirming clause (reverse traversal adds no edges/types, stays within the fixed slice → no hub fan-out). [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) **Q38** (edge-richness empirical) and **Q39** (`delegates_to` litigation, triggered once agents are fully built) opened. **[glossary](../reference/glossary.md) reviewed — no edit owed**: per [D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md)'s own propagation ruling, knowledge-graph-schema-granularity terms stay out of the glossary ("it sits at concept altitude… schema-granularity detail there would pin a build-spec leaf and risk rot"); "reverse adjacency" is a query-direction sub-detail of the already-glossed *Knowledge* concept and the [D-116](0116-q27-3-re-litigation-the-knowledge-retrieval-interface-operat.md) op-set, self-defined inline here. [engine-architecture](../architecture.md) reviewed — no edit owed (role/index unaffected). Build-owes attach to engine-template #37.

## Why

The "neighbors() = [module] only" symptom was largely a **forward-only-query artifact**, not graph poverty: the connective tissue (policies, checks, module membership) already links surfaces via stored forward edges, reachable in reverse at zero new grammar and zero budget (fixed slice). Surfacing it is the highest value-per-cost fix to the cold-session comprehension gap [D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md) targets — a strict improvement, biggest for connective entities. The genuinely-missing collaboration edges need declaration grammar that is premature (agents unbuilt) and locked-surface surgery, so they are captured ([Q38](../reference/open-questions.md)/[Q39](../reference/open-questions.md)), not forced now.

## What we ruled out

**Persist reverse edges in the graph** (rejected — reverse is a query-time direction over the single stored forward edge, [D-116](0116-q27-3-re-litigation-the-knowledge-retrieval-interface-operat.md)/[D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md) no-reverse-edges; persisting them breaches gate 3). **Expand the structural-neighbors budget slice to fit reverse candidates** (rejected — breaks budget-neutrality; the fixed slice + trim order already bound it; reverse candidates compete, they don't enlarge). **Add reverse traversal to the on-demand answer only, leaving the cold-start render forward-only** (rejected — the orientation block is the render #37 showed starved; the fix belongs in the pushed walk, within its fixed slice). **Pull `delegates_to` forward now** (rejected — needs new frontmatter grammar on the locked [skills](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md) surface (litigation) and the agent half has no endpoint until agents are built; [D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md) deferral holds; captured in [Q39](../reference/open-questions.md)). **Edit the locked knowledge/attention/boot docs to mandate reverse traversal** (rejected — the render/walk population is a declared build-spec leaf; pinning it in a locked doc risks rot and no law bars forward-only today; direction is build-owed). **Add a "reverse adjacency" glossary entry** (rejected — [D-203](0203-enrich-the-derived-knowledge-graph-schema-a-build-spec-leaf.md) precedent keeps graph-schema-granularity terms out of the glossary; the term is self-defined inline and below concept altitude). **Leave it forward-only and only disclose sparsity** (rejected — the connective tissue exists and is free to surface; forward-only needlessly starves the orientation block).
