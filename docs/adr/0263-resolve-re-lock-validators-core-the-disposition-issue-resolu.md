# Resolve: re-lock `validators-core` (the `disposition-issue-resolution` check) — the sole carrier of D-262

## The decision

Re-lock [validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md), the sole carrier of [D-262](0262-authorize-the-disposition-issue-resolution-check-a-17-7-mech.md). The landed text — the check in the `provides` cell, the "Plain checks against externally-*defined* artifacts" paragraph, and the negative-fixture co-location — passed its mandatory **landed-text cold audit** (the irreversible-relock gate, CLAUDE.md) before this re-lock. **The four-lens audit** (faithfulness/propagation · architect/seam · non-engineer-operator · adversarial, no shared context — D-262's named carrier gate, the adversarial lens added for a new merge-blocking gate): **faithfulness CLEAN** (matches D-262, anti-changelog-clean, internally consistent, links resolve, every verify-no-edit disposition confirmed against disk); **architect/seam SOUND** (validators-core is the right home, data-only holds, **no hidden re-lock** on check/validation/core/control-plane/policies — the check is an *instance* of locked grammar/law).

  **The convergent finding (architect + adversarial, independently): the false-witness risk.** The check is the negative-fixture meta-check roster's **first hard `custom/script` unit whose negative path needs a live `gh` call**, and D-262's "statically-decidable failure path, no carve-out" justification did not reconcile with the meta-check — fail-closed-on-unreachable made "API down" indistinguishable from "issue absent," so the fixture could be witnessed to bite via the *outage* path, not the aimed one. **Folded** (the [D-242](0242-resolve-the-d-241-audit-memory-read-enablement-the-landed-fo.md)/[D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md) fold-before-relock practice): the check now emits **two distinct findings** — `disposition-issue-unresolved` (the aimed bite) and `disposition-issue-unevaluable` (the fail-closed verdict) — so the fixture (a seeded PR body citing a **sentinel-nonexistent** issue) asserts the *unresolved* id by **set-membership** and the outage path cannot false-witness (offline, the unit emits `unevaluable`, the asserted id is absent, and the meta-check reddens loudly). The **carve-out conclusion stands** (the unit *can be made to fail in CI* — [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md)'s operative test — so it carries a fixture) with its justification **corrected** from "statically-decidable" to "exercised in CI under live `gh`"; and the meta-check job **materializes the same `gh`+`issues: read` runtime** for this unit, an instancing of validation's locked "the CI job materializes that runtime" commitment — a **build-spec leaf, no validation re-lock**.

  **Adversarial coverage findings (the `hard` tier is narrower than it looks):** an **uncited** disposition is unchecked (the consciously-declined control-plane machine-parseable-citation mandate, [D-262](0262-authorize-the-disposition-issue-resolution-check-a-17-7-mech.md)), and **any** real engine-labeled issue satisfies resolution (existence, not relevance). **Resolved by honest disclosure** in the artifact warrant (green shows the cited issues are real — not that everything was logged, not that the cited issue is *this* finding's, not that a disposition was right), **not** by widening scope (either fix would reopen the days-old [D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md) control-plane lock for marginal gain). **The merge-DoS finding** (a hard fail-closed check blocks merges during a GitHub issue-API outage): **rejected-with-rationale** — fail-closed is the locked [D-257](0257-resolve-re-lock-validation-the-negative-fixture-meta-check-l.md) `custom/script` contract applied consistently ([§15](../principles.md): a guardrail that can't run is not trusted), not a defect this check introduces; the operator-seat resolution is the **two distinct reds** (act vs wait), not a tier change — the non-engineer-operator lens explicitly concurred (keep `hard` + fail-closed; the fix is surfacing).

  **The non-engineer-operator findings** (three operator-facing strings — the green-warrant bound, the outage red, the fabrication red — had no committed delivery path and risked absorption into the [R34](../reference/risks.md)/engine-template **#288** general-warrant residual): **resolved** by a distinct, named **must-land build-owe — engine-template [#292](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/292)** — requiring the two distinct red messages and the green-warrant line at the PR surface (slugs never rendered), explicitly **not** discharged by #288 (a `custom/script` verdict is none of #288's named surfaces).

  **A focused two-lens remediation re-check** (non-engineer-operator · architect/seam — the [D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)/[D-254](0254-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-filler-renders-the-o.md) precedent, because the fixes touched the meta-check witnessing and the operator-trust surface) ran on the folded text: **both RESOLVED**; the architect lens raised one further SERIOUS — the meta-check's new live-`gh` dependency was unstated (risking a falsely-red offline meta-check, or an implied validation execution-model change) — **folded in-line** (the runtime-materialization clause above). **NIT folded:** the finding-disposition citation corrected to **[D-019](0019-authoring-grammar-locked-end-state-as-laws-not-leaves.md)** (the authoring-grammar lock that established the routing; D-168 is the unrelated operator-policy-override re-lock). The precondition D-262's anti-choice named ("the cold audit runs later") is now met — the audit ran, blocking/serious findings resolved-or-rejected-with-rationale. `python3 lock.py --relock modules/validators-core/README.md --decision D-263`; `python3 validate.py` green. The non-locked verify-no-edit dispositions [D-262](0262-authorize-the-disposition-issue-resolution-check-a-17-7-mech.md) recorded stand unchanged.

## Why

validators-core is D-262's only carrier, so the relock lands here after its landed-text audit. The audit earned its keep: the convergent architect+adversarial finding caught that this is the first non-offline meta-check unit and that the "statically-decidable" justification was wrong — the *conclusion* (no carve-out) survived against validation's actual "can be made to fail in CI" test, the *reasoning* was corrected, and the two-distinct-findings fix closed the false-witness hole; the operator lens caught that the honest strings had no delivery path, now the named build-owe #292. The teeth stay honest: a real non-AI correlate (the Issue object) at the `hard` tier, fail-closed per the locked contract, proven to bite by a fixture that witnesses the *aimed* finding, with its narrower-than-it-looks coverage disclosed in the warrant rather than overclaimed. Authorize ([D-262](0262-authorize-the-disposition-issue-resolution-check-a-17-7-mech.md)) → resolve-after-landed-text-audit (D-263) kept the irreversible re-lock behind its own clean gate, the [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)/[D-256](0256-authorize-the-every-hard-check-is-proven-to-bite-re-litigati.md) pattern.

## What we ruled out

**Lower the tier to `soft`** (rejected — a false citation has a real non-AI correlate and is operator-legible; soft reopens the §17 false-green concern the operator lens flagged). **Treat API-unreachable as a disclosed not-applicable rather than fail-closed** (rejected — the carve-out is bounded to "no statically-decidable failure path in CI," not a transient outage; a not-applicable-on-outage contradicts the locked [D-257](0257-resolve-re-lock-validation-the-negative-fixture-meta-check-l.md) fail-closed law and would require a validation re-lock). **Widen the check to mandate machine-parseable citations, or verify the cited issue is the finding's** (rejected — reopens the fresh [D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md) lock and the relevance check has no robust correlate; disclosed in the warrant instead). **A single finding id for unresolved + unevaluable** (rejected — set-membership could not then distinguish the aimed bite from the outage path, the false-witness the audit caught). **Fold the operator strings into #288 / the R34 residual** (rejected — a `custom/script` verdict is none of #288's named surfaces; without the distinct must-land #292 the honest strings never reach the operator). **Re-lock validation/check/core** (rejected — the check instances their locked grammar/law/runtime commitment; the meta-check runtime provisioning is a build-spec leaf, not a new law).
