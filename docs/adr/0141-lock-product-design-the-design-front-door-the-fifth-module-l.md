## Why

product-design is the first non-required-spine optional lock and the first whose cold audit surfaced a genuine locked-doc contradiction — the [D-120](0120-lock-core-the-root-module-the-closure-wave-s-terminal-ratifi.md) "(this is called…)" leak-channel closure had not propagated to this `designed` sibling, which still carried the pre-closure framing. Per the propagation matrix this is exactly the kind of latent inconsistency a lock must not freeze; the burden sits on the `designed` doc to adapt to locked `core`, not on `core` to change ([lock discipline](#)), so the remediation is a designed-doc reword + a superseding-by-conformance citation, not a re-litigation. Resolving Q30 in this pass (rather than leaving it open) is the right close because locking product-design closes Q30's named home (its own lock pass); free prose is the operator-trust-safe shape (a structured form would risk a fill-in-the-blanks ceremony the front door exists to avoid), and the consumer is shape-agnostic, so the resolution defers only a concrete future option, never a v1 capability ([complete final-state](#)). The two-lens re-check is the proportionate gate for a blocking remediation on a trust-relevant operator surface (the [D-128](0128-reconcile-routine-mode-s-stance-marker-framing-to-d-088-the.md)/[D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md) remediation-audit precedent).
