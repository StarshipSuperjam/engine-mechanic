# Litigate engine-template #427: make the SDD spec drive the build — adopt a derived **spec-obligation matrix** (splitting [R16](../reference/risks.md) coverage-vs-correctness) and travel the **conformance-enforcement floor** to deployed repos as [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md)'s enforcement layer, conditional on a `locked` `docs/spec/` — re-locks of product-design / build-orchestration / qa-review land under D-288 / D-289 / D-290

*Decided 2026-07-06 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

A build-conformance delta audit of the `engine-template` build (a substantial set of confirmed non-conformances, most of them under-builds) root-caused the misses to systematic conformance-machinery blind spots and opened engine-authored [engine-template #427](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/427), routing three coupled design questions here (correctly not self-served from the build). This entry rules all three; it is the **litigation alarm + operator authorization** (direction confirmed in-session over an `AskUserQuestion` before editing, then a mandatory cold audit after) **and the design of record**. The re-locks of the three locked carriers land under **[D-288](0288-resolve-re-lock-product-design-installs-the-conformance-enfo.md)/[D-289](0289-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-referent-section-run.md)/[D-290](0290-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-lens-carries.md)** after their mandatory landed-text cold audit (already run, below); the carriers are **edited-but-not-relocked** until then (the [D-285](0285-litigate-engine-template-363-the-layer-2-erasure-grammar-was.md)/[D-286](0286-resolve-re-lock-memory-the-layer-2-erasure-grammar-is-one-or.md) authorize→resolve pattern; `validate.py` flags their fingerprints until the relocks).

  **The ruling — (1) adopt a durable, derived spec-obligation matrix; split [R16](../reference/risks.md).** Per-PR conformance was re-derived from prose with no durable enumerated record — no denominator, no continuous reverse sweep, no cross-session memory of the not-yet-built remainder, the mechanism behind the under-build class. The [spec-obligation matrix](../reference/glossary.md) (build-conformance §4) is a **derived-committed artifact + a coverage check over it** (the [knowledge-graph](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) [§3](../principles.md)/[§19](../principles.md)/[D-011](0011-knowledge-graph-state-is-derived-not-hand-authored.md) derive-don't-hand-author pattern — never obligations enumerated inside a check callable), one row per obligation **fingerprint-pinned to its source span by a content digest**. **Two honest tiers:** against a **structured `docs/spec/` criteria table** (a `shape`-validated surface) the row keys to its **criterion-cell digest at its validated table position** — fully derived, criterion-granular; against **prose design docs** (the engine self-build's own corpus, no criteria table) it pins to a **heading-delimited span digest** — span-granular, the enumeration a curated act, honestly weaker. The pin is **content-derived, never a stable semantic identity** (a reworded obligation re-opens as changed). **R16 splits:** the *coverage* residual (mode c, silent under-build) is **closed** by the matrix over the obligations it enumerates; the *correctness* residuals (a self-consistent semantic divergence, b wrong-spec reproduction) stay **irreducible**. The matrix is a **derived index, never a trusted second referent** — the human-readable span stays ground truth and the lens re-derives from *it*, never from the matrix rows.

  **The ruling — (2)+(3) the conformance-enforcement floor travels to deployed repos as the SDD enforcement layer, conditional on a `locked` `docs/spec/`.** [§20](../principles.md)/[D-266](0266-new-principle-20-spec-conformance-is-the-standing-target-no.md)/[D-267](0267-clarify-20-s-instrument-retirement-clause-the-conformance-ca.md) settled that the maintainer *instrument* (build-conformance) retires at v1 while the conformance *capability* ships as build-orchestration's `spec-conformance` review. #427 names the shipped floor's full rigor: the [conformance-enforcement floor](../reference/glossary.md) — the spec-obligation matrix (coverage denominator), the **adversarial divergence-hunter** posture on qa-review's `spec-conformance` lens, and the **deployed-environment demonstration harness** (the standing correlate the operator-runnable how-verified rows retire into) — travels whole, pointed at the *product's own* `locked` spec, never engine-planning ([§13](../principles.md) wall holds). **Conditional and per-locked-row:** each leg bites only on criteria whose `docs/spec/` row is `locked` (lock is per-doc, read from the base commit's `status:`); with nothing locked the floor is the disclosed no-op, so it **never blocks a staged/MVP product** the operator scopes (§20). **The reconciling premise:** SDD ([D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)/[D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)) introduced exactly the `locked docs/spec/` §20 assumed the product lacked — so a locked spec row *is* a frozen spec, and the same rigor that built the Engine applies fractally to the product build it produces (`engine-planning : engine :: docs/spec/ : product`). This **extends** §20, not reverses it, and makes "machinery stays whole everywhere" ([D-267](0267-clarify-20-s-instrument-retirement-clause-the-conformance-ca.md)) literal — the deployed engine ships the whole floor, not a lightened one.

  **Ownership holds the [D-066](0066-the-4-4-review-lens-roster-two-stage-suites-mirroring-the-en.md) separation:** [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md) owns the coverage leg (matrix) and produces the referent + operator-runnable rows; [qa-review](../spec/modules/qa-review.md) owns the judgment leg (adversarial `spec-conformance`) and runs the demonstration harness as its disclosed dry-run; [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) runs them at the merge. **No hard `depends` added** — the judgment/demo legs ride qa-review's optional install (a repo without it still gets the mechanical matrix leg, disclosed). **control-plane needs no change** — the matrix rides the single ruleset-bound PR-validation check, adding no required-check name; **design-review / modules/README.md / module-order** own no floor edit (independently confirmed by the architect lens).

  **Ledger fold — coverage-granularity settled criterion-granular.** lifecycle-U14 and optional-modules-U10 asked capability- vs criterion-level coverage, blocked on "no criterion-ID scheme exists without inventing forbidden structure." The matrix's *derived* source-pinned rows **are** that criterion-ID scheme — a derived index is blessed by §3/§19, never the hand-authored parallel namespace the objection feared — so both resolve **criterion-granular**, discharging the D-247(4)/[D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)/[D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)/[D-254](0254-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-filler-renders-the-o.md) "every locked acceptance-criterion traced to committed work" letter. **Build-owe:** the shipped `coverage.py` is capability-granular and owes the upgrade to criterion-granular — an `engine-template` issue (via `.engine/tools/issue_author.py` + `engine` label). #427 itself is **COMMENTED, never closed** (disposition is the operator's call), citing D-287–D-290.

  **Cold audit (two rounds, no shared context; the landed-text gate before the irreversible re-locks).** Direction confirmed with the operator before editing. **Round 1 — four lenses** (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect): adversarial and operator returned no blocking (2 serious + nits each, folded); the **technical-feasibility lens returned one BLOCKING** — the reused knowledge-graph fingerprint mechanic pins per-*surface*, not per-*span*, so "stale-flags exactly its rows" was undeliverable as first written — plus two serious (generator/check split; per-doc lock granularity); the architect lens confirmed clean ownership/propagation. **All blocking/serious were resolved in the landed text** — the span-identity primitive named and made decidable (content digest; criterion-cell-at-table-position for `docs/spec/`, heading-span for prose), the generator/check split stated, per-doc lock stated, the agentic legs scoped to locked rows, "re-derive from span never from matrix" carried into the deployed description. **Round 2 — focused landed-text re-audit** (feasibility+adversarial · faithfulness+architect): confirmed **all five findings genuinely closed, no new contradiction**, and caught one authoring-rule-1 change-narration phrase at product-design's *Re-litigation propagates* bullet — **folded** (stated current-state). No finding was rejected-with-rationale; all resolved.

  **Non-locked propagation (landed under this entry):** build-conformance (§4 the matrix + two-tier span identity + generator/check split; §7 the floor travels via SDD; §10 instrument-retires / floor-travels, conditional on a locked spec — kept distinct from the Q31 engine-*self*-conformance open question, left intact); [risks](../reference/risks.md) **R16** (the coverage/correctness split; mode c closed by the matrix); [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) two new terms — *Spec-obligation matrix*, *Conformance-enforcement floor* (no third term minted, [R6](../reference/risks.md)/[D-255](0255-sharpen-the-post-v1-revisit-triggers-for-product-knowledge-g.md) anti-sprawl); [principles §20](../principles.md) (product clause: shipped-review depth conditional on a `locked` `docs/spec/`, still won't block an MVP — a high-blast-radius framing edit, **not** a fingerprint-locked doc, so logged here, no `lock.py`); [engine-architecture](../architecture.md) (the axis carries the floor — index kept truthful); conformance-relitigation-ledger (lifecycle-U14 / optional-modules-U10 annotated resolved; the conformance-edge + operator-ruling summary lines refreshed).

  **Carriers (locked, edited under this entry; re-lock under the named resolve entries after the landed-text audit above):** [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md) → **[D-288](0288-resolve-re-lock-product-design-installs-the-conformance-enfo.md)**; [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) → **[D-289](0289-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-referent-section-run.md)**; [qa-review](../spec/modules/qa-review.md) → **[D-290](0290-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-lens-carries.md)**. `validate.py` flags exactly these three fingerprints until their relocks. **Id allocation:** tail was D-286, no forward reservation above it; D-287–D-290 allocated monotonically.

## Why

the under-build class the audit surfaced is a *coverage* failure (no denominator, no reverse sweep), mechanically closable by a derived matrix without touching R16's irreducible *correctness* residual — the honest split. The floor's travel is not new scope but the naming (and completing) of the capability D-267 already ships: SDD's `locked docs/spec/` is the frozen-spec anchor §20 assumed absent, so the regime that built the engine applies to the product build it produces — conditional on the operator's own lock, so autonomy and the MVP guard hold. The derived criterion-ID scheme dissolves the coverage-granularity blocker the ledger had parked. The two-round cold audit caught and closed a real feasibility gap (span-identity) before an irreversible re-lock.

## What we ruled out

**Keep re-derivation, no durable matrix** (rejected — leaves the denominator/reverse-sweep gap that produced the 72% under-build class; the audit's own root cause). **Leave R16 unchanged** (rejected — the doc would be silent on the now-closable coverage mode; honest-tier requires naming which residual closed and which stays irreducible). **Keep §20 as-is, charitable lens only** (rejected — leaves "the spec means little to the build outcome" unaddressed for deployed repos, and under-delivers on D-267's "machinery stays whole everywhere"). **Travel the floor unconditionally** (rejected — would pressure a deliberately-staged/MVP product, violating §20's "won't block an MVP the operator scopes"; the per-locked-row conditional reconciles rigor with autonomy). **Hand-author a criterion-ID scheme** (rejected — a hand-authored parallel ID namespace is the forbidden structure §3/§19 reject; the *derived* source-pinned row identity is the blessed form). **Make qa-review a hard `depends` of product-design so the full floor is always present** (rejected — breaks the [D-066](0066-the-4-4-review-lens-roster-two-stage-suites-mirroring-the-en.md) lens-separation and product-design's no-hard-depends; the as-installed model, mechanical matrix leg always present + judgment/demo legs riding qa-review's optional install, is the faithful shape). **Treat the §20 change as in-design elaboration, not re-litigation** (rejected — §20's product clause turns on "no frozen spec," which the SDD lock makes conditionally false; a semantic change to a settled law re-litigates [D-266](0266-new-principle-20-spec-conformance-is-the-standing-target-no.md)/[D-267](0267-clarify-20-s-instrument-retirement-clause-the-conformance-ca.md) properly, safe-over-slim, with the full cold audit). **Fold unrelated Wave-D items (close-linkage [D-284](0284-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-submit-time-close-li.md), cost-estimate lifecycle-U03) into the build-orchestration re-lock** (rejected — [R6](../reference/risks.md) surgical scope; the #427 re-lock carries only floor deltas).
