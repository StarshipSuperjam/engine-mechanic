# Re-litigate `memory`: ledger write-integrity law; reframe usage-importance to cued-recall + recoverability; FTS5-latency honesty; tag-index pin; bare-machine restore bound

*Decided 2026-05-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Under the litigation-alarm protocol (operator-approved as part of the cognitive-substrate remediation plan) and after a **dedicated four-lens cold audit** of the proposed edits (adversarial, architect/cross-system, technical-feasibility, cognitive-science), re-litigate [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) (last locked by [D-078](0078-citation-accuracy-re-litigation-repoint-stale-q4-references.md)) with five scorecard-surfaced changes: **(1) Ledger write-integrity is now a stated law** — the foundation M1/M6/M11 silently assumed: local-ledger **writes are serialized** (single-writer discipline or an exclusive advisory lock; a bare `O_APPEND` is atomic only within `PIPE_BUF` ~4 KB and episodic records exceed it, so two live sessions could tear a line — the concrete lock is a build-spec leaf, the serialization requirement is the law), and **reads are line-resilient** (FTS5 rebuild + scan floor skip+report a malformed line, tolerate a torn trailing line, and reject a partial write via a record terminator rather than accepting a partial-but-valid-JSON line), scoped to the local ledger and distinct from the backup repo's branch-per-namespace concurrency. **(2) Usage-importance reframed** (M3 was metric-circular + lossy): frecency tracks utility **for cued, recurring recall only** — not frequency=importance; a rare-but-critical record that stops being cued (incl. lexical paraphrase/synonymy misses) **decays in ranking but never in existence**, its guarantee being **recoverability, not ranking** (demote-not-delete, delete-only-on-evidence, operator undo), and closing the cue-gap is the **semantic-recall module's** job, not frecency's. **(3) FTS5/latency honesty:** the scan fallback preserves recall **availability, not latency**; memory **detects** FTS5-absent and boot **renders** the scent's degraded-latency disclosure ([§16](../principles.md)); "ship our own FTS5-enabled SQLite" is a **build-spec feasibility decision** (prebuilt-binary vs a source-build fallback that strands a non-engineer; weighed against §12/§5), **not mandated** — the scan fallback stays the honest floor. **(4) Tag-index pin (M5):** open tags are a secondary **structured** filter, **not indexed into the FTS body**, so BM25 ranks the narrative and tag drift never dilutes term statistics. **(5) Restore bound (M13):** restore-offer needs no CLI/path knowledge **once the project repo is present** (the committed destination pointer is what the fresh instance reads); recovery onto a **bare machine with nothing cloned** first requires the project repo — the bound is stated, not implied away. `python3 lock.py --relock systems/cognitive/memory/README.md --decision D-081`; ratified_by D-081. The integrity law and the M3 reframe are genuine design changes, which is why this is a re-litigation with a dedicated cold audit rather than an in-place correction. **Boundary notes (from the cold audit):** (a) frecency is a **memory-owned input** to [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md)'s deterministic ranking, never attention state — determinism is "same inputs → same ordering," and a time-varying input does not violate it, so this edit does **not** perturb attention's "no decay state / no ML" law; (b) this edit does **not** resolve [Q17](../reference/open-questions.md)'s self-tuning-vs-determinism contradiction (which concerns re-weighting attention's policy, untouched here) — the frecency-as-input clarification must not be read as closing Q17.

## Why

The efficacy-scorecard audit found M3 metric-circular and lossy (frecency buries rare-but-uncued records), a **missing** ledger write-integrity law that M1 (abandoned-session recovery), M6 (recall-always-answers), and M11 (backup) all silently assumed, the scent's single-digit-ms latency unbacked when FTS5 is absent, M5 underspecified on whether tags dilute the FTS body, and M13's bare-machine recovery gap. The pre-relock cold audit corrected three overreaches in the drafted edits: E1's "O_APPEND alone is atomic" (false above `PIPE_BUF` — serialization is load-bearing), E2's "role-weight protects rare-critical" (the demotion score multiplies by frecency, so role-weight is a per-type prior, not a per-record protection — the honest guarantee is recoverability via the reversibility floor), and E3's scent-disclosure wording (boot's to render, per §16 — memory only detects). Rigor: a re-litigation that **does** touch design surface (a new law + a reframed claim) gets the dedicated cold audit ([D-018](0018-cold-session-design-audit-required-before-any-lock.md) discipline), unlike the honesty-only [D-080](0080-re-litigate-state-honesty-name-the-floor-s-known-unbounded-f.md). Propagation per the matrix: [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) (re-locked end-state), this entry; [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) "Frecency" scoped to cued-recall utility + recoverability (was overclaiming "importance"), "Memory ledger" verified still truthful (no edit); [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) D-030 "observe-don't-predict" sentence verified still truthful after the reframe (observe-don't-predict holds — no edit, discharged by re-read); [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) R2 notes the bare-machine restore bound; [scenarios/first-run.md](../architecture.md#first-run-provisioning) and [scenarios/upgrade-the-engine.md](../architecture.md#upgrading-the-engine) reconciled to the restore precondition and availability-not-latency; [modules/memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5](../spec/modules/memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5.md) checked (stub, no-op).

## What we ruled out

**State E1 atomicity as "`O_APPEND` alone"** (rejected — atomic only ≤`PIPE_BUF`; multi-KB records require serialization, per technical-feasibility). **Claim role-weight protects rare-critical records** (rejected — the demotion math multiplies by frecency; the honest guarantee is recoverability via demote-not-delete + evidence-only deletion). **Mandate bundling an FTS5 SQLite** (rejected — a new foundation binary must justify itself against §12/§5; kept as a build-spec feasibility decision with the scan floor as the honest fallback). **Route M3/M5/M13 to the Q15 empirical battery** (rejected — these are design/honesty gaps fixable in the doc now). **Land the integrity law as a silent correction** (rejected — it is a new law; it routes through re-litigation + dedicated cold audit). **Add a hard demotion floor under role-weight now** (rejected — a new mechanism beyond the surfaced finding; the reversibility floor already gives the honest recoverability guarantee, and an importance-aware admission score is the deferred ranking build-spec leaf).
