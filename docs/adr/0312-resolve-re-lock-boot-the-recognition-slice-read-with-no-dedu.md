# Resolve: re-lock `boot` (the recognition-slice read with no dedup; the §15 weakening-alarm class retired; the sole-write claim reconciled; the platform cap under the degrade-loud law) — a carrier of [D-309](0309-litigate-the-grammar-and-boot-cluster-ledger-grammar-core-u0.md)

*Decided 2026-07-16 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Re-lock [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md), a carrier of [D-309](0309-litigate-the-grammar-and-boot-cluster-ledger-grammar-core-u0.md). The landed text: pack **step 2 reads the surface catalog's recognition slice** ([ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md)'s consumption law owns the rationale; boot states the step), carried on **every render with no dedup** — a few hundred characters never earned a mechanism. The **§15 weakening-alarm class is retired** (nothing ever produced it; [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) owns detection and discharges the relay at the merge ack gate) with its three dependent sites reconciled. The **sole-write** claim reconciles by attributing each write to the substrate that owns it. The platform's **hook-output cap** joins the degrade-loud law: the pack measures itself, the slice sheds at the **lowest tier** (recognition is posture and fail-open), the **shed notice joins the must-push set** so it reaches the operator rather than only the model, the [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md)/boot order composition is stated (attention ranks candidates to budget and never sees the slice; boot measures the assembled pack against the character cap last), and the **whole chain is named posture** ([§7](../principles.md)) — hook, guard, and relay each fail-open; the guarantee remains the protected-branch merge. The scent dedup's **honest bound** is stated rather than left implicit. `python3 lock.py --relock systems/lifecycle/boot/README.md --decision D-312`; `validate.py` reports this fingerprint clean.

## Why

boot's three amendments are each the letter-conformant direction and none invents grammar. The read was always [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md)'s own claim — only its unit and tier are made honest. The retirement removes a promise [§15](../principles.md) never asked for and no producer ever met. The sole-write reconciliation applies canon's existing attribution move. The cap is a pre-existing latent defect the pass surfaced and the doc had never recorded; leaving it undocumented in the one pass that opened boot's degradation law would have been the same silence the cluster came to close.

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock without naming the open filed items** (rejected — a lock is an affirmative act of ratification, and an unqualified one would read to a cold session as *audited clean*, which is the docs-over-claiming failure mode this very cluster convened to fix; the round-2 operator lens named this precisely). **Fold the four filed items now** (rejected — they are separable pre-existing defects each needing its own ruling; folding them converts a three-item cluster into an open-ended redesign of boot, and deciding them on the fly is the invent-structure move CLAUDE.md forbids). **File the scent-dedup contradiction rather than fold it** (rejected — unlike the four above, that contradiction was *fold-introduced*: this pass stated the falsifying premise at `:72` while `:385` asserted the opposite in the same doc, and ratifying a doc that contradicts itself on the one fact the amendment turned on is not defensible; the fix was cheap and in-class). **Restate ontology's rationale here** (rejected — duplicated rationale across two independently re-lockable docs is a drift generator; the law lives in ontology and boot references it).

## Further record

[D-309](0309-litigate-the-grammar-and-boot-cluster-ledger-grammar-core-u0.md)'s landed-text audit surfaced four pre-existing boot defects this cluster did not author and did not fold: **`lifecycle-U28`** (the present-marker's false-positive direction — the hook-independent `CLAUDE.md` floor hands the model the all-clear string, so an ungrounded session can render a plausible all-clear, and the operator's single grounding primitive rides model behaviour rather than mechanism), **`lifecycle-U29`** (*"a problem vanishing means the engine verified it is fixed"* against the intent-exit's retirement), **`lifecycle-U30`** (the retired-marker write riding model discretion, with no retired-findings readout), **`lifecycle-U31`** (the leftover-license relay's ungrounded private-by-default reassurance).
