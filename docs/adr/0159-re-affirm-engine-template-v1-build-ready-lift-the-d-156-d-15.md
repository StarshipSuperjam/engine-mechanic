## Why

[D-156](0156-name-the-engine-s-execution-substrate-a-group-scoped-uv-mana.md) reopened build-ready one day after [D-155](0155-declare-engine-template-v1-s-design-build-ready-for-the-firs.md) because the pressure-test the operator commissioned proved the four gates can miss a foundational gap (the never-named execution substrate). The honest close was not to re-declare on the same evidence but to **discharge the named condition**: pin the one real residual the new pressure-test found (F1), audit the fix cold, re-lock, and re-run the genesis dry-run on the corrected corpus. That chain is complete and clean, so the provisional status lifts on the same [§17](../principles.md) consent basis as D-155 — the maintainer's verdict on a tiered bundle, the AI assembling, tiering, and presenting but never self-issuing. The dry-run re-run earned its keep by exercising the F1 convention through the [memory-substrate](../spec/modules/memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5.md) module (the first dep-carrying group) and confirming a builder constructs it without inventing; the Builder lens's over-tagging (build-spec leaves read as gaps) was caught by orchestrator ground-truth exactly as the dry-run §4 discipline intends.
