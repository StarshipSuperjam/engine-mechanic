## Why

A lock is hard to reverse and is a ratification act in its own right, so the gate is a *fresh* cold-session audit commissioned by the ratifying session — not a reuse of the authoring audit. It ran with five lenses (the four-lens floor plus a settledness lens, because "is this good design" and "is this ready to freeze" are distinct questions), surfaced one genuine cross-seam contradiction with a locked neighbor, and that was folded in the non-locked docs without weakening or touching the locked audits decision. The design is settled (every dependency locked; only concrete-value leaves remain), the operator gave the explicit go that lock-discipline named as the last outstanding gate, and the honest tiers are intact (pure posture; floor-load substantially-reliable-not-airtight; the guard defense-in-depth; "bounded" by posture + the audits third-case stale-surfacing). Locking now closes the fresh downstream surface that [D-193](0193-resolve-the-d-192-conduct-surface-re-litigation-landed-text.md) deliberately held `designed` to settle.
