`python3 validate.py` green.

## Why

The cross-corpus audit is the third build-readiness gate — after mechanical `validate.py` and the per-system cold-session audits — and it catches the one defect class reading docs in isolation cannot: two locked docs that disagree only when held together. AF-1 is the single finding that survived both a default-refute killer and a source re-read: two locked foundations flatly disagree on who owns the degraded-work consent gate. Even though the correct owner is recoverable (boot redirects to build-orchestration), a live contradiction between locked docs should not ship into construction, where a builder wiring state's degraded-count path would be told to look for a "boot consent gate" boot says it does not own. The fix lands on `state` because boot and build-orchestration already agree — adapting the current divergent doc to the settled cross-doc truth, never re-opening the gate's owner (the lock-discipline default). The eight nits are real but cosmetic (parentheticals, wording, an annotation) and either self-correct at validate-time or carry near-zero blast radius; deferring them as a logged backlog keeps re-lock churn off the pre-build critical path while preserving the record in the ledger and here. N-5 is a free annotation completing D-120's propagation. The clean result — one localized mis-attribution and a cosmetic tail across 41 locked docs — is itself meaningful evidence the design is build-ready.

## What we ruled out

**Defer AF-1 too** (rejected — a live contradiction between two locked cognitive/lifecycle foundations is a genuine seam defect, not cosmetic; shipping it into construction risks a builder binding to a consent gate the named owner disclaims). **Batch all eight nits now** (rejected — the operator's call; touching ~6 locked docs to fix parentheticals and wording is re-lock churn unjustified before the first build PR, and the backlog is logged and batchable later). **Edit `boot` or `build-orchestration` instead of `state`** (rejected — they hold the correct, mutually-consistent attribution and are the canonical gate owner; the fix adapts the sole divergent doc, never the settled ones). **Treat the dropped solo-ruleset-weakening finding as a real gap needing a new live-ruleset detector** (rejected — over-claim; the design discloses the solo residual honestly in six places and routes the structural close to team identity, exactly the [§15](../principles.md)/[§7](../principles.md) honest-tier posture; the audit's job was to confirm that disclosure is internally consistent, which it is — the habituation sliver remains parked in [Q18](../reference/open-questions.md)). **Rewrite the dry-run-ledger genesis section** (rejected — the run-record discipline is one `Resolved by D-NNN` annotation, never a rewrite or "UPDATE —" banner, preserving the frozen ledger as a faithful record).
