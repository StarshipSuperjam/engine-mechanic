# Hold a post-v1 `product-knowledge-graph` module stub (product-side structural knowledge)

*Decided 2026-05-28 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Name and stub a **post-v1 optional module**, [product-knowledge-graph](../spec/modules/product-knowledge-graph.md) (`status: stub`, `depends: core`), in the **Future optional** catalog tier — built **out of v1 scope**, the slot held so the grammar has a home. It is the product-side analogue of the [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) foundation: a **derived structural graph of the product's own code** (modules, files, dependency/reference edges) integrated into the cognitive substrate, extending the structural "knowledge" leg from the engine's self-map to the product so a cold session reasons over the product's shape without re-deriving it live each session. It is derived-not-hand-authored, holds structure-not-belief (the [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) wall), reuses the swappable knowledge representation/retrieval seam ([R8](../reference/risks.md) bounds hub-explosion at product scale), and is read-only over product source with an engine-owned gitignored index (the [§13](../principles.md) wall). It is **deliberately absent from the v1 dependency graph** and the WBS sort; `core` is its only certain root. Distinct from the experimental [engine-knowledge-graph](../spec/modules/engine-knowledge-graph.md) (semantic recall over the memory ledger). Its operator-facing-vs-engine-infra placement is decided at promotion (likely engine-infra).

## Why

A session-long examination of the [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) foundation established that the v1 KG is the **engine's structural self-map** ([D-042](0042-procedural-content-grounding-surface-cluster-designed-the-bo.md): the engine "self-describes via the derived self-map + knowledge graph"; `specification` is not a catalogued surface) — it covers governed ontology surfaces, not the product's code, and not even product-design's product-owned arc42/C4 docs. v1 reasons over the product by reading code live + [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) beliefs + product-owned design docs; it does **not** externalize the product's *structure* as a graph. That is a deliberate v1 bet (live-read is cheap and current; a committed code-graph drifts) but a real limitation for large products, which the operator named as the obvious next add-on. Naming the stub now (the [D-095](0095-cut-expression-contracts-disposition-prose-organization-cove.md) `clean-code` precedent) gives the grammar a home so the later build is **additive, not a refactor** ([R6](../reference/risks.md) deferral discipline: the end-state stays specified, capability layers). It touches **no locked doc**: the knowledge foundation stays the engine self-map; this attaches as a separate optional graph. Propagation: [modules/product-knowledge-graph/README.md](../spec/modules/product-knowledge-graph.md) stub authored; [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) Future-optional catalog row + descriptive paragraph (and the section intro softened — post-v1 stubs' operator-facing-vs-engine-infra placement is set at promotion, since this one leans engine-infra unlike `clean-code`); this entry. The cognitive foundations are **not** edited (the stub holds a slot; its integration design — additive vs. a justified knowledge re-litigation — is deferred to its session).

## What we ruled out

Build it in v1 (rejected — the operator scoped it out; v1 must prove the substrate first, and product-structure indexing is a substantial design, [R6](../reference/risks.md)). Leave it unnamed until needed (rejected — naming the slot now keeps the later add-on additive rather than a system refactor, the whole point of holding end-state stubs, [R6](../reference/risks.md)). Fold it into the experimental [engine-knowledge-graph](../spec/modules/engine-knowledge-graph.md) (rejected — that is semantic recall over the memory ledger, a different mechanism and target; the product structural graph is its own module). Edit the locked [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) foundation now to admit product coverage (rejected — premature; the stub's integration may ride additively, and any knowledge re-litigation is a decision for its design session, not now). Add it to the v1 dependency graph / WBS (rejected — post-v1; `core` is its only certain root, deps re-derived later). Assume it is operator-facing like `clean-code` (rejected — it is cognitive infrastructure the operator would not know to choose; placement is decided at promotion, leaning engine-infra like `engine-knowledge-graph`).
