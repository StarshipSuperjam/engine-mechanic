# Lock `audit-library` (the engine self-audit delivery): the tenth module lock; remediate the substrate-realization honesty findings

*Decided 2026-05-30 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Ratify and **lock** [audit-library](../spec/modules/audit-library.md) (`status: designed → locked`) — the `required` module that ships the engine's self-audit (the audit persona, the seed concern-list + concern-entry schema, the audit-digest generator, the digest-staleness check, and the default `audit-prep` cron workflow), the **tenth module lock**, bringing locks.yaml to **39 docs** (29 systems + 10 modules). It became lock-eligible when Q32 (keep persona-files, [D-145](0145-resolve-q32-keep-the-locked-persona-file-agent-realization-n.md)) and Q33 (GitHub Actions default + Cloud Routines option, [D-146](0146-resolve-q33-github-actions-stays-the-default-audit-substrate.md)) resolved. **Both module-lock gates met:** the **build-readiness precondition** at the dry-run (dry-run-ledger: BUILD-READY on the GitHub-Actions substrate, with the [D-146](0146-resolve-q33-github-actions-stays-the-default-audit-substrate.md) rework annotated there as disclosed-un-exercised additive deliveries on the unchanged default substrate), and the **HARD four-lens cold-session design audit**. The **architect lens returned RATIFY** (the [§16](../principles.md) delivery-layer seam holds — the module delivers the audit laws and re-authors none; the memory-backup read is a **pure consumer of memory's locked [D-061](0061-lock-the-memory-system-wave-2-the-episodic-ledger-store-q3-b.md) export, widening nothing** — confirmed against locked [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)/[provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md), no litigation alarm; the Cloud-Routine↔Routine-stance disambiguation is clean; R6 scope and `depends: validators-core` hold). **The other three lenses returned FIX-FIRST on substrate-realization honesty findings — one blocking, several serious — all remediated in-doc before the lock** (designed-doc prose, no locked-doc touch): **(blocking, operator)** a one-year token expiry had no stated recovery path → added the expiry→stop→staleness-notice→re-`setup-token` path; **(serious, adversarial+technical)** the auth prose rested on a pre-cutover "draws on subscription, no metered billing" framing that the **2026-06-15 Agent-SDK credit split** (verified: a separate, finite, monthly, opt-in-gated allowance) makes current-truth-false within 15 days → reframed to the post-cutover steady state as primary, the one-time opt-in named as a precondition and the exhaustion risk disclosed; **(serious, adversarial)** "arms itself, nothing to forget" over-claimed → softened to the honest comparative staleness-baseline win; **(serious, adversarial+technical)** the invocation over-pinned a bare-CLI form and dropped [D-133](0133-type-the-cron-fired-audit-persona-re-litigate-agents-role-tr.md)'s non-bare discovery leaf and the model-tier guarantee → relaxed to laws-not-leaves grammar (non-interactive; never `--bare`; top-level `--agent`; **`--model` pinned to the judgment tier** since the platform does not reliably apply frontmatter `model:` to a `--agent` session — protecting the locked audits "judgment model-tier" guarantee — the concrete vehicle a build-spec leaf); **(serious, technical+operator)** the memory-backup read elided that a *separate private repo* needs cross-repo access (the default workflow token is own-repo-scoped) and that the remedy is an operator step owned by [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md) → named both, and framed the no-backup disclosure as **actionable** (clears, not habituates). The technical-feasibility lens **ground-truthed every platform fact against current docs** (`-p`/`--bare`/`--agent`/`--model`; the one-year `setup-token` token; the 2026-06-15 separate Agent-SDK credit + one-time opt-in; the own-repo-scoped default `GITHUB_TOKEN`). A **focused two-lens cold re-check (technical-feasibility + faithfulness) of the remediated passages returned CLEAN** on both — every prior finding genuinely resolved, every platform fact confirmed, no locked-doc edit — with one presentational nit (the opt-in is a distinct account action, not part of `setup-token`) folded before the lock. **Propagation (floor + the remediation + the ledger annotation):** the module doc (the remediated scheduled-run/memory sections + `status: locked` via `lock.py`) + the locks.yaml entry (ratified_by D-148) + the frozen dry-run-ledger one-line "Extended by D-146" annotation (the status-pointers/run-records discipline — annotated, not rewritten) + this entry. **No edit owed** to [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) (design-maturity not catalog-tracked [D-090](0090-flesh-the-validators-core-module-doc-to-designed-the-engine.md); `required | validators-core` row + graph edges unchanged), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md), [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md), [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) ([R17](../reference/risks.md) already opened at [D-146](0146-resolve-q33-github-actions-stays-the-default-audit-substrate.md); R14 untouched), or the locked audits/agents/validation/control-plane/memory/provisioning/core docs. **No locked-doc edit, no litigation alarm** — the lock consumes and delivers the locked audits laws and changes none. `python3 lock.py modules/audit-library/README.md --decision D-148`; `python3 validate.py` green.

## Why

audit-library was the highest-stakes of the three Phase-C locks because its scheduled-run section was freshly reworked ([D-146](0146-resolve-q33-github-actions-stays-the-default-audit-substrate.md)), so the cold audit scrutinized new prose rather than settled design — and it earned its keep: three lenses caught substrate-realization honesty defects (a 15-days-out billing change demoted to a footnote; an "arms itself" over-claim; an unstated token-expiry recovery path; an elided cross-repo precondition; an over-pinned invocation that dropped the model-tier guarantee) the architect lens and the prior dry-run had not surfaced — the validate-against-the-live-system discipline again. All were bounded designed-doc prose fixes adapting the doc to current platform truth and honest-tier ([§7](../principles.md)) discipline, none touching a locked doc, so they landed in-line and the focused re-check confirmed them clean. The lock proceeds on the architect's clean seam verdict, the remediated honesty, and the re-verified platform facts.

## What we ruled out

**Lock audit-library on the first cold audit without remediation** (rejected — one blocking + several serious honesty findings; locking through them would freeze a soon-false billing framing and an unstated recovery path into a `required`-core doc). **Re-litigate locked audits to pin the model-tier realization or the substrate** (rejected — the `--model` pin realizes the locked "judgment model-tier" guarantee without changing the law, and GitHub Actions stays the default so the substrate framing is untouched; both are delivery-layer). **Treat the memory-backup read as needing a memory-doc change** (rejected — it is a pure consumer of the existing [D-061](0061-lock-the-memory-system-wave-2-the-episodic-ledger-store-q3-b.md) export, the §16 consume-never-widen discipline, architect-confirmed). **Skip the focused re-check and lock on the remediation alone** (rejected — the remediation was substantial, platform-fact-heavy, and resolved a blocking finding on a trust-relevant surface, so a focused cold re-verification is the proportionate gate, the [D-128](0128-reconcile-routine-mode-s-stance-marker-framing-to-d-088-the.md)/[D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md)/[D-141](0141-lock-product-design-the-design-front-door-the-fifth-module-l.md) remediation-audit precedent; the operator chose it). **Add Local Desktop as a third substrate** (rejected — out of the [D-146](0146-resolve-q33-github-actions-stays-the-default-audit-substrate.md) GitHub-Actions-default + Cloud-Routine-option resolution; a future addition, not this lock's scope). **Edit catalog/architecture/glossary/risks to mark the lock** (rejected — floor only; design-maturity not catalog-tracked and R17 already opened).
