## The decision

Record the outcome of the module packaging-lock sweep run this session and **defer five modules' locks** per the operator's per-module decisions, capturing each gating thread durably in [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) (Q32–Q35) so a future session resumes coherently. **Locked this sweep (each on its own clean four-lens cold-session audit + operator go):** [validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md) ([D-138](0138-lock-validators-core-the-base-self-validation-rule-corpus-th.md)), [memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5](../spec/modules/memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5.md) ([D-139](0139-lock-memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5-the-memory-floor-the-third.md)), [routine-mode](../spec/modules/routine-mode.md) ([D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md)), [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md) ([D-141](0141-lock-product-design-the-design-front-door-the-fifth-module-l.md)), [migration-discipline](../spec/modules/migration-discipline.md) ([D-142](0142-lock-migration-discipline-product-migration-governance-the-s.md)), [external-contribution](../spec/modules/external-contribution.md) ([D-143](0143-lock-the-external-contribution-module-the-cross-repo-packagi.md)) — **six modules**, bringing locks.yaml to **36 docs** (29 systems + 7 modules incl. `core`); the L2/L3 **required spine is fully locked**. ## Why

The sweep validated the cold-audit gate's worth: the **technical-feasibility lens — the one that ground-truths against the live platform rather than the docs — caught every platform error or impossibility in the optional modules** (routine-mode's fictitious "Default/Auto" permission mode and stale "not introspectable" claim; audit-library's no-API-to-enable-auto-audit substrate; github-projects-sync's no-API-to-enable-auto-add; dependency-discipline's paid-plan unlock + the `allow-ghsas` §15 mechanics), **all of which the other three lenses and the prior dry-run had missed** (the validate-against-the-live-system discipline). The required spine (validators-core, memory-substrate) audited clean; the optional modules carry systematically more platform-fact risk. Deferring rather than rushing each substantive finding into an in-line fold honored the operator's per-module judgment (operator-scopes-procedure-vs-closure) and kept every lock landing on a sound doc. Opening Q32–Q35 gives the deferred threads a durable, workspace-native home (the prior record was only the session and a project memory).

## What we ruled out

**Lock all eleven designed modules this session** (rejected — five carried blocking/serious findings the audits surfaced; locking through them would freeze known defects). **Remediate every deferred finding in-line and lock now** (rejected per module — the operator chose focused passes for the substantive ones; routine-mode and product-design *were* remediated-and-locked because their fixes were bounded conformance corrections, while the agent-persona mechanism, the audit substrate, the github-projects setup, and the §15 call are larger threads warranting their own passes). **Re-litigate locked `agents` / `audits` now** (rejected — deferred pending the [Q32](../reference/open-questions.md)/[Q33](../reference/open-questions.md) decisions; do not re-open a locked doc before the decision that would require it is made). **Leave the deferred threads only in the session transcript + the project memory** (rejected — unresolved threads awaiting a decision belong in [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md), the authoritative durable home; memory is background continuity, not the workspace record). **Log a separate decision per deferral** (rejected — the deferrals are one coherent sweep outcome; a single capping entry plus the four open questions is the proportionate record, the locks already carrying their own entries).
