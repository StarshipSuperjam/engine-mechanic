## Why

The dry-run did its job again — it surfaced a cross-document grammar gap invisible to a single-doc reader (each locked doc is internally fine) but fatal to a builder (the audit persona, a **required**-core artifact, cannot be typed). The signal is trustworthy: three independent assessments (builder, orchestrator ground-truth, reviewer) reached BLOCKING against the verbatim locked text, and the lone "nit" dissent rested on a reading (carry-no-role) the text forbids. This is the same shape as [Q28](../reference/open-questions.md) and the genesis [Q27](#) — a downstream consumer presupposing a form the locked upstream surface never pinned — and the same shape as [D-122](0122-enumerate-the-branch-ruleset-protection-floor-in-control-pla.md) finding #1 (a locked doc's role implies a contract its text never wrote). It is the **second distinct litigation alarm** of the module-by-module sweep, both surfaced by exercising a downstream module against the locked grammar. Recording it now rather than burying it honors finding-disposition; parking rather than stopping honors the operator's AFK directive to complete the sweep and map the full blast radius before any locked-doc surgery.
