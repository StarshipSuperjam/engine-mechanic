# Resolve the D-241 audit-memory-read enablement: the landed four-lens audit confirmed the design sound and caught three operator-experience findings (one blocking), all remediated; [audit-library](../spec/modules/audit-library.md) + [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md) + [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) re-locked; engine-template #224 build-owe filed

## The decision

The [D-241](0241-authorize-completing-the-audit-s-off-repo-memory-read-enable.md) enablement landed across three locked docs and two non-locked carriers, completing the deferred turn-on for the scheduled self-audit's read of the off-repo memory backup. **What landed and where.** **(audit-library, "Coverage of local memory")** the **two-part precondition** (backup exists *and* the run is granted access) named as one gated outcome; the **least-privilege read-only credential** (a fine-grained `contents:read` token scoped to the single vault repo, a repo secret, distinct from the own-repo token and the `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN`); the **committed-pointer-for-CI** requirement; the **shared-vault blast-radius** disclosure with the per-project escape; the **private-only digest belief-content gate**; the **corrected disclosure** naming which precondition is unmet and the **credential-specific recovery** (never the unrelated `claude setup-token`); plus the register, "Honest coverage", and un-exercised updates. **(provisioning, build-spec leaf)** a new **"audit's saved-memory read turn-on"** leaf: provisioning commits the pointer and walks the operator through the credential grant as a **heavy-consent trust gate**, ending it with an **engine-run test read** (a positive correlate the grant works), steering to a **no-expiry** token where a personal account allows and falling back to the credential-specific re-arm where an org caps lifetime — a [§16](../principles.md) consumer grant that does not widen [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)'s mechanism. **(audits, "The audit digest")** the **digest-contract privacy bound** — belief content gated on repo visibility, the [§7](../principles.md)-honest residual (a committed digest cannot be unpublished), distinguished from [D-238](0238-resolve-the-d-237-memory-backup-shared-vault-flip-the-four-l.md)'s vault-flip surface. **(constraints.md)** the cross-repo-read platform fact (own-repo `GITHUB_TOKEN` cannot reach a separate private repo; the least-privilege fine-grained PAT is the vehicle; no per-path scoping; personal-vs-org no-expiry). **(risks.md)** **[R31](../reference/risks.md)** for the standing CI-resident vault-read credential. **Verify-no-edit, logged:** **[memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)** (the read is the [§16](../principles.md) pure consumer [D-146](0146-resolve-q33-github-actions-stays-the-default-audit-substrate.md)/[D-148](0148-lock-audit-library-the-engine-self-audit-delivery-the-tenth.md) already ruled needs no memory change; the backup law's "one operator credential reads every namespace" already covers and supports the audit read; [D-007](0007-memory-data-is-local-and-gitignored-substrate-ships-empty.md) governs the store, not the derived digest — no sentence became false); **[engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md)** (the master indexes the `audits` node at index altitude only; the cross-repo read is sub-index delivery detail — D-241 named it "check + disposition," resolved here to no-edit-owed); **[scenarios/first-run.md](../architecture.md#first-run-provisioning)** (the scenario walks first-run at a higher altitude and does **not** depict the memory-backup setup leaf at all, so adding only the audit-read credential step would be an asymmetric over-depiction — D-241 named it a "carrier," resolved here to no-edit-owed; a scenario-completeness pass for the backup family is a separate matter); **repository-topology law 5** (the committed destination pointer is the law's named config-not-data carve-out — provisioning committing it is the carve-out doing its job); **[glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md)** (no new term; "Audit digest" stays at definition altitude, the [D-238](0238-resolve-the-d-237-memory-backup-shared-vault-flip-the-four-l.md) precedent); **[modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md)** (catalog row + dependency graph unchanged — the credential is provisioning-owned, not a new audit-library `provides`). **The audit.** The **landed-text four-lens cold audit** (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect, no shared context) confirmed the **design sound** — the adversarial lens found the [§16](../principles.md) consumer seam airtight and the digest gate [D-007](0007-memory-data-is-local-and-gitignored-substrate-ships-empty.md)/[D-238](0238-resolve-the-d-237-memory-backup-shared-vault-flip-the-four-l.md)-coherent; the architect lens found the blast radius complete and every verify-no-edit disposition sound (incl. boot/telemetry/build-conformance, which relay or build-verify the generator-enforced gate and need no edit); the technical-feasibility lens **ground-truthed every platform fact against current GitHub/Anthropic docs** (the own-repo-token cross-repo limit, the single-repo `contents:read` PAT, no per-path scoping, personal no-expiry vs org re-cap, `actions/checkout` committed-files-only, the Cloud-Routine-performs-no-read characterization). Re-locks land in dependency order (memory unedited, so omitted): `python3 lock.py --relock systems/guardrails/audits/README.md --decision D-242`; `… systems/infrastructure/provisioning/README.md …`; `… modules/audit-library/README.md …`; `python3 validate.py` green. ## Why

The audit did its job twice over: the four-lens pass confirmed the **design** (the seam, the bound, the blast radius, the platform facts) while the operator lens caught that the **operator-experience** layer under-delivered on D-241's own promises — a re-arm asserted but unwritten, a no-expiry steer unbacked, a correctness signal that was only the delayed dark-digest — exactly the burden-of-proof-on-the-engine surface a non-engineer is most exposed to, the [validate-against-the-live-system](#) and non-engineer-operator disciplines biting where they should. All three fixes adapt the designed prose to the audit's own prescriptions without disturbing the seam adjudication: the **engine-run test read** is the §17/north-star behavioral correlate (a positive "it works," not an AI assurance), the **credential-specific re-arm** closes a [§7](../principles.md) reporting-without-remediation gap, and the **no-expiry fallback** removes an over-claim — so they landed in-line and the focused re-check confirmed them clean, the [D-147](0147-lock-the-design-review-and-qa-review-suites-the-v1-review-le.md)/[D-148](0148-lock-audit-library-the-engine-self-audit-delivery-the-tenth.md) remediation-audit precedent. The two carriers D-241 named were resolved to verify-no-edit on inspection (the architect lens independently confirming both), **recorded here rather than silently dropped** — the propagation matrix's honest-disposition discipline. Memory stayed untouched as the [D-146](0146-resolve-q33-github-actions-stays-the-default-audit-substrate.md)/[D-148](0148-lock-audit-library-the-engine-self-audit-delivery-the-tenth.md) pure-consumer precedent dictates; the re-lock set is the three docs that actually carry the new grammar.

## What we ruled out

Lock through the three operator-experience findings (rejected — the blocking re-arm gap would freeze a dead-end that mis-routes a non-engineer to the wrong credential a year out, ranking with a silent edit to a locked doc; the [§7](../principles.md) honesty bar requires the remediation that names which credential lapsed and the matching recovery). Edit memory to record the audit read or the test read (rejected — [D-146](0146-resolve-q33-github-actions-stays-the-default-audit-substrate.md)/[D-148](0148-lock-audit-library-the-engine-self-audit-delivery-the-tenth.md) settled the read as a pure consumer needing no memory change, and the test read is likewise a pure read; touching memory would re-introduce exactly what those decisions kept out and over-widen the re-lock set). Force-edit [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) and [scenarios/first-run.md](../architecture.md#first-run-provisioning) because D-241 named them carriers (rejected — on inspection both are sound verify-no-edit: the master indexes at altitude and the scenario omits the whole backup-setup family, so a credential-only addition would be asymmetric; the honest move is to log the disposition, not manufacture an edit). Run a fresh full four-lens re-audit of the remediation (rejected — the fixes were bounded, audit-prescribed designed-doc prose resolving a trust-surface blocking finding, so the focused two-lens re-check is the proportionate close, the [D-148](0148-lock-audit-library-the-engine-self-audit-delivery-the-tenth.md) precedent; the operator did not require more). Fold D-241 and D-242 into one entry (rejected — the authorize/resolve split is the litigation-protocol convention, [D-237](0237-authorize-flipping-the-memory-backup-default-to-a-shared-vau.md)/[D-238](0238-resolve-the-d-237-memory-backup-shared-vault-flip-the-four-l.md)).
