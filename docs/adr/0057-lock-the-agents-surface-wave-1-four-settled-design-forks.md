# Lock the `agents` surface (Wave 1); four settled design forks

*Decided 2026-05-25 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Ratify and lock [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) to its designed end-state: the spawnable **subagent-persona** surface the [build orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) invokes at a gate (cold-context reviewer or scoped-write worker). The **persona template** is the core grammar (routing fields `role`, `lens`, `model-tier`, `permissions`, `output-contract`); the personas are additive modules ([Q1](../reference/open-questions.md)); the roster is **derived by presence** from frontmatter (file-drop, no wiring — [§14](../principles.md), Risk [R5](../reference/risks.md)). A Wave-1 advance. Four operator-settled forks are ratified: **(1) output-contract** — each finding carries `{severity ∈ blocking|serious|nit, message, location ref (file/line)}`; the agent **reports**, the orchestrator **dispositions** (fix/log-issue/escalate) per the locked finding-disposition [policy](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md); its [schema](../spec/systems/surfaces/schemas.md) instance is **`.v1`-versioned and fixture-tested** ([D-046](0046-harness-comparative-mining-a-guardrail-integrity-gap-a-contr.md)), authored at build time and only *specified* here. **(2) `role`** — a **closed 3-value set** {`plan-review`, `worker`, `pre-submission-review`}; a non-member role is a coherence finding (an "unknown role" is impossible by construction). **(3) `model-tier`** — **concrete tiers** {`opus`, `sonnet`, `haiku`} (durable platform model *aliases*, not versioned IDs that rot). **(4) lens recognition** — **by consumption** ([§14](../principles.md), no central list): an agent whose lens nothing in the orchestration consumes is a coherence finding, while *which* gate consumes *which* lens is **deferred to the `designed` build-orchestration** (principle stated, gate model not frozen). The meta-contract record is completed with its missing `name`/`purpose` rows, and the platform substrate is named honestly: routing fields are **engine-governed-and-read** frontmatter distinct from the platform-enforced keys (`name`/`description`/`tools`/`permissionMode`/`model`), inert to the platform by convention. Catalog membership is additive — **no ontology re-lock** ([D-042](0042-procedural-content-grounding-surface-cluster-designed-the-bo.md)).

## Why

`agents` anchors entirely on **locked** neighbors (validation `coherence` kind, finding-disposition policy, module-system, ontology, schemas, templates); its only `designed`-neighbor seam — build-orchestration — is consumed via **pure deferral** (the [D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md)/[D-056](0056-lock-the-tools-surface-wave-1.md) pattern), so a future build-orchestration lock cannot force an agents change, and the closed `role` set is a [D-054](0054-lock-the-operations-and-docs-surfaces-wave-1-partial.md)-style **decided risk** (downstream consumes the locked grammar; build-orchestration's gate skeleton already mirrors exactly these three roles). The four-lens cold-session design audit ([D-018](0018-cold-session-design-audit-required-before-any-lock.md): adversarial / technical-feasibility / non-engineer-operator / architect, no shared context) ran on the edited doc; technical-feasibility verified every platform fact firsthand against the current sub-agents docs (`.claude/agents/` file-drop discovery, `name` identity field, the read-only-via-`tools`/scoped-write-via-`permissionMode`+`PreToolUse` split, the durable model aliases, and that subagents cannot spawn subagents — consistent with "the orchestrator is the session, not a persona"). Adversarial and architect returned no blocking/serious and confirmed the gate model is deferred not frozen. The **two non-engineer-operator serious findings were resolved before the lock**: (a) the coverage story over-claimed — a lens *no gate consumes at all* is invisible — fixed by an explicit **honest-limit** clause that routes the affirmative "what was reviewed vs not" roll-up to build-orchestration's plan-gate risk assessment and makes no completeness guarantee this surface cannot keep; (b) the Coherence section routed findings "to the operator" in internal jargon — fixed by stating the operator-facing rendering is **plain language** ("no security review ran on this change") and that `§14`/dangling-check-kind/role-lens vocabulary is maintainer-only (operator-communication law + leak guard). Nits also folded: the role→gate vs lens→gate seam clarified, "pass-through"/"not reviewed" rewritten to "did not run a review," and the inert-extra-keys claim framed as convention not guarantee. Propagation checked and **stands with no edit**: the `glossary.md` `Agent (persona)`/`Persona template`/`Derived roster` entries still match (the output-contract internal shape was unspecified there); `engine-architecture.md` already indexes agents truthfully; [R5](../reference/risks.md) is *confirmed* by the file-drop/derived-roster design (it closes only when the wiring library is built — no R5 edit). The `core`-`provides` mapping stays the [D-054](0054-lock-the-operations-and-docs-surfaces-wave-1-partial.md) deferred-forward obligation (modules/core is a stub).

## What we ruled out

Make `role` or `model-tier` open/additive like `lens` (rejected — role maps to build-orchestration's fixed gates and the tier split is deliberate; an open role weakens "role implies the gate," and concrete durable aliases do not rot). Have the agent recommend a disposition in the output-contract (rejected — blurs the locked finding-disposition seam; the agent reports, the orchestrator decides). Write the gate→lens consumption map into the locked agents doc (rejected — freezes the `designed` build-orchestration's gate model, the lock-atop-a-churning-neighbour failure; state the §14 principle and defer the map). Maintain a central recognized-lens list (rejected — reintroduces a list installs must mutate, against [§14](../principles.md); recognition is by consumption). Claim full review-coverage at this surface (rejected — the lens set is open; the affirmative coverage view is build-orchestration's plan-gate duty, and over-claiming breaks the operator's trust). Batch with the tools lock (rejected — independent surfaces, sequential per operator, each its own audit/decision/validate across a reground).
