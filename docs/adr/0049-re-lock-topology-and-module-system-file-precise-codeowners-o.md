# Re-lock topology and module-system: file-precise CODEOWNERS ownership and the coherence infra-artifact carve-out

*Decided 2026-05-25 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Under **explicit operator approval**, re-litigate **two coupled locked docs** under one decision (the [D-043](0043-surface-set-completion-re-lock-ontology-and-hooks-to-clear-t.md) coupled-relock precedent, since they are one semantic event), so the engine/product wall can express **file-precise** CODEOWNERS ownership that coexists with a product's own content in shared paths. **(1) [Repository topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md):** the wall's engine ownership is the **module manifests' `provides` union together with the foundation's own infrastructure-artifact set** (the engine manifest, the root `CLAUDE.md`, the engine-owned `.github/` files including `CODEOWNERS` itself), **file-precise rather than whole-directory** — so where a product co-occupies a Claude-native path the engine owns only its own files; and law 1 is reconciled to "**exclusively occupies `.engine/`**, otherwise contributes engine-owned keyed entries to the shared paths (`.claude/` contents, root `CLAUDE.md`, `.github/`, `.mcp.json`, `.gitignore`) without claiming the container," extending the keyed-coexistence clause from `.mcp.json`/`.gitignore` to all of them. **(2) [Module system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md):** coherence leg 3 is carved to "every engine file maps to exactly one `provides`, **or is a named foundation infrastructure artifact**" (the same named set), since those files are not surface instances and so are not surface-grouped in any `provides`; without the carve-out the locked check would flag them as orphans, the contradiction the file-precise wall makes live. Both docs are re-locked under this decision (`lock.py --relock`). The combined four-lens cold-session audit ([D-018](0018-cold-session-design-audit-required-before-any-lock.md)) on the authored docs surfaced the leg-3 contradiction (adversarial lens) and confirmed the rest of the wall lockable (architect lens); the operator chose this resolution over the two alternatives below.

## Why

Brownfield coexistence ([D-048](0048-provisioning-delivery-designed-end-state-brownfield-capable.md)) requires the engine to own its *files* within a shared directory, not the whole directory — whole-directory `.claude/**` ownership cannot express "engine owns its files, product owns its own." Deriving ownership from `provides` ([principle §3](../principles.md)) makes that file-precise and reuses the manifest's one-file-one-owner mapping, but the highest-trust engine files (the manifest, root `CLAUDE.md`, control-plane `.github/` artifacts) are deliberately *not* surface-grouped `provides`, so a bare `provides`-union wall would leave them unowned and a literal reading of leg 3 would flag them as orphans. Naming the infrastructure-artifact set in both docs closes the wall hole and resolves a latent ambiguity that predated this pass (leg 3 never said whether infra artifacts were "engine files" it governed). The two edits are one event — the leg-3 carve-out exists solely to make the topology wall consistent — so they re-lock together under a single combined audit, per [D-043](0043-surface-set-completion-re-lock-ontology-and-hooks-to-clear-t.md).

## What we ruled out

Fold the infra artifacts into the required `core` package's `provides` to avoid touching module-system (rejected — strains the locked "`provides` ... grouped by surface" definition, since infra artifacts are not surfaces; it would itself need a clarifying module-system edit, so it does not cleanly avoid the locked doc, and the named-set carve-out is the more honest model). Keep topology greenfield-true (whole-directory ownership) and handle brownfield file-precise ownership as provisioning mechanism only, re-locking nothing (rejected by the operator — leaves the locked wall wording unable to express the coexistence the contributor principle requires, the smaller blast radius not worth the standing imprecision). Re-lock topology alone and leave module-system's leg 3 contradicting it (rejected — a silent contradiction between two locked docs is exactly what the lock mechanism exists to forbid). Two separate decisions, one per doc (rejected — they are one surface-set-of-files-ownership event; splitting fractures the rationale, [D-043](0043-surface-set-completion-re-lock-ontology-and-hooks-to-clear-t.md) precedent).
