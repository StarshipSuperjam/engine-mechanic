## Why

core owns the validation engine (the dispatcher + the five closed kind callables), so the entry point the meta-check rides and the core-kind fixtures that co-locate with those callables are core's — the [D-207](0207-authorize-the-dependabot-pr-contract-exemption-a-ci-author-a.md) seam-bullet path for the entry point (a real, thin extension) and the forced co-location of fixtures with their callables, both proportionate to the kernel ([§12](../principles.md): forced, not new scope). Topology verify-no-edit is the correct, lighter adjudication of the conditional co-carrier: its own two-tier model already absorbs the fixtures namespace as a leaf, so editing it would be the unwarranted heavier path. The bounded three-lens gate (the kernel edit is two clauses) caught the unrecorded topology disposition and the entry-point over-framing before the lock.

## What we ruled out

**Add a repository-topology placement clause for the fixtures namespace** (rejected — topology's "laws now, leaves later" + law 1 already govern a non-surface `.engine/` leaf, as `.engine/tools/` and `.engine/boot/` demonstrate; a clause would reopen a locked Tier-1 doc for a leaf it already covers — the structure-for-zero-gain anti-pattern [D-213](0213-authorize-the-human-facing-front-door-re-litigation-the-root.md)). **Frame the entry point as free reuse of the coherence invocation** (rejected — target-substitution is a genuinely new affordance; the [§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md) honesty posture names it as the added extension). **Carve the core-kind fixtures to validators-core or a sibling** (rejected — fixtures co-locate with their callables, which are core's; carving them would strand them from the kinds they test, the forced-co-location [§12](../principles.md) keeps them in core, not new scope). **Run the full four-lens audit** (rejected — a bounded two-clause kernel edit; the focused three-lens-with-feasibility is the proportionate gate, the buildability check folded into the adversarial lens).
