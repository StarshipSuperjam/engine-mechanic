## Why

`memory-substrate` is the next required-spine lock — upstream-anchored on the now-locked `core` (its sole dependent, the stub `engine-knowledge-graph`, adapts to it via the stable `search` dependency target [D-086](0086-cognitive-foundations-as-required-packages-reconciliation-me.md); the lock-discipline rule governs dependencies, not dependents), and its build-readiness precondition was met at L2. As the first state-wiring required module, its highest-stakes claims are the wiring reversibility, the hook coexistence, and the circular-dependency avoidance — all four lenses confirmed these resolve by the locked module-system seam discipline and the presence-bound interface, not by assertion. The dry-run is the buildability precondition, not a substitute for the cold audit, which this lock passed in its own right. The folded `.mcp.json` notation nit is a platform-fact correctness alignment with the locked grounding docs, not a design change; every other nit is dispositioned leave/advisory with rationale, none touching a locked doc.
