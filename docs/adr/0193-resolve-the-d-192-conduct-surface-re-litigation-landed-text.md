*Decided 2026-06-14 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The [D-192](0192-authorize-the-conduct-surface-codes-of-conduct-a-tier-3-pros.md) change-set is landed. The mandatory landed-text cold audit ran with four independent no-shared-context lenses (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) on the as-written [conduct](../spec/systems/surfaces/conduct.md) surface + the locked-neighbor edits ([modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md), [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md)) + the non-locked carriers; a **dry-run build simulation** of the conduct slice (**Slice CD**) followed. **Outcome: six serious folds applied; four findings rejected with rationale (two rested on lens errors); one dry-run finding resolved; the new surface lands `designed`; two foundations re-locked.**

  **The audit + dispositions.**
  - **Folds applied** (to the non-locked conduct doc + the re-locking provisioning + first-run): (1) the [§15](../principles.md) treatment is reframed so **mechanical-immunity + committed-review are the load-bearing, day-one protections** and the validation guard is **defense-in-depth** — a `validators-core` `soft-warn` rule that ships **with** the conduct slice (no window), checking conduct *content*, never making conduct enforce; (2) the floor-load section names the **in-repo `@import` approval-dialog caveat** (in-repo, not external — not subject to the first-time external-import prompt; confirmed at build-spec); (3) the **disable-list** is given a recommended realization (a frontmatter `disables` list on the operator layer; exact schema a leaf) — resolving the dry-run's one finding; (4) the **"bounded"** claim names its backstop (the [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) retirement-default probe; no hard numeric cap); (5) the **visibility** claim is softened (the active stance is committed/readable, *and may* be surfaced via the [status verb](../reference/glossary.md)); (6) the **first-run seed is disclosed** to the operator in plain language (the plan-mode-default disclosure posture), never silently installed.
  - **Rejected with rationale.** **(adversarial, "the §15 guard does not exist when conduct ships" — BLOCKING)** rejected: `validators-core` comes online **mid-`core`** and each surface slice ships its own checks ([D-175](0175-correct-d-174-s-validators-core-timing-it-comes-online-mid-c.md)/[D-176](0176-sharpen-d-175-the-validators-core-module-seed-supersession-l.md)), so Slice CD ships the guard *with* conduct; and §15 safety rests on mechanical-immunity + committed-review, not the guard (the guard is defense-in-depth) — folded as the §15 reframe above, not a blocker. **(architect, "boot was edited but marked verify-no-edit" — BLOCKING)** rejected as a **lens error**, ground-truthed: [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) was **not** edited (`validate.py` shows exactly two lock-fingerprint failures — core, provisioning; a boot edit would show a third); the cited lines are boot's existing [D-189](0189-authorize-the-operator-checkout-boundary-re-litigation-confi.md) stranded-checkout surfacing. **(feasibility, "≥2 `@import` per file unverified")** rejected: current Claude Code docs show multiple imports per `CLAUDE.md` (supported). **(operator, "hidden authoring verb")** rejected: `/engine-help` derives its listing from the committed operator-typed verb files (the [§14](../principles.md) discovery axis), so the conduct-authoring verb is discoverable by construction; its name is a build-spec leaf.
  - **Dry-run — BUILDABLE-WITH-GAPS → clean.** Cross-doc consistency passed (the two-layer model, tier-3 pure-posture, rides-`core`, the seed/preserve, and the per-rule-id composition agree across conduct/core/provisioning/the roadmap); all major deferrals are named build-spec leaves with owners; the one finding (the under-determinate disable-list form) is resolved by fold (3).

## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious finding resolved before re-lock; it ran (four lenses) plus a dry-run, the genuine findings were folded in the as-written text, two lens findings were ground-truthed as errors and rejected, and the design is faithful to [D-192](0192-authorize-the-conduct-surface-codes-of-conduct-a-tier-3-pros.md): a tier-3 pure-posture `conduct` surface, floor-`@import`ed hook-independent, two layers composed per-rule-by-id, seeded-and-preserved so the operator's stance travels, riding `core` by the §12 cannot-be-an-extension rule. The new surface lands `designed` (not locked) by lock-discipline — a fresh downstream surface settles before it locks — while the two **edited** foundations re-lock (a locked doc cannot be left edited-but-unlocked). The honest tiers (pure posture; floor-load substantially-reliable-not-airtight; the guard defense-in-depth; "bounded" by posture + audits-flagging) are named, never over-promised.

## What we ruled out

**Lock the `conduct` surface this pass** (rejected — lock-discipline lands a fresh downstream surface as `designed` to settle and gather build-conformance evidence; locking it together with the edited foundations is the system+module-pair lock the module-vs-system lock-gate discipline warns against; it locks in a later pass on the operator's explicit go). **Edit [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) to enumerate the conduct `@import`** (rejected — boot's floor list is the grounding-story essentials, not an exhaustive `CLAUDE.md` inventory; core enumerates the floor content, as it already does for never-strand-main). **Edit [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) to name conduct in law 5** (rejected — law 5's general provides-membership rule already classifies `operator.md`; naming every preserved artifact is not required, and the minimal-change default governs). **Edit [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md)/[check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md)** (rejected — the weakening guard is a `validators-core` rule (data); the locked dispatcher/kind/grammar is unchanged; D-192's edit-listing of them was imprecise and is corrected here). **A hard numeric cap on the conduct set** (rejected — "bounded" is posture (the every-session attention cost) with the audits retirement-probe the backstop; a numeric cap is arbitrary, the [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) thin-floor precedent).
