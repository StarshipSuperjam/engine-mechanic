# Resolve the D-215 operator-prose-register re-litigation: landed-text cold audit, two docs re-locked

*Decided 2026-06-18 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The [D-215](0215-authorize-the-operator-prose-register-re-litigation-sharpen.md) change-set is landed and **re-locked**. The mandatory landed-text cold audit ran with **four** independent no-shared-context lenses (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) on the as-written edits in [surfaces/docs](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md) and [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) plus the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) carrier. **Outcome: no fatal flaw; three serious folded (register-edge distinctness, the honest-tier overclaim, the false-positive bound); the rest dispositioned.** Re-lock set: **2**.

  **The audit + dispositions.**
  - **Adversarial — one "blocking" resolved-by-fold + rejected-on-rationale; serious folded.** The lens charged the register edge is a *synonym* for "never dumbed down." **Resolved + rejected:** the two edges are genuinely **orthogonal** — a doc can be complete and accurate yet condescending in *how it addresses the reader* (the failure that actually shipped); the synonym reading came from the landed wording leaning on "padding the obvious"/"over-explain", which blurred quantity into register. **Fold:** the register clause ([glossary](../reference/glossary.md) + [docs surface](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md)) is retightened to *stance* — "never explaining at length what they plainly already grasp **as if they could not**" — with the orthogonality stated outright ("complete and accurate yet talking down"). **Serious — §7 honest-tier overclaim (folded):** "the law's teeth / caught rather than shipped" dressed a *recommend-only* audit as enforcement and contradicted the audits "Report, never heal" commitment. **Fold:** all three sites now say the probe **surfaces a condescending doc for remediation** at the audit tier (a recommendation the operator adjudicates), never a hard gate. **Serious — mis-promotion ([D-192](0192-authorize-the-conduct-surface-codes-of-conduct-a-tier-3-pros.md)) (dispositioned):** the new register edge is the operator's **deliberate, audited call** — the operator was explicitly offered a "no law edit" option and declined it in favour of "law-as-rubric + audit teeth", which is exactly the deliberate-operator condition D-192's mis-promotion rubric requires for a corpus law (not a session generalizing a quirk).
  - **Technical-feasibility — PASS, no blocking; serious folded into one bound.** Confirmed register-judgment is within the audit persona's existing judgment envelope (it already judges truth/usefulness/retirement; the rubric is now a stated standard), and the doc-probe reliably has a target each cycle (v1 ships ≥1 orientation doc). **Serious (folded):** register is more subjective than a broken cross-reference — without a bound it risks false positives that train the operator to dismiss the probe. **Fold:** posture law 3 now bounds the register judgment **conservative and audience-aware** — weigh a doc against its *intended* reader (a thorough beginner doc is not condescension), flag only clear talking-down, never stylistic preference, and a disputed register finding is a **legitimate decline** (law 1's no-quota / declining-is-a-real-choice).
  - **Non-engineer-operator — no blocking; the fix judged "real, not theater"; folds shared.** The standard is recognizable and the prose itself was found to *practice what it preaches* (non-condescending). The teeth-honesty and false-positive concerns are the same two folds above; the operator-adjudication lane (a register finding the operator disputes is closed without a rewrite) is now stated in posture law 3.
  - **Architect — one "blocking" reframed to an honest deferral; one serious dispositioned; consistency confirmed.** The four prose sites (glossary, docs two-edges, docs anti-drift, audits law 3) tell one consistent story. **"Blocking" (reframed):** the [D-215](0215-authorize-the-operator-prose-register-re-litigation-sharpen.md) phrase that [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) "inherits the law by reference" overstated it — check's `message` standard does **not** cite the operator-communication law by name. **Disposition:** corrected here — check is register-*adjacent* ("never written only for an engineer") and its message quality is **already audits-reviewed as posture** ([check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md)), but the explicit register edge and the *docs-scoped* doc-probe do not reach `check` messages this pass; extending register coverage to check messages is **explicitly deferred / revisitable**, the operator's docs-focused scope honored, no false-inheritance claim and no check edit. **Serious (dispositioned, no edit):** the audits "## Design commitments" is silent on register — but it is a high-level summary, and register-judgment is already subsumed by **"Judgment, not mechanism"** + **"Report, never heal"**; once the "teeth" overclaim was folded, no incoherence remains. Other locked docs citing the law generically ([control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md), [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)) stay true — a richer law does not stale a "governed by the law" reference.

  **Folds applied before re-lock:** [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) (register clause → stance + orthogonality + honest tier), [surfaces/docs](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md) (the two-edges section + the anti-drift tail → orthogonal edges, honest tier, recommend-not-block), [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) (posture law 3 → honest tier + the conservative/audience-aware bound + legitimate-decline).

  **Re-lock set (2):** [surfaces/docs](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md), [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md). **Re-lock:** `python3 lock.py --relock` run on both under this decision; `python3 validate.py` green. **Carrier edited (non-locked):** [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) (the *Operator-communication law* definition). **Verify-no-edit (logged, architect-confirmed):** [surfaces/check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) (register coverage of check messages explicitly deferred — above), [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) (mechanical, never prose register), the generic law-citations in [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) / [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md). **No [open-questions](../reference/open-questions.md) opened/resolved; no [deviations](../reference/prototype-deviations.md) entry.** Still **42 docs locked** (2 re-locked, none added).

  **Build-owes (engine-template, downstream — not authored here):** rewrite `docs/getting-started.md` to the sharpened operator-communication law (substance + register); realize the audit doc-probe's **register judgment** in the audit persona's instructions — conservative, audience-aware, recommend-not-block, with the operator-decline path.

## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious resolved before re-lock; it ran (four lenses), surfaced no fatal flaw, and the three seriouses (register distinctness, the §7 overclaim, the false-positive bound) were folded before the re-locks. The landed text is faithful to [D-215](0215-authorize-the-operator-prose-register-re-litigation-sharpen.md): the register edge is a *distinct stance* standard (orthogonal to substance), routed into the audits doc-probe as a **conservative, recommend-not-block** rubric — the teeth being the probe *examining* register where it never did, honestly tiered as audit-judgment, not a hard gate. The operator's deliberate choice of the law edit (over the offered no-law-edit option) satisfies the [D-192](0192-authorize-the-conduct-surface-codes-of-conduct-a-tier-3-pros.md) mis-promotion bar; check-message register coverage is honestly deferred, not falsely claimed.

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock without the landed-text audit** (rejected — HARD lock gate). **Keep "the law's teeth / caught rather than shipped"** (rejected — §7: dressing a recommend-only audit as a hard gate, and it contradicted "Report, never heal"; the honest tier is *surfaced for remediation*). **Leave the register judgment unbounded** (rejected — false positives train the operator to rubber-stamp-dismiss the probe, the law-1 failure mode; the bound is conservative + audience-aware + legitimate-decline). **Let the register clause keep the "padding the obvious / over-explain" quantity framing** (rejected — it blurred substance into register and fed the synonym charge; retightened to stance). **Keep the [D-215](0215-authorize-the-operator-prose-register-re-litigation-sharpen.md) "check inherits the law by reference" wording** (rejected — factually overstated; corrected to an honest register-adjacent + explicit-deferral framing). **Edit [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) to add the register edge this pass** (rejected — the operator's docs-focused scope; deferred/revisitable, not silently claimed-covered).
