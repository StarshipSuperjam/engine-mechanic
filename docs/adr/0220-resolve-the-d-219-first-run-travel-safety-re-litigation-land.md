## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious resolved before re-lock; it ran (four lenses), found no fatal flaw — the affected-set arithmetic and `custom/script` feasibility were re-verified against the live `origin/main`, and the invariant is byte-consistent across its five homes — and the one serious finding (the path-read leg's static reach) was folded into validation's coverage bullet as a [§7](../principles.md) honesty caveat before the re-locks. The landed text is faithful to [D-219](0219-authorize-the-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-a-retire.md): a property-defined provisioning-local invariant (reference-closure, strict no-guard), a mandated hard CI `custom/script` check honest to its mechanical reach, the topology anchor, and the operator-communication message law — with the build realization and the computed-path residual correctly left as build-owes.

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock without the landed-text audit** (rejected — HARD lock gate). **Leave the check described as uniformly static across all four legs** (rejected — the path-read leg is statically best-effort; an unqualified claim is the very [§7](../principles.md) overclaim D-219 rejects for the guard exemption). **Widen the *invariant* to match the check's static reach** (rejected — the property is total by design; it is the *check* whose mechanical reach is scoped, the honest mechanical/posture split). **Treat the adversarial lens's "Opened by [D-220] dangles" as a wording defect** (rejected — it is the expected forward-reference this resolve + re-lock close in the same pass, the R27→[D-218](0218-resolve-the-d-217-derived-committed-artifact-reconcile-re-li.md) convention). **Restate the no-guard clause in every reference home** (rejected — the reference homes defer to provisioning as definition-of-record; restating risks the [D-218](0218-resolve-the-d-217-derived-committed-artifact-reconcile-re-li.md)-class drift).
