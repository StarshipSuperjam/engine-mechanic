# New principle [§20](../principles.md): spec-conformance is the standing target; no construction milestone licenses an under-build — kill phase-deferral as a build license, scoped to engine-integrity (not the product the Engine builds)

*No date was recorded for this decision in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The operator (non-engineer sole maintainer), about to start build-conformance review toward engine-template v1, observed the build is **hopelessly hampered by scaffolding**: build sessions keep hedging work as "not worth doing until post-v1" or "not needed until operating in a deployed repo," and the builds get more half-measured each pass. **Grounding found the spec does not cause this — it mandates the opposite** ([engine-architecture](../architecture.md): the engine is a *stateless builder* that reads from committed files and does not branch on "am I deployed"; [§4](../principles.md): the template ships the *machinery* with empty stores; boot/state/memory/knowledge/attention/guardrails run **identically** template-and-deployed). The hedging is **build-session posture**, not a spec defect, leaking at two seams the spec left open: the construction-governance `CLAUDE.md` framing ("genesis stretch … superseded at M1") that primes a provisional posture, and an **unbounded deferral grammar** (dry-run's "deferred to a later named owner") that build sessions stretch to cover *phase* targets — M1 was the last such target, v1 the next, the pattern repeating. **The operator explicitly minted a standing law** (the feedback_dont-generalize-steers-into-laws bar met — the operator's own audited call, not an AI over-generalization): the spec is the **complete final state**; every deviation from it is driven to **full conformance**, in measured doses by *build order* (1 PR or 23, each a complete dose parking nothing); **zero** cases of the system not driving to compliance. **§20 is the standing generalization of [D-003](0003-specify-the-full-end-state-before-the-first-build-pr.md)** (whole end-state settled before build; capability layered by build order, never by cutting from the end-state) — D-003 is a decision, §20 makes it a standing tie-breaker. Four clauses: (1) every build step drives its slice to full spec capability, done only when nothing it covers is left partial; (2) **no construction milestone (M1, engine v1, "once deployed") is a deferral license** — a named owner is a concrete build step / the stage-0 seed / the first-run apply, never a milestone; (3) **the boundary is membership in the spec, and the spec — not the build session — decides it**: a capability is in-spec unless a `locked` doc or logged decision explicitly scopes it out, so the sanctioned non-builds are only the engine==product construction-repo differences (stage-0 §6, [D-111](0111-resolve-q22-q23-the-construction-repo-carries-no-codeowners.md)) and an explicit out-of-v1 scope-out; (4) **it binds the Engine to the Engine spec and does not reach a product the Engine builds**. **Carriers** (all freely-revisable, no lock touched, no litigation alarm): [principles §20](../principles.md) (the authored new principle) + the two mirror instruments' adjudication grammar — dry-run §2/§8 (a "named owner" excludes a construction milestone; CONFIRM a parked-behind-milestone finding) and build-conformance §4/§8 (a slice built partial/stubbed/milestone-deferred is a DIVERGES, even if it passes its own tests) — and stage-0 §6/§7 (a new harness rail; supersession at M1 swaps *which governance file is active*, never how completely a slice is built).

  **The four-lens cold audit** (adversarial · architect · non-engineer-operator · faithfulness/propagation, no shared session context, against the drafted text) ran before authoring; **blocking/serious findings folded:** **(faithfulness BLOCKING)** the draft's "three engine==product *absences* (instantiator lifecycle, CODEOWNERS, manifest)" **miscounted** — stage-0 §6 lists three *consequences* of the degeneracy, only CODEOWNERS truly *absent* (the manifest is present-but-hand-seeded, the `CLAUDE.md` present-but-distinct, the instantiator *not running* the root cause); **corrected** to "the instantiator never runs in a repo never generated from itself, so no rendered CODEOWNERS, a hand-seeded manifest, and a distinct construction-governance `CLAUDE.md`." **(convergent BLOCKING — adversarial+architect+operator)** the out-of-scope clause's "membership in the final state" was a **build-session judgment call** — the reclassification leak that reopens the release valve; **closed** by anchoring membership to a `locked` doc or logged decision ("a build session may not reclassify in-spec work as out-of-scope"). **(operator)** "phase-milestone" collided with the *product* milestones an operator may direct → renamed **"construction milestone,"** disambiguated as the engine's own M1/v1. **(architect)** the [§13](../principles.md) anchor was over-read as a literal firewall → re-grounded on **two autonomous specs** (engine vs. operator-intent), with a plain operator guarantee added ("the engine will neither block an MVP product the operator scopes nor silently degrade its own machinery to fit one"). **(architect+operator)** altitude/clarity tightened to principle-level prose. **Rejected findings** (over-tagged, ground-truthed against the source): the "§20 contradicts the locked audits-auto-tuning deferral ([D-076](0076-lock-the-audits-system-re-founded-for-the-deployed-repo-hygi.md)/Q17) and the post-v1 [engine-mechanic](../spec/systems/lifecycle/external-contribution.md) locus ([D-102](0102-cross-repo-external-contribution-as-a-first-class-v1-operati.md)/[D-184](0184-resolve-the-d-183-issue-authoring-grammar-correction-landed.md))" alarm — clause (3b) **exempts** them and faithfulness confirmed [Q11/Q17/Q18/Q21/Q31/Q37](../reference/open-questions.md) are genuine out-of-v1 scope-outs (non-members §20 blesses), not parked in-spec work; and the "stage-0 is a locked-doc edit" alarm — stage-0-harness.md §1 and locks.yaml confirm the `wbs/` group is freely revisable.

  **Product scope — answers the operator's Q2** ("how would this affect a product the Engine builds in a deployed repo?"): §20 binds **engine→engine-spec only**, enforced by the two instruments that **retire at v1 and never ship in a generated repo**. The deployed operator may direct an intentionally staged/MVP product; the engine stays whole everywhere ([§4](../principles.md)/[§5](../principles.md)) and never degrades its machinery to fit a staged product.

## Why

the engine's north star is to cold-start *full* work on any project and earn trust on evidence; an engine-template that builds *itself* at half capability, pleading "I'm only a template," fails that bar on its own repo. The spec already mandates a whole, stateless engine — so the honest fix is not new machinery but to (a) state the standing rule the build kept dodging ([§20](../principles.md), generalizing [D-003](0003-specify-the-full-end-state-before-the-first-build-pr.md)), (b) tighten the two instruments' adjudication so a milestone-parked under-build reads as the divergence it is, and (c) correct the construction-governance framing that primed the provisional posture. The membership anchor is the load-bearing fold: without it, "scoped out of v1" is a build-session escape hatch and §20 merely renames the disease; with membership decided only by a `locked` doc or logged decision, the release valve closes. Scoping §20 to engine-integrity (not the product) via the [§13](../principles.md) two-autonomous-specs framing answers the operator's Q2 without a runtime mechanism — the instruments simply do not exist in a deployed product repo.

## What we ruled out

**Encode as an application of existing principles / a [goals-and-quality](../reference/goals-and-quality.md) sharpening, no new principle** (rejected — the [D-261](0261-establish-the-artifact-warrant-discipline-a-7-17-application.md)/[D-262](0262-authorize-the-disposition-issue-resolution-check-a-17-7-mech.md) "no new principle" precedent applies where existing principles already cover the case; here none states the standing rule, and the operator *explicitly minted* a governing law, clearing the [R6](../reference/risks.md)/contract-threshold bar). **Merely discourage phase-deferral rather than forbid it** (rejected — a discouraged release valve is still a release valve; the operator's directive is zero cases). **Add a mechanical hard-gate that blocks under-builds** (rejected — "is this slice fully built" has no clean non-AI correlate; dressing it as a `hard` check would be the [§7](../principles.md) posture-as-mechanism trap; it rides build-conformance's surfacing + the merge gate like the rest of conformance, residual [R16](../reference/risks.md)). **Leave "membership in the final state" to build-session judgment** (rejected at audit — the reclassification leak; anchored to `locked` docs/decision-log). **Mint a glossary term or a new risk entry** (rejected — [R6](../reference/risks.md)/[D-255](0255-sharpen-the-post-v1-revisit-triggers-for-product-knowledge-g.md) anti-sprawl; §20 is the anchor, the residual rides [R16](../reference/risks.md)). **Edit the built engine-template `CLAUDE.md` from this workspace** (rejected — layer discipline; the spec layer authors the stage-0 §7 rail, the build layer realizes it as a tracked owe).
