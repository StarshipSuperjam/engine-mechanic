# Name the deferral seam: a new descriptive principle §16 (the ownership-axis sibling of §14)

*Decided 2026-05-26 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Add **[principle §16](../principles.md) — "Deferral seams — the integrator relays; the owner detects and owns"** and a matching [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) "Deferral seam" term. The principle fixes a disposition the design already runs everywhere but had never named: at a seam between two systems, **detection of the upstream condition and the upstream mechanism stay with the owning substrate; the integrator binds to the seam's stable *channel contract*, not to the enumerated *set* of what it relays.** The integrator still owns its acting-mechanism *over* the channel (telemetry dedupes/promotes/auto-resolves, [close](../spec/systems/lifecycle/close.md) gates on the recorded subset, [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) ranks, [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) orders and renders) — "relay" names the ownership boundary, not an absence of work (the precision guard against a dumb-pipe reading). **Descriptive, not a new mechanism:** §16 codifies behavior already locked into boot, attention, telemetry, close, and provisioning, so **no design law and no locked doc changes.** Propagation per the matrix: `principles.md` (§16 appended), `glossary.md` ("Deferral seam" added), this entry. `engine-architecture.md` is unaffected (it indexes *systems*, not principles); no `CLAUDE.md`/`README.md` change (a section added to an existing top-level doc, not a new doc).

## Why

The disposition is the design's dominant decoupler, independently re-derived under five local vocabularies — boot's "Seams are deferrals," [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md)'s "It reads; it never owns," [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md)'s "cognition emits, telemetry acts," [close](../spec/systems/lifecycle/close.md)'s "close only triggers it and never gates it," and [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md)'s "Boot stays read-only — it reads and surfaces, never restores." It is the **ownership-axis sibling** of [§14](../principles.md) (the *discovery axis*): §14 answers *which providers are present* (derive from presence, never a central list an install mutates); §16 answers *who owns what across a boundary*. The two compose without collapsing — boot's surfacings are pure §16 with zero §14 presence-binding; a check-suite roster is pure §14; telemetry's findings inbox is both — and both ride the seam discipline [§12](../principles.md) credits with fault-containment. The payoff is a lighter change-propagation web at no cost to operability: a new upstream producer/item attaches additively, an owner's later settling cannot force the integrator's, and a cold-session audit cites §16 instead of re-deriving the split. The cost was just paid in evidence: the [D-077](0077-lock-the-provisioning-system-terminal-foundation-lock-the-bo.md) two-pass provisioning audit re-derived the deferral seam **three times** for want of a name — the adversarial/architect [blocking] on provisioning asserting boot *compares* versions (resolved on boot's deferral law + the [D-076](0076-lock-the-audits-system-re-founded-for-the-deployed-repo-hygi.md) no-re-lock precedent), the bootstrap surfacing-split restated as three clean owners, and a pass-2 architect [blocking] on a sentence that re-described boot's offer "instead of deferring to it." Embodied across [D-029](0029-cognitive-substrate-is-one-workflow-a-2-store-1-register-1-c.md) (push-not-pull scent), [D-059](0059-lock-the-state-system-wave-2-head-the-committed-cursor-recon.md)/[D-062](0062-lock-the-attention-system-wave-2-the-policy-plus-function-pr.md) (state and attention reads-never-owns), [D-063](0063-lock-the-boot-orientation-system-wave-2-terminal-the-integra.md) (boot's seams-are-deferrals lock), [D-075](0075-lock-the-telemetry-system-guardrails-arc-head-the-triage-vol.md) (telemetry emit/act), [D-076](0076-lock-the-audits-system-re-founded-for-the-deployed-repo-hygi.md) (additive item = a leaf, not a re-lock), and [D-077](0077-lock-the-provisioning-system-terminal-foundation-lock-the-bo.md). Rigor is proportionate ([D-074](0074-sweep-the-stale-q1-references-resolved-by-d-066-d-068-re-loc.md)/[D-078](0078-citation-accuracy-re-litigation-repoint-stale-q4-references.md) reasoning): the HARD cold-audit lock gate is **not** triggered because nothing locks and no design surface is created; the gate is `validate.py` (link + structure + unchanged lock fingerprints across all 27 locked docs) plus a four-item self-check (non-redundancy with §14, the precision guard, faithfulness to each cited locked passage, the leak guard). §16 is written in maintainer vocabulary and carries the §12/§14 leak guard.

## What we ruled out

**Fold it into [§14](../principles.md)** (rejected — conflates set-membership-by-presence with ownership-across-a-seam; §14's own glossary entry already fences the discovery axis off as "distinct from the closed wiring seam," and the deferral seam is a third distinct axis, shown by boot's surfacings being pure §16 with no presence-binding). **Edit the 27 locked system docs now to back-cite §16** (rejected — unlike the [D-078](0078-citation-accuracy-re-litigation-repoint-stale-q4-references.md) case of a locked doc citing a *resolved question as open*, omitting a cite to a principle that postdates a doc's lock is **no current-state falsehood**, so no re-litigation is forced and `validate.py` flags nothing; back-citation rides each doc's next legitimate edit, the [D-074](0074-sweep-the-stale-q1-references-resolved-by-d-066-d-068-re-loc.md)/[D-078](0078-citation-accuracy-re-litigation-repoint-stale-q4-references.md) pattern of doing locked-doc citation fixes as their own justified re-litigations rather than bundling them into an unrelated pass). **Treat §16 as locked / settled-of-record** (rejected — principles are living framing docs, not locked design docs; §16 evolves in place like §1–§15, and the lock machinery does not apply to `principles.md`). **Defer it or merely log the observation** (rejected — the operator chose to author it now; leaving the design's dominant decoupler unnamed keeps costing per-seam re-derivation, as [D-077](0077-lock-the-provisioning-system-terminal-foundation-lock-the-bo.md) demonstrated). **Bundle provisioning's Finding 1 into this pass** (rejected — it was already resolved inside the [D-077](0077-lock-the-provisioning-system-terminal-foundation-lock-the-bo.md) lock; this is a standalone §16-only pass).
