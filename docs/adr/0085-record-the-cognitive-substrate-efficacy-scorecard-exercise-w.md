# Record the cognitive-substrate efficacy-scorecard exercise (workspace process); route its acceptance gates and build-spec cautions

*Decided 2026-05-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Record, as **workspace process** (layer discipline — not an Engine surface; kept out of the design docs like [Q15](../reference/open-questions.md)), the **cognitive-substrate efficacy-scorecard** exercise: a trustworthy pre-build scoring of [state](../spec/systems/cognitive/state.md), [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md), [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md), [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) + [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) by five independent cold lenses (adversarial, technical-feasibility, non-engineer-operator, cognitive-science/CoALA, IR/retrieval-quality) against each claim's resting metric, separating internal-validity / claim→metric-traceability (scored now) from empirical efficacy (deferred with a named test). It found the **architecture earned** and surfaced the defects remediated in [D-080](0080-re-litigate-state-honesty-name-the-floor-s-known-unbounded-f.md)–[D-084](0084-re-litigate-boot-constraints-correct-the-post-compaction-pla.md). This entry closes the exercise by routing its two non-design-law residues: **(1) acceptance gates → [Q15](../reference/open-questions.md)** (enriched with the named gates — attention's expert ranking fixture authored fixture-before-weights; memory/scent retrieval-quality evals with recall@k/precision@k bars and paraphrase pairs; the M3 longitudinal feedback-loop test; the K3 100%-gap-recall test; the operator-perception battery S3b/S6b/M9b/M13/B9b/B11b on true non-engineers incl. first-timers); **(2) build-spec cautions** that change no locked law but bind future build passes — bundle-an-FTS5-SQLite **feasibility** (prebuilt binary vs a source build that strands a non-engineer; weighed against §12/§5); the scent's "no strong match" **threshold** set by an empirical precision/silence sweep, not guessed; the `search` **interface contract** exposing the score/k semantics a reranker needs (so the semantic-module swap stays clean); the control-plane guard distinguishing an **authorized-empty read from a swallowed 4xx** (state S7); and the memory **backup/privacy re-check cadences** (M10/M12). **No relock; no design law changes** — a process record and a routing entry.

## Why

The scorecard is a workspace-process instrument (the pre-build sibling of Q15's post-build benchmark), so its home is the decision-log + Q15, never the locked Engine docs (layer discipline, per the workspace's standing rule that the planning layer stays separate from the Engine-being-designed layer). Recording it preserves the auditability of *why* [D-080](0080-re-litigate-state-honesty-name-the-floor-s-known-unbounded-f.md)–[D-084](0084-re-litigate-boot-constraints-correct-the-post-compaction-pla.md) happened and honors the finding-disposition rule — every surfaced finding is fixed-in-line ([D-080](0080-re-litigate-state-honesty-name-the-floor-s-known-unbounded-f.md)–[D-084](0084-re-litigate-boot-constraints-correct-the-post-compaction-pla.md)), logged as a build-spec caution, or routed to the [Q15](../reference/open-questions.md) empirical gate; none is buried in chat. The litigation-alarm re-litigations carried the design-law and honesty fixes; this entry carries the residue that is empirical (→ Q15) or build-spec (→ cautions). Propagation per the matrix: [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) Q15 enriched (workspace-process, update-in-place — Q15 is sharpened, **not** resolved, so it is not deleted), this entry; no system-doc, glossary, or architecture change (the cautions bind build passes, not current laws; the exercise creates no Engine surface).

## What we ruled out

**Write the scorecard into the Engine design docs** (rejected — layer discipline; it is workspace process, fenced like Q15). **Encode the build-spec cautions as locked laws now** (rejected — they are leaves that bind the build pass; legislating them pre-build is the laws-not-leaves violation, and FTS5-bundling needs its own feasibility pass). **Resolve and delete Q15** (rejected — Q15 is sharpened, not resolved; the benchmark is not built). **Leave the acceptance gates only in this session's output** (rejected — finding-disposition; they belong in Q15 where the release gate is tracked).
