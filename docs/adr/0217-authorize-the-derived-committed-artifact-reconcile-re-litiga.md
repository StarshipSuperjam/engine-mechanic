# Authorize the derived-committed-artifact reconcile re-litigation: a §19 source-determinism law + naming artifact-regeneration in build-orchestration's integrate (engine-template #136 concurrent-PR graph conflict)

*Decided 2026-06-18 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Authorize a re-litigation (litigation alarm) on explicit operator approval to (a) add a new cross-cutting principle **§19 — derived-committed artifacts are source-deterministic; conflicts on them are spurious**, and (b) name **derived-artifact regeneration as part of the locked [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) `integrate` step** — touching locked [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md), [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md), and [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md); the non-locked [principles](../principles.md), [glossary](../reference/glossary.md), and [risks](../reference/risks.md); and the `designed` [scenarios/build-session](../architecture.md#build-session-lifecycle).

  **The litigation alarm.** Nearly every PR regenerates the committed, fingerprint-gated **derived artifacts** — the [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) graph (`.engine/knowledge/graph.json`) and the [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md) self-map (`.engine/self-map.md`). Because they are committed and almost every PR rewrites them, two concurrent PRs collide on them; the contention hardens from an `engine-ci` "stale graph" red into a GitHub `CONFLICTING` merge **a non-engineer gate-holder cannot clear** (it needs a rebase they cannot perform). This was hit live — engine-template **PR #125** went `CONFLICTING` on `graph.json`, recovered by a manual rebase → regenerate-last → force-push — and is a **standing condition of post-M1 multi-PR / routine builds**. It is a direct leak of the [D-024](0024-the-engine-is-upgradeable-versioned-packages-upgraded-by-ove.md) value ("merge conflicts on customized engine files are exactly what a non-engineer cannot resolve") through a gap. engine-template #136 is the build session's litigation escalation, written *for* an engine-planning session to decide and comment the resolution back.

  **The decision — a property-defined law + a named integrate step, the operator's "principle §19 + graph.json/self-map only" choice.** A four-lens cold plan-audit (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) ran against the proposed plan; it found **no fatal flaw** — the adversarial and feasibility lenses independently traced the live generators and confirmed every field of `graph.json` and line of `self-map.md` is a pure function of the reconciled source tree, so a *real* divergence lives in a **source** file (out-of-class) and regeneration from the reconciled tree is genuinely **lossless** — and its blocking/serious findings are folded into the authorized design:
  - **§19 (new principle, [principles](../principles.md)).** A *derived-committed artifact* is a committed file whose **entire content is a deterministic function of the committed source tree** (same tree in → byte-identical out); any authored content, or dependence on a run-date / at-time judgment / per-instance state, **disqualifies** it. Two laws: (1) generation is **source-deterministic** (canonical serialization; the CI fingerprint gate *exercises* it, an explicit regenerate-twice round-trip test *enforces* it — determinism owned by [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md)); (2) a merge/rebase conflict on a member is **spurious** — resolved solely by clearing the conflict and **regenerating from the reconciled tree**, never a hand-merge or side-pick; the operator never has to *resolve* it (an AI session does — owned by [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) integrate post-M1, an interim session before). Membership is **by property, not a frozen list** (the v1 roster and instructive non-members live in the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md)); it is the merge-time corollary of [§2](../principles.md)/[§3](../principles.md) and the integrator-regenerates-an-unowned-artifact mechanism is [§16](../principles.md).
  - **Integrate ([build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md), locked).** The single-writer orchestrator reconciles the base and **regenerates every §19 member from the reconciled tree as the final authoring step**, so the PR is regenerated-and-current before review; on a member conflict it **clears and regenerates unconditionally** (not dependent on a side-pick succeeding); **reconcile-before-merge is load-bearing** (GitHub's server-side merge cannot run a local merge driver); a quiet plain-language Review-record line discloses the auto-regeneration (pulled, no imperative relay marker); in [external-contribution](../spec/systems/lifecycle/external-contribution.md) regeneration runs in the engine fork-main context, never onto the product/upstream branch.
  - **Two corrections to the #136 framing (confirmed by the audit).** Sub-question 1 **dissolves** — the as-built `knowledge-coverage` check is faithful fingerprint-coverage (`mode: fingerprint`, `knowledge_gen.check()` re-derives-and-byte-compares), **not** an as-built byte-compare divergence; nothing to reconcile on the gate. Catalogs are **excluded** from the class — authored governance + derived coverage, so a catalog conflict is real and regenerating would destroy authored content.

  **Scope. Re-lock set — 3** (all currently locked → re-locked, none added): [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md), [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md), [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md). **Carriers (non-locked, authored this pass):** [principles](../principles.md) (§19), [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) (the *Derived-committed artifact* term), [risks](../reference/risks.md) ([R27](../reference/risks.md)), [scenarios/build-session](../architecture.md#build-session-lifecycle) (the integrate-regeneration line — `designed`, not a re-lock). **Verify-no-edit (confirm at the landed-text audit):** [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) (its "up to date before merging" omission already names the integrate-reconcile as the behavioral carrier this change strengthens — no edit), [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md) (its `provides` already names the generators; §19 is a law not a provides-item — no edit, so the count holds at 3), [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) (named §19's determinism owner, but the round-trip test is a build-leaf under its existing check mechanism — no design edit). **Build-owes (engine-template, downstream):** the post-M1 integrate class-reconcile; a **required** determinism round-trip test; a **required** named operator-reachable "reconcile this PR" recovery path + a plain-language degradation message for a failed regeneration (the pre-M1 / unattended-routine `CONFLICTING` case); sorting the glob in `module_coherence.provides_claims()` (defense-in-depth); an optional `.gitattributes` custom merge driver; the self-map commit-boundary-hook asymmetry note. The re-locks land under the **Resolve** entry ([D-218](0218-resolve-the-d-217-derived-committed-artifact-reconcile-re-li.md)) after the mandatory landed-text cold audit.

## Why

The litigation-alarm protocol authorizes a locked-doc change on explicit operator approval, and the gap is real: the locked design already assigned stale-base reconciliation to the orchestrator's integrate ([control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md), [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)) but nowhere named that a **textual conflict on a derived-committed file** is resolved by regeneration, nor stated determinism as a law for the class — so a non-engineer-hostile `CONFLICTING` state could (and did) arise. A property-defined principle is the right altitude because the class spans four systems (knowledge + ontology own the artifacts, validation enforces determinism, build-orchestration resolves), and property-not-list membership matches the corpus idiom ("lock laws, not membership"). The plan-audit's findings — the §7 enforcement overclaim, the audit-digest mis-sweep, the operator interim gap — are folded into the authorized wording before any edit, which is what makes this a litigated change rather than a session reflex.

## What we ruled out

**De-commit the graph / build it at CI or boot** (rejected — costs [§1](../principles.md), zero-boot-latency, reviewable PR diffs, and the offline cold-start truth the trust model leans on). **Strict "require branches up to date" / a vanilla merge queue** (rejected — already rejected at [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) as the very non-engineer-hostile blocked-merge this avoids; viable only as a team-tier opt-in). **A runbook-only fix, no design change** (rejected as the *durable* answer — fine as the pre-M1 interim, but it leaves the [D-024](0024-the-engine-is-upgradeable-versioned-packages-upgraded-by-ove.md) value violated whenever two PRs overlap). **Distributed laws in knowledge + ontology, no principle** (rejected — the operator's choice was the centralized §19; a four-system law restated in two locked docs duplicates and cannot bind a future third member). **Include catalogs in the class** (rejected — authored governance; regenerate-to-resolve would destroy content). **"Generation is deterministic, enforced by the fingerprint gate" as a hard claim** (rejected — §7 overclaim; the gate *exercises*, the round-trip test *enforces*). **"Reconcile-before-merge makes CONFLICTING impossible"** (rejected — it narrows the race, not eliminates it; the honest claim is spurious-and-no-engineer-recoverable).
