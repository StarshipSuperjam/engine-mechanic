# Litigate engine-template #361: a PR accidentally auto-closes an issue it declares only "Part of" (four incidents; the #355 authoring mitigation slipped); a submit-time **close-linkage consistency pre-flight** in build-orchestration's Review-record fill — re-litigate the [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) close-claims disposition; re-lock of [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) lands under D-284

*Decided 2026-07-05 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

A build session opened engine-labeled [engine-template #361](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/361) and routed it here. **The symptom:** GitHub auto-closes an issue from any `close`/`fixes`/`resolves #N` keyword — including one buried in prose — in the PR body **or a commit message**, so a pull request gets accidentally set to close an issue it only partly addresses; it has bitten **four times** (PRs #159→#37, #177→#136, #191→#171, #359→#274), each caught only by the operator by eye, and the engine then reports a wrong backlog at boot. **The diagnosis: this re-litigates the [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) close-claims disposition.** D-252's text is about operator-runnable acceptance steps; it named `Closes #N` only in passing as a GitHub-native correlate that "already exists," and the build crystallized that into "D-252 declined new merge checks over close-claims" — [PR #355](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/pull/355)'s *Out of scope* landed the authoring-discipline mitigation (the PR-template note) and stated a mechanical guard "stays a deliberate **D-252 re-litigation** if it is ever wanted." **#361 is that request, and the mitigation is insufficient:** the #355 note is posture, and incident #4 (#359→#274) landed **after** #355 shipped — the discipline demonstrably slipped. For a repo whose **burden of proof is on the engine** (the operator is a non-engineer who should not have to catch this by eye), an honest mechanical guard is warranted. **This entry is the litigation alarm + operator authorization** (given in-session across a four-lens plan-stage cold audit → an instrument re-decision) **and the design of record**; the **re-lock of [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) lands under [D-284](0284-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-submit-time-close-li.md)** after the mandatory landed-text cold audit — the carrier **edited-but-not-relocked** until D-284 (the [D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)/[D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md) authorize→resolve pattern; `validate.py` flags its lock fingerprint until the relock).

  **The grammar (operator-authorized; RESHAPED by the plan audit from the issue's first-draft "narrow CI check" framing to a submit-time pre-flight):** the instrument is **not** a CI check but a **close-linkage consistency pre-flight** the orchestrator runs at **submit, before it opens the PR**, filling the result into the [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) **Review** section — the same D-252/[D-254](0254-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-filler-renders-the-o.md) Review-fill channel this issue re-litigates. Its laws: **(1) Machine-decidable comparison, no intent-guessing.** The pre-flight compares the set the PR **will** close — GitHub's computed `closingIssuesReferences` (the body-keyword linkage) **plus** the closing keywords in the **integrated commit messages** (which that field does not reflect, and which only the submit-time orchestrator holds) — against the closing intent the PR **declares** in its own structured Scope/Out-of-scope. Two contradictions are decidable without reading intent: an issue the PR will close while its scope declares it only **"Part of #N"**, and the **comma-trap** undercount (`Closes #1, #2` links only `#1`, leaving `#2` silently open). **(2) Self-correct at the source, else surface.** Where the contradiction is unambiguously an accidental keyword (the structured scope declares "Part of #N" and carries no deliberate close line), the orchestrator **corrects its own PR body before opening** — the [§6](../principles.md) nudge realized at the source, on the engine's own control-plane artifact, **never** a read or edit of product scope (the [wall](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) holds); where intent is genuinely ambiguous it does **not** auto-edit and surfaces a plain-language line in the Review record. **(3) Not a gate; honest tier named.** The comparison is mechanical but its delivery rides the AI-authored Review record, so it inherits Review's **posture-truthfulness** tier and its "the engine's own account, your approval is the real gate" framing ([D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md)/D-252) — the [§17](../principles.md) residual named, not hidden, and **bounded by the operator's own independent view** (GitHub itself shows "will close #N" at the merge). Scoped honestly to body-declared and integrated-commit closes; a close reaching the issue by a path the orchestrator cannot see at submit is named as outside its reach.

  **Plan-stage cold audit (four independent no-shared-context lenses — adversarial · technical-feasibility/GitHub-facts · non-engineer-operator · architect): it REJECTED the first-drafted instrument and redirected to the one above.** The session's (and the issue's) first design was a **narrow soft CI check** (`custom/script`, [validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md), the [D-262](0262-authorize-the-disposition-issue-resolution-check-a-17-7-mech.md)/[D-263](0263-resolve-re-lock-validators-core-the-disposition-issue-resolu.md) shape at a soft tier). **Two BLOCKING findings killed it:** **(adversarial B-1, verified against the locked docs)** the negative-fixture meta-check is **`hard`-scoped** ([check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md):90-148, [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md):58-59, [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) *Negative fixture* — "every in-scope **hard** check," "expected **hard** finding") while its roster enumerates *all* `custom/script` instances, so the **first soft `custom/script`** lands in an unspecified gap — the "rides existing locked machinery, no validation re-lock" claim is **false**, forcing an unbudgeted validation/check re-lock; **(adversarial B-2, verified against [§6](../principles.md)/D-263)** a non-blocking CI check is the **§6 dead zone** — it reaches neither the AI mid-session (§6's nudge audience) nor a walk-away operator (non-blocking, unread), and [D-263](0263-resolve-re-lock-validators-core-the-disposition-issue-resolu.md) already **rejected `soft`** for this check-class on §17 grounds (reporting-dressed-as-remediation, [§7](../principles.md)). **The operator selected the audit's own recommended alternative — the submit-time Review-record pre-flight — which is not a check and not a gate, so neither blocking finding applies.** Findings folded: **feasibility TF-1** (`closingIssuesReferences` is **body-keyword-only, not commit-message**) is turned from a blind spot into a **covered** case — the submit-time orchestrator reads the integrated commits directly; **TF-3** (`gh ≥ 2.72.0` / `gh api graphql` fallback) a build-spec leaf; **operator B1** (draft the three operator strings now, not by role-label) — done (the scope-contradiction / undercount / could-not-read heads-ups, all naming the closing line as the fix); **operator S1** (a soft signal needs a visibility mechanism) **dissolved** — the Review record is inherently the artifact the operator reads at consent; the **"Part of #N" parse-fuzziness** (adversarial SERIOUS-3 / operator N1) largely dissolves because the parse is over the engine's **own structured Scope**, not arbitrary human prose. The **landed** Option-B text gets its own cold audit under [D-284](0284-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-submit-time-close-li.md).

## Why

a repo whose burden of proof is on the engine cannot leave a mechanically-catchable, four-times-recurring miss to a posture mitigation that already slipped. The honest instrument is **neither** the issue's soft CI check (the §6 dead zone that also snags the `hard`-scoped fixture machinery) **nor** a hard CI gate ([§6](../principles.md)-disproportionate for a *recoverable* miss — reopen the issue — plus a fail-closed-on-outage merge-DoS and a fuzzy-parse false-block on an honest PR), but a **submit-time mechanical pre-flight surfaced in the Review record**: it reaches §6's AI audience at the source (self-correct before the PR opens) and §17's operator in the consent bundle, with no gate and the honest tier named. It reuses the D-252/[D-254](0254-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-filler-renders-the-o.md) Review-fill channel this very issue re-litigates — the [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md) "improvement on the engine's own grammar" shape, not a bolt-on. Authorize-now / resolve-after-landed-text-audit keeps the irreversible re-lock behind its own clean gate ([D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)/[D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)).

## What we ruled out

**The issue's narrow soft CI check** (rejected — the plan-stage audit's two BLOCKING: the `hard`-scoped negative-fixture machinery would force an unbudgeted validation/check re-lock, and a non-blocking check is the §6 dead zone reaching neither audience; [D-262](0262-authorize-the-disposition-issue-resolution-check-a-17-7-mech.md)/[D-263](0263-resolve-re-lock-validators-core-the-disposition-issue-resolu.md) already rejected soft for this class). **A hard CI check** (considered — the strongest, the only form that catches the miss when *neither* the AI nor the operator looks — rejected here: §6 counsels against mechanical refusal for a recoverable miss, fail-closed-on-outage is a merge-DoS, and a fuzzy "Part of #N" prose parse can false-block an honest PR; the operator chose the no-friction both-audiences instrument). **Keep the D-252 disposition as-is / authoring discipline only** (rejected — the #355 note is posture and slipped four times, incident #4 after it landed). **A new check kind or surface** (rejected — no new grammar; the pre-flight rides build-orchestration's existing submit-time Review fill). **Auto-edit the PR body on *any* contradiction** (rejected — where intent is genuinely ambiguous the orchestrator must not guess product scope; it surfaces for the operator instead — the [R9](../reference/risks.md) wall). **Home the surfaced line in the Validation section** (rejected — Validation is mechanical-check results; the pre-flight is a submit-time self-account, Review's judgment-layer home, the [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md)/D-252 taxonomy). **Write the [D-284](0284-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-submit-time-close-li.md) resolve entry now** (rejected — it records a landed-text cold audit that runs later; authoring it pre-audit would dress un-audited as audited, the [§7](../principles.md) violation this pattern prevents).
