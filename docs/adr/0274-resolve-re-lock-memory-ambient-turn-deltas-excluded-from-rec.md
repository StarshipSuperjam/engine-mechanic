## Why

memory is D-273's only carrier (the bundle holds the implementation, not the recall-membership contract), so the relock lands here after its landed-text audit. The audit earned its keep decisively: the adversarial lens caught that the fix as first written **left the #332 disease fully intact on the FTS-absent recall floor** — a blocking hole that single-author end-of-pass judgment missed — and that the "intrinsic field" framing under-specified the very kind-discriminator option (b)'s "nothing to carry" simplicity rests on; the operator lens caught that the recoverability promise had no floored delivery path. All folded to a recall-read property applied uniformly, an honest kind-discriminator, and a content-floored disclosure, before the irreversible lock. The authorize→resolve-after-landed-text-audit split kept the re-lock behind its own clean gate ([D-264](0264-authorize-git-native-retention-for-the-pre-migration-memory.md)/[D-265](0265-resolve-coupled-re-lock-of-memory-memory-substrate-sqlite-ft.md)).

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock the bundle too (honor the D-273 heading's "coupled" framing literally)** (rejected — the landed bundle text does not shift; a re-lock of an unchanged doc is empty churn, and the architect+faithfulness lenses confirmed the recall-membership contract is the system doc's alone). **Keep the index-rebuild framing and add a separate plain-scan clause** (rejected — two statements of one rule invite drift; the honest fix is to locate membership at the recall *read* once, covering every path). **State the discriminator as a positive delta field** (rejected — a delta is ambient/untyped by *Capture*; inventing a delta-side flag would contradict the typing model and re-open the S1 ambiguity). **Defer the disclosure content to the build-owe** (rejected — the operator-facing "your verbatim is recoverable" guarantee is the §7/§17 trust core of the change; only its wording is a leaf, the content is canon). **Write a per-delta exclusion readout into boot** (rejected — exclusion is not a forgetting event and carries no undo handle; whole-session exclusion collapses to one digest line, a build-owe).
