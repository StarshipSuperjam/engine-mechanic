# Sharpen the post-v1 revisit triggers for `product-knowledge-graph` and `clean-code` from operator-intuition to engine-observable signals (no locked re-litigation)

*Decided 2026-06-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Replace the intuition-only "when to build it" framing on the two deferred post-v1 stubs with a **concrete, engine-observable pressure signal per module** — a distinct signal each, because the two gate different capabilities on different axes. **[product-knowledge-graph](../spec/modules/product-knowledge-graph.md)** (the product-side structural map — the ctx/code-skeleton analogue): the signal is **cold-session structural live-read straining the bounded cold-context budget [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) allocates at boot** as the product grows — the [D-105](0105-hold-a-post-v1-product-knowledge-graph-module-stub-product-s.md) "live-read is cheap and current" bet inverting at scale — explicitly distinct from [R8](../reference/risks.md) (dense-graph hub-explosion, a representation risk) and [R9](../reference/risks.md) (design-artifact drift); it replaces "the obvious add-on once the v1 cognitive substrate has proven its value." **[clean-code](../reference/module-catalog.md)** (per-language code-style governance — the standards-management analogue): the signal is a **quality-consistency pressure** — recurring cross-session code-style divergence or repeated operator style-steers on engine-authored code, the point where a posture-only style stance (the [conduct](../spec/systems/surfaces/conduct.md) behavioral floor, which never enforces) and [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)'s learned `preference`/`lesson` enrichment stop holding consistency and the deferred *enforced* standards-injection seam into the worker [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) is earned; it fills a slot that previously named no trigger. **Both stay `stub`/unlocked/post-v1.** The triggers are **commissioning criteria — conditions the operator/engine recognize by observation that justify opening the module's design session — not v1 features**: v1 builds no detector or meter for either signal. **Propagation:** the two stub docs + the two [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) catalog blurbs (the product-knowledge-graph blurb carried the same stale phrasing; the clean-code blurb gains the symmetric clause) + this entry; **no locked doc moves**, `python3 validate.py` green, no lock fingerprint changes. **Verify-no-edit (dispositions recorded, the [D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md) discipline):** [D-105](0105-hold-a-post-v1-product-knowledge-graph-module-stub-product-s.md)/[D-095](0095-cut-expression-contracts-disposition-prose-organization-cove.md) rationale (append-only history — superseded by this entry, never edited), wbs/README.md + wbs/module-order.md + eadr-canon-manifest (a trigger is a when-to-build signal, not a dependency edge — both stubs stay outside the v1 build-order sort, no edit), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) (trigger-silent catalogued role — no edit), [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md) (no new/renamed term), [risks.md](../reference/risks.md)/[open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md)/locks.yaml (untouched, per the anti-choices).

## Why

A vague "the operator names it as the obvious next add-on" trigger is **out of character** with a design whose north star puts the **burden of proof on the engine to surface evidence** — everywhere else the engine carries the case on the operator's behalf; here the call to stand up two real capabilities rested on the operator's unaided intuition. The two triggers differ because the axes differ: product-knowledge-graph is a **structural-context-budget** pressure (the [D-105](0105-hold-a-post-v1-product-knowledge-graph-module-stub-product-s.md) cost bet inverting at scale), clean-code a **code-style-consistency** pressure (posture stops being enough; enforcement is earned) — a single generic trigger would mis-fit one. The live-read-cost pressure was **un-formalized anywhere** ([D-105](0105-hold-a-post-v1-product-knowledge-graph-module-stub-product-s.md)'s rationale named it in passing; no risk or open question carried it), so naming it on the module it gates is the matrix-faithful home. The pass grew from the operator's "does the engine produce context-ready software?" line: the v1 answer is native-exploration + the locked product-owned C4/structural model ([D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)/[D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md)), and these two stubs are the parked structural-map / standards-management axes — so the lock-free improvement available now is to make *when to build them* evidence-based rather than intuitive. A focused two-lens cold review (adversarial · architect) ran on the draft: the architect confirmed propagation complete with no gaps; the adversarial lens caught two — the budget referent was an ungrounded composite ("attention cold-start budget"), corrected to the grounded "bounded cold-context budget [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) allocates at boot" ([boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md): "Boot owns the event model; attention owns the budget within it"), and the clean-code trigger overclaimed [conduct](../spec/systems/surfaces/conduct.md)'s scope (conduct carries behavioral stance, not coding standards), reframed to posture-stops-being-enough-so-enforcement-is-earned, which also sharpens the conduct/clean-code seam.

## What we ruled out

**Re-litigate locked [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md) ([D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)) to add a product-side cold-start/resume artifact** — rejected: product-design already frames the arc42/C4 structural model as a living, first-class, build-consumed, product-owned output and [D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md) already names it as product-knowledge-graph's canonical derivation source, so the "first-class structural model" groundwork is locked-in; arc42's intro + the `docs/spec/` master already orient a cold session, so no resume gap justified reopening a just-locked keystone (lock discipline). **Add a new [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) entry for live-read-cost-at-scale** — rejected: low-severity, conditional, already mitigated by deferral — it is the deferral *rationale*, not a live v1 threat — so a new R-entry would be register sprawl (the [R6](../reference/risks.md) scope-discipline analogue); the signal lives on the module it gates, which cross-references R8/R9 for legibility. **Commit v1 to auto-detect/alert on either signal** — rejected as scope creep into v1 for post-v1 modules; the trigger is a documented commissioning criterion, and any automated surfacing is the module's own design-session concern. **Encode a numeric threshold in either trigger** — rejected: kept qualitative-but-concrete; the exact threshold is a post-v1 design-session determination, not a v1 commitment. **Use one shared trigger for both stubs** — rejected: they gate different capabilities on different axes (structural-context-budget vs. code-style-consistency); a generic trigger would under-specify both.
