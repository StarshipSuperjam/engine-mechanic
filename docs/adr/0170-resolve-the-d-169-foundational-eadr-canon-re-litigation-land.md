# Resolve the D-169 foundational-eADR-canon re-litigation: landed-text cold audit clean, five foundations re-locked

*Decided 2026-06-04 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The [D-169](0169-add-the-foundational-eadr-canon-the-engine-ships-its-own-why.md) foundational-eADR-canon change-set is landed and **re-locked**. The mandatory landed-text cold audit (five independent lenses — adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect · cognitive-substrate) plus a focused re-verification ran; the **operator lens returned clean** (no maintainer-vocabulary leak, the anti-explosion story legible, no operator friction, the stress-test gate actionable), and the design findings were resolved before re-lock:
  - **(blocking — overlay classification)** The canon-vs-instance split was sharpened from "by path" to **engine-owned-set membership at distinct paths**: the **foundational canon** rides [`core`](../spec/modules/core.md)'s `provides` (engine-owned → overlaid wholesale on upgrade, escalate-upstream), while a deployment's **per-instance eADR stream** is in no `provides` (deployment-authored → preserved), classified with **no content marker** (literal directory names a build-spec leaf). This is now homed at all three classification sites — the overlay ([provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md)), the preserved-vs-overlaid ownership law ([repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) law 5, which gains a **deployment-authored committed content** preserved category beyond config), and the **coherence carve-out** ([module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md), whose operator-config carve-out is extended to cover the deployment-authored instance stream so coherence does not read it as an orphan).
  - **(serious — cold-start)** The forward-edge wording was grounded in locked behavior: the canon adds **no forward `ratifies` edge** and [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) **persists no reverse edges**, so **no persisted edge targets a canon eADR** and the cold-start adjacency walk never pulls it in as ambient bulk; it surfaces only on a citation/scent/search match. [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) and [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) therefore needed **no edit** (assessed clean), as did [templates](../spec/systems/guardrails/templates.md)/schema and [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md).
  - **(accounting — supersedes [D-169](0169-add-the-foundational-eadr-canon-the-engine-ships-its-own-why.md))** D-169's non-locked-propagation line said "close the rationale-loss risk"; ground-truthing confirms **no such named risk exists in [risks.md](../reference/risks.md)** — the gap is [Q31](../reference/open-questions.md)'s, which this change addresses by shipping the rationale. The risks.md propagation actually owed and landed is the **[R8](../reference/risks.md)** not-instantiated note (no forward edge → no hub fan-out) and the **[R14](../reference/risks.md)** relation (the engine-mechanic reads the canon to build N+1). No new risk is opened (the canon-update rule + the stress-test gate + the propagation discipline cover canon-currency).
  **Re-lock set (5):** [contracts](../spec/systems/surfaces/contracts.md), [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md), [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md), [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md), [module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md) — the latter two added by the audit beyond D-169's named pair, as the new content category needs homes in the ownership law and the coherence carve-out. **Re-lock:** `python3 lock.py --relock` run on the five under this decision; `python3 validate.py` green. A **pre-existing ambiguity** in topology law 1 ("exclusively occupies `.engine/`" vs. allowing preserved operator/deployment-owned content within it) is **inherited from [D-167](0167-take-up-q17-component-a-authorize-a-five-foundation-re-litig.md)/[D-168](0168-resolve-the-d-167-operator-policy-override-re-litigation-lan.md)** (operator config already lives preserved under `.engine/`), **not** a regression of this change; it is left as-is (a future cleanup candidate), not re-litigated here.

## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious finding resolved before re-lock; it ran (five lenses + a focused re-verification), and the design cleared once the overlay-classification mechanism was made concrete (provides-membership, homed in topology law 5 + the module-system coherence carve-out) and the cold-start guarantee was grounded in knowledge's no-reverse-edges. The re-lock set grew from D-169's named pair to five because introducing a new "deployment-authored committed content" category obliges **every** file-classification site (overlay, ownership, coherence) to recognize it — the propagation-matrix consequence of the new category, not scope creep. The accounting correction follows the append-only discipline (D-169 stands as authored; this entry supersedes its "rationale-loss risk" miscount — the [D-168](0168-resolve-the-d-167-operator-policy-override-re-litigation-lan.md)-corrects-[D-167](0167-take-up-q17-component-a-authorize-a-five-foundation-re-litig.md) pattern).

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock only contracts + core (D-169's named pair) and leave the instance stream an orphan / the overlay unspecified** (rejected — coherence would mis-handle a deployment's own eADRs and the overlay could clobber them; the classification needs homes in topology + module-system). **A frontmatter `foundational:` marker to classify canon vs instance** (rejected again — the provides-membership rule classifies by the engine-owned set the overlay/CODEOWNERS already compute; no content marker, no file-parsing overlay). **Edit attention/knowledge to keep the canon off cold-start** (rejected — the no-forward-edge + no-reverse-edges properties already guarantee it; no edit needed). **Re-litigate topology law 1's "exclusively occupies" wording** (rejected — pre-existing, inherited from D-167/D-168, not worsened here; out of scope). **Open a new "rationale-loss" or "canon-drift" risk** (rejected — Q31 carries the rationale gap now closed; canon-currency is covered by the update-rule + stress-test gate + propagation discipline).
