# Recenter the spec on "the AI is the thing made trustworthy": reframe the operator from deficiency to role, and end banned-word-lists (clarity over jargon is judgment, never a filter)

*Decided 2026-06-21 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Correct a framing the operator surfaced: the spec characterized the operator primarily by **deficiency** ("a non-engineer who *cannot* read code / debug / hand-edit"), repeated in the docs every session reads first, and that priming kept **regenerating** paternalistic copy and **banned-word-lists** in the live engine-template build (six divergent `_FORBIDDEN` substring tuples forbidding ordinary words — `provides`, `sync`, `structural`, `precedence`, `schema`, `foundation`, `override`), even though the formal laws already forbade both. The corrected center: the operator's non-code-literacy constrains exactly **one** thing — *trust cannot be routed through code review* — which is the whole reason the audit / cold-session / behavioral-demo apparatus exists. **The thing being made trustworthy is the AI, not a fragile user.** Two changes realize it. **(1) Reframe the priming (role, not deficiency):** the grounding docs (CLAUDE.md north star, [goals-and-quality](../reference/goals-and-quality.md) stakeholder #1 + north star, [constraints](../reference/constraints.md) operator bullet, [engine-architecture](../architecture.md) §1/§2) recast the operator as **a capable adult who builds *through* the engine rather than by reading code; not assumed to read code, so the burden of proof is on the engine** — every hard constraint kept verbatim, only the deficiency *framing* and rescue tone removed. The **maintainer "cannot read code"** constraint ([constraints](../reference/constraints.md), [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) *Maintainer*) is **load-bearing and untouched**. **(2) End banned-word-lists:** the [operator-communication law](../reference/glossary.md) (canonical) and [§12](../principles.md) gain a clause — clarity over needless jargon is part of the law's **substance** edge, **realized by judgment, never a banned-word list**: no word is forbidden and **no forbidden-word list may be created** (a list invites list-growth and teaches that word-banning is a writing function); whether prose leans on jargon is a **semantic judgment** the [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) doc-probe and the per-PR build-conformance review own, **never a mechanical word filter** ([§7](../principles.md): a substring check grades prose mechanically). This is distinct from the §12 leak-guard (keep *internal machinery* out of operator narration — a relevance judgment, not a vocabulary ban). Opens [R30](../reference/risks.md). The live remediation (remove the `_FORBIDDEN` tuples; reframe the one condescending doc, engine-template [#135](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/135); reconcile contorted copy; scrub governance against re-growth) is build-owed as engine-template issues, not authored here.

## Why

Point-fixes never held because the deficiency priming — read first every session — regenerated the pathology faster than leaves could be patched; the fix is at the root (the priming + an explicit no-list rule), the only thing that stops re-derivation. The no-list rule is not new policy but the honest [§7](../principles.md) tier already stated by the [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) surface ("the validator cannot grade prose"): the live build had dressed a posture as mechanical enforcement, and that anti-pattern produced the ordinary-word bans. A four-lens cold-session design audit (adversarial / technical-feasibility / non-engineer-operator / architect, no shared context) ran against the landed change-set; its findings were folded before this entry: the "two-vs-three edges" contradiction with locked [surfaces/docs](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md) (clarity-over-jargon re-homed under the **substance** edge, not minted as a third edge, so docs needs no edit); the see-it-work over-claim (corrected — construction demos are maintainer-layer, distinct from the deployed operator's §17 evidence); and [R30](../reference/risks.md) severity honesty (post-v1-concentrated, not "operator-correctable"). The reframe was verified not to soften the load-bearing operator/maintainer "cannot read code" constraint.

## What we ruled out

**Keep curating the word-lists (prune ordinary words, keep the rest)** (rejected — the operator's directive is no banned-word lists at all; a list of any kind invites list-growth and teaches word-banning as a writing function, and grading prose by substring is the §7 anti-pattern). **Purge the word "non-engineer"** (rejected — an accurate factual descriptor; the problem is the deficiency *framing* and rescue tone, not the word). **Mint a new principle / a "third edge" for jargon** (rejected — clarity-over-jargon is already *never engineer-shorthand*, i.e. the substance edge; a third edge contradicts the locked [surfaces/docs](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md) two-edge decomposition ([D-064](0064-lock-the-interfaces-surface-final-wave-2-lock-single-active.md)/[D-069](0069-core-module-seam-walk-the-demarcation-operationalized-glossa.md) refine-don't-mint) and forces a needless second re-lock). **Leave the priming, fix only §12** (rejected — the priming is the regeneration source; fixing §12 alone lets the pathology regrow). **Soften the operator/maintainer "cannot read code" constraint** (rejected — load-bearing for the entire no-code-review trust model, [§17](../principles.md)/[§6](../principles.md)/[§15](../principles.md)).
