# Litigate engine-template #363: the Layer-2 erasure grammar was widened to batch multiple records under one operator-merged single-purpose PR, but the locked clause names "the target" singular; elaborate the underspecified cardinality (one-or-more) + name the batched-consent floor — re-lock of [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) lands under D-286

*Decided 2026-07-05 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

A build session merged [engine-template PR #362](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/pull/362) (memory Slice B of the [#274](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/274) anchor), widening the Layer-2 physical-erasure grammar so **one operator-merged single-purpose erasure PR clears a batch of records** (the committed proposal went `{"target": <id>, "cost": <line>}` → `{"targets": [<id>, …], "costs": [<line>, …]}` — parallel arrays), and correctly did **not** edit `engine-planning` from the build — opening it as engine-authored [engine-template #363](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/363) and routing it here. **The trigger:** the locked [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) *Layer 2* clause reads singular — the committed Issue/PR *"names the target by a **stable, content-free record id**"* and *"states the plain-language cost"* — so the plural committed shape has no grammar. **The diagnosis — clarify-and-confirm, NOT a build divergence (the [D-277](0277-litigate-engine-template-353-first-run-dead-on-arrival-in-a.md) shape does not apply; the [D-279](0279-litigate-engine-template-360-the-memory-sweep-reads-the-raw.md) Slice-A shape does):** cardinality was **underspecified, never fixed** at one. Ground-truth of the whole Layer-2 section shows **no clause fixes one-record-per-PR**: `single-purpose` traces to [D-209](0209-authorize-the-ledger-compaction-re-litigation-bound-the-appe.md)'s rejected anti-choice *"Bundle erasure into a multi-purpose PR"* — it binds the request's **purpose** (erasure-only, nothing else riding along), never the **count**; a batch is *many erasures of one purpose*, the opposite of the laundering D-209 forbade. The three load-bearing guarantees of the naming clause all **survive batching**: content-freeness ([D-007](0007-memory-data-is-local-and-gitignored-substrate-ships-empty.md)), the stable id that survives the rewrite, and *the operator never meets an offset or record-id*. The erasure **marker stays singular** (the observer mints one `operator-adjudicated-erasure` per named target under the **shared** merge SHA, via the unchanged `enact_erasure`), so *"marker keyed by the merge identity … removes the target idempotently"* stays literally true **per marker**, and the [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) **adjudication stays per-record** — only the *consent act* is batched, never the adjudication (the erasure firewall is untouched). **This entry is the litigation alarm + operator authorization** (given in-session over a four-lens plan-stage cold audit) **and the design of record**; the **re-lock of [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) lands under [D-286](0286-resolve-re-lock-memory-the-layer-2-erasure-grammar-is-one-or.md)** after the mandatory landed-text cold audit — the carrier **edited-but-not-relocked** until D-286 (the [D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)/[D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md) authorize→resolve pattern; `validate.py` flags its lock fingerprint until the relock).

  **The laws the carrier settles (a four-lens plan-stage cold audit sharpened scope and wording; none is a deferred leaf):** **(1) Cardinality is one-or-more, at the committed-proposal boundary only.** A single-purpose erasure PR may name **one or more** targets; each is named by its own stable, content-free record id and paired with its own plain-language cost. The observer fans out to **one singular marker per named target** under the shared merge SHA; compaction removes **each** idempotently. `single-purpose` binds purpose, never count. **(2) The batched-consent floor is operative, not a gloss.** Consent is to the **whole enumerated set**: the operator is shown the plain-language cost of **each** record and approves or declines the batch **as a whole** — **never a bare total** (identical costs may be grouped into explicitly-counted rows, but a count standing in for the enumeration is never sufficient consent, the [§17](../principles.md) un-weighable-consent trap). An **ill-formed batch is rejected whole**, never partly enacted — the operator never consents to a set they were not shown. This is the direct entailment of D-209's anti-rubber-stamp posture at N>1, not new scope. **(3) The firewall and per-record adjudication are unchanged.** Audit adjudication remains per already-retired record; batching bundles only the merge-consent act. Physical erasure stays reachable **only** through the audit-adjudicated, merge-gated path; no Layer-1 routine gains reach to erasure.

  **Plan-stage cold audit (four independent no-shared-context lenses — adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect): none rejected the clarify-and-confirm verdict; the scope and wording were sharpened.** **Adversarial ACCEPT + SERIOUS×3** — confirmed [D-209](0209-authorize-the-ledger-compaction-re-litigation-bound-the-appe.md) wrote the referent underspecified (never a fixed cardinality) so the verdict holds; **glossary [*Ledger compaction*](../reference/glossary.md) is NOT verify-no-edit** — its *"erases a record … a single-purpose erasure PR"* is a residual singular universal (the [D-279](0279-litigate-engine-template-360-the-memory-sweep-reads-the-raw.md)-B1 / [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)-B1 missed-path pattern), given the plural touch; **[memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md):235's opening *"removes a record"*** is a second in-region singular one paragraph above the plural edit → folded (adjudicated **each**); the consent floor must **lead** as the operative, falsifiable clause (a volume-pressured cold builder must not read a bare count as sufficient), and the entry must reconcile the batch against D-209's rejected multi-purpose-PR anti-choice → both folded. **Technical-feasibility FAITHFUL** — verified against the merged code (`build_proposal`/`write_proposal` parallel arrays with a `targets`/`costs` length check on **both** write and read; `enact_from_merged_prs` loops one `enact_erasure` per target under the shared `merge_commit_sha`; `compact.py`/`records.py` untouched; whole-batch reject + legacy single-target back-compat; per-target crash-resumability via the `seen` set), with one SERIOUS **wording** fold — the shipped `_collapse` **groups identical cost lines into explicitly-counted rows**, so *"one line per record / never a bare count"* would **overstate**; the faithful floor is *"each record's cost … never a bare **total**"* (grouping with explicit counts is allowed; a bare total is not). **Non-engineer-operator — no blocking on the verdict, BLOCKING on the floor's carrier** — batching one irreversible merge over N records is only safe if the per-record cost floor is bound to the operator-facing PR body with a **non-AI correlate**; satisfied by law (2) + the structural `targets`/`costs` 1:1 length-check and whole-batch reject (the correlate), and by stating consent is **all-or-nothing** (approve-all / keep-all). The **decline-is-all-or-nothing** fact is owed at consent **now**, not only parked for the future slice → folded into law (2); the **large-batch partial-keep / re-propose-remainder** *mechanism* is genuinely a later consent-model decision → parked as [Q40](../reference/open-questions.md). No new operator-facing content-floor disclosure is owed beyond the enumeration the floor already mandates. **Architect — no blocking** — **memory is the sole locked carrier** ([D-061](0061-lock-the-memory-system-wave-2-the-episodic-ledger-store-q3-b.md); [D-209](0209-authorize-the-ledger-compaction-re-litigation-bound-the-appe.md)/[D-210](0210-resolve-the-d-209-ledger-compaction-re-litigation-landed-tex.md) homed the whole erasure grammar inside memory), [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md)/[boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) are **count-agnostic consumers of the gate**, not coupled carriers (the [D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md)-shape trap checked and cleared — this is the arity of a grammar memory owns, no contract claim changes); propagation set **complete** (independent sweep found no omission beyond the :235 singular); the enumeration floor is **proportionate**, not [R6](../reference/risks.md) creep (it is the batched realization of the locked anti-rubber-stamp posture) and belongs in the memory system doc; **no build-slice vocabulary** ("Slice B/C") in the locked carrier — the law is arity/provenance-agnostic; the cost line stays at **floor-altitude** (the operator-facing wording is an audits/boot build-spec leaf); D-285/D-286 id allocation correct (tail D-284, no forward reservation); **no dry-run owed** (no module-graph edge moves — a system-doc law enrichment, the [D-279](0279-litigate-engine-template-360-the-memory-sweep-reads-the-raw.md)/[D-280](0280-resolve-re-lock-memory-the-consolidation-sweep-curates-harne.md) precedent).

## Why

the locked doc names *"the target"* singular while the merged build names a batch, and the *reason* the batch is legitimate — cardinality was underspecified and `single-purpose` binds purpose not count — was **unwritten in canon**, the same unwritten-contract ambiguity that produced [#360](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/360)/[#363](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/363). The honest, minimal fix is the [D-279](0279-litigate-engine-template-360-the-memory-sweep-reads-the-raw.md) move: a narrow re-litigation that closes the ambiguity in the doc the cold builder *does* read, confirming #362 conformant (it honors every guarantee the singular clause protected) while naming the one thing batching genuinely adds — a consent surface over an irreversible act that must show **each** record's cost, never a bare total. The capability is memory's by [D-061](0061-lock-the-memory-system-wave-2-the-episodic-ledger-store-q3-b.md), so the grammar and its floor land in the memory system doc and its glossary terms; the bundle enumerates implementation and widens nothing. The authorize→resolve-after-landed-text-audit split keeps the irreversible re-lock behind its own clean gate ([D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)/[D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)).

## What we ruled out

**Keep the clause singular and revert/rework #362 to one-record-per-PR** (rejected — the maintainer directed batched reclamation, "one merge clears the backlog, proportionate to session velocity"; the singular was incidental phrasing, never a litigated bound, and [D-209](0209-authorize-the-ledger-compaction-re-litigation-bound-the-appe.md) already spoke of "targets" plural — reverting would entrench a shape the design never chose). **Mark glossary [*Ledger compaction*](../reference/glossary.md) verify-no-edit** (rejected — the adversarial lens showed *"erases a record … a single-purpose erasure PR"* is a residual singular universal a cold reader reads as a 1:1 binding; leaving it is the [D-279](0279-litigate-engine-template-360-the-memory-sweep-reads-the-raw.md) anti-choice "fix only the headline word" that was rejected there). **Word the floor *"one plain-language cost line per record, never a bare count"*** (rejected — the feasibility lens ground-truthed the shipped `_collapse`, which groups identical costs into explicitly-counted rows; that phrasing would make the locked law false against the build — the honest floor is *each record's cost, never a bare **total***). **Harden the large-batch partial-keep / re-propose-remainder mechanism now** (rejected — it is a genuine consent-model decision the current slice's rare small crash-duplicate batches do not force; parked as [Q40](../reference/open-questions.md) for the consolidated-raw slice, with the all-or-nothing decline **disclosed now** so nothing is hidden — [§7](../principles.md)). **Carry build-slice vocabulary ("Slice B/C") into the locked carrier** (rejected — layer discipline; the law is arity/provenance-agnostic, the slice names live in this entry and [Q40](../reference/open-questions.md)). **Mint a new glossary term or risk entry** (rejected — [R6](../reference/risks.md)/[D-255](0255-sharpen-the-post-v1-revisit-triggers-for-product-knowledge-g.md) anti-sprawl; the existing *Ledger compaction*/*Operator-adjudicated erasure* terms carry the plural and the marker-singular/removal-set-idempotent design keeps R24 unchanged). **Write the [D-286](0286-resolve-re-lock-memory-the-layer-2-erasure-grammar-is-one-or.md) resolve entry now** (rejected — it records a landed-text cold audit that runs later; authoring it pre-audit would dress un-audited as audited, the [§7](../principles.md) violation this pattern prevents).
