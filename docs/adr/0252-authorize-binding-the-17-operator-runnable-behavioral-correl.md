# Authorize binding the §17 operator-runnable behavioral correlate to the PR's Review record (operator-runnable acceptance steps + reason-named no-op); re-litigate locked [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) + [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) (authorization + design; re-locks land under D-253/D-254)

*Decided 2026-06-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The operator (the non-engineer sole maintainer) observed that the PR-body completeness check enforces that the contract's sections are *present and filled* but cannot verify they are *accurate*, and pushed to bind more high-risk PR claims to machine/behavioral correlates so the prose is a consent layer over machine-linked evidence. **Grounding found the principle is already canon** ([§7](../principles.md): tiers named honestly, never dress posture as mechanism; [§17](../principles.md): confidence is bounded by how much of a change has a non-AI mechanical/behavioral correlate, and the design states that bound) and **four of the six correlates the operator named already exist** — "tests pass" is the **Validation** section (the CI results the operator sees green/red); "Closes #N" is GitHub-native + the [D-247](0247-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-committed-spec-referent.md) work-Issue→spec path read; "guardrail touched" is the [§15](../principles.md)/[D-051](0051-guardrail-integrity-the-builder-cannot-silently-weaken-its-o.md) weakening guard + acknowledgment; "knowledge regenerated" is the [§19](../principles.md)/[D-023](0023-check-system-locked-validator-architecture-the-check-surface.md) fingerprint coverage gate. The one genuinely unserved, high-value gap is **§17's behavioral-demonstration class — "the one class that routes around AI judgment" — is not surfaced at the PR/merge moment**, the place informed consent happens; today it lives only in the optional `demo` subcommand ([D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md)) and in [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md)'s acceptance-criteria typing ([D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)), never bound to a build's PR. **This entry is the litigation alarm + operator authorization** (given across a full design session — observation → plan approval → escape-hatch refinement → five-lens shape audit → the "fold into Review" landing choice) **and the design of record**; the **re-locks land under per-carrier resolve entries** (D-253 control-plane, D-254 build-orchestration) after each carrier's mandatory **landed-text cold audit** — the [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)/[D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md) authorize-then-resolve pattern. **The shape was cold-audited this session** (five independent lenses — adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect · engine/product-wall, no shared context; **wall verdict: HOLDS**); its blocking/serious findings are resolved into the design below.

  **The design.** The PR contract's **Review** section ([D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md); existing, already presence-gated, operator-read at the merge) gains new *content*, filled by [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) at **submit**: the **operator-runnable acceptance steps** for the change, rendered **verbatim** from the realized `locked` spec's **operator-runnable** how-verified rows ([D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)) via the existing **work Issue → spec doc → acceptance criteria** path read ([D-247](0247-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-committed-spec-referent.md)), **or** a **reason-named disclosed no-op** — never a silent absence (the corpus disclosed-no-op grammar, [D-207](0207-authorize-the-dependabot-pr-contract-exemption-a-ci-author-a.md)/[D-247](0247-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-committed-spec-referent.md)). The bounded no-op reasons: **(i)** all criteria engine/CI-internal (refactor / internal-plumbing / doc-only — name the class + the non-operator correlate that carries it, e.g. "behavior-preserving; the existing checks still pass"); **(ii)** operator-runnable criteria exist but cannot run in the operator's environment (point to the engine-side correlate); **(iii)** no `locked` spec to surface (the no-op build-orchestration already emits); **(iv)** trivial fast-path (the existing minimal truthful line). **Honest tier:** the steps are **posture-truthfulness, exactly like the rest of Review** — surfaced, the engine's own account, the operator's merge the real gate — **never a new hard gate**. **No new section, no new contract field, no new required check, no new check kind; [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) does not re-lock.** The only *mechanical* correlate stays the **existing spec coverage check** ([D-247](0247-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-committed-spec-referent.md)/[D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md): every `locked` criterion traced to committed work); the steps add no mechanical teeth — they surface the §17 behavioral correlate at the moment of consent. **`demo` is severed from the operator-runnable slot** ([D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md)/[D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md): a `demo`/`demo-*` subcommand is AI-run Python the operator cannot run — an AI-mediated tier; a criterion whose only correlate is an in-tool demo is "on the engine's account" (reason i), never an operator-runnable step — fusing them dresses the AI-mediated tier as the non-AI class, the [§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md)/[D-225](0225-recenter-the-spec-on-the-ai-is-the-thing-made-trustworthy-re.md) anti-pattern). **Verbatim projection, relay-only** (the wall HOLDS): build-orchestration renders the operator-typed rows **verbatim**, never authoring, grading, paraphrasing, or curating recipes (that would be a semantic judgment of product content — the [R9](../reference/risks.md) breach [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md) keeps off product artifacts); a read-only projection at the merge moment, and the engine never depends on a product spec existing (the disclosed no-op). **Operator-facing rendering rules** (build-spec leaves in build-orchestration, bound by the plain-language law): the two buckets render in plain language ("things you can confirm yourself" / "things I checked for you"), **never the raw typing tokens** ([D-120](0120-lock-core-the-root-module-the-closure-wave-s-terminal-ratifi.md)/[§12](../principles.md) leak guard); an **unrun recipe is a promise, not proof** — never stacked with a green check as "two greens," the honest caveat riding *in the artifact the operator reads*, not only a spoken line a walk-away operator misses ([§17](../principles.md), the [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md) Review-rendering rule); the recipe is an **offer for when the change matters, not a duty on every merge** ([goals §6](../reference/goals-and-quality.md) low-ceremony); prefer a **screen the operator clicks** over a paste-this-command, and a CLI-only check the operator realistically will not run is typed "on the engine's account," not operator-runnable; the no-op **explains the cause plainly** and never leans on "the tests pass" as if equal to the operator seeing it work; the steps inherit the **behavioral-attestation "must be able to fail" shape as posture** (a recipe that can only succeed is not evidence) — guidance, **not** a merge gate.

  **Surgical relationship to prior decisions** (only the named clause moves; the rest STANDS): **[D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md)** — the **Review** section + its honest tier (presence hard, truthfulness posture) + the Review-distinct-from-Validation/Claude-involvement taxonomy STAND; **ADDED** — Review also carries the operator-runnable acceptance steps (or reason-named no-op) as posture content, and the "**one** section whose subject the operator cannot independently corroborate" clause is **refined**: that property attaches to the **review judgment**, while the **operator-runnable steps are the one part the operator *can* corroborate by running** (the §17 class). The [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md) anti-choice "**fold Review into Validation**" STANDS rejected — homing the steps in Review (not Validation, "mechanical-check results") is exactly what keeps the mechanical-vs-judgment line the shape audit flagged. **[D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)** — the criterion→how-verified table + the operator-runnable vs engine/CI-internal typing + the "never one 'all green'" readout STAND, now **consumed** by build-orchestration at submit; **product-design takes no re-lock** (consumption is the integrator's — [§16](../principles.md)/[§14](../principles.md) — mirroring [qa-review](../spec/modules/qa-review.md) consuming the referent without a depends edge, [D-248](0248-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-referent-is-t.md)). **[D-247](0247-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-committed-spec-referent.md)** — the path read + the disclosed-no-op predicate STAND, now **also** feeding the Review steps render through the **same** channel, not a parallel one. **[D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md)** — the promoted `demo` subcommand class STANDS as an AI-mediated tier; **clarified** it is never an operator-runnable step. **[§17](../principles.md)/[§7](../principles.md)** — **APPLIED, not amended; no new principle** (the binding is §17's behavioral correlate realized at the deployed operator's merge; [R6](../reference/risks.md)/the contract-threshold keep principles exceptional — the architect lens confirmed).

  **Carriers + sequence** (each locked re-lock under its own resolve entry, after its landed-text cold audit, dependency-ordered — the contract owner first, the filler second): [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) (D-253, the **bounded** Review-paragraph edit — the contract-shape owner), then [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) (D-254, the **filler** — the render + no-op + the operator-rendering build-spec leaves). **[validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) and [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md) take no re-lock** (no new gate; consumer of its own typed rows). **Non-locked propagation** after the re-locks: [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) (a term only if one is truly needed — prefer reusing "operator-runnable acceptance steps"/the `Acceptance criteria` term and **avoid "demonstration,"** which the architect lens flagged collides with the `Behavioral attestation` tier), [scenarios/build-session.md](../architecture.md#build-session-lifecycle) (the submit step now fills the Review steps), and **recorded verify-no-edit dispositions** ([engine-architecture](../architecture.md), [scenarios/product-design-intake.md](../architecture.md#product-design-intake), [R9](../reference/risks.md), [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md), wbs) — the [D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md) "dispositions recorded, not skipped" discipline.

## Why

The operator's push is correct in principle and the grounding made it precise: the design already *says* prose is a consent layer over machine/behavioral correlates ([§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md)), and most of the named correlates are built — so the honest move is **not** a new "verification layer" (and emphatically not a CI exit-0 gate on a demo, which an AI scripts to its own chosen path — the [D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md)/glossary "must be able to fail" reason the *maintainer* runs and varies it). The single unserved gap is that **§17's behavioral class is not surfaced where consent happens**. Binding it to the **Review** record — the judgment-layer the operator already reads at the merge — is reuse of locked substrate ([D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md) Review + [D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md) typed rows + [D-247](0247-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-committed-spec-referent.md) path read + the [D-207](0207-authorize-the-dependabot-pr-contract-exemption-a-ci-author-a.md) disclosed-no-op), not a bolt-on, the [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md) "improvement on the engine's own grammar" shape. The five-lens shape audit earned its keep and **shrank** the change: it severed the `demo`-in-slot tier-dressing, moved the steps out of Validation (where a green-adjacent surface would have *widened* the §17 over-trust trap), and downgraded a proposed hard presence-gate to posture — because gating a no-op whose class-selection is uncorroborable AI self-judgment is a rubber stamp that dresses posture as mechanism ([§7](../principles.md)), with no non-AI correlate to gate on. What remains is a modest, honest, posture-tier addition that maximizes the share of a change carrying a behavioral correlate and **names the residual** where none exists — §17 verbatim. Authorize-now / resolve-per-carrier keeps each irreversible re-lock behind its own landed-text audit ([D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)/[D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)).

## What we ruled out

**Build the full six-correlate "verification layer"** — rejected (four already exist; treating it as a large material change re-derives settled design). **A required CI check that runs the demo and gates on exit 0** — rejected (an AI-scripted exit-0 shows only the AI's chosen path; a demonstration must be able to fail — [D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md)/glossary; it manufactures false confidence, the [§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md) trap, and revives the [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)-rejected agentic-verdict-as-gate / [D-038](0038-session-lifecycle-re-founded-on-native-substrates.md) close-friction spiral). **Compose the operator-runnable slot with the `demo` subcommand** — rejected (the operator cannot run Python tools — [D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md)/[D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md); it dresses an AI-mediated tier as the non-AI class). **Home the steps in the Validation section (or a new green-adjacent section)** — rejected (Validation is "mechanical-check results"; folding judgment/behavioral evidence in reopens the [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md)-rejected fold-into-Validation and worsens the "never one 'all green'" collapse). **A new hard presence-gate / new required check on the steps** — rejected (no non-AI correlate to gate; a rubber stamp dressing posture as mechanism; the steps ride Review's existing presence at the posture tier, the mechanical correlate stays the existing coverage check). **A distinct labeled "see it yourself" block separate from Review** — considered (the operator-lens honesty-of-kind preference) but **not taken** (the operator chose folding into Review; Review already carries the "engine's own account, your approval is the real gate" framing that guards over-trust, and folding mints the least new contract grammar). **Mint a new principle** — rejected (the binding applies [§17](../principles.md); [R6](../reference/risks.md)/contract-threshold keep principles exceptional). **Log-and-close with no re-lock** — considered (after the audit the gain is modest, since [D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md) already types the rows) but **not taken** (the operator authorized the binding; surfacing the correlate at the *merge moment*, where consent happens, is the value [D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md)'s spec-time typing does not deliver). **Re-lock [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md)/[product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md)** — rejected (no new gate or kind; consumption imposes no new obligation — the [D-248](0248-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-referent-is-t.md) consume-without-an-edge precedent).
