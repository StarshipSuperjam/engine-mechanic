# Resolve the D-185 plan-mode-default re-litigation: landed-text cold audit clean, two foundations re-locked

*Decided 2026-06-08 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The [D-185](0185-authorize-a-two-foundation-re-litigation-ship-a-native-plan.md) change-set is landed and **re-locked**. The mandatory landed-text cold audit ran with four independent no-shared-context lenses (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) on the as-written [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) + [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md) edits plus the non-locked carriers ([scenarios/first-run.md](../architecture.md#first-run-provisioning), [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md)). **Outcome: no blocking and no serious *design* findings; the precision folds below were applied in the as-written text before re-lock.** Re-lock set: **2**.

  **The audit + dispositions.**
  - **Architect — PASS (no blocking/serious/nit).** Propagation complete per the matrix; the three no-edit claims verified sound against the as-written text: [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) law 1 already owns operator-scalar ownership in the shared `.claude/settings.json` (the engine owns only its keyed entries; the operator owns the rest), so **no topology re-lock**; [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md) keyed-registration + [§15](../principles.md) surface untouched (`defaultMode` is a native field, not a hook wire); [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) asserts nothing now contradicted (the Routine-override *law* lives in modes; the non-interactive-posture *mechanism* is build-orchestration's existing [D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md) leaf). Two-axis separation (stance vs native permission mode) consistent across the four docs; the overlay seam is correct; R6 respected (native `/config`, no new surface).
  - **Technical-feasibility — PASS.** No unsupported platform claim: precedence `user < project < local` is named and handled (write-only-on-adoption, never on decline); whether `plan` is honored from project settings is **correctly deferred to the build-spec leaf** (the [D-178](0178-resolve-the-d-177-plan-file-carve-out-landed-text-cold-audit.md)/[D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md) re-verification discipline), with [D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md)'s "`defaultMode: auto` ignored from repo settings" cited as exactly why the platform field must be re-verified live; `~/.claude/settings.json` is a readable JSON file; `/config` is the real change path (the platform config surface exposes `permissions.defaultMode` — verified this session).
  - **Adversarial — no blocking/serious (2 nits, folded/noted).** modes↔provisioning wording is internally consistent (yield, travel semantics, §15 tier, Routine override all agree); the [D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md) citation in [D-185](0185-authorize-a-two-foundation-re-litigation-ship-a-native-plan.md) was **ground-truth-verified against the full D-140 text** — D-140 sited the routine preset operator-side as never-routine-mode's-to-wire and reasoned "were it committed, core/build-orchestration would own it," so it does **not** forbid a committed `defaultMode`; the citation is accurate (the ground-truth-citation discipline honored). The seam-ownership nit was **folded** (below).
  - **Non-engineer-operator — copy-law precision, folded.** The build-spec-leaf **copy laws** were sharpened so a non-engineer is protected by construction: the leaf now requires the copy convey **nothing global changed and the setting removes no safety gate** (the Explore write-gate + merge review both stand), drops the maintainer phrase "non-weakening ergonomics" from the law, and pins what counts as an *existing preference* (a present non-`plan` `defaultMode`); `/config` is named as the change path and its surfacing of `permissions.defaultMode` is added to the build-spec re-verification. (Literal operator copy stays a build-spec leaf by design — the audit checked the *laws* governing it, not wording.)

  **Folds applied before re-lock (both in the re-locked docs).** (1) [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) build-spec leaf: the **Routine-override mechanism** is attributed to [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)'s [D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md) leaf (modes owns only the override *law*), removing a seam over-claim; `/config`-surfaces-`defaultMode` added to the live re-verification. (2) [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md) build-spec leaf: copy law strengthened (removes-no-safety-gate + existing-preference definition; "non-weakening ergonomics" jargon dropped).

  **Matrix close-out — verified-no-edit (logged, not silent):** [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) (law 1 covers the operator scalar — no clause needed), [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md) (Explore gate + keyed registration unchanged), [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) (routine *mechanism* is its existing [D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md) leaf; the override *law* is modes'). **[risks.md](../reference/risks.md) assessed — no clause:** the feature is posture with graceful degradation (if the platform ever fails to honor the field, the session opens in the default mode and the Explore gate still holds), the platform-field dependency is build-spec-re-verified, and the operator-confusion residual is handled by the copy law + legibility (platform mode indicator + boot stance line) + the native `/config` escape — no standing architectural risk to register. **Non-locked carriers edited:** [scenarios/first-run.md](../architecture.md#first-run-provisioning) (the conditional plan-mode-default step + note), [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md) (the new **Native permission-mode default** term + the **Mode (stance)** two-axis clarification), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) §7 (the one-line native-default note). **No [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) opened/resolved; no [deviations](../reference/prototype-deviations.md) entry.** The **build-repo follow-up** (engine-template: keep settings.json free of a static `defaultMode`; implement the instantiator detect/adopt/yield step; re-verify `plan` is honored from repo settings) is **not** authored in this workspace — to be filed against the engine-template build after this lock.

  **Re-lock set (2):** [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md), [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md). **Re-lock:** `python3 lock.py --relock` run on both under this decision; `python3 validate.py` green.

## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious finding resolved before re-lock; it ran (four lenses), returned **no blocking/serious design findings**, and the precision folds (one seam-ownership nit, the non-engineer copy-law sharpenings) were applied in the as-written text before re-lock. The re-lock set held at the [D-185](0185-authorize-a-two-foundation-re-litigation-ship-a-native-plan.md)-authorized **2** — the audit confirmed [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) law 1 already covers the operator-scalar seam and that hooks/build-orchestration are untouched, so no third foundation was drawn in (contrast [D-184](0184-resolve-the-d-183-issue-authoring-grammar-correction-landed.md), where the landed-text audit *did* expand the set). The design is faithful to [D-185](0185-authorize-a-two-foundation-re-litigation-ship-a-native-plan.md): two axes never conflated, the yield deploying-operator-scoped with named travel semantics, §15 untouched (posture, not enforcement), Routine never stalls, no new surface.

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock without the landed-text audit** (rejected — HARD lock gate; skipping it ranks with a silent edit). **Fold the non-engineer copy items as literal operator strings in the locked doc** (rejected — operator copy is a build-spec leaf by design; the locked doc fixes the *law* the copy must satisfy, not the wording). **Edit append-only [D-185](0185-authorize-a-two-foundation-re-litigation-ship-a-native-plan.md) to sharpen its [D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md) citation** (rejected — append-only; the citation was ground-truth-verified accurate, and this entry records that verification). **Add a topology clause / third re-lock for the operator scalar** (rejected — law 1 already owns "engine owns only its keyed entries; the rest stays operator/product-owned"; an added clause is redundant churn on a locked foundation). **Add a risks clause** (rejected — posture with graceful degradation + build-spec re-verification + copy-law/legibility mitigations leave no standing architectural risk; a forced entry is noise). **Attribute the Routine-override mechanism to modes** (rejected — [D-140](0140-lock-routine-mode-the-unattended-routine-entry-the-fourth-mo.md) homes the non-interactive posture in build-orchestration; modes owns only the law that it overrides — the fold corrects this).
