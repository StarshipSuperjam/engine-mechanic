# Litigate the grammar-and-boot cluster (ledger `grammar-core-U09` / `grammar-core-U10` / `lifecycle-U11`): boot's cold-start pack reads the surface catalog's **recognition slice**; agent instance names take the `engine-` prefix; boot **retires** its unproduced §15 weakening-alarm class and reconciles its sole-write claim — re-locks of ontology / boot / agents land under [D-310](0310-resolve-re-lock-ontology-the-coverage-attestation-bounded-to.md) / [D-312](0312-resolve-re-lock-boot-the-recognition-slice-read-with-no-dedu.md) / [D-313](0313-resolve-re-lock-agents-the-engine-naming-rule-the-read-only.md)

*Decided 2026-07-16 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

A design session working the conformance re-litigation ledger raised the **litigation alarm** (CLAUDE.md §103–107) on three items whose owning docs are locked, and the operator authorized each ruling in-session before any locked-doc edit. Declared a **cross-doc cluster** under the ledger's own batching law (*a locked doc re-locks once, resolving all of its items together*): [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md), [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md), and [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) are **edited under this entry and re-locked under [D-310](0310-resolve-re-lock-ontology-the-coverage-attestation-bounded-to.md)/[D-312](0312-resolve-re-lock-boot-the-recognition-slice-read-with-no-dedu.md)/[D-313](0313-resolve-re-lock-agents-the-engine-naming-rule-the-read-only.md)** after the mandatory landed-text cold audit; they are **edited-but-not-relocked** until then (the [D-296](0296-litigate-engine-template-427-residual-three-l1-l2-l3-audits.md)/[D-298](0298-litigate-the-deployment-eadr-collision-the-operator-s-per-in.md)/[D-307](0307-litigate-engine-template-446-incremental-consolidation-the-b.md) authorize→resolve pattern; `validate.py` flags all three fingerprints until the re-locks). Clustering is load-bearing, not convenience: boot carries both `grammar-core-U09`'s pack amendment and `lifecycle-U11`, so resolving them separately would re-lock boot twice.

## Why

each of the three rulings is the **letter-conformant** direction, and none invents grammar. (1) The read was always the law's own claim; only its *unit* (slice, not file) and its *tier* (posture) are made honest. The unit follows from the law's **own substance** — definitions bounded, instances lazy — and from [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md)'s budget: the **governance** fields are the pull-request author's business and are noise a cold session pays context for. It does **not** follow from the platform cap, which does not bind the choice. (2) The prefix is what the identifier law already requires; the build simply never conformed, and the fix touches **no** locked grammar. (3) The retirement removes a promise §15 never asked for and no producer ever met — the cheaper and more honest half of the same defect class the eADR fix closes. Cost: the slice is ~275–485 chars, and the prefix is 11 mechanical files with one live invocation site.

## What we ruled out

**Read the catalog *whole*** (rejected — **on relevance, not arithmetic**. This pass's first reading rejected it as **12,271** chars against a **10,000** cap; **that arithmetic was wrong** and the landed-text audit caught it — the cap binds each output *value*, not the event total, so the catalog fits as its own value and the whole read is **feasible**. It is rejected because the **governance** fields are the pull-request author's and are noise a cold session pays context for; the law's own *definitions-bounded, instances-lazy* substance asks for the recognition fields and no more. The corrected premise is recorded here rather than left to read as though the cap decided it). **Narrow the ontology law to aspirational** (rejected — documents the hole instead of closing it; the read is cheap and buys real posture). **Add the read to *rescue* `eADR-0016`'s "never"** (rejected — a **category error**: a fail-open posture mechanism cannot ground an absolute; "add the read" and "narrow the claim" were never alternatives — the eADR is corrected either way). **Declare the catalog exempt from attention's budget ("consumes no attention *item-slots*")** (rejected — mints an accounting unit [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md)'s locked doc does not have, and [D-062](0062-lock-the-attention-system-wave-2-the-policy-plus-function-pr.md) gives attention the budget within boot's event model; the honest, existing-vocabulary statement is that the catalog is a **step-2 input read before ranking**, never a ranking candidate. **What is rejected is minting the unit, not the placement** — the round-2 audit caught this anti-choice condemning the very wording it prescribes, so the reasoning is stated properly here: [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) partitions *candidates*, and an input is not a candidate, so the slice sits outside that partition **by construction, not by exemption**. That is also the right place for it — attention compresses the *orientation* category on a high-debt start, and compressing the grammar a session recognizes file-kinds by, precisely when the session is most loaded, is backwards. The slice is not therefore unbounded: its bound is the **cap guard**, under which it sheds at the **lowest tier**, first and before any ranked content). **Claim the slice is "bounded by construction" / "read once"** (rejected — both false: surfaces *"attach additively without a re-lock"* two sentences below the law, and the pack **re-renders on every resume** per [D-269](0269-litigate-q18-engine-template-313-resolve-cross-session-anti.md); the bound is the cap guard, not an adjective — and not a dedup, which this entry's corrected reading drops as unimplementable). **Build boot's §15 weakening alarm** (rejected — weakening is a **diff** property and boot is a cold read with no PR/base-head pair; boot would own **detection**, against [§16](../principles.md); a local read cannot inherit the guard's trusted-base non-falsifiability; a committed baseline digest is rewritable by the very PR it judges — the §15 prohibition itself; and a digest can only ever report *"config changed"*, the exact wording §15 forbids). **Affirm agent path-confinement** (rejected — D-020's rationale forecloses it; agent names are knowledge-graph entities and bare command-line tokens; and it would require carving a surface exemption into a universally-quantified grammar law two decisions after its re-lock, against D-020's *"every future surface inherits it instead of re-discovering it"*). **Fold `eADR-0024` into this cluster** (rejected — its owning doc is [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md), a Wave C batch; it lands in the ledger as its own item rather than inflating this cluster).

## Further record

### The amendment (design of record) — (1) `grammar-core-U09`, the catalog read

Three locked/shipped sources assert the catalog is read whole at boot ([ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md)'s consumption law, [D-019](0019-authoring-grammar-locked-end-state-as-laws-not-leaves.md) cut (1), shipped `eADR-0016`); boot's locked pack never listed it, so the build faithfully never read it. **Ruling: boot reads it** — but as the **recognition slice**, not the file. **Every** surface *definition* is read (the law's substance — definitions bounded, instances lazy — holds intact), carrying only the **recognition fields** (name, location); the **authoring fields** (authority, lifecycle, `governing_schema`, template) are the pull-request author's, never the cold session's. The slice **re-renders with the pack** on every `SessionStart`; there is **no dedup**. At ~275–485 chars the repetition does not earn a mechanism, and the obvious mechanism does not exist: the scent's already-surfaced store is keyed on `session_id`, which the platform **re-issues as a fresh UUID on resume** — reported verbatim at [anthropics/claude-code#12235](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/12235), a citation held **honestly weak**: it is closed as a duplicate with no successor cross-reference and describes an eight-month-old version, so it evidences the behaviour without establishing it is current, and the landed-text audit could not reproduce a resumed chain to confirm it cold. **The ruling does not rest on it**: dropping a dedup is correct whether or not `session_id` is stable, because at ~275 chars the repetition never earned a mechanism. The platform fact is why the *obvious* mechanism is also unavailable, not why the dedup goes. **Tier, named honestly ([§7](../principles.md)):** the read is **posture** — hook-dependent and fail-open. The merge **coverage gate** catches a surface *directory* that drifts in or vanishes, **at directory granularity**; an uncatalogued instance **inside an existing bucket** is an authoring judgment at the pull request, **not a mechanical catch** (the gate's own message disclaims that leg verbatim). **The ontology law states the property and names boot as its consumer; boot states the pack step. The rationale lives in ontology and is referenced, never restated** — duplicated rationale across two independently re-lockable docs is a drift generator.

### (2) `grammar-core-U10`, the agent prefix

Agent instance names take the **`engine-` prefix**. **The ontology identifier law is not amended — it already reaches agents:** it is universally quantified over *every* surface instance carrying a **human-facing identifier** ("not merely a file path"), and [D-020](0020-engine-instance-identifiers-are-engine-namespaced-decision-r.md)'s rationale **expressly forecloses** the discharge the agent surface leaned on — *"Path-namespacing under `.engine/` does not cover bare identifiers"* — the exact `.claude/`-is-an-engine-corner move. An agent name is demonstrably **not merely a file path**: the shipped knowledge graph promotes it to an entity id (`agent:audit`), one of the law's three named triggers **verbatim**, and `--agent audit` is a bare token on a live workflow command line. [skills](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md):51–57 reaches the **opposite** conclusion from the **identical** `.claude/`-corner premise on the same platform, **citing D-020 to do it**. Only [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) is edited — it gains the naming rule its `:39` "engine-namespaced" claim already asserts but the build does not satisfy.

### (3) `lifecycle-U11`, boot's alarm class and sole-write claim

**(a) Boot retires its §15 weakening-alarm class.** [§15](../principles.md) places its surfacing at the **merge** — *"hard-gated at the human merge, surfaced in plain language"* — and [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md):76–84 **owns detection and already discharges** the plain-language relay at the ack gate. Boot's `:86` was the **only** promise of a boot-time channel in the corpus and **nothing produced it**: the same unproduced-claim defect as `eADR-0016`. The three sites presupposing the channel (`:220` fingerprint input, `:245` un-actionable residual, `:270` retire-eligibility) reconcile with it. Boot's existing **protection-off** alarm still covers the gate-is-off case. **(b) The "sole local write" claim** reconciles by **attributing each write to the substrate that owns it** — canon's own existing move, already applied to memory (*"the memory consolidation sweep that rides `SessionStart` is memory's operation, not orientation"*): modes' stance **clear** is modes' operation invoked at boot's moment, and the **boot slice** is knowledge's regenerable, gitignored cache. Boot's sole **orientation** write stays the presentation ledger. The read-only **law** (scoped to *derived or committed* state) was never violated — only the *count* was false.

### The platform cap (surfaced by this pass's feasibility lens; a pre-existing latent defect, not introduced here)

Claude Code caps a hook's output **strings** at **10,000 characters** — the cap binds **each value**, not the event total (when several hooks return `additionalContext` for one event, Claude receives all of the values) — and, past it, **silently** writes the payload to a file and substitutes a preview, which would strip boot's present-marker and **manufacture a false "boot did not ground" signal**, the precise inverse of boot's degrade-loud law. Measured on the live build — a **dated observation, not a constant**, since the pack is live-signal-driven: it renders **~7,166** chars quiet (reproduced exactly by two independent measurements), while two alarm-heavy reconstructions measured **~7.8k** and **~9.3k** — so the alarmed figure is a **range, not a constant**, and it has no fixed ceiling at all: the pack embeds unbounded live strings (the finding register), so a long enough register inflates it arbitrarily. That unboundedness, not any particular headroom number, is the point — the bound is the guard, never a measurement. There is **no size guard anywhere** in the build and **no mention of the cap** in [constraints](../reference/constraints.md). The cap is documented in [constraints](../reference/constraints.md) under this entry, and boot's degrade-loud law is stated to cover it: **the pack measures itself and sheds its lowest-priority category with a named notice rather than overrunning silently** (the shed mechanism is a build-spec leaf; the shed notice must reach the **operator** via the must-push relay, not merely enter the pack — see the audit outcome below). **The cap does not decide the slice-vs-file question**: the catalog (4,659 chars) would fit as its own value. The slice is chosen on relevance, not arithmetic.

### The shipped `eADR-0016` is corrected regardless of the read — a build-owe, authored in `engine-template` and nowhere else

This is the category error the pass corrected in itself: adding a **posture-tier** read **cannot** make an **absolute** true; the shipped `eADR-0006` forbids exactly that (*"a hard rule that can never fail is posture wearing a machine's name, which this law forbids"*). **Both** of 0016's legs fail: *"the catalog is read whole at boot"* (false as shipped) **and** *"an uncatalogued surface is not recognized"* (the coverage check disclaims that leg and is directory-granular where the claim implies file-granularity). The eADR canon is **engine-template's**; this workspace holds no eADR record and authors none. What this entry fixes here is the **design-side correlate** the build conforms *to* — ontology's coverage bound and its consumption law's tier — leaving the artifact's text to the build-owe below.

All **33** shipped eADRs were verified against the build: of **12** genuine absolute/mechanism claims, **10 TRUE**, **1 FALSE** (`eADR-0016`), **1 OVERCLAIMED** (`eADR-0024`).
