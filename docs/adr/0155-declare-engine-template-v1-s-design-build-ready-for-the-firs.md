# Declare engine-template v1's design build-ready for the first build PR (the build-ready capstone; evidence-to-maintainer consent)

*Decided 2026-05-31 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

On the maintainer's **informed consent on evidence** ([principle §17](../principles.md)), declare the **engine-template v1 design build-ready**: a cold builder can construct the engine from these docs alone — beginning the first build PR (the stage-0 seed → the M1 self-construction crossover → the topological module build order) — without inventing structure. This is the terminal act of the **build-ready capstone**. **Build-ready is a judgment-tier verdict** ([§7](../principles.md)), not a certification: it states the maintainer's judgment that the assembled evidence is strong enough to begin reversible, human-gated construction; ## Why

The design has converged — mechanically intact, per-system audited, end-to-end buildable, and now cross-doc coherent after the one residual the capstone re-run caught and D-154 fixed. The capstone's purpose was to convert "sound and buildable" into evidence the **non-engineer maintainer can weigh and consent to** — the §17 trust gate applied to the construction-release decision. Declaring build-ready as a **judgment-tier verdict on a tiered bundle**, rather than an AI-issued certification, is the honest posture: the mechanical legs (validate, the dry-run record, the lock count) the maintainer verifies directly; the AI legs (the cold audits, the re-run) earn their weight from independence and adversarial pressure, and their bound is named, not dressed as assurance the gate-holder can check. The verdict releases only reversible, human-gated construction, so the confidence it asserts is proportionate to what a missed design-time nit can actually cost. It is fitting that the final gate before the line was the one that caught the last defect: the capstone re-run is the reason a live `attention`↔`boot` contradiction does not enter construction.

## What we ruled out

**Self-issue the declaration with no operator-consent step** (rejected — §17: the build-ready verdict releases an unsupervised AI builder and must rest on the maintainer's informed consent, never an AI-issued stamp; this was the plan-audit's load-bearing correction). **Declare on D-151's clean evidence without a fresh re-run** (rejected — the corpus changed after D-151 via the `state` + six D-153 re-locks, and run 2 proved the point by catching S4, a live contradiction D-151's clean result did not cover). **Frame "build-ready" as a certification or an unqualified property** (rejected — every evidentiary leg is mechanical or confidence-raising-not-certifying; the honest verb is a judgment-tier verdict, §7). **Treat build-ready as release-ready** (rejected — efficacy is the acceptance-benchmark's pre-release gate on the assembled engine, out of reach pre-build). **Lock the corpus as a whole or mint a new declaration mechanism** (rejected — the 41 per-doc fingerprints already freeze the design; a corpus-level lock is redundant mechanism, and the declaration is a decision-log entry, the established [D-151](0151-whole-corpus-design-audit-re-litigate-state-af-1-to-fix-the.md)/[D-152](0152-resolve-q15-author-the-pre-release-acceptance-benchmark-the.md) shape). **Index the declaration in [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) or pointer it into wbs/README.md** (rejected — the D-152 layer-discipline precedent keeps workspace-process artifacts out of the Engine master, and the build-ready state is derivable, so a wbs/README pointer is the derivable-state restatement the status-pointer discipline deletes).
