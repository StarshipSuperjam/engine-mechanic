# Procedural / content / grounding surface cluster designed; the boundary law and the derived-binding principle

*Decided 2026-05-24 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Design the remaining surface cluster as **laws not leaves**, anchored by a **procedural-surface boundary law** and a new [principle §14](../principles.md) **derived binding by presence**. **(1) Principle §14** (precisely scoped): where the engine discovers *which* providers/implementations/members are present, it derives that set by **presence + self-declaration**, never a central list an install mutates — the **discovery axis**, *distinct* from the closed **wiring seam** (`hook`, `mcp`, `ontology-entry`, `permission`, `gitignore`), which stays the mechanism for keyed reversible shared-state edits. **(2) The boundary law:** deterministic code → [`tool`](../spec/systems/surfaces/tools.md); a swappable-impl protocol contract → [`interface`](../spec/systems/surfaces/interfaces.md); operator-facing explanation → [`docs`](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md); a procedure performed by reading-and-following → routed by trigger × context to [`agent`](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) (spawned at a gate, isolated), [`skill`](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md) (auto-invoked, in-session), `command` (operator-typed verb, thin), or [`operation`](../spec/systems/surfaces/operations.md) (the shared body, ≥2 referencers or a human-in-loop runbook); "what a system *is*" is **not a catalogued surface**. Three governing laws: **one procedure, one home**; **derived binding by presence**; the operations **anti-sprawl heuristic** ("is this only one skill's private depth?" — a judgment bar, never a hard gate). **(3) Six surfaces move `stub → designed`** (operations, skills, commands, docs, interfaces, tools), each with its eight-field meta-contract record; their *definitions* are `provided` by the `core` package so coherence's one-owner mapping holds; surfaces attach to the [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md) catalog **additively, no re-lock** (the locked doc's own "catalogued when designed" clause). **(4) `specification` is not a catalogued surface** — the engine self-describes via the derived self-map + knowledge graph (honors [§3](../principles.md)); "specification" survives only as a **doc-nature** (the word the locked ontology uses for *itself*), so the locked ontology text stands and only the [contracts](../spec/systems/surfaces/contracts.md) "different surfaces" phrasing needs a follow-on reconciliation. **(5) Operator docs floor** (the one laws-not-leaves exception): v1 ships at least one **operator orientation doc** as a named deliverable — what the engine is, how to direct it, how to discover commands, and that default-on self-audit proposing retirements is normal hygiene. **(6) Interfaces** name their own **default/fallback implementation** (degradability [§5](../principles.md) auditable at the contract) and **surface an active fallback to the operator at runtime** (reuse the locked module-system/[D-028](0028-module-system-locked-end-state-as-laws-not-leaves.md) inactive-substrate disclosure law); v1 interfaces are `search` and the knowledge representation/retrieval leaf; **`check-kind` is deliberately excluded** here (it is "registered through wiring" in locked validation; calling it by-presence now would contradict locked docs — it joins the interface set only in the validation re-lock). **(7) `tool` is the `code` surface** with **no template and no governing schema** (governed by tests/checks). **(8) [audit-library](../spec/modules/audit-library.md) module → `designed`, `status: default-on`** (a removable module, not a foundation — [§12](../principles.md)); `provides` the audit persona + seed concern-list + `audit-prep` cron workflow, `wires` **nothing** (all file-drops, active by presence); `depends` on telemetry/validation/agents; locks later behind a cold audit. **(9) Root `CLAUDE.md`** (resolves [Q6](../reference/open-questions.md)) is designed into [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) as the **hook-independent grounding floor** below the boot pack — minimal, carrying the [D-030](0030-memory-ledger-canonical-observe-don-t-predict-capture-lexica.md) memory-authority routing and the wall reminder; **path-scoped nested `CLAUDE.md` is out of v1** (it loads lazily/unreliably). Closes [Q5](../reference/open-questions.md) and [Q6](../reference/open-questions.md); narrows [Q7](../reference/open-questions.md) to v1 skill *membership*. **Re-examined and confirmed no re-litigation needed:** the locked wiring model ([D-012](0012-provisioning-is-two-subsystems-on-one-manifest-grammar-modul.md)/[D-022](0022-hooks-locked-as-foundation-11-as-laws-not-leaves.md) `hook`/`mcp` seams), suite derivation ([D-023](0023-check-system-locked-validator-architecture-the-check-surface.md)), and asymmetry ([D-026](0026-the-engine-is-an-embedded-team-member-contributor-not-compon.md)) already embody the discovery/wiring split §14 names. **No locks or re-locks land in this pass** — the two locked-doc reconciliations below are committed as dedicated follow-on cold-audited passes.

## Why

This cluster is the layer atop the locked grammar that says where a procedure lives, how a swappable implementation binds, and what a human reads; it could not be designed piecemeal because the procedural surfaces are mutually defining (a clean `operation` boundary requires fixing `skill`/`command`/`agent` at once) and an unprincipled split is exactly how the prototype's surface zoo regrew ([R6](../reference/risks.md)). The boundary law routes by *invocation trigger × execution context*, the only axes that actually differ across the procedural surfaces; "one procedure, one home" plus the operations ≥2-referencer heuristic are the anti-duplication controls. §14 names a pattern already instanced three times (roster, suite membership, and now interface impls + workflow activation), so it earns elevation to a principle — but scoped to *discovery*, because the locked wiring seam is a genuinely different mechanism and a blanket "everything binds by presence" would falsify the locked `hook`/`mcp` model. The cold-session four-lens audit (adversarial / technical-feasibility / non-engineer-operator / architect) ran against the plan; its findings were resolved before this entry: the **adversarial blocking** finding (designing `interfaces` to call check-kind "by presence" while merely *flagging* the validation reconciliation was a cosmetic dodge that still contradicts locked validation) was fixed by **excluding check-kind from v1 interfaces entirely**; the §14 over-reach and the `specification`-strands-the-ontology findings were fixed by the precise §14 scoping and the doc-nature clarification; the operator **blocking** finding (laws-not-leaves would let the `docs` slot ship empty and strand a non-engineer) was fixed by the **named orientation-doc floor** and runtime fallback surfacing; the architect **blocking** findings (`wbs/` and the ontology-catalog companion edits omitted; which package `provides` the records unspecified) were folded into propagation. Technical-feasibility verified every platform fact (skills/commands/agents paths and the always-resident skill-description cost; GitHub workflow-by-presence and the public-repo 60-day scheduled-workflow auto-disable; `CLAUDE.md` hook-independent auto-loading; nested-`CLAUDE.md` lazy/unreliable loading, which *validates* the v1 exclusion; the code-surface no-schema/no-template stance).

## What we ruled out

Treat the seven items as co-equal independent surfaces to design in isolation (rejected — they are mutually defining; the deliverable is the boundary law, not seven designs). Fold `operations` into skills and retire it (rejected — three real procedures need cross-invoker reuse or are human-in-loop runbooks; it clears the ≥2-referencer bar). Drop `interfaces` into module-system/schemas (rejected — a protocol contract differs from a data schema and from the closed wiring seam; `search` + knowledge-retrieval are real). List `check-kind` as a v1 interface now (rejected — contradicts locked validation/module-system; defer to the validation re-lock). Catalogue `specification` as a surface (rejected — hand-authored design docs drift, [§3](../principles.md); the engine self-describes via derived output). Make §14 a blanket "no central registries" law (rejected — falsifies the locked `hook`/`mcp` wiring seams; scope it to discovery). Defer the operator orientation doc as laws-not-leaves membership (rejected — strands a non-engineer; the docs floor is the deliberate exception). Make audit-library `required` (rejected — [§12](../principles.md) keeps foundations minimal; audits is not one of the 11) or `optional` (rejected — a non-engineer will not enable self-audit they do not know they need; default-on). Include path-scoped nested `CLAUDE.md` in v1 (rejected — lazy/unreliable loading; policies/checks are the dependable substrate). Bundle the two locked-doc reconciliations into this pass (rejected — re-locks are irreversible and get dedicated cold-audited passes, the [D-034](0034-re-litigation-hooks-block-budget-example-retargeted-off-the.md)/[D-035](0035-re-litigation-policies-retargets-the-contract-threshold-narr.md)/[D-036](0036-re-litigation-contracts-retargets-the-default-session-narrat.md) precedent).
