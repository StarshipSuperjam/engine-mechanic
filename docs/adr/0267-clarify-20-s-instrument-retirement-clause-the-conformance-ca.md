# Clarify [§20](../principles.md)'s instrument-retirement clause: the conformance *capability* ships deployed (build-orchestration); only the maintainer build-apparatus retires

*No date was recorded for this decision in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Scrutinizing the just-landed [§20](../principles.md) ([D-266](0266-new-principle-20-spec-conformance-is-the-standing-target-no.md)), the operator caught that its clause "enforced by the construction-time dry-run and build-conformance instruments (both retire at v1, neither ships in a generated repo)" read as **self-undermining** — it leaned on the very "not needed once deployed" logic §20 forbids, and a cold build session could misread §20 as making spec-conformance a *construction-only* concern (the precise irony §20 must not invite). **Grounding confirmed the principle sound but the wording misleading:** the dry-run and build-conformance instruments are **maintainer build-apparatus** — the L1/L2 class of `validate.py`, `lock.py`, the cold-session audit, and this whole workspace, none of which ships — **not engine machinery**. The conformance *capability* (cold review of AI-built code against a spec, surfaced in plain language for a non-engineer) **ships and stays whole in every deployed repo** as [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)'s spec-conformance review ([core](../spec/modules/core.md), [D-254](0254-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-filler-renders-the-o.md)), aimed at the product's spec ([product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md), [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)); the engine self-build cannot reuse those lenses against engine-planning without breaching the [§13](../principles.md) wall (build-conformance §7), which is exactly why the separate maintainer instrument exists and retires. **The §20 clause is refined** to say so: enforced *while the engine is built*, the instruments retire as build-apparatus (not because conformance stops mattering), and the capability persists deployed via build-orchestration. The **discriminating test** is now implicit in the wording: an absence violates §20 only if it leaves the *deployed engine less capable at its job* — a retired build harness does not; a half-built boot would. **Honest residual named (unchanged, still open):** whether a deployed engine runs a *standing* self-check of its OWN shipped machinery against its OWN shipped design rationale (the eADR canon) is [Q31](../reference/open-questions.md) — a logged scope-out §20's membership test treats as conformance, not under-build. A same-session sharpen of D-266's §20 (no lock; no propagation beyond the build-orchestration cite, which already ships and is unchanged); `validate.py` green.

## Why

the operator's misread is the proof the clause needed fixing: §20's whole purpose is to forbid "not needed until deployed," so a sentence that *sounds* like it exempts conformance from the deployed engine undercuts the principle at the point a builder reads it. The honest structure is two-layer — §20 governs building the engine *whole* (construction-time, enforced by retiring apparatus), and §4/§5 keep it whole once shipped (runtime, shipped properties) — and the product is safe not because conformance is *absent* deployed but because the deployed conformance review is aimed at the operator's *evolving* spec, never a frozen one. Stating that in §20 forecloses the misread for every future build session.

## What we ruled out

**Leave §20 as authored and correct only the conversational explanation** (rejected — the misread is in the doc's own words; a build session reading §20 cold would draw the same wrong inference, and §20 is the one principle that cannot afford to read as construction-only). **Drop the instrument-retirement clause entirely** (rejected — the fact is true and load-bearing for the product-scope guard; the fix is to explain *why* retirement is consistent with §20, not to hide it). **Open a new principle or a Q for the deployed self-conformance gap** (rejected — it is already [Q31](../reference/open-questions.md), correctly scoped out of v1; §20's membership clause already covers it).
