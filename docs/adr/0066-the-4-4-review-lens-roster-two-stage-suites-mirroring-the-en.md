# The 4+4 review-lens roster: two stage-suites mirroring the engine's own cold-session audit

*Decided 2026-05-26 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Resolve the lens-membership part of [Q1](../reference/open-questions.md) by designing the v1 review-lens roster as **two agent-suite modules** — [design-review](../spec/modules/design-review.md) (four `role: plan-review` lens personas) and [qa-review](../spec/modules/qa-review.md) (four `role: pre-submission-review` lens personas) — and reconciling [build orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)'s placeholder lens lists ("design"/"QA" umbrellas) to the real roster. **Design stage** (plan-review): `product-intent` (right problem, owns acceptance criteria + the criteria-quality verdict), `architecture` (structural soundness), `feasibility` (build/ship/operate), `risk-governance` (security-by-design, abuse, compliance, resilience). **QA stage** (pre-submission): `spec-conformance` (built-vs-spec, restates verified-versus-unverified criteria), `usability` (utility, friction, accessibility), `technical-integrity` (code/architecture/performance/reliability health), `security-governance` (safe to release). The eight are **additive `lens` values across the two existing `role`s** on the locked [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) surface — **no grammar change, no re-lock**: the lock fixes laws not membership, the lens axis is open and additive, and *which gate consumes which lens* is build orchestration's concern, exactly the open decision the locked doc hands to Q1. The roster is grounded as the **product-facing analogue of the Engine's own cold-session design audit** ([D-018](0018-cold-session-design-audit-required-before-any-lock.md)). Two of the eight consume the [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md) referent (`product-intent`, `spec-conformance`); the rest are referent-informed. The QA lenses are the **judgment** layer above the locked [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) suite + CI (the **mechanical** layer) — they complement, never duplicate ([§7](../principles.md)). A QA lens may **dry-run** the code in an ephemeral, discarded worktree: "read-only reviewer" means *no mutation of authoritative state*, not *no execution*, so this needs **no edit to the locked agents surface**. The `retroactive` lens stays a separate optional lens (it reviews merged work). All three modules land `designed`.

## Why

A single "design" persona and a single "QA" persona cannot carry the breadth of design and quality review; the agents surface always intended a multi-lens roster ("which suites ship is Q1"). The 4+4 taxonomy was provided by the operator and aligns strikingly with the Engine's own four lock-time audit lenses, so the roster stands in a traveled lane rather than as a fresh invention. The **falsifiable separation test** proves the referent-producer↔lens-roster split is real, not leaked: D-066 is landable *even if [D-065](0065-product-design-front-door-design-the-q14-intake-module-as-a.md) were dropped entirely* — the lenses review all build work and do not depend on product-design existing. The "medium-change default" introduces no fourth concept: it is the plan-gate risk assessment's proposal over the derived lens set (the existing installed-modules + memory + attention inputs), and the eight findings resolve to one synthesized recommended call + trade at the plan gate, not eight raw verdicts dumped on a non-engineer. The four-lens cold-session audit confirmed: the no-re-lock claim rests on the agents design-commitment (open lens axis), not on "examples are illustrations"; an unconsumed lens is a coherence finding, so build orchestration records consumption of all eight; a spec-less change makes the two referent-gated lenses a disclosed no-op, never a silent green; and 4 cold-context reviewers per gate sits inside the practical concurrent sweet spot, with the cost estimate honest about per-gate batching and tier sensitivity.

## What we ruled out

Bundle the lenses into [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md) (rejected — gates general build review behind a product-layer intake module; the lenses review all work, so they are a separate concern, provable by the falsifiable test above). Keep a single "design" persona and a single "QA" persona (rejected — the placeholder umbrellas cannot carry the real breadth; this was the original under-specification). One undifferentiated review-lens module (rejected — the two stage-suites mirror the design-versus-QA distinction and let an operator install by stage). Encode the medium-change default as a new committed list (rejected — a fourth concept the locked grammar lacks; it is the risk-assessment's proposal over the derived set). Read "read-only reviewer" as forbidding execution (rejected — it forbids mutation of authoritative state, not a sandboxed dry-run in a discarded worktree; reading it strictly would be a re-litigation trigger to raise explicitly, not assume). Fold `retroactive` into the eight (rejected — it reviews already-merged work, orthogonal to reviewing a proposed change).
