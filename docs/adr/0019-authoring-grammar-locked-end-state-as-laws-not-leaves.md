# Authoring grammar locked end-state, as laws not leaves

*Decided 2026-05-22 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Design and lock the **authoring grammar** — the shape of everything authored in the engine — as a cohesive system, expressed as laws rather than catalog membership. The lockset is [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md), [contracts](../spec/systems/surfaces/contracts.md), [policies](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md), [schemas](../spec/systems/surfaces/schemas.md), and [templates](../spec/systems/guardrails/templates.md). Nine cuts: **(1)** the surface **catalog** is a single schema-governed data file whose governance fields are authored and whose coverage is derived and gated; surface *definitions* are read whole at boot, *instances* are slug-named files indexed by directory listing. **(2)** The locked **self-referential core** is exactly `{contract, policy, schema}`; every other surface (including `tool`) grows additively, and authority rank is a per-surface catalog field. **(3)** Authority, enforcement, and escalation are **three orthogonal axes** — authority is four tiers (decisions ▷ standing rules ▷ mechanics/guidance ▷ derived) with the comparison rule "higher wins; same-surface supersession; same-tier or ambiguous → escalate," enforcement is the three tiers of [principles §7], and the collision rule is "mechanical enforcement never adjudicates authority → escalate." **(4)** **Two lifecycle vocabularies** (`decision`, `artifact`) assigned by catalog field, not per-surface state machines. **(5)** The **schema layer** is JSON Schema 2020-12, catalog-routed, reaching structured files whole and prose frontmatter only, evolving additively-free with breaking changes gated by a version bump plus a migration. **(6)** A **template** is scaffold-plus-shape-spec in one, referenced by the catalog; section structure is the control and length is a `soft-warn` budget, never a hard cap. **(7)** `contract` is the decision-record (ADR) surface, distinct from specifications; file-per-decision, append-only, no `rejected` state (rejection is an anti-choice within the prevailing contract). **(8)** `policy` is the tier-2 standing-rule surface; the contract-threshold policy controls ADR over-production via posture + a structural presence check + telemetry. **(9)** The **escalation/finding-disposition policy** is the runtime third axis: every finding takes a durable disposition (fix in line / log an issue / escalate), enforced by a `Stop`-hook close-ritual disposition summary. Scope is bounded two ways: **laws not leaves** (the v1 surface set, Q5, stays open; surfaces attach additively) and **authoring grammar only** ([module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md) is excluded and stays `designed` pending the module set, Q1). Resolves Q8 and the D9 lifecycle scope.

## Why

The authoring grammar is the spine every surface stub hangs from; it cannot be designed piecemeal because its parts are mutually defining (a surface record is the join of authority, lifecycle, schema, template, and enforcement). Locking laws rather than catalog membership follows the topology precedent ([D-016](0016-repository-topology-as-a-foundational-substrate-product-owns.md)): it ratifies the durable rules now without front-running Q1/Q5/Q7, and lets each surface be ratified additively when designed. Naming authority, enforcement, and escalation as orthogonal axes removes the grammar's worst latent contradiction (a hard gate silently overriding a higher-authority decision). The contract-threshold and finding-disposition policies answer two demonstrated operator pains — runaway ADR production and findings that die in chat — with honest tiering (presence is mechanically checkable; significance and genuineness stay posture, backstopped by telemetry and the close gate). The cold-session design audit (D-018) ran with four lenses (adversarial, technical-feasibility, non-engineer-operator, architect); its one blocking finding (the changelog sink was undesigned) and serious findings (tier reservation stated as law, contract-template field reconciliation, the `Stop`-hook block softened to its true strength with human review as backstop, logged findings re-surfaced to the operator at boot, the stale glossary `Surface` definition) were resolved before the lock.

## What we ruled out

Freeze the full v1 surface catalog now (rejected — front-runs Q1/Q5/Q7 and makes the cold-session audit unwieldy; the laws do not depend on membership). Include module-system in the lock (rejected — it is entangled with the unsettled module set, Q1). One unified lifecycle (rejected — it conflates a proposed decision with an unmerged artifact and loses the `deprecated` state) and per-surface lifecycles (rejected — R6 over-build). A fine per-surface authority total order (rejected — asserts precedence relationships we lack grounds for; four tiers with escalation on ties is more honest). Hard line caps on prose and a hard cap on ADRs per session (rejected — blunt instruments that fight the living-document goal and punish legitimately large work; soft-warn budgets and a significance bar fit principle §6). A `rejected` ADR state (rejected — anti-choice within the prevailing contract is the proven model; a graveyard of rejected files is needless). Leave finding-disposition as posture (rejected — that is precisely the unenforced posture that already fails; the close-ritual gate makes it durable).
