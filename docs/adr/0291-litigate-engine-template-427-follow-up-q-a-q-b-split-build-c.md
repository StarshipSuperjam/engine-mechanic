# Litigate engine-template #427 follow-up (Q-A/Q-B): split build-conformance §8's conformance-reviewer/adversarial-divergence-hunter **pair** into two distinct [qa-review](../spec/modules/qa-review.md) lenses — `spec-conformance` (systematic) + a new fifth `divergence-hunter` (adversarial) — which also owns a **narrow, diff-introduced over-build** catch (whole-repo dead-code stays [technical-integrity](../spec/modules/qa-review.md)'s); re-locks of qa-review / build-orchestration land under D-292 / D-293

*Decided 2026-07-11 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

While building the first slice (A1) of the [conformance-enforcement floor](../reference/glossary.md) [D-287](0287-litigate-engine-template-427-make-the-sdd-spec-drive-the-bui.md) ruled, a build session's plan gate surfaced two coupled follow-up questions the D-287 ruling does not settle, and routed them back via [engine-template #427](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/427) (correctly not self-served from the build). **Q-A:** is the adversarial **divergence-hunter** a *distinct fifth QA lens* or a *posture folded into `spec-conformance`* (as [D-290](0290-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-lens-carries.md) shipped it)? **Q-B:** who owns **over-build / dead-code** detection (a built surface tracing to no obligation)? This entry rules both; it is the **litigation alarm + operator authorization** (direction confirmed in-session over two `AskUserQuestion` rounds *before* editing, then the mandatory cold audit) **and the design of record**. The re-locks of the two locked carriers land under **[D-292](0292-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-8-pair-split-across-two-lenses.md)/[D-293](0293-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-roster-divergence-hunter.md)** after their mandatory landed-text cold audit; the carriers are **edited-but-not-relocked** until then (the [D-285](0285-litigate-engine-template-363-the-layer-2-erasure-grammar-was.md)/[D-286](0286-resolve-re-lock-memory-the-layer-2-erasure-grammar-is-one-or.md) authorize→resolve pattern; `validate.py` flags their fingerprints until the relocks).

  **The trigger — a real inconsistency between the instrument and the shipped floor.** The maintainer-construction instrument the shipped floor derives from (build-conformance §8) runs the **conformance-reviewer** and the **adversarial-divergence-hunter** as an **independent pair** — two cold contexts — and the engine-template construction `CLAUDE.md` deliverable gate names two; but [D-290](0290-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-lens-carries.md) folded them into one *posture* carried by `spec-conformance` ("no new grammar"), making the shipped floor **less independent than the instrument it derives from** and leaving over-build homeless.

  **The ruling — (Q-A) split the §8 pair faithfully across two distinct `role: pre-submission-review` lenses.** `spec-conformance` becomes the **systematic conformance reviewer** (derive each obligation from the `docs/spec/` span, mark it met/diverged/untested, resolve no doubt charitably, restate verified-vs-unverified) — it **sheds the adversarial-divergence-hunter posture wording** but stays structured and non-charitable. A **new fifth lens `divergence-hunter`** carries the §8 adversarial-divergence-hunter brief (assume a divergence exists and hunt it, default-to-divergent). The two are **coupled** — the hunter runs whenever `spec-conformance` runs against a `locked` row, two decorrelated cold contexts — so this is **not** the "charitable single-lens posture" D-290's anti-choice rejected; it is *more* adversarial rigor. Both **re-derive from the span, never the matrix rows** ([R16](../reference/risks.md)), judge **only `locked` rows**, and are the disclosed no-op absent a `locked` spec. [D-066](0066-the-4-4-review-lens-roster-two-stage-suites-mirroring-the-en.md)'s open/additive lens axis ("the lock fixes laws, not membership") makes the fifth lens a roster-membership change, not a grammar change — a file-drop on the [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) surface (no re-lock there), consumed by build-orchestration so it does not dangle. **This supersedes D-066's "4+4 = eight lenses" count → 4+5 = nine** (design-review stays a quartet; qa-review becomes a quintet).

  **The ruling — (Q-B) narrow + split.** The `divergence-hunter` owns only the **narrow, deliverable over-build**: a surface *this PR's diff introduces* that no obligation asks for, surfaced as an orchestrator-**ground-truthed suspicion** (build-conformance §4) phrased to the operator as a question — **never** a whole-repo dead-code verdict, **never** "any code not tracing to a `locked` row." **Whole-repo dead-code / orphan / reachability health goes to the existing `technical-integrity` lens** (its referent-free code-health charter). Explicit boundary, no double-count: `divergence-hunter` owns *spec-divergent* over-build (a conformance question needing the obligation set); `technical-integrity` owns *health* over-build (maintainability). The independence earned by the split targets the **semantic-misbuild class specifically** — the irreducible AI-on-AI residual ([R16](../reference/risks.md) mode a) that the floor's two *non-AI* legs (the mechanical [spec-obligation matrix](../reference/glossary.md), the demonstration harness) structurally cannot catch — which is the dispositive reason a second decorrelated cold context earns its own roster member here.

  **Cold audit (plan-stage, four independent no-shared-context lenses — adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect): it REJECTED the first-drafted scope of Q-B and reshaped it.** The session's first design charter'd the fifth lens with a **backward, whole-repo over-build sweep** ("a built surface tracing to no `locked` obligation"). **Convergent BLOCKING/SERIOUS across all four lenses killed that scope:** **(1, all four)** on a partially-`locked` product spec ([§20](../principles.md)) *most legitimate code* traces to no `locked` obligation (draft-scope, plumbing, framework glue, refactors, tests, the `engine==product` construction differences), so the sweep is a **false-positive storm** aimed at a non-engineer who cannot read code to refute it — colliding head-on with §20's "never blocks an MVP the operator scopes"; **(2, feasibility+adversarial)** over-build has **no mechanical denominator** (nothing enumerates every built surface) and "orphan/never-called path" is a **whole-repo reachability property** a per-PR cold lens reading only the diff cannot deliver, with no mechanical §7 tier beneath it; **(3, feasibility+adversarial)** the whole-repo sweep would need the **matrix as referent**, breaking the [D-287](0287-litigate-engine-template-427-make-the-sdd-spec-drive-the-bui.md)/[D-288](0288-resolve-re-lock-product-design-installs-the-conformance-enfo.md) "re-derive from the span, never the matrix rows" tier locked days earlier. **The operator selected the audit's recommended alternative — narrow (diff-introduced, spec-relative, a ground-truthed suspicion) + split (whole-repo health → `technical-integrity`)** — which none of the three findings touch. Further folds: **(adversarial)** the split must not silently reopen D-290's charitable-lens weakness → the hunter is **coupled** to run with `spec-conformance`, and `spec-conformance` is the *systematic* (not charitable) reviewer; **(operator)** "over-build" is new maintainer vocabulary → **added to build-orchestration's [§12](../principles.md) leak-guard list**, rendered to the operator as plain consequence; **(architect)** the promote-vs-add framing is resolved as a **faithful split of the §8 pair** (D-291 supersedes D-290's fold, explicitly not the rejected charitable single lens); **(adversarial)** the "instrument runs a pair so the floor must too" argument is non-dispositive on its own → the rationale rests on the **semantic-class independence the non-AI legs cannot cover**, plus the operator's explicit call. The **landed text** gets its own cold audit under [D-292](0292-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-8-pair-split-across-two-lenses.md)/[D-293](0293-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-roster-divergence-hunter.md) before the irreversible re-locks.

## Why

the shipped floor should carry the same independence as the instrument it derives from, and the semantic-misbuild class — the one residual the mechanical matrix and the demonstration harness cannot catch — is exactly where a second, decorrelated adversarial cold context earns its keep; folding it into one posture ([D-290](0290-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-lens-carries.md)) was the minimal move that traded that independence away and left the governance sources contradicting each other. The honest fix restores the §8 pair as two coupled lenses without weakening `spec-conformance` (it was always the *systematic*, no-charity reviewer, never charitable). Over-build is a genuinely new failure axis absent from R16/§20, and the plan-stage audit proved the whole-repo backward sweep undeliverable and §20-hostile; the narrow diff-introduced suspicion + the split to `technical-integrity` keeps a real, buildable over-build catch (AI-generated code the operator cannot see, serving no spec purpose) without the false-positive storm. The authorize→resolve-after-landed-text-audit split keeps the two irreversible re-locks behind their own clean gate ([D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)/[D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)).
