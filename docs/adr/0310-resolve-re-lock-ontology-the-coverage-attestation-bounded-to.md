# Resolve: re-lock `ontology` (the coverage attestation bounded to what the gate re-attests; the consumption law gains its actor, its recognition/governance field split, and an honest posture tier) — a carrier of [D-309](0309-litigate-the-grammar-and-boot-cluster-ledger-grammar-core-u0.md)

*Decided 2026-07-16 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Re-lock [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md), a carrier of [D-309](0309-litigate-the-grammar-and-boot-cluster-ledger-grammar-core-u0.md). The landed text: the catalog's *coverage* attestation is bounded to **directory granularity** and explicitly disclaims the leg its own shipped check disclaims (*no uncatalogued surface-shaped instance is in use* — a general rule cannot tell an uncatalogued surface from a legitimate non-surface bucket), joining the governance fields as an **authoring judgment**; and the **consumption law** names [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) as the definitions' consumer, splits **recognition** fields (read at cold start) from **governance** fields (the pull-request author's), attributes the cold-start bound to the *instances'* laziness rather than to the catalog read, and states the tier honestly — recognition is **posture** (the pack is fail-open), an invented kind cannot **land** (the coverage gate stops a drifted directory at the merge), and an uncatalogued instance inside an existing bucket is caught by neither. **The round-2 audit added one correction:** the coverage sentence had said "every catalogued surface has its `.engine/<surface>/` home" — a **false absolute**, since the platform dictates [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md)' and [skills](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md)' slots under `.claude/`, and the shipped gate compares against each record's own `location` field rather than assuming a path. The claim is now bound to `location`, which is the field that carries that truth. Gated on the two-round landed-text cold audit recorded in [D-309](0309-litigate-the-grammar-and-boot-cluster-ledger-grammar-core-u0.md), whose blocking and serious findings were resolved or rejected-with-rationale before this lock; `python3 lock.py --relock systems/grammar/ontology/README.md --decision D-310` then ran, and `validate.py` reports this fingerprint clean.

## Why

ontology owns the catalog's consumption property, so the law states it and names its consumer — the shape its self-map paragraph already uses, and what makes the law falsifiable instead of a floating passive assertion. The coverage bound is not new policy: it reconciles the doc to what the shipped gate has always said it does, closing an over-claim of the same class as the one this cluster came to fix.

## What we ruled out

**Restate boot's rationale here** (rejected — duplicated rationale across two independently re-lockable docs is a drift generator; the law lives here and boot references it). **Fix the consumption law and leave the `:36–38` coverage over-claim standing** (rejected — same defect class, same doc, in the one pass that was already opening and re-locking it). **Amend the identifier law for `grammar-core-U10`** (rejected — the law already reaches agents; only the agent surface was non-conformant, so ontology carries no U10 edit).
