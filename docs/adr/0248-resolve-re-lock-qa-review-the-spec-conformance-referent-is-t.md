# Resolve: re-lock `qa-review` (the `spec-conformance` referent is the committed `locked` spec) — fourth carrier of D-244

*Decided 2026-06-23 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Re-lock [qa-review](../spec/modules/qa-review.md), a bounded carrier of [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md). Three edits to the `spec-conformance` lens: **(1)** its referent is named **the committed `locked` spec** ([product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md)'s `docs/spec/` corpus), judged built-vs-spec — not an Issue-carried/thin spec; **(2)** it is the **judgment** half of the spec floor, **complementing the mechanical coverage check** (every `locked` criterion *traced* to committed work, product-design-provided) — "a persona judges conformance, a check gates traceability, never duplicating," so mechanical **traceability moves out of the lens's judged dimensions into the coverage check**; **(3)** the disclosed-no-op trigger is now **"no `locked` spec to check against"** (none exists, or only a `draft`), matching build-orchestration's predicate. **The proportionate gate** for a bounded carrier — a **focused two-lens cold re-check** (faithfulness/propagation + architect/seam, no shared context): **no blocking, no serious, no nit owed in qa-review.** Both lenses confirmed faithfulness to [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)/[D-247](0247-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-committed-spec-referent.md); that traceability is **relocated (covered), not dropped** (mechanical = every criterion traced to committed work; judgment = whether the built work conforms); the no-op consistent with [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md); anti-changelog-clean prose; and [D-066](0066-the-4-4-review-lens-roster-two-stage-suites-mirroring-the-en.md)'s 4+4 roster + "each gate consumes all four" + the referent-producer↔lens-roster separation + qa-review's **`depends: core`-only** (consuming product-design's referent and naming its coverage check adds **no** module edge — "the resolution holds whether or not product-design is installed") all intact. **Sharpens the [D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md) carry-forward** (architect NIT): product-design's `provides` must add the **mechanical coverage check** with the exact shape both consumers now name — migration-discipline-shaped (read-only, `depends: core`, presence-derived suite, self-declaring), self-removing on engine removal, tracing every `locked` acceptance-criterion to committed work. `python3 lock.py --relock modules/qa-review/README.md --decision D-248`; `python3 validate.py` green.

## Why

qa-review is referent-agnostic to *how* the spec resolves (it consumes "the referent"), so the change is bounded — name the referent as the committed `locked` spec and split mechanical traceability from semantic conformance per the locked "a persona judges, a check gates — never duplicate." The focused two-lens re-check is the proportionate gate ([D-141](0141-lock-product-design-the-design-front-door-the-fifth-module-l.md)/[D-242](0242-resolve-the-d-241-audit-memory-read-enablement-the-landed-fo.md)). The coverage-check attribution is an honest in-pass forward reference to product-design (D-250), the last carrier.

## What we ruled out

**Keep "traceability" as a judged dimension of `spec-conformance`** (rejected — it duplicates the mechanical coverage check the locked validation forbids; traceability is decidable and belongs to the check, conformance is the semantic judgment). **Add a `depends` edge to product-design** (rejected — consuming a referent is not a dependency; the no-op-when-no-spec keeps qa-review installable alone, the [D-066](0066-the-4-4-review-lens-roster-two-stage-suites-mirroring-the-en.md) separation). **Run the full four-lens audit** (rejected — a bounded carrier; the focused re-check is the proportionate gate). **Define the coverage check's mechanism in qa-review** (rejected — it is product-design's to provide; qa-review references it, [D-250](0250-resolve-re-lock-product-design-the-full-sdd-module-redesign.md) defines it).
