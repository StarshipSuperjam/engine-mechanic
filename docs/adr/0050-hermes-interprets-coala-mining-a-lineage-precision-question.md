# hermes-interprets-coala mining: a lineage-precision question; CoALA-as-narration rejected

*Decided 2026-05-25 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Mine `loboroboto/hermes-interprets-coala` (a CoALA-oriented Hermes agent deployment) against the design, on the operator's question of its value for the Engine's agent personas and cognitive substrate. Disposition: surface one **documentation-precision** item as **[Q16](../reference/open-questions.md)** — D-033's *semantic = [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md)* shorthand can misread, since CoALA-semantic is belief-bearing while [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) is structural/derived; where distilled beliefs live is already settled ([memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)'s `decision`/`lesson` roles), so Q16 is a lineage-**wording** question, logged-not-resolved. No doc is locked/re-locked and no locked doc is edited; the disposition is one open-question plus this append-only entry (the [D-046](0046-harness-comparative-mining-a-guardrail-integrity-gap-a-contr.md)/[D-047](0047-product-spec-intake-design-it-native-not-bundled-the-build-t.md) external-mining precedent).

## Why

The repo independently grounds in CoALA and **corroborates** the Engine's episodic/semantic/procedural decomposition and reversibility-first evaluation — confirmatory, not additive. Its value as a source of *new* design is near-nil: its three CoALA-meta skills implement CoALA as **narrated in-context procedure**, exactly what [D-033](0033-ground-the-cognitive-substrate-in-established-standards-line.md) decided against ("cite vocabulary, import no machinery") and what its §5 leak guard bans from runtime narration; and its persona model is a single persistent agent plus independent-peer coordination, a different shape from the Engine's orchestrator-spawns-scoped-subagents model ([agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md)). A four-lens cold audit (adversarial / architect / non-engineer-operator / technical-feasibility) ran against this disposition; it confirmed the repo and CoALA facts, corrected the reflection anti-choice's anchor (it restates the [D-008](0008-memory-and-knowledge-are-distinct-substrates.md) never-synthesize wall, not a [D-030](0030-memory-ledger-canonical-observe-don-t-predict-capture-lexica.md) capture-gating point), narrowed Q16 from a false "design gap" to a wording question, and replaced a boundary-tripping example ("operator prefers X" — a cross-project personal fact that belongs in the contributor's built-in auto-memory notebook, not the project ledger, per [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md)) with a project-scoped one.

## What we ruled out

Adopt `coala-reflection`'s judgment-gated episodic→semantic **belief promotion** (rejected — it crosses the [D-008](0008-memory-and-knowledge-are-distinct-substrates.md) wall that the two substrates "never synthesize each other," and predicts importance at write time against [D-030](0030-memory-ledger-canonical-observe-don-t-predict-capture-lexica.md)'s observe-don't-predict; the append-only ledger already preserves conflicting records without overwrite). Adopt `coala-skill-induction`'s **runtime self-mutation of procedural memory** (rejected — procedural capability is the governed [skills](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md) surface, installed by file-drop / discovery-by-presence and upgrade-safe under human review, not a store the session rewrites mid-cycle; this is also the [Q13](../reference/open-questions.md) guardrail-integrity posture — the builder must not silently re-author its own behavior). Adopt `SOUL.md`-style **per-agent voice** for [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md) (rejected — agents are read-only/scoped instruments with a uniform `output-contract`, not personas; persona-voice narration is the maintainer→operator leak the [D-033](0033-ground-the-cognitive-substrate-in-established-standards-line.md) §5 guard and the [D-025](0025-fault-containment-is-earned-at-the-seams-not-conferred-by-mo.md) "microkernel" precedent forbid). Import the CoALA **decision-cycle as a narrated skill** (rejected — the Engine realizes the decision procedure mechanically via [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) + [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) + [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md), and narrating CoALA terms violates the leak guard). Log Q16 as a substrate **design** gap (rejected — the belief-home is already settled; only the wording is open). Drop the finding entirely (rejected — the wording strain is real and sits against a verbatim-quoted canonical statement; logging-not-resolving it is the honest middle path).
