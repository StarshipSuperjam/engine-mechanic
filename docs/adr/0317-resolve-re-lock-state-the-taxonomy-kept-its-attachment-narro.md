## Why

every one of the six is a landed-text audit finding, and three of them ((1)'s phrase collision, (3)'s wrong surface, and the whole `milestone` bound) were **defects this pass introduced or prescribed itself** — which is the case for [R35](../reference/risks.md) stated in evidence rather than in principle. The `milestone` outcome is the clearest instance: a rule that reads what the platform has beats a rule that infers what it lacks, and it is *simpler*, so the audit made the design smaller rather than larger. The taxonomy-survives constraint held: `build-orchestration`'s citation resolves, and the re-lock set did not grow on that axis.

**Keep [D-315](0315-amend-d-314-correct-its-operator-authorship-premise-the-buil.md)'s due-date rule** (rejected — undefined in its own common case; see (a)). **Pin a null policy and tiebreak (`NUMBER`/`CREATED_AT`) to save the due-date rule** (rejected — it is feasible, but it buys a single "current" no consumer needs, keeps the stalled-Milestone bias, and still needs the unpinned join key; the honest read is strictly smaller). **Render `none set` unless exactly one is open** (rejected — a *false* render, not a cautious one: Milestones plainly exist). **Let the operator name the current Milestone in config** (rejected — mints a surface the build-plan already covers and taxes an operator who wants to walk away). **Trim `:71` to the Milestone leg** (rejected — it was mis-subjected, not half-dead; trimming would have dropped the debt register's degrade silently). **Delete the four-kind taxonomy** (rejected — strands [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md):29-32 and grows the re-lock set). **Leave `:122-133` attributing "next" to boot** (rejected — boot surfaces it nowhere; a builder would rebuild `#394`'s hole).

## Further record

### The `milestone` bound: [D-315](0315-amend-d-314-correct-its-operator-authorship-premise-the-buil.md) prescribed a rule and its own focused audit killed it

D-315(3) directed: *"where a build-plan drives, the earliest open Milestone in the build-plan's phase order; where none does, the earliest open Milestone by due date, disclosing the basis"*. Two cold lenses (feasibility; architect/operator) independently refuted it on the landed text, and **the landed bound is not what D-315 prescribed** — this entry is where that is recorded, D-315 being append-only. **(a) `due_on` is optional and nullable** (verified against the live GraphQL schema — `dueOn: DateTime`, no `!` — and the REST create docs; milestones without due dates are the norm, not an edge). In the fallback's **common case** — several open, none with a due date — *"earliest by due date"* **selects nothing**, `none set` would be false, and GitHub does not document how nulls sort. The fix replaced an assumed platform **concept** (`active`) with an assumed platform **field** (`due_on`): the same defect, one layer down. **(b) The join key was never pinned.** *"Deterministic, because build-orchestration emits precisely those Milestones"* backs the **emission**, not the **back-mapping**: nothing states how a tool maps an open Milestone to a build-plan phase, Milestone titles are mutable, and the build-plan is **living** (*"re-sequences as work lands"*, [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md):145) — emission at T confers no key at T+1. **(c) The pick was unforced and biased.** Nothing consumes this field but the operator's display, so electing one buys nothing — and a due date records *when someone hoped*, not *what is being worked*, so a stalled Milestone keeps an open, past-due date and would be pinned as "current" **indefinitely**. **The landed bound therefore reads rather than infers:** the field carries the **open Milestone set as found** — the one where exactly one is open, **all of them named** where several are, `none set` where none are open (**not** a claim the project keeps none). **Where a build-plan drives, its phases *are* those Milestones, so the open set already is the open phase** — which dissolves (a), (b) and (c) at once: no due date, no join key, no heuristic, no second arm. The honest-bound sentence is corrected to match: `milestone`'s **provenance** (engine-planned under a build-plan; operator-kept otherwise) is not its **derive**, and the derive elects nothing. **Rendering is [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)'s** ([D-319](0319-resolve-re-lock-boot-relay-state-s-milestone-selection-bound.md)) — the prescribed text had `state` legislating a render, a §16 inversion, while boot's own closed list of card laws carried neither the disclosure nor the several-open case, so a builder implementing boot from boot would have shipped neither.
