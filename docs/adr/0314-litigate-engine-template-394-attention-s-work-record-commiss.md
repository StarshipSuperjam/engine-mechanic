# Litigate engine-template #394: attention's work-record commission was **superseded, not phantom** — the backlog/Milestone read is retired to its right owner (the operator-approved build-plan); re-locks land under [D-315](0315-amend-d-314-correct-its-operator-authorship-premise-the-buil.md) / [D-316](0316-resolve-re-lock-attention-the-work-record-commission-retired.md) / [D-317](0317-resolve-re-lock-state-the-taxonomy-kept-its-attachment-narro.md)

*Decided 2026-07-16 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

A **build session** working [engine-template #394](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/394) raised the **litigation alarm** on the issue that reported it, declining to invent grammar the design lacked. It is right. [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md):50 names *"open Issues as deferrals/backlog, Milestones as the plan"* as candidate work that *"attention **orders**"*, but `attention`:29 / [D-117](0117-q24-q27-2-re-litigation-the-attention-ranking-function-form.md) pin the ranking form to **five** budget categories with no slot to receive either, and [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md):84,94 scope the debt register to **engine-labeled** Issues — the operator's backlog is their **un-labeled** Issues. The build session **measured** the consequence: ranking 50 backlog issues returns a byte-identical `attention-result.v1` to ranking none — **dormant code**, which the v1 stance forbids. **Ruling (operator-approved this session): the read is retired from attention — on *wrong owner*, not on "never owed".** This entry is the **litigation alarm + authorization**; it authorizes editing two locked cognitive foundations plus a conditional third, and the **re-locks land under the resolve entries after the mandatory cold audit on the landed text**.

## Why

this is **not** the prose-weakening [#394](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/394) warned against (*"do not edit prose to describe the degenerate reality"*), and the distinction is load-bearing: the commission was **real**, and two decisions moved its subject matter to a **better owner** while the doc never caught up. The fix adapts the divergent text to the settled cross-doc truth — the [D-154](0154-build-ready-capstone-re-run-re-litigate-attention-s4-to-fix.md) precedent and the lock-discipline default — rather than rebuilding a capability the design deliberately re-homed. The build session's reading of `:50` was **correct**, which is precisely why the contradiction was worth raising rather than papering over.

## What we ruled out

**Attention ranks the backlog** (rejected — the build-plan already carries that ordering under **operator authorship**; ranking it by engine weights second-guesses a decision the operator locked, and a deferred Issue is a plan, not context). **A sixth category + budget slice** (rejected — what `:50` most plainly implies and the only shape under which attention truly *orders* the backlog, but it re-litigates [D-117](0117-q24-q27-2-re-litigation-the-attention-ranking-function-form.md)'s pinned form and spends a cold AI's bounded context on work defined as deferred — the rabbit-holing [D-010](0010-attention-is-a-first-class-surface.md) created attention to prevent). **Surface it outside the five** (rejected — non-dormant without touching D-117, but attention still would not be *ordering* it, and it adds an operator-facing surface the design never specified). **Justify the ruling as "the read was never designed"** (rejected — **false history**; [D-059](0059-lock-the-state-system-wave-2-head-the-committed-cursor-recon.md)/[D-062](0062-lock-the-attention-system-wave-2-the-policy-plus-function-pr.md) ratified it affirmatively, and an anti-choice reasoned from a fabricated record is worse precedent than the over-claim it corrects). **Read `:50` as a negative claim whose enumeration is mere taxonomy** (rejected — `attention`:48 is the heading *"What it governs"* and `:50` is a **positive charter bullet** under it; a builder reading it cold reads a commission because it **was** one). **Fix `attention` alone** (rejected — three sites carry the claim; fixing one restages the [D-151](0151-whole-corpus-design-audit-re-litigate-state-af-1-to-fix-the.md)→[D-154](0154-build-ready-capstone-re-run-re-litigate-attention-s4-to-fix.md) twin-miss a third time in the same doc pair). **Narrow *"what's next"* to in-flight only** (rejected — an operator with filed Issues would read it and reasonably conclude their backlog was *considered and ranked below* rather than *never looked at*: the precise false belief [§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md) forbid).

## Further record

### The corrected history — this is a supersession, not a phantom

The read **was designed and twice ratified**. [D-059](0059-lock-the-state-system-wave-2-head-the-committed-cursor-recon.md) (state's ratifying lock) *removed* a committed **candidate work list** and installed this formulation as its deliberate replacement: *"'what's next' is the native git/GitHub record: branches/PRs = in-flight, Issues = deferrals/backlog, Milestones = plan, ordered by attention"*; [D-062](0062-lock-the-attention-system-wave-2-the-policy-plus-function-pr.md) (attention's own ratifying lock) affirms it: *"It governs budget allocation, **work prioritization** (ordering the native git/GitHub record — branches/PRs/Issues/Milestones — not a committed list, per [D-038](0038-session-lifecycle-re-founded-on-native-substrates.md)/[D-059](0059-lock-the-state-system-wave-2-head-the-committed-cursor-recon.md))"*. **Two later decisions superseded it and never propagated to `:50`:** (1) **[D-117](0117-q24-q27-2-re-litigation-the-attention-ranking-function-form.md)** narrowed the ranking form to the five budget categories; (2) **[D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)** gave the operator's own approved ordering a home — the operator *accepts and `locks`* the spec, which [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md):171-175 decomposes into a committed **build-plan (ordered phases)** plus **un-labeled work Issues**, which [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md):32-37 groups under native **Milestones**; *"Build this Issue" is the deliberate act that enters Build*. `attention`:50 is the **unpropagated residue** of the pre-D-117, pre-D-244 design.

### The ruling in full — wrong owner

Attention's five categories budget **a cold AI's context window**; they do not rank the operator's roadmap. **A backlog Issue nobody is working on is not context — it is a plan.** The operator's ordering already exists and **they authored and accepted it** (the build-plan and its Milestones). Attention ranking that backlog by its own weights would be **the engine second-guessing an ordering the operator locked**. In plain language, the operator-facing form of this ruling: *when you file an Issue, the engine does not rank it or raise it on its own — you name the build, or point at the Issue; your ordering lives in the build-plan and milestones you approved, which the engine reads when it builds rather than second-guessing with its own priority list. What it shows you unasked is what is in flight, what is broken, and where the milestone stands.*

### The edit set is six sites across two docs, not one clause

A plan-stage cold audit (four lenses) caught three the proposing session had missed, and **all four lenses independently flagged the same one**: **(1)** `attention`:50, the charter bullet; **(2)** `attention`:9 (*"and what work it picks up"*), the same over-claim in the doc's opening; **(3)** [state](../spec/systems/cognitive/state.md):35-36, the verbatim twin; **(4)** `state`:71, **mis-subjected** rather than half-dead — `:69-70` (offline-answerable) and `:71-74` (degrades-with-GitHub) partition the same reads and `:72`'s *"State's committed values"* fallback is exactly `:69-70`'s pair, so `:71` is re-subjected to the two reads that genuinely degrade-with-fallback (the standing-situation live projection `phase`+`milestone`, and the engine-labeled debt register); **(5)** `state`:119-122, the **operator-facing** twin (*"boot surfaces in-flight, recently-shipped, and **queued/next work** … **ordered by attention over the native sources**"*) — the doc's own answer to *"locking State tiny does not orphan the operator's 'where are we'"*, and the sentence an operator would cite if they felt misled; **(6)** [glossary](../reference/glossary.md):110, where *"what's next"* is enumerated **distinctly from** *"ranked work"* on the status dashboard and, after this ruling, would have **no producer** — it is given one: **the approved build-plan / build Issue** (operator ruling this session).

### Hard constraint — narrow the attachment; never delete the taxonomy

[build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md):29-32 (locked) operationalizes *"the **locked state division** … the Milestone is the plan; the build Issue is that plan's machine-readable decomposition — not a new 'deferral/backlog' Issue semantics, and never a committed work-inventory (state forbids that)"*. The four-kind **taxonomy of the native record survives verbatim** in `state`; only the *"ordered by attention"* **attachment** is corrected. Deleting the taxonomy would strand that locked citation and widen the re-lock set. The corrected text must also **not under-claim**: `attention`:29/:49 pin **five** partitions and [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md):73 reproduces all five — the over-claim is traded for **the truth**, never for an under-claim naming only two.

### The honest generalization — recorded because it is trust-load-bearing

`attention`:50 shipped **locked**. It survived its per-system cold audit, the [D-151](0151-whole-corpus-design-audit-re-litigate-state-af-1-to-fix-the.md) whole-corpus audit, and **[D-154](0154-build-ready-capstone-re-run-re-litigate-attention-s4-to-fix.md) — a re-litigation of this exact doc pair for this exact twin-miss defect class**.
