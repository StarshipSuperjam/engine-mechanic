# Resolve: re-lock `validators-core` (the negative-fixture meta-check instance — self-covering, module-kind fail-closed) — third carrier of D-256

*Decided 2026-06-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Re-lock [validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md), the third carrier of [D-256](0256-authorize-the-every-hard-check-is-proven-to-bite-re-litigati.md). Edits: a new **"The negative-fixture meta-check"** section + a `provides` clause + a design commitment, carrying the meta-check **instance** — a `custom/script` rule `validators-core` owns outright (the sibling of `reference-closure`), enforcing [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md)'s proven-to-bite invariant: it runs each in-scope hard logic-unit against its discovered negative fixture and asserts the expected `hard` finding, **failing closed** on a present in-scope unit whose fixture is absent (the absence read against the unit's roster presence — the validator's kind registry for kind callables, the check-rule directory listing for `custom/script` instances). **Self-covering** per [§15](../principles.md) (carries its own negative fixture; in its own scope; terminates the regress, no meta-meta-check). Each fixture co-locates with its unit's owner — `custom/script` fixtures with the rule (here), module-kind fixtures with the module's callable (drop/remove both, [§14](../principles.md)/[R5](../reference/risks.md) reversibility), the five closed-core kinds' fixtures with their callables in [core](../spec/modules/core.md) (the D-260 adjudication). No sixth kind; `data-files-only`, `wires: none`, `depends: core` all hold. **The focused three-lens cold audit** (faithfulness/propagation · architect/seam · adversarial, no shared context — the bounded-carrier gate): **no blocking.** Faithfulness — faithful to [D-256](0256-authorize-the-every-hard-check-is-proven-to-bite-re-litigati.md), correctly defers the law to [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) ([D-257](0257-resolve-re-lock-validation-the-negative-fixture-meta-check-l.md)) and the grammar/rosters to [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) ([D-258](0258-resolve-re-lock-check-the-by-presence-negative-fixture-gramm.md)), anti-changelog-clean, no contradiction with data-files-only/wires-none. Architect — ownership single-mastered (instance here; law/grammar/core-kind-fixtures elsewhere), manifest truthful (no new edge; `depends: core` covers the dispatcher entry point + the fixtures-namespace read), [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) verify-no-edit (the [D-220](0220-resolve-the-d-219-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-land.md) rule-data-no-graph-change precedent). **Two serious, fixed in-line:** **(a)** the `provides` one-liner over-claimed "bites" vs the honest ceiling → "**witnessed to bite** (or a disclosed not-applicable)"; **(b)** "settled with the core adjudication" asserted a *pending* settlement → softened to an honest **forward reference** ("core's to ship, the core/topology adjudication's to settle"), the [D-257](0257-resolve-re-lock-validation-the-negative-fixture-meta-check-l.md) precedent. Plus roster-wording re-synced to [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) verbatim ("the validator's kind registry"; "check-rule directory listing"). **Forward references to D-260** (the dispatcher run-one-rule entry point; the fixtures-namespace topology clause; and — newly pinned here — the five closed-core kinds' fixtures co-locating in core, core's obligation to ship them) are the accepted authorize-then-resolve transient ([D-220](0220-resolve-the-d-219-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-land.md)/[D-257](0257-resolve-re-lock-validation-the-negative-fixture-meta-check-l.md)); **D-260 must explicitly close all three** so none is orphaned. `python3 lock.py --relock modules/validators-core/README.md --decision D-259`; `python3 validate.py` green.

## Why

the meta-check instance is rule-data `validators-core` owns (the engine-self-validation corpus), exactly the `reference-closure` `custom/script` shape, so it lands here — deferring the law to validation and the grammar to check. The bounded three-lens gate ([D-245](0245-resolve-re-lock-state-the-milestone-honest-bound-first-carri.md)/[D-248](0248-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-referent-is-t.md)/[D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md)) earned its keep: it caught the `provides` over-claim and the "settled" over-assertion before the lock, and confirmed the meta-check rests on core via `depends: core` with no new edge. The core dependencies stay honest forward references (the [D-257](0257-resolve-re-lock-validation-the-negative-fixture-meta-check-l.md) precedent), closed at the next carrier (D-260).

## What we ruled out

**Assert the core entry point / core-kind fixtures as settled core facts** (rejected — D-260 has not adjudicated; the [D-257](0257-resolve-re-lock-validation-the-negative-fixture-meta-check-l.md)-precedent forward-reference phrasing keeps validators-core true whatever D-260 decides). **Reorder D-260 before this re-lock** (rejected — [D-256](0256-authorize-the-every-hard-check-is-proven-to-bite-re-litigati.md) set instance-before-engine-adjudication; validation and check already forward-reference D-260 the same way, and the softened phrasing is robust, so reordering would desync the just-landed carriers). **Own a new kind or detection mechanism for the meta-check** (rejected — it is a `custom/script` rule, the `reference-closure` shape; data-files-only holds; no sixth closed kind, [R6](../reference/risks.md)). **Run the full four-lens audit** (rejected — a bounded instance carrier; the focused three-lens is the proportionate gate).
