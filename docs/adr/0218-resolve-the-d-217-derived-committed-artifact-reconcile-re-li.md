# Resolve the D-217 derived-committed-artifact reconcile re-litigation: landed-text cold audit, three docs re-locked

*Decided 2026-06-18 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The [D-217](0217-authorize-the-derived-committed-artifact-reconcile-re-litiga.md) change-set is landed and **re-locked**. The mandatory landed-text cold audit ran with **four** independent no-shared-context lenses (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) on the as-written edits in [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md), [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md), and [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md) plus the [principles](../principles.md) §19 / [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) / [risks](../reference/risks.md) / [scenarios/build-session](../architecture.md#build-session-lifecycle) carriers. **Outcome: no fatal flaw; the core design verified sound (two lenses independently re-traced the live generators and confirmed conflicts on the class are spurious-and-lossless and determinism holds today); three nits folded; the rest dispositioned.** Re-lock set: **3**.

  **The audit + dispositions.**
  - **Adversarial — no blocking; one nit folded.** Confirmed no internal contradiction across the eight landed texts, no [§7](../principles.md) overclaim slipped back (the gate is *exercises* everywhere; `CONFLICTING` never called impossible), no constructible non-spurious member conflict, no anti-changelog cruft. **Nit (folded):** the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) non-member list mis-justified `uv.lock` as "per-instance state" — `uv.lock` is a shared template artifact whose real disqualifier is that it resolves against the external package index, not the committed source tree alone. **Fold:** the glossary term now separates `engine.json` (per-instance install state) from `uv.lock` (external-index-resolved).
  - **Technical-feasibility — PASS, no findings.** Re-verified against `engine-template` `origin/main`: the serialization characterization (`json.dumps(sort_keys=True, …) + "\n"`, sorted entities, LF), the *exercises-not-enforces* tier, "server-side merge cannot run a local driver," "never `merge=ours`" (the built-in `ours` driver keeps a stale copy; regenerate-to-resolve must be a *custom* driver), "clear-and-regenerate beats a side-pick an add/delete-vs-modify defeats," and the [R27](../reference/risks.md) "unsorted glob does not reach committed bytes" claim (guarded by the sorted `inventory`, the single-owner coherence rule, and `sort_keys`) all check out as written.
  - **Non-engineer-operator — no blocking; the honesty tier judged correct.** "Operator never *resolves*" vs "`CONFLICTING` never *occurs*" lands correctly in all three load-bearing sites; the quiet pulled Review-record disclosure is the right trust call; [R27](../reference/risks.md)'s plain-language degradation is operator-facing, not maintainer-only. **Nit (folded into the build-owe):** make the interim/unattended "reconcile this PR" recovery path **boot-surfaced** (detect-and-offer, the stranded-checkout pattern) so a walk-away operator returning to a `CONFLICTING` PR is *offered* the fix rather than meeting a red banner.
  - **Architect — propagation complete; consistency confirmed; one attribution nit folded.** Confirmed [engine-architecture](../architecture.md) needs no edit (no system/surface added; it carries no exhaustive principle index), no other scenario needs the integrate line ([external-contribution](../architecture.md#contributing-to-an-external-repo) already states fork-main regeneration), the [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md) + [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) no-edits are sound, every relative link resolves, and the membership definition + determinism tier are byte-consistent across §19 / glossary / knowledge / ontology / build-orchestration. **Nit (folded):** [R27](../reference/risks.md)'s body dual-referenced `[D-218]` for the law (whose home is §19); the body now reads "[§19] names a class," keeping "Opened by [D-218]" (the resolve-entry convention, per [R26](../reference/risks.md) → [D-212](0212-resolve-the-d-211-security-floor-re-litigation-landed-text-c.md)). *(The lens's two "blocking" items — the un-run re-lock and the missing D-218 entry — were the in-progress state this very entry and the re-lock step complete; they are not design defects.)*

## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious resolved before re-lock; it ran (four lenses), found no fatal flaw — the core insight (a derived-committed artifact holds nothing that is not a function of source, so its conflicts are spurious and regeneration lossless) was independently re-traced against the live generators — and the three nits (the `uv.lock` justification, the [R27](../reference/risks.md) attribution, the boot-surfaced recovery path) were folded before the re-locks. The landed text is faithful to [D-217](0217-authorize-the-derived-committed-artifact-reconcile-re-litiga.md): §19 is a property-defined cross-cutting law at the honest [§7](../principles.md) tier (the gate exercises, the round-trip test enforces), the integrate step names regenerate-from-the-reconciled-tree as the single-writer's job, and the two #136 framing corrections (no gate divergence; catalogs excluded) hold.

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock without the landed-text audit** (rejected — HARD lock gate). **Treat the architect lens's "missing D-218 / un-run re-lock" as design defects** (rejected — they were the in-progress state this entry and the re-lock step complete, not flaws in the change). **Keep the `uv.lock` "per-instance state" justification** (rejected — factually wrong; `uv.lock` is a shared template artifact, disqualified by external-index resolution, not per-instance-ness). **Leave the interim recovery path merely "invocable"** (rejected — a walk-away operator would meet a red banner; boot-surfacing it, detect-and-offer, keeps the operator offered-a-fix rather than stranded). **Edit [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) to spell out boot-surfacing** (rejected — it is a build-spec realization detail of the named recovery path, correctly a build-owe, not locked-design grammar).
