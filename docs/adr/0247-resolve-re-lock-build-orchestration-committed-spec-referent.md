## Why

build-orchestration is the keystone: it owns the spec-referent resolution (now a path read of the committed corpus) and Milestone production (from the build-plan), so it is where the control-plane contradiction had to close and where state's milestone forward references resolve symmetrically — all three edited system docs now agree. The full audit was the proportionate gate (a substantial keystone carrier) and earned its keep by catching the §17 operator-rendering gap — the precise trust trap this whole re-litigation exists to avoid, where structure manufactures confidence the verification does not deliver. The teeth are stated at the honest tier the locked validation/§7 require: a mechanical check on *traceability* (decidable) plus a posture *judgment* on conformance (the operator's merge is the wall), never an agentic verdict dressed as a mechanical gate (the [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md) anti-choice). The product-design carry-forward is the expected, authorized cost of locking the module last; its stale :67-72 is the in-pass transient D-250 closes.
