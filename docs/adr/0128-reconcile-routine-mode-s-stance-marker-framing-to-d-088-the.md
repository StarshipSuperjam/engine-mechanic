# Reconcile routine-mode's stance-marker framing to D-088 (the entry does not set the activating signal)

*Decided 2026-05-29 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Reword the [routine-mode](../spec/modules/routine-mode.md) "Wiring nothing" bullet that re-introduced the framing [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md) logged as a rejected anti-choice — "the **stance signal** the entry sets." The [D-124](0124-dry-run-the-routine-mode-module-build-step-build-ready-surfa.md) routine-mode dry-run (a well-calibrated reviewer; orchestrator ground-truth against [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md) and [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md):129–137,149) confirmed it a real but **non-build-blocking** `designed`-doc accuracy defect, routed for remediation before routine-mode's packaging lock. The bullet now makes the **session stance marker** — modes' ephemeral, session-keyed, non-committed signal of the active stance (a [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) build-spec leaf) — the subject: it sits outside the closed wiring-seam vocabulary (not a `gitignore`/`permission`/`hook` wire), it *reflects* the stance and does **not** authorize entry — Routine's authority is the operator-configured schedule + the frozen scope-locked build Issue ([D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md)) — so `routine-mode` neither sets nor wires it. The module's opening (entry authority = the operator schedule + the frozen scope-locked Issue) and its design commitments were already correct and are unchanged; the cross-reference token on the permission-posture bullet is aligned to "session stance marker." **Not a litigation alarm:** the remedy edits the `designed` routine-mode doc only; locked [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) and [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md) are correct as-is and untouched.

## Why

Finding-disposition — a surfaced defect is fixed in line, not buried. The bullet's *mechanical* claim (the marker is not a wire) was right, but it cast "the entry" as the actor that "sets a stance signal," exactly the mechanism [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md) rejected (entry is a pre-authored operator act; the authority is the schedule + frozen scope-lock; the marker is target-contract-driven, never a model-set or by-default self-election). The fix shifts the grammatical actor off "the entry" onto the marker/modes rather than swapping the verb (a "the entry records" phrasing would re-commit the same trap), and grounds the authority half in locked [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md):129–137. A two-lens cold-context plan audit (propagation-completeness/architect + faithfulness/adversarial, no shared session context) ran against the remediation plan before approval and confirmed the defect is the *sole* spot in the file carrying the framing (every other "stance" use is correct), no locked doc is touched, and the reword must keep the actor-shift (folded). Propagation per the matrix: [modules/routine-mode/README.md](../spec/modules/routine-mode.md) reworded; this entry. Verified no-change with rationale: locked [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) + [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md) (correct as-is — the module was out of step with them, not the reverse), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) (indexes systems not modules), [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md) (no new/renamed term — `Mode (stance)` already covers it), [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) (design-maturity not catalog-tracked; `routine-mode` stays `designed`). `python3 validate.py` green. No locked doc edited.

## What we ruled out

Leave the "the stance signal the entry sets" framing (rejected — it re-introduces the [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md) anti-choice; a reader infers the entry elects the stance by setting a signal, when the authority is the operator schedule + frozen scope-lock). Swap "sets" → "records" while keeping "the entry" as the subject (rejected — the same mischaracterization in disguise; the marker, not the entry, must be the actor). Treat it as a **litigation alarm** / re-litigate [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) (rejected — modes and [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md) are correct; only the `designed` module doc drifted, so no locked doc changes). Record it as a **build-blocking** gap (rejected — [D-124](0124-dry-run-the-routine-mode-module-build-step-build-ready-surfa.md) ground-truthed that the builder produces correct artifacts regardless; it is a designed-doc accuracy fix owed before the lock, not a build blocker). Defer it into the future status-staleness sweep (rejected — it is a distinct, already-adjudicated [D-124](0124-dry-run-the-routine-mode-module-build-step-build-ready-surfa.md) remedy with its own owner; bundling unrelated fixes obscures both).
