# Resolve: re-lock `attention` (the work-record commission retired to the plan's owner; the retired read cleared from the ranking function's **inputs**, not only its charter) — a carrier of [D-314](0314-litigate-engine-template-394-attention-s-work-record-commiss.md)/[D-315](0315-amend-d-314-correct-its-operator-authorship-premise-the-buil.md)

*Decided 2026-07-16 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Re-lock [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md), a carrier of [D-314](0314-litigate-engine-template-394-attention-s-work-record-commiss.md) as amended by [D-315](0315-amend-d-314-correct-its-operator-authorship-premise-the-buil.md), resolving the `#394` litigation on this doc. **Four sites landed.** **(1) `:50`, the charter bullet** — the *Work prioritization* bullet keeps its ordering rule (*unblocked first; blocking debt ahead of features*) and now names what attention actually ranks: the **in-flight** native record (open branches and pull requests) together with the open engine-labeled issues of [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md)'s debt register, the read [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md):277 already enumerates. It then names the plan's owner **out loud** rather than leaving a silence a future session would re-derive from: the plan is the build-plan's, engine-decomposed from the spec the operator accepted and `locked`, living, grouped under Milestones by [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) — *"absent a build-plan, build-orchestration plans the Milestone itself"* — and **attention neither reads nor re-ranks it, because a deferred issue is a plan, not context for a cold session.** **(2) `:25-27`, the ranking function's *inputs*** — the seventh site, found by the landed-text adversarial lens after four other lenses missed it ([D-315](0315-amend-d-314-correct-its-operator-authorship-premise-the-buil.md)(2)): the input list still said *"the native git/GitHub work record"*, the unqualified four-kind term-of-art, so the retired commission survived on the **input** side of the very doc being re-locked. Narrowed to the in-flight slice plus the merged pull requests whose bodies carry the recent decisions — `:49`'s own naming. **(3) `:9`, the definition line** — *"and what work it picks up"* (selection, which is the operator's act and build-orchestration's record) became *"the order of the in-flight and blocking work it ranks"*, naming the **set** as well as the verb after the plan-stage lenses flagged the verb-only fix as a residual over-claim. **(4) No under-claim:** `:29`/`:49` still pin **five** partitions and [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md):73 still reproduces all five; `:50` is scoped to *candidate work*, of which only in-flight and blocking debt **are** work — verified by two lenses as not a fresh intra-doc divergence. `python3 lock.py --relock systems/cognitive/attention/README.md --decision D-316`.

## Why

the fix adapts the divergent doc to the settled cross-doc truth ([D-154](0154-build-ready-capstone-re-run-re-litigate-attention-s4-to-fix.md)'s precedent) and adds nothing: no sixth category, no new surface, no new vocabulary, no mechanism. Site (2) is the one that justifies the whole gate — it is the [D-151](0151-whole-corpus-design-audit-re-litigate-state-af-1-to-fix-the.md)→[D-154](0154-build-ready-capstone-re-run-re-litigate-attention-s4-to-fix.md) twin-miss class recurring a third time in this doc pair, on the input side where no prior sweep had looked, and it would have shipped under a fresh lock. Sites (1) and (3) are the difference between a doc that is *not wrong* and a doc a cold builder cannot mis-build from: naming the plan's real owner forecloses re-deriving the sixth-category question from silence, which is exactly how `#394` arose.

## What we ruled out

**Fix `:50` alone** (rejected — `:25-27` carries the same commission on the input side, and `:9` states the retired charter in the doc's definition line; a builder anchors on all three). **Name only two partitions as "what attention orders"** (rejected per [D-314](0314-litigate-engine-template-394-attention-s-work-record-commiss.md)'s hard constraint — the over-claim is traded for the truth, never for an under-claim; the five-category form is untouched). **Leave `:9`'s verb narrowed but its set unnamed** (rejected — the landed-text adversarial lens showed the unnamed set made `:9` the loosest statement of the narrowed charter in the corpus, in the line a cold builder anchors on). **Say attention does not re-rank the plan because the operator authored it** (rejected — false; see [D-315](0315-amend-d-314-correct-its-operator-authorship-premise-the-buil.md)(1). The reason is that a plan is not a cold session's context and [D-117](0117-q24-q27-2-re-litigation-the-attention-ranking-function-form.md)'s form has no slot for one — a reason that needs no fiction).
