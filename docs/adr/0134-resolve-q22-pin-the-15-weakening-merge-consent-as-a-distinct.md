# Resolve Q22: pin the §15 weakening-merge consent as a distinct, deliberate acknowledgment (re-litigate + re-lock control-plane); decline the Q22(b) active duty

*Decided 2026-05-30 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

**Re-litigate the locked** [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) ([D-122](0122-enumerate-the-branch-ruleset-protection-floor-in-control-pla.md)/[D-132](0132-own-the-engine-label-scheme-in-control-plane-re-litigation-r.md)) to resolve [Q22](../reference/open-questions.md) — the merge-gate-consent item, both halves, hereby deleted (distinct from the wbs/dry-run-ledger.md / [D-111](0111-resolve-q22-q23-the-construction-repo-carries-no-codeowners.md) finding-ID `Q22`, the genesis construction-repo CODEOWNERS question — a separate number-space in that frozen dry-run artifact, unrelated and untouched here). **Q22(a) — the §15 weakening-consent gesture (toward a *distinct deliberate gesture*).** The locked text was under-determined: control-plane:115 read "the operator's merge click **is** informed consent" (the *ordinary*-merge Identity section) while [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md):150-151 and the §15 origin [D-051](0051-guardrail-integrity-the-builder-cannot-silently-weaken-its-o.md) read weakening "hard-blocks the merge **until** the operator's informed consent … the human acknowledgment is the **pass condition**" (a separate artifact). The §"protection floor" now pins the **strength** of that §15 invariant as a contract: the weakening hard-gate's pass condition is an acknowledgment **act distinct from the ordinary one-click merge** — **exactly one additional deliberate affirmative act** (floor: not the bare click; ceiling: no more than one act, not a banner to scroll past, not a ceremony gauntlet); its prompt **names, in plain operator language, the concrete protection that weakens and what the AI could then do unwatched**, never maintainer vocabulary (the [§12](../principles.md) leak-guard binds the surface, because for a weakening change the wording is what makes the consent *informed*). The **D-051 guard mechanism is unchanged** — the acknowledgment artifact is an **input** to the *same* frozen-named, trusted-source-evaluated §15 guard (diff-read only, never executes head code), never a parallel required check a bypass could satisfy on its own. The **artifact form stays a build-spec leaf**, constrained at law level to a single bounded affordance the operator cannot fail at by mistyping — a pull-request checkbox or an operator-*applied* (bootstrap-provisioned, never hand-created) label, **never** a typed phrase, never a command to run — with ownership fixed against a Q27-style unowned form (control-plane owns the contract; [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) owns the guard's check wiring; the form is concretized at build time under the PR contract). It is the **second beat of one §15 consent spine**, not a second gate: the plan-gate §15 headline ([build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md):127-131) is informed consent *before the spend*, this merge acknowledgment is the distinct *final* gesture the ordinary click does not satisfy — so build-orchestration's plan-gate behaviour is unchanged. **Honest tiering is preserved, not over-claimed:** in **solo** the engine commits as the operator and holds the operator's credentials, so it *could* produce the artifact itself — the gesture makes weakening **non-silent and deliberate** (no machine detection in solo) and stays *cannot weaken silently*, never *cannot weaken at all*; the structural close remains the operator's choice of **team** identity. The **Identity** section carves "the merge click is informed consent" to *ordinary* changes. **Q22(b) — native blocked-merge surfacing (toward *passive plain-language pre-arming*).** The "Conversation resolution" floor rule gains a **passive surfacing obligation**: when an unresolved thread greys the merge button (a native GitHub state a non-engineer cannot self-diagnose), it is surfaced in plain language at the merge — why it is blocked, that the operator may clear it once they have read the comment and accept it (never a bare "one click fixes it"), and how to reach a hidden/post-rebase-unreachable thread — the rendering riding build-orchestration's **existing** Review-record build-spec leaf (no build-orchestration edit). The active **detect-and-narrate / offer-to-resolve duty is declined**: auto-resolving a thread that flagged a concern would gut the finding-disposition spine the rule serves. control-plane is **re-locked under this decision**: `python3 lock.py --relock systems/infrastructure/control-plane/README.md --decision D-134`.

## Why

Justified re-litigation, not drift ([D-051](0051-guardrail-integrity-the-builder-cannot-silently-weaken-its-o.md)/[D-122](0122-enumerate-the-branch-ruleset-protection-floor-in-control-pla.md)/[D-132](0132-own-the-engine-label-scheme-in-control-plane-re-litigation-r.md) posture) — completing an under-determined locked contract Q22(a) itself flagged as ambiguous; pinning the **strength** of a §15 governance invariant is exactly the call a build-time implementer must **not** make silently. The **HARD cold-session design audit** ran four independent cold-context lenses (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) against the proposal; their blocking/serious findings were folded **before** the relock, materially reshaping it: **[BLOCKING]** the draft restated D-051's already-recorded hard-block as if new and reached into the artifact *form* → narrowed to the distinct-act invariant, D-051's mechanism explicitly unchanged, form left a leaf; **[BLOCKING/feasibility]** in **solo no acknowledgment artifact is AI-proof** (the engine holds the operator's token) → the contract reaffirms honest tiering (deliberateness/non-silence lever, no machine detection in solo, team is the only structural close) and a "surfaced at boot" over-claim was dropped, with the threat model stated (careless bundling + rubber-stamp, not a forging engine); **[SERIOUS]** the artifact must inherit D-051's **trusted-source + frozen-name** binding as an *input* to the one guard, never a parallel check; **[SERIOUS]** the form excludes a free-typed phrase (mistype trap), friction is bounded to exactly one act, and concrete-consequence plain-language wording is a **law**; **[SERIOUS]** the plan-gate vs merge-time consent is reconciled as a two-beat spine with build-orchestration no-touch; **[SERIOUS]** [surfaces/check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md):57 added to verify-no-touch, the glossary edits enumerated, and Q22(b) placed concretely in control-plane's **own** conversation-resolution rule (riding this relock) rather than a second build-orchestration relock. ## What we ruled out

in solo the engine holds the operator's token, so the gesture is a non-silence lever, and team is the only structural close). **Pin the artifact form in the locked doc** (rejected — the form is a build-spec leaf; only its law-level constraints are pinned — the distinct act, the one-act ceiling, the plain-language concrete-consequence wording, the trusted-source/frozen-name security binding, and ownership). **A free-typed acknowledgment phrase** (rejected — a mistype trap for a non-engineer and at odds with the never-fail-by-mistyping / no-CLI constraint; checkbox or applied label only). **An acknowledgment that is its own parallel required check** (rejected — a bypass could satisfy it independently; it must be an *input* to the one frozen-named trusted-source §15 guard). **Q22(b): adopt the active detect-and-narrate / offer-to-resolve duty** (rejected — auto-resolving a thread that flagged a concern guts the finding-disposition spine; a new behavioural law and scope creep against low-ceremony; the operator chose passive). **Q22(b): home the pre-arming as a new build-orchestration build-spec-leaf bullet** (rejected as the primary home — a second relock; the surfacing obligation rides control-plane's own conversation-resolution rule already being relocked, and the rendering uses build-orchestration's *existing* Review-record leaf, keeping it to one relock). **Edit build-orchestration / provisioning / principles §15** (rejected — [§16](../principles.md) relays: each binds the channel/contract and none asserts the gesture form this change pins; verify-no-touch, logged). **Re-park the Q22(b) stranded-at-button case as a new open question** (rejected — the operator chose passive with eyes open; it is named as an accepted v1 residual, not an unresolved thread). **Skip the cold-session audit or edit the locked doc without re-litigation** (rejected — the CLAUDE.md HARD gate;
