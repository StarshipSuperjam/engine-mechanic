# Surgical coupling pass on the `core-build-roadmap`: declare cross-slice contracts at their producers + author the `operation`/`skill` grammar slices; reject heavy re-clustering

*Decided 2026-06-03 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Applied the operator-agreed **surgical coupling pass** to the non-locked `core-build-roadmap` (backed up first to `Claude_Files/core-build-roadmap.backup-2026-06-03.md`, byte-verified, outside the validator ROOT — the workspace is not git-tracked). Grounding the remaining `core` slices against the locked spine ([hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md), [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md), [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md), [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md), [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md), [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md), [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md), stage-0, module-order) established that the slices are **additive-by-design** and already topologically ordered, so a heavy re-cluster would fight the [§12](../principles.md)/[§14](../principles.md)/[§16](../principles.md) seam discipline. The genuine cross-slice risk is a small set of **producer→consumer contracts**, now declared at the producing slice with `owes →`/`owes ←` pointers (the D-162 discipline): **(a) attention's ranking output** (slice 12 → boot 20 / scent 23 / build-orch 24 — form locked, only serialization open, named a versioned schema-governed result at the producer); **(b) the engine-domain label** (slice 18, the first producer — see the corrected resolution below); **(c) the boot present-marker token** (slice 19 ↔ 20 — a shared constant named once at 19, rendered byte-identically at 20). Authored the two grammar slices the roadmap already self-flagged as gaps — **Slice OG** (`operation` grammar, builds before 20/21/22/24) and **Slice SG** (`skill` grammar, builds before 26) — **grammar-only**, with the operation field-set grounded on the source's frontmatter table + [D-126](0126-dry-run-the-audit-library-module-not-build-ready-the-audit-p.md) (**not** "enumerated nowhere"), the per-instance operation-hood classification **deferred to confirmation at each lifecycle slice** (catalog-coverage justifies the grammar; whether each procedure is itself an operation instance is the source's call), and the skill `invocation`→platform-flag mapping **routed to live-platform verification** at the plan gate (not asserting the workflow's `claude-code#26251` claim). Added the `/engine-help` two-part-listing grounding ([modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md), [D-119](0119-resolve-q26-pin-the-engine-help-verb-index-kind-discovery-me.md)/[D-087](0087-resolve-q7-v1-skill-membership-close-deviation-d2-the-wbs-de.md)) to slice 26. **Propagation:** `wbs/core-build-roadmap.md` (slices 12/18/19/20/21/22/24/26 + new Slices OG/SG + the grammar-gap section + a build-status note); this entry. **No locked doc touched** — the locked spine was *read to ground the pass*, never edited; `python3 validate.py` green.

## Why

The cohesive recluster is a **single-author** job — the orchestrator-is-single-writer principle the failed 3-proposer workflow ([D-163](0163-correct-d-162-s-8-is-a-build-conformance-deviation-fix-the-b.md)) violated; agents validate the *parts* of a built artifact, they do not author the whole. Naming each producer→consumer contract at the producer attacks the operator's core failure mode — a forward-referenced piece silently made optional/prose/brittle with no path back — by making the consumer's needs visible *at the producing slice*, without fusing work into diffs a non-engineer cannot consent to ([§17](../principles.md)).

## What we ruled out

**Heavy re-clustering of the lifecycle spine** (the 12-cluster dry-run output) — rejected: the spine is additive-by-design ([hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md) fixes laws+slots and consumers attach; [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) hands off without forcing neighbour changes), so big clusters fight the design and yield un-consentable diffs; ground-truthing also refuted the dry-run's headline findings (operation field-set "enumerated nowhere" is false; `/engine-routine` is owned by **locked** [routine-mode](../spec/modules/routine-mode.md), not a `core` slice; "settings.json born at 20" is correct and "K8 orders 21 before 20" was an artifact of the dry-run's own mis-ordering). **Re-litigating any locked doc** — rejected: every seam resolved *within* the locked grammar (the each-producing-system clause, the build-spec-leaf deferrals, the catalog-coverage law). **Asserting the workflow's platform-flag / field-set claims** — rejected: routed to live-platform/source confirmation per the validate-against-the-live-system discipline. **Integer-renumbering 17–27 to insert OG/SG** — rejected: letter-labels + explicit build-before-consumer constraints avoid churning every downstream reference (build order is the dependency sort, not the slice number).
