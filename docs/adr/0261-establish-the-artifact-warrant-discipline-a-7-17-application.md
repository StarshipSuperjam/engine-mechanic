# Establish the artifact-warrant discipline (a §7/§17 application, no new principle): a generated artifact states what a green result shows, what it does not, and what review still covers; re-litigate locked [module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md) + [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md) to close their coherence/coverage warrant gaps

*Decided 2026-06-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The operator observed the self-map / module / knowledge-graph systems are healthier and the engine-template's knowledge-coverage check now names its own bound well ("green means the graph matches the surfaces, not that every derived edge is semantically right"), and flagged the standing risk that **generated artifacts read as authoritative even when their derivation logic is wrong** — directing that the humility be kept everywhere: every generated artifact carries the same explicit bound (what it shows / what it does not show / what review still covers). **Grounding found the principle is already canon** ([§7](../principles.md): tiers named honestly, never dress posture as mechanism; [§17](../principles.md): confidence bounded by a non-AI correlate, the bound *stated*; the tier-4 *describe, never govern* rule in [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md)) and **pervasively practiced design-side** — the [audit digest](../reference/glossary.md) is the in-corpus exemplar (plain-language bound at the point of consumption), with [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md) ("counts and trends; never reads for meaning") and the [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) graph (structure-not-belief, fingerprint-gated) alongside. The unserved gap is **consumption-side** (the bound sits in maintainer docs, not where the operator/AI meets the artifact) plus **two genuine design-doc gaps**: [module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md) coherence never said a green check does not prove the modules *function*, and [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md) surface-catalog coverage never said green does not attest the catalogued **governance fields** are *right*. **This establishes the discipline as a §7/§17 APPLICATION — no new principle** (the [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) Review-correlate and [D-256](0256-authorize-the-every-hard-check-is-proven-to-bite-re-litigati.md) negative-fixture siblings both applied §7/§15/§17 without minting one; [R6](../reference/risks.md)/the contract-threshold keep principles exceptional), carried by **(1)** a glossary **Artifact warrant** term — the shows/does-not-show/review-covers triple, **proportionate** (prominent where the bound is least obvious, light where self-evident), presence by-construction for committed artifacts and **posture** for surfaced renderings, accuracy via the per-PR build-conformance review with register/jargon drift caught by the [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) prose-probe, the **sibling of [Negative fixture](../reference/glossary.md)** (that proves a `hard` check *bites*; this states what a *green* result does and does not show); **(2)** a change-propagation-matrix rule binding every new generated artifact; **(3)** the two design-doc gaps closed by re-litigating the two locked docs. **A focused three-lens cold audit** (faithfulness/propagation · architect/seam · non-engineer-operator, no shared context — the [D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md)/[D-245](0245-resolve-re-lock-state-the-milestone-honest-bound-first-carri.md) bounded-edit gate) ran on the landed text; **blocking/serious findings folded** (the [D-242](0242-resolve-the-d-241-audit-memory-read-enablement-the-landed-fo.md)/[D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md) fold-before-relock practice, no separate re-check): the module-fitness attribution **narrowed** from "audits judge whether it works" to the audits **retire-candidate probe** (the function-probe is scoped to absence-of-exercise, not general fitness); the ontology **jargon** "authored-content review" **replaced** with an honest authoring-judgment line that hands the operator no impossible step ([§17](../principles.md) part-3 honesty); **proportionality made visible** (the coherence over-trust gap stated prominently, the coverage residual lighter); **[R34](../reference/risks.md) raised to Medium** with the consumption surfaces named. **Re-locks under this entry: [module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md) + [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md).** **Honest tier ([§7](../principles.md)):** the discipline is **design-side canon now**; the consumption-side surfacing (the plain-language line riding boot / the PR body / the coherence- and coverage-check renderings) is a **must-land build-owe** in `../engine-template`, realized by the build ([D-003](0003-specify-the-full-end-state-before-the-first-build-pr.md) design-precedes-build) — **not claimed rendered everywhere**; the residual is [R34](../reference/risks.md). **Verify-no-edit dispositions:** [principles.md](../principles.md) untouched by design (no new principle); [engine-architecture](../architecture.md) carries no principle index (no new system/surface); build-conformance/[audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md)/[validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) gain no new gate or kind (consumers of the existing review/probe); the strong docs (audit-digest, knowledge, telemetry, [state](../spec/systems/cognitive/state.md), [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md)) already embody the bound in concept → no re-lock; neither re-lit doc has a module-bundle or scenario that re-asserts a green=correct claim → nothing owed beyond the glossary term + matrix rule + [R34](../reference/risks.md).

## Why

The operator's push is correct and grounding made it precise — the design already says a green/derived result is a consent layer over a bounded correlate ([§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md)) and the audit digest already lives it, so the honest move is not a new principle but to **name the application** (glossary term + matrix rule) and **close the two docs that visibly violated it**. This is the third member of the check-honesty family beside [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) (the §17 behavioral correlate on the Review record) and [D-256](0256-authorize-the-every-hard-check-is-proven-to-bite-re-litigati.md) (the negative-fixture meta-check), and it cross-links [Negative fixture](../reference/glossary.md). The focused three-lens gate ([D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md)/[D-245](0245-resolve-re-lock-state-the-milestone-honest-bound-first-carri.md)) earned its keep: the architect lens caught the over-attribution of fitness to audits (a real seam error against the locked [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) function-probe scope), and the operator lens caught the "authored-content review" jargon that would have handed the non-engineer an impossible step — both folded. Landing design-side now while naming the consumption-side build-owe honestly ([R34](../reference/risks.md)) is the [§7](../principles.md) honest tier, not posture dressed as done.

## What we ruled out

**Mint a new principle (§20)** — rejected (the binding applies [§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md); the [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)/[D-256](0256-authorize-the-every-hard-check-is-proven-to-bite-re-litigati.md)/[R6](../reference/risks.md)/contract-threshold precedent keeps principles exceptional; the operator's standing-vehicle call). **Leave the two design-doc gaps to open-questions + a build-owe** — rejected (each is a one-clause description that visibly contradicts the new discipline; closing them now is proportionate). **Mass-re-litigate every artifact doc for consumption-side warrants** — rejected (disproportionate; the strong docs embody the bound design-side, and consumption-side surfacing is build-owe, not a design rewrite). **Attribute general module fitness to audits** — rejected (the [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) function-probe is scoped to the retire-candidate question; claiming general fitness mis-states the locked seam). **Name an operator "authored-content review" step for governance-field rightness** — rejected (the non-engineer cannot perform it; [§17](../principles.md) part-3 must name a real correlate or honestly name the residual as an authoring-time judgment, which the landed clause does). **Run the full four/five-lens audit** — rejected (bounded clause additions to two locked docs; the focused three-lens is the [D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md)/[D-245](0245-resolve-re-lock-state-the-milestone-honest-bound-first-carri.md) proportionate gate).
