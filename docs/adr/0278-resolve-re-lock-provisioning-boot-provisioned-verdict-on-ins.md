## Why

the coupled re-lock lands here after the landed-text audit because provisioning and boot together carry one contract (the verdict mechanism + its operator surfacing). The audit earned its keep: the faithfulness lens caught the "restored" history-narration in two carriers before the irreversible lock; the operator lens caught two §7-honesty edges in the locked onboarding string; and the adversarial+feasibility lens ground-truthed the bootstrap-gap, botched-case-circularity, and #323-gate-direction questions against the actual build, confirming the design is buildable and correctly directed. The three structural-invariant confirmations (boot Six-as-new-class, provisioning two-broken-states intact, S8 untouched) held the blast radius exactly where D-277 scoped it. The authorize→resolve-after-landed-text-audit split kept the re-lock behind its own clean gate ([D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)/[D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)).
