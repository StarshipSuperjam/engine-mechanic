# Add the foundational eADR canon: the Engine ships its own *why* (litigation alarm + authorization)

*Decided 2026-06-04 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Establish that the engine-template ships a bounded **foundational eADR canon** — the Engine's own design rationale, carried inside the Engine as committed [contract](../spec/systems/surfaces/contracts.md) instances — closing the gap that the eADR surface ships **empty** while the rationale for the Engine's structural laws lives only in this `engine-planning` workspace, which **a deployed Engine's builders cannot reach** (a cold session, the [engine-mechanic](../spec/systems/lifecycle/external-contribution.md) building version N+1 in a separate repo, or another adopter). Without a shipped rationale a builder retools a structural law blind; this is the missing prerequisite [Q31](../reference/open-questions.md) assumes. **This entry is the litigation alarm + authorization**; it authorizes editing the locked foundations below, and the **re-locks land under the resolve entry** after the mandatory cold audit on the landed text — the [D-167](0167-take-up-q17-component-a-authorize-a-five-foundation-re-litig.md)→[D-168](0168-resolve-the-d-167-operator-policy-override-re-litigation-lan.md) pattern. A five-lens cold **plan**-audit (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect · cognitive-substrate) reworked the design before this authorization; what is authorized is the audited design.
  **Design (final, post-plan-audit):**
  - **Grain — law-selected (~29 candidate laws), not 1:1 with the log, not one-per-doc.** The canon distills the **founding** structural decisions (cross-cutting invariants + system-defining charters) per the criteria in wbs/eadr-canon-manifest.md: an Engine structural law + clears the [contract-threshold](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md) + not a reconciliation/re-lock. Membership is operator-approved up front; the manifest is the build slice's map.
  - **Pure decisions, not specifications** — each eADR records the ratifying decision + rationale + anti-choice + significance, never a current-state narrative (holds [D-045](0045-re-lock-contracts-a-specification-is-a-doc-nature-not-a-cata.md)). The *what* stays the derived self-map; the *why* is the eADR.
  - **Canon-update rule** — a law changes only by **supersession** (a new linked eADR, never an edit); a reconciliation/re-lock **folds into** the law's eADR, never spawns one. The set moves only on rare, gated re-litigation of a structural law — churn, not count, is the bound; this is the [§13](../principles.md)-walled + threshold-gated answer to ADR explosion.
  - **Template-owned via path separation** — foundational eADRs are **engine-owned**, overlaid wholesale on upgrade (escalate-upstream); instance-authored eADRs are **deployment-owned**, preserved across overlay. This rides the [D-168](0168-resolve-the-d-167-operator-policy-override-re-litigation-lan.md) preserved-vs-overlaid partition ([repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) law 5, "configuration is not data") decided **by path**, so **no new grammar field** is invented.
  - **On-demand, no forward edge** — foundational eADRs are reached by the per-prompt scent / search / direct committed-file read, **never** pushed into the cold-start boot pack; **no forward `ratifies` edge is derived** (it would bloat [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md)'s structural-neighbours budget, need an undefined derivation rule, and instantiate the [R8](../reference/risks.md) hub condition — all dropped). Reverse-citation supplies linkage without fan-out. KG generation is therefore **unchanged** (entity-per-eADR, supersession links), and the canon **degrades to git-native** (committed files readable before the index regenerates).
  - **A build slice, not a module** ([D-093](0093-cut-the-adr-discipline-module-vestigial-its-content-already.md) stands) — the canon is shipped *content* on `core`'s existing contracts surface; it adds no module/surface/friction mechanism. It is authored by a dedicated **post-M1** WBS slice (the structural systems must be built/locked first; the canon is **not** in the M1 self-construction threshold, so [D-101](0101-pin-the-stage-0-self-construction-threshold-to-a-concrete-mo.md)/[D-107](0107-author-the-wbs-module-build-order-the-builder-crossover-reso.md) stand), executed in `engine-template` **before the planning→engine handoff**.
  - **Two gates** — a planning-layer **transplant gate** (the canon must be complete + cold-audited before the workspace hands off; [§17](../principles.md) evidence) and a build-time **full multi-agent stress-test workflow** on the final authored set before the canon PR merges into `engine-template`, witnessed in the PR-body contract (the operator's added closure).
  - **New principle [§18](../principles.md)** — the Engine carries its own *why*; design rationale is a shipped, durable artifact.
  **Authorized re-lock set (land under the resolve entry after the landed-text audit):** [contracts](../spec/systems/surfaces/contracts.md) (the canon grammar) and [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md) (reconcile "accumulate per-instance"). **Assess; re-lock only if text changes:** [provisioning](../spec/systems/infrastructure/provisioning.md) (the eADR path lanes), [attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md) (canon off the cold-start pack), [knowledge](../spec/systems/cognitive/knowledge.md) (generation unchanged), [templates](../spec/systems/guardrails/templates.md)+schema, [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) (the maintainer canon slice stays out of deployed-Engine build scope). **Non-locked propagation:** [principles §18](../principles.md); wbs/eadr-canon-manifest.md + the post-M1 slice and transplant gate in wbs/; CLAUDE.md scaffold/handoff note; [glossary](../reference/glossary.md); [engine-architecture](../architecture.md); [risks](../reference/risks.md) (close the rationale-loss risk; [R8](../reference/risks.md) not instantiated); [open-questions](../reference/open-questions.md) (Q31 prerequisite met); a [D-093](0093-cut-the-adr-discipline-module-vestigial-its-content-already.md) reconciliation note. `python3 validate.py` green at each step.

## Why

The Engine self-describes its *what* by derivation ([§3](../principles.md)), but a rationale and its rejected alternative are authored judgments that cannot be derived, so the *why* must be carried — and a deployed Engine is a standalone artifact whose builders never had this workspace. Seating the canon **now** is cheapest: the foundations are mostly unbuilt and there are zero deployments to migrate (the [D-167](0167-take-up-q17-component-a-authorize-a-five-foundation-re-litig.md) timing argument). It is a justified re-litigation, not silent drift — the lock stops drift, not warranted edits — and the five-lens cold plan-audit reworked the design before authorization (dropping the ratifies edge, choosing path-separation over a new marker field, reframing the justification off workspace-retirement, holding the specification-is-not-a-contract line, and bounding the canon by the criteria + update-rule). The path-separation rides the freshly-decided [D-168](0168-resolve-the-d-167-operator-policy-override-re-litigation-lan.md) preserved-vs-overlaid partition, so the provisioning touch is an application of an existing law, not a new one.

## What we ruled out

**Ship the eADR surface empty (status quo)** (rejected — the Engine then ships amnesiac about itself; the rationale dies with the unreachable workspace and Q31 has no substrate to check against). **Derive the canon at boot from the shipped decision-log** (rejected — the decision-log is planning-layer and never ships; there is nothing in a deployed Engine to derive from — which is *why* a transplant is needed). **A full 1:1 distillation of all ~168 decisions** (rejected — layer-conflation of planning history with Engine law, and the prototype's explosion by another name; the criteria select ~29 founding structural laws). **Resurrect an `adr-discipline` module to own the canon** (rejected — [D-093](0093-cut-the-adr-discipline-module-vestigial-its-content-already.md) stands; the canon is content on the existing surface, no module/surface/friction added). **A frontmatter `foundational:` marker field for the overlay** (rejected — path-separation is decidable by the overlay's existing path mechanism and rides [D-168](0168-resolve-the-d-167-operator-policy-override-re-litigation-lan.md)'s partition; a content field would force the overlay to parse files). **Derive a forward `ratifies` edge** (rejected — cold-start bloat, undefined derivation rule, R8 hub condition; reverse-citation suffices). **Author the canon pre-M1 / fold it into core's build** (rejected — it is not needed for self-construction; post-M1 placement keeps the M1 threshold [D-101](0101-pin-the-stage-0-self-construction-threshold-to-a-concrete-mo.md)/[D-107](0107-author-the-wbs-module-build-order-the-builder-crossover-reso.md) truthful). **Defer the whole thing post-v1** (rejected by the operator — the gap is present in every deployment and cheapest to seat before any deployment exists).
