# Litigate engine-template #276: make the Explore→Build switch on plan-acceptance legible to the assistant; correct the falsified "a `PostToolUse` hook cannot inject conversational text" claim; re-litigate locked [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) (+ [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md)) — coupled re-lock lands under D-271

*Decided 2026-06-28 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Under the litigation-alarm protocol (operator-authorized this session over a **four-lens cold plan-audit**) and following the [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)→[D-180](0180-resolve-the-d-179-build-entry-augment-landed-text-cold-audit.md) precedent, **re-litigate locked [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md)** to close the legibility gap engine-template **#276** surfaced, and **correct the falsified platform claim** the locked design rests on. **This entry is the litigation alarm + operator authorization + the design of record**; the **coupled re-lock lands under [D-271](0271-resolve-the-d-270-plan-acceptance-legibility-augment-landed.md)** after the mandatory **landed-text cold audit** — the [D-262](0262-authorize-the-disposition-issue-resolution-check-a-17-7-mech.md)/[D-264](0264-authorize-git-native-retention-for-the-pre-migration-memory.md) authorize-then-resolve pattern, carriers **edited-but-not-relocked** until D-271 (the sanctioned transient — `validate.py` flags their lock fingerprints until the relock).

  **The defect (#276, engine-opened).** On plan-acceptance, the modes `PostToolUse(ExitPlanMode)` hook flips the session stance signal to Build (live-verified by the build session: with the real session id the signal reads `build`). But **the assistant is never told the flip happened** — its only stance information is boot's start-of-session briefing, which always says Explore (boot resets stance to Explore every session, [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md)). So the assistant acts on the stale "you are in Explore" belief: it re-ran the entry verb redundantly, and the same gap could make a session refuse a write it is allowed to make or **report the wrong stance to the operator** — a direct hit on the locked *"stance is always operator-legible"* guarantee. A second, **separable** defect (`modes.py stance` without `--session` silently returns the safe default `explore`) is **not** a litigation matter — the default-to-Explore is correct locked law; only the plain invocation's failure to auto-resolve the session id is wrong — and is routed as a build-side leaf fix.

  **The falsified claim (§17 gold standard — the [D-171](0171-correct-the-falsified-platform-ignores-a-pretooluse-deny-cla.md)/[D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md) pattern repeating).** [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)'s legibility bullet (:1344) rests modes' acceptance-path law on *"a `PostToolUse` hook cannot inject conversational text, so the acceptance path is announced by build-orchestration's announced kickoff … not by the hook."* Verified against current Claude Code this session: a `PostToolUse` hook's `hookSpecificOutput.additionalContext` **does reach the model** — *"injected as a system reminder that Claude reads as plain text"* (10,000-char cap, file+preview overflow), distinct from operator-facing chat. So the claim is **false**. **Surgical supersession (the [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)→[D-070](0070-lock-the-modes-system-wave-3-head-three-stances-on-two-axes.md) pattern):** this entry supersedes **only** that one sentence; every other [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)/[D-180](0180-resolve-the-d-179-build-entry-augment-landed-text-cold-audit.md) law stands — human-only entry, the discriminator is the `PostToolUse(ExitPlanMode)` *event* (not a `permission_mode` value), rejection fires no event, fail-safe-to-Explore ([D-180](0180-resolve-the-d-179-build-entry-augment-landed-text-cold-audit.md)'s added clause), no "stronger gate" over-claim, Routine untouched, [R11](../reference/risks.md) de-risk. The §17 banner is kept to **two separable acts**: **(a) the fact correction** — *forced* (a falsehood in a locked trust doc corrodes auditability, [§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md)); **(b) the mechanism augmentation** — a *design choice the falsified fact merely permits*, justified on its merits by the stated defect, authorized on those merits, not as compelled by (a).

  **The design (augment; final text + landed-text audit land under [D-271](0271-resolve-the-d-270-plan-acceptance-legibility-augment-landed.md)).**
  - **Set + inject.** The acceptance hook **sets the Build stance signal AND injects a terse assistant-internal stance directive** (a `PostToolUse` `additionalContext` system reminder) that names the new Build stance and directs the next turn into [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)'s announced kickoff. Push-not-pull (boot's §scent posture): don't rely on the model overriding its stale boot briefing from memory.
  - **Operator announcement stays build-orchestration's, exactly once.** The injected line is **assistant-facing machine context, not the operator announcement** — it *triggers* the kickoff, never replaces it. The operator still meets the entry through the kickoff ("*opening a draft pull request and planning the work*").
  - **Inert-by-construction on resume.** The injected line is a **turn-local directive, never a durable stance record**. Three laws make a replayed stale nudge inert (current Claude Code **replays** saved `additionalContext` on `--resume`/`--continue`, not re-runs it): the **signal is the sole durable record** (cleared at SessionStart → Explore) and the assistant **derives/reports stance from the live signal, never the line**; **boot's session-start stance line is authoritative** over any replayed nudge (resume re-renders it reliably via `source:"resume"`, [D-269](0269-litigate-q18-engine-template-313-resolve-cross-session-anti.md)/[constraints](../reference/constraints.md)); and the **kickoff is guarded by a live-signal re-read** — proceed only if the signal still reads Build. So a replayed nudge over a cleared signal triggers no write (the `PreToolUse` gate reads the cleared signal), no kickoff, no stance misreport.
  - **Do-not-relay + no marker.** The injected line carries **no operator-facing copy**, is **do-not-relay verbatim**, and carries **no imperative relay marker** (the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) operator-presentation-relay reserves that marker set for governance alarms / close-gate / grounding-failure) — closing the §12 jargon-leak and double-announce risks. It is governed by the §scent **push** posture but is a **directive, not the scent's attributed-and-unverified pointer** (no "verify before asserting" import).
  - **No partial-failure split-brain.** Signal = sole durable record, line = strictly advisory: signal-set-but-injection-lost → benign (operator still meets the kickoff); injection-delivered-but-signal-unset → inert (kickoff/writes re-check the signal → Explore → fail-safe).
  - **[D-171](0171-correct-the-falsified-platform-ignores-a-pretooluse-deny-cla.md) discipline.** The **durable principle** goes in the locked doc (*"the acceptance hook sets the stance signal and injects an assistant-internal stance directive that triggers the announced kickoff; the operator announcement stays build-orchestration's, exactly once; the signal is the sole durable record, authoritative over any replayed nudge; resume-safe; human-only; fail-safe to Explore"*); the **dated mechanism** (`PostToolUse` `additionalContext`, the 10,000-char cap, the resume-replay behavior) lives in the build-spec leaf + this log, cited to the current `code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks` reference, **re-verified at the slice build**.

  **Contemporaneous capture + named build-slice precondition.** The accept→flip behavior is live-confirmed **today** by #276 itself (the build session observed the flip this session), the contemporaneous capture superseding reliance on [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md)'s 2026-06-06 log. A doc-reading lens inferred PostToolUse does *not* fire on `ExitPlanMode` (PermissionRequest used instead); **rejected as over-inference** — the events are distinct and independently firing, `PermissionRequest` fires *before* the operator decides (so it cannot witness accept-vs-reject), and the live evidence contradicts it; PostToolUse remains the only clean accept discriminator. The **named blocking build-slice precondition** (D-179's false-fire conformance, extended): re-verify on current Claude Code that `PostToolUse` fires on `ExitPlanMode`-**accept** (not reject / not leave-without-approving / not resumed-subagent) and that `additionalContext` reaches the model; plus the two new conformance tests — a resumed session **neither acts nor reports** Build from the replayed line, and the Build-entry operator announcement is **present and singular**.

  **Carriers.** **Edited (locked; re-lock under [D-271](0271-resolve-the-d-270-plan-acceptance-legibility-augment-landed.md)):** **{[modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md)** (the acceptance-path "sets the stance signal and nothing else" paragraph rewritten to set+inject with the resume-safety, do-not-relay, and partial-failure laws; the plan-acceptance build-spec leaf gains the injection mechanism + the conformance set), **[hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md)** (the `PostToolUse` owner row's modes behavior widens from "set the Build stance" to "set the Build stance + inject the assistant-internal stance directive" — still non-blocking, block budget untouched)}**. **Verify-at-edit, recorded no-edit (the [D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md) discipline):** [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) + [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) *operator-presentation relay* (the injected nudge lands in the channel boot owns but is governed by the existing relay law — the assistant relays stance from the signal, the nudge is do-not-relay/no-marker, and boot's session-start stance line is authoritative — the authority stated in the **modes leaf**, the new mechanism's owner, so **boot needs no edit**; **escalation flag:** boot was re-locked under [D-269](0269-litigate-q18-engine-template-313-resolve-cross-session-anti.md) — if the landed-text audit finds a genuine boot edit, it becomes a coupled re-litigation surfaced to the operator, never silent); [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) (the kickoff owns the operator announcement and never claims the assistant learns stance only via the kickoff — no edit); [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md) (the `PostToolUse` wire's enumeration — validation · telemetry · modes — is **unchanged**; ## Why

A locked **trust** document that asserts as settled a platform fact the current platform disproves is the exact [§17](../principles.md) corrosion [D-171](0171-correct-the-falsified-platform-ignores-a-pretooluse-deny-cla.md)/[D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md) exist to catch — and here it stranded a real capability: the assistant could not learn its own stance flip, so it misreported stance / repeated ceremony, hitting the *operator-legible* guarantee head-on. The deterministic injection is the engine's own **push-not-pull** posture applied where it was previously (wrongly) believed impossible; it relies on no model discretion and is bounded at every seam (signal is the sole durable record; the merge wall remains the only unbypassable wall). The four-lens cold **plan**-audit reshaped the design before authorization and earned its keep: it converted a naïve "inject the stance" into the **inert-by-construction** resume model (the BLOCKING resume-replay finding — defense-in-depth had covered only *writes*, not the *report* and *phantom-kickoff* failure modes), added [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)/[glossary](../reference/glossary.md) as the missed relay carrier (BLOCKING), made the line **do-not-relay/announce-once** (SERIOUS double-announce + §12), specified the **partial-failure** safety (SERIOUS), separated the §17 fact correction from the mechanism choice (SERIOUS), and required the **contemporaneous** capture + named build-slice re-verify (SERIOUS). Keeping the durable principle in the locked doc and the dated mechanism in the leaf + log applies the [D-171](0171-correct-the-falsified-platform-ignores-a-pretooluse-deny-cla.md) lesson so a future platform rename cannot re-falsify a locked trust doc.

## What we ruled out

**Grounding-only — teach the kickoff/runbook "acceptance already flipped you," no lock change** (rejected — leans on the model overriding its stale boot briefing from memory, the exact pull-on-discretion the engine avoids, and leaves the falsified [D-179](0179-augment-interactive-build-entry-with-plan-acceptance-correct.md) claim standing in a locked trust doc, a §17/auditability hit; the operator chose re-litigation over this). **Have the hook do the operator announcement** (rejected — no reliable hook→operator channel, [constraints](../reference/constraints.md); build-orchestration's kickoff owns the operator announcement, exactly once). **Inject a durable "you are in Build" stance record** (rejected — resume-replay would resurrect a Build belief into an Explore-reset session; the line is a turn-local directive, the signal stays the durable record cleared at SessionStart). **Treat #276's `modes.py stance` footgun as a litigation matter** (rejected — the safe-default-to-Explore is correct locked law; only the session-id auto-resolution is a build-side leaf). **Adopt the "PostToolUse doesn't fire on ExitPlanMode" doc-inference** (rejected — over-inference contradicted by live evidence; the binding re-verify is the named build-slice precondition). **Bake the `PostToolUse`/`additionalContext` field names + cap into the locked docs** (rejected — dated, version-brittle; they live in the build-spec leaf + log, the [D-171](0171-correct-the-falsified-platform-ignores-a-pretooluse-deny-cla.md) discipline). **Edit the locked docs without the landed-text cold audit** (rejected — HARD lock gate; the audit + re-lock land under [D-271](0271-resolve-the-d-270-plan-acceptance-legibility-augment-landed.md)).
