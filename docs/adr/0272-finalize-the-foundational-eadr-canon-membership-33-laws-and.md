# Finalize the foundational eADR-canon membership (33 laws) and produce the litigated, stress-tested recommendation for engine-template

*No date was recorded for this decision in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Finalize the foundational [eADR](../spec/systems/surfaces/contracts.md) canon at **33 laws** (C1–C15, S1–S18) and author its **litigated recommendation** in wbs/eadr-canon/ — a derived, spec-driven distillation engine-template adjudicates before authoring its own `eADR-####` corpus ([principles §18](../principles.md)/[D-169](0169-add-the-foundational-eadr-canon-the-engine-ships-its-own-why.md)). Membership was settled by an **adversarial-bounded** multi-agent pass over the candidate ~29 (eadr-canon-manifest.md): **all four flagged merges rejected** (C2+C3, C15+S13, C14→S6, S14→S1 — each carries disjoint anti-choices; the seam is the content); **S3 split** into the one-workflow *integration* law (D-029) plus standalone charters S16 state (D-059), S17 attention (D-062/D-117), S18 boot (D-063), the knowledge-derived decision (D-060) folding into C2/S4; **S15 conduct added** (the operator-stance-floor surface [D-192](0192-authorize-the-conduct-surface-codes-of-conduct-a-tier-3-pros.md)/[D-195](0195-ratify-lock-the-conduct-surface-fresh-five-lens-cold-session.md), absent only because the map predated it); **C6 and C14 kept** over per-law exclusion arguments (C6's anti-choice — dressing posture as machine-enforced — is genuine; C14 re-sourced to its founding one-history decision [D-019](0019-authoring-grammar-locked-end-state-as-laws-not-leaves.md), with D-035/D-036 the sink refinement, since D-036 alone is a re-lock that never founded the law); **§20/[D-266](0266-new-principle-20-spec-conformance-is-the-standing-target-no.md) excluded** (maintainer build-apparatus that retires at v1; its deployed residue rides S10). The recommendation then **passed a full multi-agent stress-test** (faithful distillation with per-law source-quoting · completeness & boundary · cross-reference integrity · self-standing voice): **33/33 present, none smuggled, none missing, no [D-045](0045-re-lock-contracts-a-specification-is-a-doc-nature-not-a-cata.md) drift, no pairwise redundancy, the boundary holds**; all blocking (stale sibling-law cross-references from the S15–S18 renumber) and serious (voice leaks, provenance under-attribution, two body-drift items — C11 "copying-out", S7's invented anti-choice) findings resolved in-line, residual nits carried as adjudication guidance. **The recommendation is input to adjudication, not a drop-in corpus** — engine-template owns final membership, wording, ids, and the shipped body (provenance stripped, bodies self-standing); nothing was authored into, or deleted from, engine-template or this workspace's locked design here.

## Why

The canon must never become a junk drawer / ADR pile ([D-004](0004-establish-the-engine-planning-workspace-with-a-fixed-documen.md)/[D-169](0169-add-the-foundational-eadr-canon-the-engine-ships-its-own-why.md)) — the bound is **churn, not count** — so membership and the boundary needed genuine adversarial pressure, not a rubber-stamp of the candidate set. Authoring the litigated distillation in the workspace that holds every source decision front-loads the hardest work (faithful distillation) where the sources live, and hands engine-template a stress-tested source rather than a cold log to mine; the build session keeps the final adjudication, so the [§13](../principles.md) engine/product layer discipline holds (no engine-repo change originates here; the deliverable is a planning artifact a build session transplants). The stress-test earned its keep: it caught nine wrong sibling-law cross-references and a cluster of provenance under-attributions that, unfixed, would have misled a cold adjudicator about which decision a law distils.

## What we ruled out

**Rubber-stamp the candidate ~29** (rejected — the adversarial pass found a genuinely missing law (conduct/S15), a genuinely under-grained one (S3), and a mis-sourced one (C14); a stamp would have shipped all three). **Full re-derivation from the 168** (rejected by the operator — re-opens the cold-audited [D-169](0169-add-the-foundational-eadr-canon-the-engine-ships-its-own-why.md)/[D-170](0170-resolve-the-d-169-foundational-eadr-canon-re-litigation-land.md) triage for little gain; adversarial-bounded anchors on the vetted set while still breaking what it can). **Author the shipped eADR files here, or delete the staging set at handoff** (rejected by the operator — the deliverable is a litigated recommendation engine-template adjudicates and authors from, a permanent planning artifact, not shipped engine prose minted in or deleted from this workspace). **Merge any of the four flagged pairs** (rejected — convergent merge-adjudicator + boundary-architect verdicts: each pair's anti-choices are disjoint). **Keep S3 as one law** (rejected — six sources / five disjoint anti-choices cannot be one bounded pure decision without [D-045](0045-re-lock-contracts-a-specification-is-a-doc-nature-not-a-cata.md) multi-decision-summary drift; the integration eADR preserves the one-workflow invariant so the split contradicts nothing).
