# Collapse `command` into the `skill` surface: invocation is a governed axis, not a surface

*Decided 2026-05-25 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Under **explicit operator approval**, supersede the same-day [D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md) "keep two surfaces" judgment and **collapse the `command` surface into [skill](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md)**. There is now one in-session procedure surface whose **`invocation`** axis — {`model-auto` | `operator-typed` | `model-only`} — maps to the platform frontmatter (default / `disable-model-invocation: true` / `user-invocable: false`); the engine governs which value an instance takes, the instance carries the real platform fields. The former `command` is the **`operator-typed`** value, retaining its full governance (thin, delegating to an [operation](../spec/systems/surfaces/operations.md); engine-prefixed `/engine-…`; a plain-language **discovery path a committed v1 requirement**; **mode entry** the genuine v1 need, concrete verbs deferred to [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md)). Both rationing pressures survive on the one surface: model-invocable values are **attention-budgeted** (always-resident description; few/sharp; the three-part earns-a-skill bar), operator-typed carries **zero standing attention cost** and is rationed by **discoverability**. The `command` doc is **deleted**; the operator's word "command" survives as a **plain-language alias** in the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) (a verb you type, realized as an operator-typed skill), and `invocation`-axis vocabulary is maintainer-layer only — it never surfaces in operator-facing text. **Locked-doc handling:** the locked [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md) needs **no edit** (it locks laws, not catalog membership, and names no surface; its "surfaces attach additively" stance covers a membership reduction symmetrically — no re-lock). Three locked docs are reconciled: [operations](../spec/systems/surfaces/operations.md) and [docs](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md) ([D-054](0054-lock-the-operations-and-docs-surfaces-wave-1-partial.md)) and [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) ([D-049](0049-re-lock-topology-and-module-system-file-precise-codeowners-o.md)) carry `command` text → **re-locked** as faithful **naming reconciliations** (the who-invokes boundary meaning is preserved *as* the invocation axis; only the enumeration changes); [module-system](../spec/systems/grammar/module-system.md)'s "its commands under `.claude/commands/`" is an **illustration of the legacy path, not a surface-membership claim**, and **stands** (the legacy `.claude/commands/` form still works). This **locks [skill](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md)** to its unified end-state (a Wave-1 advance); `agents` and `tools` remain `designed`. It **revises the [D-052](0052-foundational-law-layer-closed-the-implementation-lock-order.md) Wave-1 runway** (drop `commands`; `skills` is the unified surface) and **amends the [D-042](0042-procedural-content-grounding-surface-cluster-designed-the-bo.md)/[D-043](0043-surface-set-completion-re-lock-ontology-and-hooks-to-clear-t.md) "surface set complete" milestone** (one fewer surface; no law changes).

## Why

The categorical-merge fact — *skill* is the platform primitive and *invocation* is a property of it — was already in [D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md), which kept two surfaces, judging the operator-typed-vs-auto distinction "real." That judgment is sound, but the split no longer **earns a separate surface**: it is the one `SKILL.md` mechanism, and modeling invocation as a surface forces an **asymmetry** — operator-typed gets its own surface while `model-only`, an equally distinct invocation state, is merely a skill flag. One axis makes all three modes symmetric values of one surface and mirrors the platform's own model (the native-substrate preference). **An earlier draft justified the collapse as refactor-runway avoidance (a third invocation mode would force a third surface). The cold-session design audit ([D-018](0018-cold-session-design-audit-required-before-any-lock.md)) disproved that: the invocation axis is closed at exactly three values (technical-feasibility verified firsthand against `code.claude.com/docs/en/skills` — `disable-model-invocation`, `user-invocable`, no fourth field), and a `model-only` skill already fits the `skill` surface as a flag, so keeping two surfaces forces no refactor. That justification is withdrawn as unsound;** the decision rests on **coherence/symmetry** — a deliberate operator choice that the engine grammar mirror the platform's reality — not on necessity. The four-lens audit (adversarial / technical-feasibility / non-engineer-operator / architect, no shared context) ran against the plan; its blocking/serious findings were resolved before this entry: **(adversarial + architect, blocking)** the alarm was under-scoped — [repository-topology](#) law 4 enumerated "Commands, agents, skills" as Claude-native surfaces, a falsified membership claim → topology added to the re-lock set ("agents, skills"); **(adversarial, blocking)** the refactor-runway argument was a re-read of D-053 with no new fact → conceded and withdrawn (above), the decision re-grounded on coherence and returned to the operator with accurate information before proceeding; **(operator, serious)** deleting the "Command" glossary entry would strand the operator's own word → kept as a plain-language alias; **(operator, serious)** "operator-typed-skill" is jargon in an operator-facing doc → the [docs](#) re-lock keeps the operator-facing text plain ("discover what commands exist"); **(architect, serious)** the [module-system](#) legacy-path illustration was adjudicated to stand, logged here. Technical-feasibility verified every invocation→frontmatter mapping and the closed three-value axis. The architect lens confirmed the meta-contract holds with **no schema/template re-lock** (the three modes are additive optional frontmatter booleans; one template scaffolds one `SKILL.md`; per-value governance is a `soft-warn` shape, not a structural fork), the module `provides`/`wires` seam is unaffected (a file drop regardless of invocation value), validation coherence orphans no `command` notion, and the collapse **reduces** [R6](../reference/risks.md) sprawl (the model case of laws-not-leaves).

## What we ruled out

Keep two surfaces per [D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md) (rejected by the operator — the split no longer earns a separate surface once invocation is one axis of three symmetric values; two surfaces leave `model-only` an asymmetric flag and diverge from the platform's merged primitive). Justify the collapse as refactor-runway avoidance (rejected — audit-disproved: the axis is closed at three and `model-only` already fits `skill`; superseding a same-day decision demands an honest reason, which is coherence, not necessity). Edit/re-lock the locked [ontology](#) (rejected — it locks laws not membership and names no surface; a membership reduction needs no ontology edit). Leave the locked [operations](#)/[docs](#)/[repository-topology](#) `command` references stale after deleting the surface (rejected — a stale reference in a locked doc to a deleted surface is the exact drift the lock mechanism forbids; faithful naming reconciliation + re-lock is the honest end-state). Re-lock [module-system](#) too (rejected — its "commands under `.claude/commands/`" is a true legacy-path illustration, not a surface claim; it stands, adjudication logged). Drop "Command" from the glossary (rejected — it is the operator's plain word; keep it findable as an alias pointing to the operator-typed skill).
