# Resolve the D-200 status-verb cold-start re-litigation: landed-text cold audit, skills + modules/core re-locked

*Decided 2026-06-15 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md) change-set is landed and **re-locked**. The mandatory landed-text cold audit ran with four independent no-shared-context lenses (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) on the as-written edits in [skills](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md) and [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md) plus the non-locked carriers ([glossary](../reference/glossary.md), [risks](../reference/risks.md)). **Outcome: non-engineer-operator and architect PASS clean; technical-feasibility independently CONFIRMED the platform premise against the live Claude Code docs and the as-built repo; the adversarial lens surfaced the substantive items, all resolved.** Re-lock set: **2**.

  **The audit + dispositions.**
  - **Technical-feasibility — CONFIRMED.** Independently verified against `code.claude.com/docs/en/skills.md` and `../engine-template` (origin/main): the live docs assert the *opposite* of the operator's observation (default skills are menu-listed, with no cold-start qualifier), so the design correctly trusts the operator's live test over the docs and does not overstate doc backing; `invocation:` is engine-owned and consumed by `engine_help.py` (lists by it) and enforced by `validators-core` (`skill_coherence_findings` / `skill_coherence_check.py`, incl. the operator-typed-without-the-flag self-election guard); `engine_status.py` is a standalone read-only CLI and the skill is a thin `uv run` wrapper, so the AI's relay path is the tool, independent of invocation mode; the flip to `operator-typed` + `disable-model-invocation: true` will list at cold start, stay in `/engine-help`, and pass coherence.
  - **Non-engineer-operator — PASS clean.** The fix restores the cold-start unfiltered view; the AI's plain-language auto-offer is preserved; no maintainer jargon reaches any operator-facing surface; the verb now behaves consistently with its siblings.
  - **Architect — PASS clean.** Propagation complete; the verify-no-edit list independently confirmed (no surviving "status verb is model-auto" claim anywhere outside the log); scope minimal/subtractive (no new field/capability/surface, [R6](../reference/risks.md)); retaining the `model-auto` category is coherent (the platform default, parallel to the zero-instance `model-only`, [D-087](0087-resolve-q7-v1-skill-membership-close-deviation-d2-the-wbs-de.md)); the skill↔tool seam is clean; re-lock scope exactly two.
  - **Adversarial — findings, all resolved.** **(B2, folded)** [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md) promised the relay's "offer status on a relevance match" cue relocates to the AI's standing context (since `disable-model-invocation` drops the skill description from context), but no edited doc stated it — now authored into the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) *Status verb* (the cue lives in the AI's standing context — the boot pack / the relay convention — not in the skill). **(S1, folded)** [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md) twice said "the three operator verbs" while its `provides` line lists five — status's flip makes the count load-bearing; corrected to "five". **(Feasibility NIT, folded)** the cold-start *mechanism* (description-loads-into-context) was stated as fact though the live docs do not document the menu timing — [skills](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md) reworded to present the operator-verified *behavior* with the mechanism as the likely cause, docs noted as silent. **(Architect N1, folded)** the `/engine-help` predicate parenthetical was clarified (the operator-invocable values; `model-only` is hidden).
  - **Rejected (logged).** **(S2)** the [deviations](../reference/prototype-deviations.md) D2 row enumerates a stale D-087-era 4-skill set — REJECTED as a [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md) fold: it is a pre-existing staleness from [D-187](0187-authorize-the-operator-presentation-relay-re-litigation-the.md)/[D-192](0192-authorize-the-conduct-surface-codes-of-conduct-a-tier-3-pros.md) (which added the status / conduct-authoring / policy-tuning verbs without updating the row), not introduced by [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md), and [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md) in fact makes the row's "zero `model-auto`" claim *true* again; full reconciliation of the v1 skill enumeration is out of [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md)'s scope ([R6](../reference/risks.md)) and is logged as a separate cleanup task, not buried. Remaining nits (the `operations` "model-auto-invoked" example now lacking a v1 referent; the dense `Status verb` glossary entry; a build-facing wbs enumeration) left as-is — harmless, the category survives, and the [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md) build-realization note covers the builder.

  **Re-lock set (2):** [skills](../spec/systems/surfaces/skills.md), [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md). **Re-lock:** `python3 lock.py --relock` run on both under this decision; `python3 validate.py` green. **Non-locked carriers edited:** [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) (*Skill* + *Status verb* + the relocated relay cue), [risks](../reference/risks.md) ([R19](../reference/risks.md)). **Verified-no-edit (logged):** [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md), [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md), [close](../spec/systems/lifecycle/close.md), [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md), [conduct](../spec/systems/surfaces/conduct.md), [operations](../spec/systems/surfaces/operations.md), [engine-architecture](../architecture.md), the `scenarios/` walkthroughs, [modules/product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md). **No [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) opened/resolved; no [deviations](../reference/prototype-deviations.md) entry** (the D2-row reconciliation is the logged separate task). Still **42 docs locked** (2 re-locked, none added). The engine-template **#102** build realization (flip the SKILL.md to `operator-typed` + `disable-model-invocation: true`; relocate the AI's status-relay cue to standing context; re-verify cold-start visibility) is the downstream build-owes, not authored here.

## Why

The lock gate requires a landed-text cold audit with every blocking/serious finding resolved or rejected-with-rationale before re-lock; it ran (four lenses), feasibility independently confirmed the platform premise, operator and architect passed clean, and the adversarial findings were folded (B2/S1, plus the feasibility + architect nits) or logged-and-rejected (S2, out of scope). The design is faithful to [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md): the status verb is `operator-typed` (cold-start typeable), the AI relays the same dashboard by running the tool directly with its cue in standing context, the `invocation:` field is retained (engine-consumed), and v1 ships zero `model-auto` skills (the category survives for a future module).

## What we ruled out

**Re-lock without the landed-text audit** (rejected — HARD lock gate). **Treat the mid-litigation `validate.py` lock-FAIL as a defect** (rejected — it is the expected state between the [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md) edits and this re-lock). **Fold the deviations D2-row reconciliation into this decision** (rejected — pre-existing [D-187](0187-authorize-the-operator-presentation-relay-re-litigation-the.md)/[D-192](0192-authorize-the-conduct-surface-codes-of-conduct-a-tier-3-pros.md) staleness, out of [D-200](0200-authorize-the-status-verb-cold-start-re-litigation-model-aut.md) scope per [R6](../reference/risks.md); logged as a separate task, not buried). **Re-lock [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)/[modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md)** (rejected — they reference the status verb generically, which survives; verify-no-edit).
