# Proposal and prototype are reference inputs, not gospel

*Decided 2026-05-22 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Treat both the original proposal and the prototype as inputs to mine. Optimize the design for the north star, keeping each one's wins and rejecting its detriments.

## Why

Neither is correct by authority; the proposal predates implementation lessons, and the prototype over-built. The goal is the arbiter.

## What we ruled out

Adopt the proposal as the spec, or adopt the prototype as the spec — both rejected as appeals to authority over fitness.
