# Lock the `close` system (Wave 3): the turn-`Stop` ritual — honest-tier finding-disposition gate over an ephemeral record

*Decided 2026-05-26 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Ratify and lock [close](../spec/systems/lifecycle/close.md) — the per-turn `Stop` ritual — to its end-state, the **second Wave-3 lock** on the [D-052](0052-foundational-law-layer-closed-the-implementation-lock-order.md) runway. Close owns exactly two `Stop` duties: **ambient [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) capture** (memory's mechanism; close only triggers it) and the **finding-disposition gate**; it dissolves the prototype's heavy close (no archive/changelog/`CLOSE_ALLOWED_GLOBS`/partial-close recovery — [D-038](0038-session-lifecycle-re-founded-on-native-substrates.md)). Every reference to close across the **23 locked docs is a deferral *to* close** (grep-verified — [hooks](../spec/systems/infrastructure/hooks.md) owns the `Stop` slot + the findings-disposition block-budget member registered by close; [policies](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md) the finding-disposition policy the gate enforces; [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md)/[validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) the `pre-close` check; [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) the ambient append + boot sweep; [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) PR-contract filling), so the lock edits **no locked doc** (the [D-063](0063-lock-the-boot-orientation-system-wave-2-terminal-the-integra.md)/[D-064](0064-lock-the-interfaces-surface-final-wave-2-lock-single-active.md) pattern) and its only `designed` neighbor, [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md), depends on close. The fixed laws: **(1) the finding-disposition gate is "posture plus a strong local block"** (locked [policies](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md)' exact tier) — **mechanical** on undispositioned entries in an **ephemeral, session-scoped findings record** (a session-runtime checklist the `Stop` hook reads, off-repo, never read across sessions — **never a committed/gitignored ledger**, which would resurrect the dissolved session archive [D-038](0038-session-lifecycle-re-founded-on-native-substrates.md) and duplicate [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md)'s open-Issues debt register [D-040](0040-telemetry-designed-end-state-native-signal-of-record-tracked.md)); **posture** for recording a raised finding (the gate's reach is the recorded subset; an unrecorded concern can slip); **the merge is the wall**, but only for findings that touch the change under review — a diffless unrecorded finding is covered only by the recording posture + boot re-surfacing, stated not papered over; the operator promise is "*everything I flagged this turn is handled*", never "nothing the engine noticed is dropped"; **(2) cap-exhaustion degrades a recorded finding to *logged*, never *lost*** — on the forced continuation (`stop_hook_active`) a still-undispositioned recorded finding is emitted down the out-of-band "log it" promotion path, never re-entered into the gate (no deadlock); **(3) the gate fails open** (hooks fail-open-and-flag) — a gate that cannot evaluate lets the turn end, auto-logs itself as a finding via the promotion path (never through the broken gate), surfaced to the operator in plain language **that same turn**; **(4) ambient capture survives an ungraceful exit** — content is the per-`Stop` append, deferred consolidation is recovered by memory's boot-time session-id sweep, never the best-effort `SessionEnd`; **(5) routine** satisfies the gate non-interactively via the log-it disposition (halt-vs-continue is [policies](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md)'/[build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)'s, not close's); **(6) subagent** ambient findings are swept by the parent `Stop` (`SubagentStop` unbound), distinct from review-lens findings dispositioned at build-orchestration's gates via the output contract. session-close (the PR submitted) is a pure deferral to build-orchestration. A **Build-spec leaves** section holds the findings-record representation and the disposition-summary wording; a [§12](../principles.md) leak-guard fences the maintainer vocabulary. Close is **pure core, no slot/filling** ([D-069](0069-core-module-seam-walk-the-demarcation-operationalized-glossa.md)).

## Why

Close is the correct second Wave-3 lock: all its upstreams are locked (memory, hooks, policies, modes, control-plane, boot) and its only churning neighbor is downstream (build-orchestration), reduced to a deferral. **Two** four-lens cold-session design audits ([D-018](0018-cold-session-design-audit-required-before-any-lock.md)) ran — one against the plan, one against the **as-written doc** (the [D-071](0071-re-lock-modes-doc-level-cold-audit-corrects-an-as-written-re.md) discipline: a lock fingerprints the authored text, which the plan audit cannot see). The plan audit reshaped the design: the architect caught that a *persisted* findings record would re-litigate locked [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md) [D-040](0040-telemetry-designed-end-state-native-signal-of-record-tracked.md)/[D-038](0038-session-lifecycle-re-founded-on-native-substrates.md) (→ reframed ephemeral/non-canonical, the [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) stance-signal pattern); the adversarial caught the self-referential fail-open deadlock (→ promotion path, never gate re-entry), the cap-exhaustion leak (→ degrade-to-logged), and the routine halt-vs-continue contradiction with build-orchestration (→ deferred); the operator caught the promise-vs-mechanism gap (→ "flagged this turn is handled"). The as-written audit then caught prose the plan audit could not see: the cap-exhaustion auto-log was asserted without an owning mechanism and not proven deadlock-free (→ attributed to the out-of-band promotion path + a no-re-entry guard); the operator-facing summary leaked "dispositioned/issue #N" jargon against the doc's own leak guard (→ plain language); the `SessionEnd` "platform may skip" asserted an unverifiable platform fact (→ sourced to the locked hooks law); and "discarded with the session" implied an OS-temp deletion the platform does not guarantee (→ "never read across sessions", honest like modes). Technical-feasibility verified every platform claim live: `Stop` blocks via exit 2 / `decision:block` feeding the reason back, `stop_hook_active` makes the cap-stop auto-log implementable, exit-2-only blocking confirms fail-open, `SubagentStop` is a distinct event, and no hook receives structured findings (so the self-maintained-record honest tier is the ceiling). Propagation per the matrix: **no target edit needed** — [scenarios/build-session.md](../architecture.md#build-session-lifecycle), [scenarios/routine-session.md](../architecture.md#routine-session), [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) ("Deferred consolidation" already cites the boot sweep), and [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) (close is index-only) already describe the reworked shape; [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) needs none (R3/R6 reference finding-disposition generically). [R6](../reference/risks.md) firmed, not closed.

## What we ruled out

Persist the findings record as a committed/gitignored ledger (rejected — re-litigates locked [telemetry](../spec/systems/guardrails/telemetry.md) [D-040](0040-telemetry-designed-end-state-native-signal-of-record-tracked.md) and rebuilds the dissolved session archive [D-038](0038-session-lifecycle-re-founded-on-native-substrates.md); the record is ephemeral session-runtime state, the [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) pattern). Claim the gate mechanically catches every concern / "nothing dies silently" (rejected — a deterministic `Stop` hook cannot see an unrecorded concern; the honest tier is mechanical-on-recorded + posture-for-recording + merge-for-diffs, matching locked [policies](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md)). Route the cap-stop or fail-open finding back through the gate (rejected — deadlocks the loop; both use the out-of-band promotion path). Have close own halt-vs-continue for routine (rejected — that is [policies](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md)'/[build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)'s; close only guarantees non-interactive satisfiability). Credit `SessionEnd` with recovery (rejected — locked hooks treats it as best-effort/non-guaranteed; memory's boot sweep recovers). Co-define session-close (rejected — build-orchestration owns the PR-is-claim-record-close; describing it twice would force a close edit at build-orchestration's later lock, the [D-070](0070-lock-the-modes-system-wave-3-head-three-stances-on-two-axes.md)/[D-071](0071-re-lock-modes-doc-level-cold-audit-corrects-an-as-written-re.md) lesson). Lock on the plan audit alone (rejected — the [D-071](0071-re-lock-modes-doc-level-cold-audit-corrects-an-as-written-re.md) discipline: the as-written text gets its own cold audit, which here caught four real prose defects). Sweep review-lens findings at the parent `Stop` (rejected — they are dispositioned at build-orchestration's gates via the output contract; only ambient subagent findings sweep to `Stop`).
