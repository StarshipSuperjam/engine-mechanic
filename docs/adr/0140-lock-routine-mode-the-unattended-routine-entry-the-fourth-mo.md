## Why

`routine-mode` is the last required-spine L2 lock — upstream-anchored on the locked `core`, no dependents, build-readiness met. The cold audit did its job: three lenses validated the design's seams against the locked sources, while the technical lens — ground-truthing against the live platform rather than against the [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md) document the other lenses (correctly, for their remits) relied on — caught two real platform-fact slips that would otherwise have been locked into the permanent record. That divergence is exactly why the technical-feasibility lens is a mandatory voice: a platform fact verified against a doc is doc-inference, not ground truth. The errors were remediable in the `designed` doc without touching a locked doc (the locked neighbors framed the posture correctly), so the fix is a designed-doc reword + a superseding log entry, not a re-litigation. Narrowing the `wires: none` conclusion to "never `routine-mode`'s to wire" is both more accurate (robust to whichever concrete mode build-orchestration's leaf selects) and faithful to [D-088](0088-justified-re-litigation-name-the-routine-entry-command-engin.md)'s own logged reasoning.

a newer entry supersedes, never an in-place edit). **Name the concrete mode (`dontAsk`/`auto`) in routine-mode** (rejected — the concrete permission configuration is [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)'s build-spec leaf [:380–381];
