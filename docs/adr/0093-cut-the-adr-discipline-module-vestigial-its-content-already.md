# Cut the `adr-discipline` module: vestigial, its content already shipped by `core` + `validators-core`

*Decided 2026-05-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

**Cut** the `adr-discipline` module (delete the stub doc; remove its catalog row, both graph edges, and its name from `engine-knowledge-graph`'s `depends`; remove its carve-out bullet from [`core`](../spec/modules/core.md)). It was a `required` package whose carve-out ([core](../spec/modules/core.md) named "the eADR decision-record stream + the contract-threshold *application*"), but every job it could own already ships elsewhere, so it is **vestigial** — a package label, not a function. The **ADR-proliferation friction the operator values is not lost**: it survives intact in `core`. Specifically: the **eADR-#### shape/schema/template/index** is the locked [contracts](../spec/systems/surfaces/contracts.md) surface (core grammar); the **friction itself** (the bar + the significance/anti-choice `hard-fail` + the `soft-warn` contract-rate signal) is the **contract-threshold policy**, a v1-core [policy](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md) riding `core` whose locked text already "stops ADR over-production"; the **capture trigger** is the build/close-flow gate ([D-087](0087-resolve-q7-v1-skill-membership-close-deviation-d2-the-wbs-de.md) chose this over a model-invoked skill); the **conformance presence checks** are [`validators-core`](../spec/modules/validators-core.md) ([D-090](0090-flesh-the-validators-core-module-doc-to-designed-the-engine.md)); and the committed **eADR instances** accumulate per-instance on `core`'s contracts surface exactly as knowledge entities do. It **fails the [Required-package rule](../reference/glossary.md)** ([D-086](0086-cognitive-foundations-as-required-packages-reconciliation-me.md)): it owns no gitignored non-regenerable store needing a migration unit, and it exposes no bound seam — the lone inbound edge (`engine-knowledge-graph → adr-discipline`) binds to the eADR entities the knowledge generator derives from `core`'s contracts surface, not to anything `adr-discipline` provides, so it repoints cleanly to `core` (already a transitive dependency via `validators-core` and `memory-substrate`).

## Why

This pass was charged ([D-092](0092-flesh-the-routine-mode-module-doc-to-designed-the-operator-e.md)-sibling plan) to resolve `adr-discipline` honestly — **author OR cut** — never pre-deciding. A **two-lens cold plan-audit with deliberately opposing charges** ran (plan inlined): one lens charged to argue the module vestigial, one charged to **steelman the strongest non-empty job**. Both converged on **cut**: the vestigial lens traced every candidate job to its real home (above); the steelman, charged to defend the package, found only one defensible artifact — an audit-library-style *delivery* of an eADR-authoring/adjudication **operation** — and judged even that "real but thin, weaker than the cut argument," because it fails both decisive Required-package tests, rests on a single possibly-foldable operation (a single-referrer non-human-in-loop operation is a fold-or-retire candidate under the locked [operations](../spec/systems/surfaces/operations.md) anti-sprawl bar), and the close-flow is its natural home per [D-087](0087-resolve-q7-v1-skill-membership-close-deviation-d2-the-wbs-de.md). The **operator was presented the verdict and the one real alternative** (cut vs. author a thin delivery module embodying the friction as a legible, tunable runbook) — flagged because the operator had described `adr-discipline` as the inspiration for this workspace's lock discipline, so the choice hinged on intent, not mechanics — and **chose to cut**, the friction staying in `core`. Propagation per the matrix + the deletion mandate: [modules/adr-discipline/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) deleted; [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) catalog row + the `core → adr-discipline` and `adr-discipline → engine-knowledge-graph` graph edges removed and `engine-knowledge-graph`'s `depends` repointed (now `validators-core, memory-substrate-sqlite-fts5`); [modules/core/README.md](../spec/modules/core.md) carve-out bullet removed and the eADR stream + contract-threshold application folded into its "what stays in core" spine (the contracts surface grammar + the contract-threshold policy + the build/close gate). Verified no-change with rationale: [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md) (`Required package` already exemplifies via memory/knowledge; `Contract`/`Policy`/contract-threshold terms unaffected — the *capability* is unchanged, only its packaging), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) (indexes systems, not modules; adr-discipline was never a foundation, §4 unaffected; §9 indexes via the catalog), wbs/README.md (unauthored), the locked [contracts](../spec/systems/surfaces/contracts.md)/[policies](../spec/systems/surfaces/policies.md)/[validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md) (already own the work — untouched). The historical [D-089](0089-flesh-the-core-module-doc-to-designed-the-kernel-partition-t.md) text naming `adr-discipline` as a sibling carve-out is **left intact** (append-only history; this entry supersedes that packaging aspect). No locked system doc edited.

## What we ruled out

**Author a thin `adr-discipline` delivery module** on the audit-library precedent (an eADR-authoring/adjudication operation as the legible embodiment of the friction) — rejected (operator's choice + the steelman's own assessment): it fails both Required-package structural tests (no owned migratable store, no bound seam), its single artifact is a fold-or-retire candidate that belongs in the close flow per [D-087](0087-resolve-q7-v1-skill-membership-close-deviation-d2-the-wbs-de.md), and the friction is already delivered by `core`, so a separate doc would manufacture redundancy against the deletion mandate and [R6](../reference/risks.md). **Author it as a packaging view anyway because `core` names the carve-out** (rejected — a carve-out line is not a function; `core`'s line was reconciled to truth by folding the eADR stream into core's spine, the honest current state). **Keep the stub indefinitely** (rejected — a held slot for a package with no non-overlapping content is exactly the scope-creep R6 and the deletion mandate forbid). **Drop the friction along with the module** (rejected and impossible — the friction is the contract-threshold policy + the gate + the checks, none of which is `adr-discipline`'s; the operator's valued capability is fully preserved).
