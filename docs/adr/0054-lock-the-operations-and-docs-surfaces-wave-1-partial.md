# Lock the `operations` and `docs` surfaces (Wave 1 partial)

*Decided 2026-05-25 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Ratify and lock two Wave-1 surfaces to their designed end-state: [operations](../spec/systems/surfaces/operations.md) (the procedural-body surface — the authoritative reading-and-following runbook a command/skill/agent enters, governed by the *one procedure, one home* boundary law and an audit-judged anti-sprawl bar) and [docs](../spec/systems/surfaces/docs.md) (the hand-authored operator-facing explanation surface, with the named v1 orientation-doc floor). These are **two independent Wave-1 surface locks ratified in one pass** — *not* a [D-043](0043-surface-set-completion-re-lock-ontology-and-hooks-to-clear-t.md)/[D-044](0044-re-lock-validation-and-check-a-check-kind-binds-by-presence.md)/[D-049](0049-re-lock-topology-and-module-system-file-precise-codeowners-o.md)-style indivisible reconciliation: the two share no seam with each other, and [D-052](0052-foundational-law-layer-closed-the-implementation-lock-order.md)'s runway makes intra-wave order soft (genuinely parallel), so batching them is a convenience the runway permits, not a coupling. Both anchor entirely on the **locked** grammar (ontology, schemas, templates, check) and lock additively against the locked [ontology](../spec/systems/grammar/ontology.md) catalog (no ontology edit, no re-lock — the ontology's own "surfaces attach additively" clause). Two pre-lock neutralizations were applied (the [D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md) tools→interfaces precedent) so neither doc freezes a claim about a still-`designed` neighbour: operations' anti-sprawl clause now defers *how* the audits layer reaches its judgment to the [audits](../spec/systems/guardrails/audits.md) surface (dropping the asserted "retirement-default posture" and probe-mechanism), and docs' v1-floor now defers the root-`CLAUDE.md` orientation-pointer *contract* to [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) (keeping only the floor guarantee — a committed orientation doc exists, so such a pointer can always resolve) and states the orientation doc is itself a **self-sufficient** command-discovery path (independent of any `/engine-help` verb [modes](../spec/systems/lifecycle/modes.md) may or may not author). The `core`-package `provides`-owner mapping for these surface definitions stays an **asserted-in-[D-042](0042-procedural-content-grounding-surface-cluster-designed-the-bo.md), deferred-to-the-`core`-pass forward obligation** (modules/core is a stub); neither locked doc body asserts it present-tense. This advances Wave 1; `commands`, `skills`, `tools`, and `agents` remain `designed` (the first three pending their own locks against the [D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md)-reconciled text, `agents` pending its output-contract schema, [D-046](0046-harness-comparative-mining-a-guardrail-integrity-gap-a-contr.md)).

## Why

Wave 1 is the unblocked head of the [D-052](0052-foundational-law-layer-closed-the-implementation-lock-order.md) runway (the Q10 gate blocks only cognitive locks), and `operations`/`docs` are the two surfaces a four-lens cold-session design audit ([D-018](0018-cold-session-design-audit-required-before-any-lock.md): adversarial / technical-feasibility / non-engineer-operator / architect, run per-surface) found clean and lockable now. The audit returned no blocking findings; its serious findings were resolved before this entry: the operations→audits posture freeze and the docs→boot pointer freeze were neutralized to deferrals (above); the one-event framing was corrected to "two independent locks" (architect lens); and the operations references to the freshly-[D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md)-churned `command`/`skill`/`agent` surfaces were adjudicated an **accepted, decided risk** — operations depends on them only at the *boundary-law (who-invokes)* level, which [D-042](0042-procedural-content-grounding-surface-cluster-designed-the-bo.md) settled and [D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md) explicitly preserved through the platform merge, so their later locks cannot force an operations change. Technical-feasibility verified both docs make no stale platform claim and that their `governing schema`/`template` meta-contract fields are consistent with the locked [schemas](../spec/systems/surfaces/schemas.md)/[templates](../spec/systems/guardrails/templates.md) surfaces. The operator lens confirmed the docs floor protects a non-engineer (self-sufficient discovery path; the self-audit-is-normal-hygiene inoculation).

## What we ruled out

Lock all five ready surfaces as one event (rejected — the prior audit found `commands`/`skills` drifted from the platform merge and `tools` dependent on `designed` interfaces; only these two are clean). Frame this as a [D-043](0043-surface-set-completion-re-lock-ontology-and-hooks-to-clear-t.md)-style indivisible reconciliation (rejected — false coupling; they share no seam, so the honest frame is two independent Wave-1 locks). Lock `operations` with its audits-posture attribution and `docs` with its boot-pointer assertion intact (rejected — freezes claims about `designed` neighbours, the lock-discipline failure the [D-053](0053-procedural-surfaces-reconciled-to-the-merged-claude-code-ski.md) deferral precedent exists to prevent). Defer both surfaces until the whole procedural cluster reconciles (rejected — these two carry no platform drift and make safe Wave-1 progress now). Edit the locked ontology to catalogue them (rejected — additive cataloguing needs no re-lock per [D-042](0042-procedural-content-grounding-surface-cluster-designed-the-bo.md)). Block on the `core` stub's `provides` mapping (rejected — it is a deferred forward obligation, not verifiable against a stub, and no locked body asserts it present-tense).
