## Further record

### (1) D-314's authorship premise is FALSE and is corrected here

D-314 ruled on *"wrong owner"* and justified it as *"the engine second-guessing an ordering **the operator locked**"* / *"they authored and accepted it"*. The locked mechanism says otherwise: [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md):145 — *"| Build-plan | committed `docs/spec/` … product-owned; **living** (re-sequences as work lands), validated for structure, **no lock gravity** |"* — and `:171-173`, where the operator *"accepts and `locks`"* the **spec** and the **engine** then *"decomposes the `locked` spec into a committed build-plan (ordered phases)"*. So the phase ordering is **engine-authored** from the operator's locked spec, it is **living**, and **nothing about it is locked**. The operator's authority is real but sits **upstream** (accepting and locking the spec) and **at selection** (*"Build this Issue" is the deliberate act that enters Build*). **The ruling is unchanged and its outcome stands, but its reason on the record is now:** the ordering is **the plan's, under build-orchestration and the build-plan** — not attention's — and attention does not re-rank it because **a deferred issue is a plan, not a cold session's context**, and [D-117](0117-q24-q27-2-re-litigation-the-attention-ranking-function-form.md)'s pinned five-category form has no slot for one. That reason needs no operator authorship and is what the landed text says. **This is the D-314 defect class recurring inside the D-314 fix** — an attachment to the wrong owner — caught by the over-claim-hunter lens; recording it is the point of [R35](../reference/risks.md).

### (2) A seventh site, inside the doc D-314 was about to re-lock

[attention](../spec/systems/cognitive/attention.md):25-27 listed the ranking function's **inputs** as *"state, knowledge adjacency, the telemetry debt register, and **the native git/GitHub work record**"* — the same unqualified four-kind term-of-art D-314 retired from `:50`, surviving on the **input** side. A cold builder reading it is told to read the whole record, finds Issues and Milestones, and has no partition for them: **the exact path that produced the measured dormant code**. Narrowed to the in-flight slice plus the merged pull requests whose bodies carry the recent decisions (`:49`'s own naming). Found by the adversarial lens; the other four missed it.

### (3) The `active GitHub Milestone` names a platform concept that does not exist — folded into this pass (operator-directed)

Verified against the live GitHub GraphQL schema and current docs by the feasibility lens: `MilestoneState` is **`OPEN | CLOSED`** only; `Milestone` exposes no `active`/`current`/`default` field; `Repository` offers only `milestone(number:)` and a `milestones` connection; [REST list-milestones](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/issues/milestones) returns an **array** and offers `sort=due_on|completeness` **precisely because several open Milestones are normal**. The design's existing hedge (`none set` when the project keeps none) covers the zero case and **not the common many-open case**. **The rule is now pinned, in [state](../spec/systems/cognitive/state.md), which owns the bound:** where a [product-design](../spec/modules/product-design.md) build-plan drives the build, the current Milestone is the **earliest open Milestone in the build-plan's phase order** (deterministic and engine-derivable — build-orchestration emits exactly those Milestones); where none does, the engine selects the **earliest open Milestone by due date and discloses that basis**, rendering the several-open case as the ambiguity it is rather than as a settled single truth ([§5](../principles.md)); `none set` when none are open. [boot](../spec/systems/lifecycle/boot.md) **relays** the bound and does not restate it ([§16](../principles.md)). This is a **pre-existing latent defect, not introduced by D-314** — it is the platform-level form of the same over-claim class, and it is exactly what [R35](../reference/risks.md) predicts a reading gate cannot catch.

### (4) The standing residual moves off R4 to its own register entry, [R35](../reference/risks.md)

D-314 parked *"a lock certifies that a design was reviewed, not that its prose was measured against a mechanism"* on [R4](../reference/risks.md). Wrong home: R4 is *"Attention left as hardcoded constants"* — attention-scoped — and it **closes** when [D-083](0083-re-litigate-attention-honesty-clarification-reference-time-a.md)'s calibration fixture lands, at which point a corpus-wide residual would evaporate unowned. **[R35](../reference/risks.md) is opened** with a scope that fits (any locked doc's prose may outrun its mechanism), a closure condition of its own (none currently foreseen — it is an accepted standing residual and the honest bound on what `locked` asserts), and the instruments that actually catch the class named: the dry-run, the **build sessions' conformance findings and litigation requests as first-class design input**, and a per-pass over-claim-hunter lens. **R4 is unchanged and stays open on its own [D-083](0083-re-litigate-attention-honesty-clarification-reference-time-a.md) terms.**

### (5) The ruling gains an operator-facing law (operator-directed)

D-314's holding was legible only in this log, which the operator does not read. [glossary](../reference/glossary.md)'s *Status verb* now commits that **the dashboard names what's-next's source in plain language** — it reads as *next up on the build you accepted*, never as a verdict on everything the operator has filed. Without it an operator who files five Issues, types the status verb and sees a build-plan item still reaches the false belief D-314's own anti-choice paid to avoid: that their five were *considered and ranked below* rather than *never ranked at all* ([§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md)).

### (6) `github-projects-sync`: the adjudication D-314 asked for comes back "edit-owed" — but the *label* moves, not the *attribution*

D-314 framed the conditional as *"if the attribution moves"*; that framing is too narrow and is corrected here. Attention **is** genuinely still the producer of the projected signal ([control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md):277 scopes the projection to the engine-labeled set; gps `:59-61` confines writes to its own fields on its own labeled items), so `:45`'s **attribution is true and stays**. What is false is the **label**: `:45`, `:73`, `:170` and `:214` call that field *"what's next"* — the phrase [glossary](../reference/glossary.md):110 now pins to a **different producer**. Two operator surfaces, one phrase, two referents — and the board is the **higher-risk** one by the module's own admission (`:143-144`: *"a non-engineer may come to read the board **instead of** the Issues"*), while `:56-57` puts the operator's own un-labeled backlog on that same board whenever auto-add is on. A *"what's next"* the engine never computes for those rows is D-314's rejected reading rendered on the surface most likely to mislead. **Re-sourcing is foreclosed** — gps is `depends: core` with *"no edge to any optional module"* (`:84`), so it cannot read product-design's build-plan. **Fix: the field is relabelled `ranked work`** across all four sites, matching the status verb's dashboard field of the same name, so the two operator surfaces say one thing; the §12 leak guard is satisfied for free (it is already operator-facing vocabulary). Producer unchanged, no new dependency, no design question opened.

D-314 reserved D-315/D-316/D-317; `validate.py` enforces **monotonic ids**, so this amendment cannot precede them. **Re-locks now land under: [D-316](0316-resolve-re-lock-attention-the-work-record-commission-retired.md) `attention` · [D-317](0317-resolve-re-lock-state-the-taxonomy-kept-its-attachment-narro.md) `state` · [D-318](0318-resolve-re-lock-github-projects-sync-the-board-s-engine-fiel.md) `github-projects-sync` · [D-319](0319-resolve-re-lock-boot-relay-state-s-milestone-selection-bound.md) `boot`.** Each doc re-locks **once**, carrying all of its fixes.
