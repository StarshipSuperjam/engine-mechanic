# Part-validation audit of the D-164 coupling pass: faithful + locked-doc-clean; seven mechanical fixes applied (incl. the scent-consumes-policy refinement)

*Decided 2026-06-03 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Ran the part-validation audit of the [D-164](0164-surgical-coupling-pass-on-the-core-build-roadmap-declare-cro.md) surgical coupling pass — re-designed per the operator's correction that **agents validate the PARTS of a built artifact, they do not author the whole** (the prior 3-proposer/reconcile workflow violated single-writer cohesion, [D-163](0163-correct-d-162-s-8-is-a-build-conformance-deviation-fix-the-b.md)). Twelve cold checkers (one per changed slice/contract + a set-coherence lens + an adversarial lens + synthesis), each fed the decision-log + module-order + the specific locked source, **calibrated hard against the prior over-tagging** (every finding must cite a source; pure-pointer thinness is not a defect; default to pass). **Verdict: the pass is faithful to the locked source and contradicts no locked doc** (all ten checker-parts returned `faithfulToSource: true` / `contradictsLockedDoc: false`; `validate.py` green). The calibration held — ~65 considered-and-rejected, **zero findings dropped for missing citations**, 6/10 parts zero-findings, and the two known-settled traps (`/engine-routine` ownership; the field-set "enumerated nowhere") were recognized as false by every checker that met them, **not re-raised**. Seven source-cited mechanical defects — all confined to the non-locked roadmap — were **ground-truthed by the orchestrator** and fixed: **S1** the attention output-contract's `owes →` wrongly named the scent 23, but the scent is attention at a *tiny budget* whose output is lexical matched-pointers, not the partition+budget result — refined so the **output contract** is owed to 20/24 and the **scent consumes slice 12's policy scent-threshold** (23 `owes ←` 12 for the *policy*, with 12 added to slice 23's deps); **S2** Slice SG's deps lacked slice 5 (its coherence-finding demo needs the `coherence` kind) → `1,2,3,5` per the Slice-16 precedent; **N1** slice 18's `owes →` gained the 24 leg; **N2** slice 25 gained `owes ←` 18 (the inherited ensure); **N3** the stale "27 slices" count → "27 integer-numbered + Slices OG/SG"; **N4** slices 20/24's operation-instance authoring made **conditional** (confirm against operations + [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md)), matching 21/22 and removing an over-assertion that boot/build-orch *are* operation instances; **N5** Slice SG's "`skill` location dir name" leaf corrected (location is platform-fixed at `.claude/skills/` by [topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) law 4, not an authored leaf). The build-status coupling note was reconciled to the S1 refinement. **Propagation:** `wbs/core-build-roadmap.md` (slices 12/18/20/23/24/25 + Slice SG + the M1/retirement counts + the build-status note); this entry. No locked doc touched; `python3 validate.py` green.

## Why

The audit is the project's own build-readiness gate applied to the built artifact, done correctly this time — checkers over a cohesive single-author build, fed canonical context, calibrated against over-tagging — and it converged cleanly (mechanical bookkeeping, no design defects, no locked-doc contradictions), which is the evidence the coupling pass is sound. Ground-truthing every admissible finding before acting, rather than relaying, is the standing discipline after [D-163](0163-correct-d-162-s-8-is-a-build-conformance-deviation-fix-the-b.md).

## What we ruled out

**Relay the audit findings as-is** (rejected — the orchestrator ground-truthed each against source; S1's fix was sharpened beyond the checker's "add `owes ←` 12" to the faithful scent-consumes-policy resolution). **Treat slice 23 as an output-contract consumer** (rejected — the scent does not consume the partition+budget contract; it reads the policy threshold, so the pointer is scoped to the policy). **Keep slices 20/24 asserting operation-instance authoring** (rejected — unverified against [modules/core](../spec/modules/core.md); made conditional per OG's own confirm-at-slice rule). **Edit [D-164](0164-surgical-coupling-pass-on-the-core-build-roadmap-declare-cro.md)'s "12 → 20/23/24" in place** (rejected — append-only; this entry refines it).
