# Litigate engine-template #360: the memory "sweep reads the raw ledger unfiltered" absolute is now false; the consolidation sweep curates harness-injected pseudo-turns out of its fuel and pending-detection (a capture-side axis, independent of recall-exclusion); re-litigate locked [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) — re-lock lands under D-280

*Decided 2026-07-04 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

A build session in engine-template surfaced a locked-doc inexactness, opened it as engine-authored [engine-template #360](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/360), and correctly did **not** edit `engine-planning` from the build — routing it here for litigation. **The trigger:** merged [engine-template PR #359](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/pull/359) (memory Slice A of the [#274](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/274) anchor) makes the consolidation sweep skip **harness-injected pseudo-turns** — a background-agent `<task-notification>` and the `/compact` continuation banner, which Claude Code injects as `user`-role turns and which were being consolidated into the operator's episodic record as junk. The locked [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) doc states the *"consolidation **sweep reads the raw ledger unfiltered**"* (*Retrieval*), now literally false. **The diagnosis — clarify-and-confirm, NOT a build divergence (the [D-277](0277-litigate-engine-template-353-first-run-dead-on-arrival-in-a.md) shape does not apply):** the word "unfiltered" was authored to protect one guarantee — [D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)'s **recall-exclusion never narrows the sweep** (recall ⊥ sweep) — never to mandate feeding *harness machinery* to the consolidator. #359 honors that guarantee (it does not narrow the sweep on the recall axis) and introduces a **new, orthogonal capture-side axis** the doc never contemplated: consolidation fuel is the operator's genuine conversation, not harness scaffolding — consistent with *Capture*'s **"episodic curation, not raw verbatim"** and the prior scaffolding-capture fix ([engine-template #331](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/pull/331), cited in [D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)). Every injected record stays physically resident and fully recoverable; nothing the design promises is lost. **This entry is the litigation alarm + operator authorization** (given in-session over a four-lens plan-stage cold audit) **and the design of record**; the **re-lock of [memory](../spec/systems/cognitive/memory.md) lands under [D-280](0280-resolve-re-lock-memory-the-consolidation-sweep-curates-harne.md)** after the mandatory landed-text cold audit — the carrier **edited-but-not-relocked** until D-280 (the [D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)/[D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md) authorize→resolve pattern; `validate.py` flags its lock fingerprint until the relock).

  **Scoping [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)'s append-only "unfiltered" (the [feasibility]/[adversarial] lenses caught the second occurrence):** the same phrasing appears in [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)'s resolve entry ("the sweep still reads the raw ledger unfiltered (fuel intact)") — that log is **append-only and is not edited**. D-274 wrote it against a **recall-exclusion threat model** (the concern was that recall-membership filtering must not shrink the sweep), and on that axis it remains **true**: recall-exclusion never reaches the sweep's fuel. #359's filter is a **different, orthogonal axis** (harness-scaffolding, not recall-membership) that D-274 never contemplated. This entry **supersedes the incidental absolute** so a future cold reader grepping "unfiltered" lands on the correction, not the stale phrasing.

  **The laws the carrier settles (a four-lens plan-stage cold audit sharpened the scope and wording; none is a deferred leaf):** **(1) Fuel-curation is a capture-side axis, independent of recall-exclusion.** The sweep withholds harness-injected pseudo-turns from **both** its consolidation fuel **and** its pending-detection (read and detection must agree, or the sweep loops on an all-injected session). This is orthogonal to the [D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md) recall-membership axis — the recall ⊥ sweep orthogonality is preserved verbatim (a delta excluded *from recall* is never removed from the sweep on that account). **(2) The boundary is mechanical, never a salience judgment.** The only content withheld is a **whole, distinctive, standalone injected block that never fuses with a human turn** — recognized by ground-truthed platform sentinels, a closed set the engine owns; a fused block (e.g. `<system-reminder>`, which Claude Code fuses with a human prompt in one captured turn — deliberately **not** filtered by #359) stays full fuel, so **no operator content is lost**; the filter may **never** extend to skipping "noisy"/"low-signal" *genuine* turns. This weld is what keeps the axis from becoming a "drop what looks noisy" license. **(3) Firewall intact — never a step toward erasure.** Each injected record stays **physically resident and fully recoverable**, is never demoted or recall-excluded *by this*; physical erasure stays reachable **only** through *Active forgetting* / *Compaction*'s audit-adjudicated, merge-gated path. The "demotion can never reach erasure" firewall holds. **(4) The all-injected-session narrowing is owned, not glossed.** Because a genuine completed turn always yields a genuine-conversation delta, a session whose deltas are *all* harness-injected has **no genuine reflection to recover** — its records stay resident, and it is correctly not flagged pending forever. The durability safety-net ("an abandoned session loses the reflection, not the content") is **not** narrowed: there is no genuine reflection in an all-injected session to lose. **(5) Injectedness is a recomputable recognition, not a curation tag.** It is a property of the record's text (recognizable by the sentinels), so the locked "ambient deltas are untyped / carry no role" invariant (*Typing*, the memory↔knowledge link) stands; whether injectedness is *persisted* as a capture-time tag or recomputed from text is a build-spec leaf.

## Why

the locked doc asserted an *absolute* ("unfiltered") that is now false, and the *reason* it is false — a second, capture-side curation axis independent of recall-exclusion — was **unwritten in canon**, the identical unwritten-contract ambiguity that produced [#332](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/332)/[#360](https://github.com/StarshipSuperjam/engine-template/issues/360). The honest, minimal fix is **not** a build-owe-only log entry a cold builder never reads (the [D-268](0268-adjudicate-engine-template-250-the-15-weakening-guard-s-guar.md) shape) but a narrow re-litigation that closes the ambiguity in the doc the builder *does* read — the [D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md) move, one axis over. #359 is confirmed conformant because it honors the guarantee "unfiltered" protected (recall-exclusion never narrows the sweep) while adding an orthogonal axis, so the [D-277](0277-litigate-engine-template-353-first-run-dead-on-arrival-in-a.md) "build must fix its side" shape does not apply. The capability is memory's by [D-061](0061-lock-the-memory-system-wave-2-the-episodic-ledger-store-q3-b.md) (sole owner of the capture+recall mechanism), so the fuel-curation law lands in the memory system doc and its glossary terms; the bundle enumerates implementation and widens nothing. The authorize→resolve-after-landed-text-audit split keeps the irreversible re-lock behind its own clean gate ([D-273](0273-litigate-engine-template-332-ambient-turn-deltas-dominate-me.md)/[D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md)).

## What we ruled out

**Keep "unfiltered" literal and revert/rework #359** (rejected — the literal reading protects no real guarantee: episodic records are curated narratives, not transcripts, and verbatim is always recoverable from the ledger; insisting on it would mandate feeding harness machinery to the consolidator, entrenching the exact defect #359 fixes — the pedantic-literal reading). **Fix only the *Retrieval* "unfiltered" word, leave *Durability* verify-no-edit** (rejected — the adversarial B1 lens showed #359 changed the *detection* predicate, so the *Durability* universal "always sweep-visible" is false on the load-bearing safety-net path; a re-lock that fingerprints a false durability sentence is the [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md) missed-path failure repeated). **Word the new bullet as "the summary is built from genuine conversation, not machinery" without the sentinel boundary** (rejected — an open door a volume-pressured cold builder walks through to "skip low-signal turns," a soft step toward content loss and the birth-prior keep/discard gate *Capture* forbids; the boundary must be the operative, mechanically-falsifiable clause). **Mint a new glossary term or a new risk entry** (rejected — [R6](../reference/risks.md)/[D-255](0255-sharpen-the-post-v1-revisit-triggers-for-product-knowledge-g.md) anti-sprawl; the existing *Ambient capture*/*Deferred consolidation* terms carry the carve-out and the coupling residual is R16-class, benign degrade-to-prior-behavior). **Add an operator-facing disclosure (the [D-274](0274-resolve-re-lock-memory-ambient-turn-deltas-excluded-from-rec.md) content-floor)** (rejected — the operator lens confirmed nothing the operator observes changes and the D-274 trigger does not fire; a disclosure here would be §7 clutter, not honesty). **Write the [D-280](0280-resolve-re-lock-memory-the-consolidation-sweep-curates-harne.md) resolve entry now** (rejected — it records a landed-text cold audit that runs later; authoring it pre-audit would dress un-audited as audited, the [§7](../principles.md) violation this pattern exists to prevent).
