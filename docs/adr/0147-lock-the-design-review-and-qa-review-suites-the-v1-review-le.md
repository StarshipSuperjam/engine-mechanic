# Lock the design-review and qa-review suites (the v1 review-lens roster): the eighth and ninth module locks

*Decided 2026-05-30 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

Ratify and **lock** [design-review](../spec/modules/design-review.md) and [qa-review](../spec/modules/qa-review.md) (`status: designed → locked`) — the two `optional` agent-suite rosters that fill build-orchestration's plan-review and pre-submission-review gates (the v1 review-lens roster, [D-066](0066-the-4-4-review-lens-roster-two-stage-suites-mirroring-the-en.md)), the **eighth and ninth module locks**, bringing locks.yaml to **38 docs** (29 systems + 9 modules). Both became lock-eligible when [Q32](../reference/open-questions.md) resolved to **keep the persona-file design** ([D-145](0145-resolve-q32-keep-the-locked-persona-file-agent-realization-n.md)) — their only gate — neither owing any edit for that resolution. **Both module-lock gates met:** the **build-readiness precondition** at the dry-run (dry-run-ledger: both BUILD-READY), and a **combined four-lens cold-session design audit** (adversarial · technical-feasibility · non-engineer-operator · architect) against the pair. **design-review returned RATIFY on all four lenses**, no blocking/serious (the "mirrors the Engine's own cold-session audit" claim is an accurate aggregate analogy already locked verbatim into [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md):143-145 and the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md), not an overstated 4→4 mapping; the risk-governance↔security-governance pairing, the disclosed-no-op, and the consume-all-four coherence relay all faithfully cite their locked owners). **qa-review carried two serious findings, both remediated in-doc before the lock** (designed-doc clauses, no locked-doc touch, no litigation alarm): (1) the architect lens found the `output-contract`→[`finding.v1`](../spec/systems/surfaces/schemas.md) binding **named in design-review but absent from qa-review** — for a doc being locked the binding to the locked finding contract must be explicit — fixed by naming "reports findings via the uniform `output-contract` while the orchestrator decides and writes" (mirroring design-review); (2) the non-engineer-operator lens found qa-review introduces the **dry-run code-execution power** with sound engineering containment but **no stated obligation to disclose to the operator, in plain language, that the engine executed their code** — the invisible side-effect-bearing action a walk-away non-engineer is most exposed to — fixed by adding that a dry-run is "disclosed to the operator in plain language … through the gate's operator-facing rendering ([build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md)), never run silently" (the obligation stated, its rendering homed to build-orchestration's existing operator-communication-law surface — a [§16](../principles.md) relay, no locked-doc edit). The technical-feasibility lens **ground-truthed every platform mechanism against current docs**: custom `.claude/agents/` personas with the engine's extra `role`/`lens` frontmatter (tolerated by convention, the locked [agents](../spec/systems/surfaces/agents.md):86-87 hedge), the file-read derived roster ([§14](../principles.md)), read-only `tools`/`permissionMode` personas, and — a strengthening — qa-review's dry-run is **more** supported than the doc assumed via the documented first-class `isolation: worktree` field; it confirmed the agent `model:`-frontmatter bug (#43869, still open) and that the design's pass-`model`-at-spawn workaround is the live path (fixed v2.1.146), a [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) realization concern, not a defect in either module. **Nit dispositions (none blocking):** the "mirror" analogy hardening, the unnamed dry-run lens, and the `status`-axis double-word were left (accurate as-is / build-spec leaf / cross-module convention). A **focused two-lens cold re-check of the qa-review remediation was offered and declined by the operator** in favor of locking on the audit-prescribed fixes + green validation. **Propagation (floor only):** each module's `status` (via `lock.py`) + the locks.yaml entries (ratified_by D-147) + this entry. **No edit owed** to [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md) (design-maturity not catalog-tracked [D-090](0090-flesh-the-validators-core-module-doc-to-designed-the-engine.md); the `optional | core` rows + `core →` edges unchanged), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) (indexes systems, not module lock-state — the roster mention is truthful), [glossary.md](../reference/glossary.md) (the lens terms are already covered), [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) ([R10](../reference/risks.md) review-proportionality is build-orchestration-owned and untouched), [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) (it already names both rosters + the consume-all-four rule as locked text, locked at [D-073](0073-lock-build-orchestration-wave-3-terminal-and-re-litigate-con.md) citing these `designed` modules — a pure status flip with no inbound edit), or the CLAUDE.md document map. **No locked-doc edit, no litigation alarm** — the lock consumes the locked agents/build-orchestration/schemas/validation/core laws and changes none. `python3 lock.py modules/design-review/README.md --decision D-147`; `python3 lock.py modules/qa-review/README.md --decision D-147`; `python3 validate.py` green.

## Why

The two suites are the matched halves of one roster ([D-066](0066-the-4-4-review-lens-roster-two-stage-suites-mirroring-the-en.md)), edit-free under the [Q32](#) keep-persona-files resolution, build-ready at the dry-run, and audited together — so a single combined cold audit and one ratifying entry is the proportionate gate, the [contract-threshold](#) discipline applied to a tightly-coupled pair. design-review audited clean; qa-review's two serious findings were bounded designed-doc clauses the audit prescribed exactly (name the output-contract channel; state the code-execution disclosure obligation), both adapting the `designed` doc to existing locked law rather than re-litigating it — so the fixes landed in-line and the lock proceeded on green validation. The pair locks atop the now-locked `core` and the locked agents/build-orchestration grammar — no churning neighbor.

## What we ruled out

**Lock qa-review with the two serious findings unresolved** (rejected — the output-contract binding and the code-execution disclosure are real gaps the cold audit surfaced; locking through them would freeze them, ranking with a silent edit to a locked doc). **Re-litigate locked build-orchestration to add the code-execution disclosure there** (rejected — the obligation is stated in qa-review and rendered through build-orchestration's existing operator-communication-law surface, a §16 relay; no locked-doc change is needed, so none is made). **Lock the two modules under separate decisions** (rejected — they are the matched review-lens pair, audited together on one combined cold audit; one ratifying entry plus two lock.py invocations is the proportionate record, the design→build→QA roster being a single [D-066](0066-the-4-4-review-lens-roster-two-stage-suites-mirroring-the-en.md) unit). **Run the focused re-check before locking** (offered; the operator declined in favor of the audit-prescribed fixes + green validation, the bounded-clause remediation not warranting a further cold pass). **Edit catalog/architecture/glossary/risks to mark the locks** (rejected — no propagation owed beyond the floor; design-maturity is not catalog-tracked and build-orchestration already carries both rosters as locked text).
