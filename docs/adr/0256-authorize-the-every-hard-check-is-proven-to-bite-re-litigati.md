# Authorize the "every hard check is proven to bite" re-litigation: a mandatory, standing, CI-enforced negative-fixture meta-check (logic-unit scope; a validation/check-system-local invariant citing [§7](../principles.md)/[§15](../principles.md), no new principle); re-litigate locked [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) + [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) + [validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md) (+ [core](../spec/modules/core.md) adjudication; [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) conditional); re-locks land under D-257…

*Decided 2026-06-27 in the design workspace.*

## The decision

The operator (the non-engineer sole maintainer) observed that `engine-ci` is now a real gate whose correctness increasingly rests on committed `custom/script` checks — so the engine's safety is migrating into *script behavior, script test coverage, plain-language messages, custom/script env handling, and the validator's interpretation of findings* — the **checker-of-checkers** problem. Today a `hard` check can be present, registered, runnable, and green-on-everything while its logic is a no-op or mis-aimed; the only adjacent treatment is [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md):23's after-the-fact telemetry flag of "a rule that never fires as **possibly inert**" — posture, not a gate. The operator's rule: **a check without a negative test is not yet a trustworthy gate.** **This entry is the litigation alarm + operator authorization** (given across a full design session — observation → plan → four-lens cold audit → re-grounding to the live tail) **and the design of record**; the **re-locks land under per-carrier resolve entries** (D-257 validation, D-258 check, D-259 validators-core, then the D-260 [core](../spec/modules/core.md)/[repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) adjudication) after each carrier's mandatory **landed-text cold audit** — the [D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)/[D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) authorize-then-resolve pattern.

  **The design.** Make *every hard check is proven to bite* a **mandatory, standing, CI-enforced** requirement, realized as a deterministic **negative-fixture meta-check**: for each in-scope hard check the meta-check runs it against a committed **negative fixture** (a seeded bad input) and asserts the expected `hard` finding by **set-membership** (the finding id/severity is present — never an order/count assert, since the finding stream is not a [§19](../principles.md) source-deterministic member). It is the **up-front enforcing correlate** to the weak after-the-fact "possibly inert" flag, and it rides the existing CI suite as a `custom/script` rule-data instance in [validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md) — the [D-220](0220-resolve-the-d-219-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-land.md) first-run reference-closure check's exact shape (hard, CI, `custom/script`, honest about its reach) — **no sixth closed kind** ([R6](../reference/risks.md)).

  **Scope — logic-unit.** The unit that must carry a negative fixture is the **check-logic unit**: every check-**kind callable** (the five closed core kinds + any module-added kind) carries ≥1 negative fixture proving the *kind* bites, and every **`custom/script` instance** carries its own (each script is its own logic). Data rules of a proven kind **inherit the kind's proof**. **Honest bound, named not papered over:** the meta-check proves *kind/logic bite*, not *rule aim* — a mis-aimed rule of a proven kind (a `schema` rule referencing the wrong schema, a `presence` rule whose glob matches nothing) is **not** caught here, and is **not** backstopped by `coverage`/`coherence` (which check surface-homes and installed-set agreement, never a rule's aim); rule-aim stays at the [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md):23 "never-fired → possibly inert" telemetry posture, which this **reconciles with** (the up-front enforcing correlate to that after-the-fact flag), not duplicates.

  **Law status — a validation/check-system-local invariant citing [§7](../principles.md), NOT principle text.** The rule "a hard check must be proven to bite" is sited as a **system-local invariant** that **instances [§7](../principles.md)** (a non-biting `hard` check is posture dressed as enforcement — the exact tier-honesty §7 forbids violating) and is reflexively kin to **[§15](../principles.md)** (the guard must not be falsifiable by what it judges). This is the exact altitude the [D-219](0219-authorize-the-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-a-retire.md) travel-safety reference-closure invariant got — and [D-219](0219-authorize-the-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-a-retire.md)'s altitude doctrine is explicit: a single-system instance "does not earn a numbered §20… promote only on a second instance." [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) (same day) reaffirmed the same discipline — "**APPLIED, not amended; no new principle**" ([R6](../reference/risks.md)/the contract-threshold keep principles exceptional). So **no new principle and no new §7 text**: [§17](../principles.md) gets only a **light touch** (the meta-check raises the share of a change carrying a non-AI correlate — the [R16](../reference/risks.md) goal), and [§19](../principles.md)'s *exercise-vs-enforce* framing is cross-referenced (the green run *exercises*; the fixture *enforces* the bite).

  **The honesty carve-outs.** The mandate is "a negative fixture per statically **decidable** failure path," **except** checks whose correct behavior is **environment-conditionally vacuous**: the [D-219](0219-authorize-the-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-a-retire.md) reference-closure check's *computed-path* leg (best-effort by its own locked text, never a guarantee) and its **no-op-after-first-run-retirement** state; and **`ci_author_exempt`** rules (whose correct outcome is a *disclosed not-applicable pass*, not a hard fail). These resolve to a **disclosed not-applicable** in the meta-check — the existing `ci_author_exempt` disclosed-no-op grammar — proven at construction where the machinery exists, never a synthetic retired-asset fixture shipped into a generated repo ([§4](../principles.md) ship-substrate-not-data / the travel-safety invariant). The **fixture unit varies by kind**: a single bad file for schema/shape/presence; a malformed **mini-tree** for the repo-global coverage/coherence kinds.

  **Fixtures discovered by presence; isolation is new grammar.** A negative fixture is **discovered by naming/location convention** ([§14](../principles.md) derived-binding), so the [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) surface gains the *law* (a hard logic-unit's bite is proven by a discovered negative fixture) and **not a ninth rule field** — no check-schema re-lock, [schemas](../spec/systems/surfaces/schemas.md) verify-no-edit. Fixtures live in a **reserved, glob-excluded, coverage-exempt namespace** (test data, not a surface), so a committed bad input neither reds the real suite nor is flagged as an uncatalogued/orphan surface — whether this needs a [repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) clause is the D-260 adjudication (if so, topology joins the re-lit set — a justified edit, not dodged).

  **Pin the `custom/script` process→result contract.** The operator's "fails closed on **missing script**, **nonzero exit**, **unreadable JSON output**" is **not in the corpus today** (the canonical `custom/script` semantics are the read-only escape hatch + derive-and-compare + no pass-token); this design **adds** the three fail-closed modes mapped onto the [`finding.v1`](../spec/systems/surfaces/schemas.md) result contract, and the negative fixtures exercise **each** mode — closing the "custom/script env handling" and "finding interpretation" risk surfaces the operator named, not only the nonzero-exit path. The meta-check needs a **run-one-rule-against-a-substituted-target** entry point on the directly-callable dispatcher; whether [core](../spec/modules/core.md) already exposes it (verify-no-edit, the [D-220](0220-resolve-the-d-219-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-land.md) precedent) or gains a seam bullet (the [D-207](0207-authorize-the-dependabot-pr-contract-exemption-a-ci-author-a.md) precedent) is the D-260 adjudication.

  **Self-reference, stated precisely.** The meta-check is itself a `hard` CI check carrying its **own** negative fixture (a seeded hard logic-unit with a missing/non-biting fixture must turn it red) — the [§15](../principles.md) non-falsifiable-guard reflexivity. This terminates the *regress* (no meta-meta-check needed); it does **not** claim liveness-against-production — the fixture proves a *witnessed* negative trips the kind today, never completeness against all negatives or stability under drift, the [§7](../principles.md) ceiling the [D-219](0219-authorize-the-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-a-retire.md)/[D-220](0220-resolve-the-d-219-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-land.md) check carries verbatim.

  **Module-added kinds own their fixture; reversibility.** A kind-providing module ships its kind's negative fixture **co-located with the callable**, so the meta-check **fails closed** on a present in-scope kind with no fixture, and uninstall is a pure file-removal — the [§14](../principles.md)/[R5](../reference/risks.md) derived-by-presence containment, [migration-discipline](../spec/modules/migration-discipline.md)-shaped self-removing ([D-220](0220-resolve-the-d-219-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-land.md) precedent), never a centralized fixture stranded as an orphan on uninstall.

  **Operator-facing honesty reaches a surface.** The new strength makes the green check *more* trusted, so the bound must reach where the operator reads, not only [risks.md](../reference/risks.md): [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md)'s *"What the operator is told"* gains the clause that a passing check means **the gate was shown to catch a known bad case — proof the gate works, not proof the change is correct** (the [§7](../principles.md)/[§17](../principles.md) over-trust guard, the sibling of [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)'s "a promise, not proof — never two greens"). The [§12](../principles.md) leak guard applies: the maintainer vocabulary (*meta-check, negative fixture, logic-unit, bite*) stays out of operator strings; the plain rendering is *"every safety check the engine runs is itself tested against an example it should catch, so a check can't quietly pass by doing nothing."*

  **D-231 asymmetry, justified.** Checks become mandatory-mechanical while [D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md)'s `demo`/`demo-*` subcommand population stays at its **optional posture floor**, because the populations differ in trust-load: a **check IS the gate the operator's trust routes through** (a green required check reads as *verified*), whereas a demo is *evidence about a surface*. A false-green check directly corrupts the operator's one independently-corroborable signal, so it earns the stricter bar; demos are unchanged here, and demo parity would be a separate [D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md) question. This is the deliberate **posture→mechanical upgrade** for the check corpus, accepting a permanent authoring tax (every future hard logic-unit must ship its negative fixture or the meta-check reds) as the trade for a trustworthy gate.

  **Reconciliation with [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)–[D-254](0254-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-filler-renders-the-o.md) (same day).** The [§17](../principles.md) operator-runnable behavioral correlate they bound into the PR **Review** record was correctly left **posture** because its class-selection is uncorroborable AI self-judgment (no non-AI correlate to gate on); the meta-check is the **converse** — a committed bad input → an asserted finding **is** a deterministic non-AI correlate — so a mechanical gate is the honest tier, exactly [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)'s "gate where a non-AI correlate exists, else posture." The meta-check **strengthens** the "tests pass = the Validation green the operator sees" correlate [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) catalogued (it makes that green honest) **without touching the Review record**: [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md) ([D-253](0253-resolve-re-lock-control-plane-the-review-record-carries-the.md)) and [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) ([D-254](0254-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-filler-renders-the-o.md)) are **verify-no-edit** (check-suite membership is derived and the CI harness "owns neither check content nor membership," so no new required-check name; the operator-honesty clause is validation's mechanical-green tier, not Review). [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md)/[validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md)/[check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) re-lock; [core](../spec/modules/core.md)/[repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) are the D-260 adjudication.

  **Build-conformance — a strengthening, not a contradiction.** wbs/build-conformance.md §10 retires each load-bearing-check behavioral falsification "once a permanent regression test covers the behavior"; the standing meta-check **is** that permanent regression target, so the checker-of-checkers falsification **retires into it** rather than evaporating at v1 — converting the [R16](../reference/risks.md) behavioral correlate for the checker-of-checkers class from a retiring construction artifact into a standing CI correlate (non-locked carrier edit).

  **Q31.** The meta-check supplies the **mechanical** floor of post-v1 engine self-conformance (CI-enforced, survives v1, no AI in the loop); [Q31](../reference/open-questions.md)'s genuine residual — a standing **AI conformance-to-design** cross-check — is untouched, so Q31 is **narrowed in place** (no banner), never closed.

  **Carriers + sequence + non-locked propagation.** Dependency-ordered: [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md) (D-257, the execution model + suite/tier + the custom/script contract pin + the operator-honesty clause), [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md) (D-258, the by-presence fixture law + the :23 reconciliation), [validators-core](../spec/modules/validators-core.md) (D-259, the meta-check rule-data instance + self-coverage + module-kind fail-closed/reversibility), then the [core](../spec/modules/core.md)+[repository-topology](../spec/systems/infrastructure/repository-topology.md) adjudication (D-260). **Non-locked propagation** after the re-locks: [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) (a "negative fixture" concept — **avoid "demonstration"**, the [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)/[D-254](0254-resolve-re-lock-build-orchestration-the-filler-renders-the-o.md) `Behavioral attestation`-collision caution; reconcile the demos-vs-checks asymmetry + the :23 flag), [principles.md](../principles.md) (the light §17 touch + §7/§15/§19 cross-refs, no new principle), wbs/build-conformance.md (the retire-into), [risks.md](../reference/risks.md) (the witnessed-negative-≠-complete residual + the false-green mitigation + the authoring-tax trade), [open-questions.md](../reference/open-questions.md) (the Q31 narrow), [engine-architecture.md](../architecture.md) (index touch only if warranted). **Verify-no-edit (dispositions recorded, the [D-251](0251-reject-engine-template-255-s-explore-gate-memory-carve-out-t.md) discipline):** [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md), [build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md), [schemas](../spec/systems/surfaces/schemas.md), [modules/README.md](../reference/module-catalog.md), [scenarios/](../architecture.md#first-run-provisioning). **Build-owe:** an engine-template issue (filed there, via the control-plane body contract + `engine` label) implements the meta-check + fixtures namespace + the pinned `custom/script` contract + authors the negative fixtures for the existing in-scope hard-check set — complete-final-state, not new-checks-only.

## Why

The gap is real and load-bearing: the north star puts the burden of proof on the engine, and a `hard` check that cannot be shown to fire is exactly posture dressed as enforcement ([§7](../principles.md)) at the one surface the operator can independently corroborate (the CI green). The honest fix is the **up-front enforcing correlate** to the weak [check](../spec/systems/surfaces/check.md):23 "possibly inert" flag, and **mechanical is the correct tier precisely because a deterministic non-AI correlate exists** — the [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) line drawn the other way for the same reason. Reusing the [D-220](0220-resolve-the-d-219-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-land.md) reference-closure shape (a `custom/script` CI check enforcing a self-referential integrity invariant, honest about its reach) keeps the foundation small ([R6](../reference/risks.md)) and the altitude correct ([D-219](0219-authorize-the-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-a-retire.md)/[D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md): no new principle). The four-lens cold audit shrank and hardened the change — it surfaced the aim-residual (logic-unit proves bite, not aim), the environment-conditional carve-outs (reference-closure no-op / computed-path / `ci_author_exempt`), the fixture-isolation grammar, the unpinned `custom/script` contract, and the operator-honesty-must-reach-a-surface fix — so the authorized design states its bounds rather than overclaiming. Authorize-now / resolve-per-carrier keeps each irreversible re-lock behind its own landed-text audit ([D-244](0244-re-litigate-product-design-into-a-first-class-spec-driven-de.md)/[D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)).

## What we ruled out

**Presence-only (a fixture exists but is not executed)** — rejected (a fixture that cannot make the check fire is the `assert True` failure mode at the fixture layer; bite must be *observed*, the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) "must be able to fail" shape). **Per-rule-instance scope (every hard rule its own fixture)** — rejected (fixture-bloat + redundant for data rules of a proven kind; the logic-unit is where bespoke logic lives, and the operator never sees the scope nuance — the trust claim is identical under both). **`custom/script`-only scope** — rejected (leaves a future hard core/module kind unproven-to-bite; the principle is general to check logic). **A sixth closed core kind (`meta`/`fixture`)** — rejected ([R6](../reference/risks.md); the closed kind set is load-bearing-closed; ride `custom/script`, the [D-220](0220-resolve-the-d-219-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-land.md) shape). **Mint a numbered §20 principle** — rejected (single-system instance; the [D-219](0219-authorize-the-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-a-retire.md) altitude doctrine + [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md) "APPLIED, not amended"; promote only on recurrence across systems). **Edit §7/§17 principle text** — considered (the operator's first instinct) but **not taken** (heavier than the system-local invariant the corpus used for the structural twin [D-219](0219-authorize-the-first-run-travel-safety-re-litigation-a-retire.md); the operator confirmed the lighter siting). **Fixture-as-posture, audited-not-enforced (the build-conformance-only / [D-231](0231-promote-the-in-tool-demo-subcommand-as-a-governed-ai-run-sta.md) optional floor)** — rejected for the check corpus (posture for the gate the operator trusts is advisory theatre at the one place mechanical enforcement is warranted; this is the deliberate posture→mechanical upgrade, authoring-tax accepted). **A CI check that runs a `demo` and gates on exit-0** — rejected (already rejected at [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md): an AI-scripted exit-0 shows only the AI's chosen path; the meta-check is categorically different — it runs a *check* against a *seeded bad input*, deterministic and falsifiable, not an AI-driven demo). **Treat this as the [R30](../reference/risks.md)/[D-225](0225-recenter-the-spec-on-the-ai-is-the-thing-made-trustworthy-re.md) banned-word / prose-grading anti-pattern** — rejected (the meta-check verifies *check behavior*, a deterministic fact, never prose quality). **Leave the over-trust bound only in [risks.md](../reference/risks.md)** — rejected (it must reach the operator-facing surface read at the merge — the [§17](../principles.md) "in the artifact" rule, the non-engineer-operator lens fix). **Reopen [control-plane](../spec/systems/infrastructure/control-plane.md)/[build-orchestration](../spec/systems/lifecycle/build-orchestration.md) (re-locked today) or [validation](../spec/systems/guardrails/validation.md)'s Review path** — rejected (membership is derived, the harness is the seam, and the honesty clause is the mechanical-green tier — the [D-252](0252-authorize-binding-the-17-operator-runnable-behavioral-correl.md)/[D-248](0248-resolve-re-lock-qa-review-the-spec-conformance-referent-is-t.md) consume-without-reopening precedent).
